    Storing with Hedonism Wines

    Secure, Professional Storage for Your Fine Wines

    Hedonism Wines is pleased to offer secure, fully insured and professionally managed storage for both under-bond and duty-paid wines and spirits. Whether purchased from Hedonism Wines or transferred from an existing collection, your wines are stored in optimal conditions until you are ready to enjoy them.

    Why Choose Storage with Hedonism Wines?

    Your wines and spirits will be stored at our specialist storage facility in Essex, just outside London. With over a decade of experience handling and storing fine wines and spirits, the facility maintains strict temperature and humidity controls to ensure your collection is preserved in optimal conditions.

    Key benefits include:

    How Our Storage Service Works

    1. Purchase en primeur, under-bond or duty-paid wines from Hedonism Wines.
    2. Enjoy twelve months of complimentary storage when you open a storage account with us.
    3. After twelve months, storage will continue at the rates outlined above, with charges invoiced quarterly.
    4. When you’re ready to receive your wines, simply request delivery – most orders are fulfilled within 3–4 working days.
