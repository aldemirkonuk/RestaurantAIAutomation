Privacy & data

What Mudavym stores, what leaves your browser, and what you control.

Cookies

Mudavym sets no tracking or advertising cookies. We don't use a cookie-consent banner because there is nothing to consent to. Your sign-in session is kept in your browser's local storage instead of a cookie, and it is cleared when you sign out.

Signing in with Google

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, and profile picture from Google to identify your account. We never receive your Google password, and signing in this way grants no access to your Gmail or Drive.

Connected integrations

Connecting Google Drive or Microsoft Excel grants Mudavym permission to write files on your behalf. We request the narrowest scopes that work — access is limited to files Mudavym creates, not your whole drive. The access and refresh tokens are encrypted before being stored, and you can revoke a connection at any time from Settings → Integrations, which also revokes it at the provider.

Product analytics

Interaction telemetry is off unless your deployment explicitly enables it and you turn on Usage analytics in Settings. When it is on, what leaves the browser is a page name, an event type, an optional element name, and a number — never text you typed, never text the app rendered, and never the contents of your inventory.

Error and performance monitoring

When a deployment configures error tracking (Sentry), crashes and slow requests are reported so we can fix them. What is sent is technical: the error type and stack trace, the page or request involved, browser and app-version details, and two opaque identifiers — your account id and your restaurant id — which mean nothing outside our own database. Your email address and your name are never sent. Reports are scrubbed of contact details, addresses and credentials before they leave the app, and request parameters are reported by name without their values. If no error-tracking key is configured, nothing is sent at all.

Sharing with partners

Data sharing with logistics and POS partners is off by default and stays off until you turn it on and confirm which partner you are connecting. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Your controls

  • Review permissions and analytics in Settings → Services & permissions.
  • Disconnect integrations in Settings → Integrations.
  • Unlink a sign-in provider or delete your account from your profile.
