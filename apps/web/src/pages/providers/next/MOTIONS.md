# ProvidersNext — motions, canonical

Two motions, both from `src/lib/mudavym/motion.ts`. This page is deliberately
the quietest in the set: the founder's MERGE verdict prized "small buckets,
less crowded", and a grid that shimmers contradicts it.

| id | token | curve · ms | fires |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pv-sheet-settle` | `settle` | HOUSE · 320ms | the TwinSheet sliding in from the right when a bucket card opens; 24px travel + fade, `prefers-reduced-motion` renders it in place |
| `pv-card-ink` | `ink` | HOUSE · 160ms | bucket-card hover/focus — border warms to the seal ring, ground lifts one paper step; nothing translates, nothing scales |

Deliberate non-motions: cards do not stagger in (a vendor roster is a
reference, not an arrival); open-order counts do not tally (they are counts of
record, not live figures); the sheet's close is instant (leaving is not a
ceremony).
