WHAT THE WORD MEANS

Müdavim — from Arabic dawām, continuance — is the Turkish word for the regular: the guest the house counts on, whose table is held, whose glass is remembered, whose tab runs on trust. The house never takes their order; it already knows. Mudavym — the brand’s own spelling, locked in ADR 0001 — is the house side of that promise built as software: an autonomous restaurant backend, wine first, then the whole kitchen, so any restaurant can treat every guest like its müdavim.

WHAT THE MARK SAYS

An A and an M interlocked into one piece of type — Aldemir’s house and the müdavim’s M sharing the same strokes, set the way a lokanta would stamp its own napkins. The A plants its foot on its baseline; the M’s diagonal walks past it and descends deeper — the regular walking in. Four small ticks sit in the counters: tally marks, visits counted. They stay the ink of the mark — quiet, structural, counted.

TRUED, NOT REDESIGNED
  • Both diagonals straightened to one 34° rake (0.68 run per unit drop) — they wandered up to 3° in the print.
  • Widths held constant: stems 72 and 71, the A’s leg 81, the M’s tip-leg 52, the gutter between them exactly 29.
  • All four counter ticks redrawn crisp and seated on their edges.
  • Terminals cut square: flat tip, level cuts. Baselines kept deliberately uneven — the A at 540, the diagonal at 574.
  • Ink-gain round-offs removed; the geometry is square-cut and lets paper do the softening.
