Turn one — five sketches on the experience

· census 102 · ADR 0112 SERVICE {{ clock }} · {{ covers }} COVERS · LINE LIVE

Five sketches, each one an argument about the reader rather than the container. Every one is live: click it, type in it, hold it, leave it. They answer the seven experience faults the founder named — interruption, losing your place, stacking, unsaved work, the phone, the keyboard, and an overlay that never says what it wants. The shapes and the words stay the policy’s: three shapes, one primitive, the close control is words, one chromatic colour, and the line behind the paper keeps moving while you read.

THE BAR  A re-skin fails. Each sketch changes what the reader can do, not what the overlay looks like.
