## Decision

**A late table climbs when it turns late (OD-236's option A), inside an absence chain that climbs at once; a Food-up climbs a fixed delay after it is raised unless it is acknowledged (option B's shape, on the acknowledge it already has).** It is answered here only because of his 16:57:09Z goal message after the unpicked prompt (› Context); OD-236 stays open in the register with this answer marked. Both halves are the narrow, reversible option: neither adds a setting, a `floor_settings` column or a new tap, so a later ruling replaces them in code alone. This is the executor's call under the founder's 2026-10-31T16:57:09Z delegation, not his pick, and so is every rule below that names no other source.

### Absence climbs at once

It applies to the Food-up and late alerts pick 5's option text names, and to a manager's nudge (§ Decision › What managers see, and attribution, in part 2). The chain is the table's waiter (the user on its live `primary` assignment), then the cover (the user on its live `cover` row, pick 3's on-break handoff; `floor_table_assignments`, owed with the port's floor tables), then the manager step. A step is passed over at once, and only when its person is known to be absent or was not reached: absence is known when the alert is sent, so waiting adds nothing.

*On break* also hands over what is open: the waiter's unacknowledged Food-ups on that table and its open late alert pass to the cover, whom the floor's alert scanner pushes a staff copy of each on its next tick, keyed on (the alert, `handoff`, the cover's user id). A cover not reached then is passed over at once: a handed-over Food-up climbs under its one climb key (meal run, threshold), and a late alert's manager copy for that window went with the waiter's (pick 3's option text, "their tables and alerts pass to the cover until 'Back'", AskUserQuestion asked 2026-10-31T03:41:53Z, Q1). *Back*, a Clock out or a new Away hands nothing over: the Food-up still climbs at `warn_after_min`, and a late table's next push reads the chain afresh.

### Known absent

**The house's clock.** Every read is on `houseFrame`'s clock, the house's zone or else its country's only zone (`apps/api-gateway/src/common/house-frame.ts:212`), never UTC (ADR 0296's DASH-G2, `0296:24`). When it resolves none, or the house row cannot be read, the floor makes no presence, Away or quiet-hours read and pushes nothing: each alert takes the failed-read path (§ Decision › ADR 0218's manager step), and `/floor` tells managers the zone is not set. The one real house is so today (`0296:73`); an owner or manager sets it in Settings (`apps/api-gateway/src/settings/settings.controller.ts:378`).

**The service day.** Every day bound is the service day, and service days run back to back. Date D's ends at the later of six elapsed hours after the midnight ending D and an hour after the last window the venue's recorded hours open on D, or at the first window they open on the next date if sooner; unreadable hours count as none, and a date with no window takes the six hours. The constants are main's (`apps/api-gateway/src/notifications/producers/service-day.ts:42`, `:51`); the combination, the cut and the last hour below are the executor's call under the delegation (OD-242). `restaurants.operating_hours` is NULL on every row (`service-day.ts:24-28`), so a day now ends six elapsed hours after midnight: 06:00, or 07:00 and 05:00 on the nights the clocks go forward and back (`:108-114`). A plan row belongs to its `shift_date`'s day; a punch to the day holding its start, at the newest row of its correction chain, or to the next day when it starts in the last hour (`SETTLE_MINUTES_AFTER_HOURS`) before its day ends; a resuming punch (`resumes_punch_id`) to its punch's day. The day before a date is `shiftLocalDate(date, -1)` (`:79-88`), never the instant less 24 hours. This bounds presence only, never ADR 0296's sales day (`apps/api-gateway/src/common/house-day.ts:150-174`).

Owed with the alert scanner: the no-show, off-shift and called-out tests below, and a flag to the manager for each case (the absence flags). Known to be absent means one of these.

- **Away,** when any of three dates falls in one of their windows: the house's calendar date now (ADR 0218's reading), the date of the service day holding now, and the `shift_date` of a plan row of theirs holding now (`shiftWindow`, `apps/api-gateway/src/notifications/producers/shift-window.ts:79-117`, across midnight at `:102`). It is read with `awayWindowsSince` from the earliest of them and `isAwayOn` (`apps/api-gateway/src/areas/area-routing.service.ts:206`; `apps/api-gateway/src/areas/area-routing.ts:121`), never `awayUserIds` (`area-routing.service.ts:168-173`). Away only narrows, as in ADR 0218: no floor push reaches a person on a date it reads as Away, and one Away on the service they work is passed over after midnight too. An open punch alone does not keep a person off Away.
- **On break:** their own *On break* tap (pick 3's handoff, recorded with the floor's acts; it writes the cover rows), in a service day not yet ended, with no *Back* and no floor act of theirs since; or a punch of theirs closed 'break' (`clocked_out_reason`) after their live assignment began, in a service day not yet ended, still their latest at the newest row of each correction chain, with no floor act since (the `a_shift_is_punched` header on `feat/a-shift-is-punched-and-food-is-up`). A cover row a plan or a manager writes makes its person the cover and nobody absent, so two waiters covering each other's tables stay present; a waiter who never clocked in is on break once they tap it. A break from an earlier service day with no *Back* does not hold, in either form, and its cover row makes nobody the cover (the absence flags ask the manager to end it). Within the day an off-shift cover is passed over. The absence flags show the manager a break that a floor act ended without *Back*; wages are unchanged.
- **Clocked out:** a punch closed by Clock out after their live assignment began, with no open punch and no floor act since. The absence flags show each such act with the Clock out and the first and last act after it, offering three remedies: correct the Clock out (a mistap); with no later punch, add one from the first act, written open and closed at a time the manager enters (the last act by default); with a later punch, once closed, correct its start to the first act (an open punch cannot be corrected). The gap is shown beside the plan on ADR 0215's day-end sheet, where no punch confirms hours (ADR 0350 § Decision › A manager marks every shift). Wages stay on planned hours; how a confirmed span counts on a clock-change night is OD-193 (open), not settled here.
- **Called out:** a begun plan row of theirs in state 'callout' (`reportCallout`, `apps/api-gateway/src/team/schedule.service.ts:917`, or a PATCH through `updateShift`, `:734`) whose window holds now or, when none of their rows does, whose service day has not ended, with no floor act of theirs since the later of the Call out and the row's start. A row not yet begun is not read, so the other tests decide until it begins. `isPresenceState` drops it (`shift-window.ts:68-71`); the assignment stays. Its Call-out time, which neither path records (`shifts.updated_at` moves on every edit, `schedule.service.ts:727`), is the first scanner tick that read the row as 'callout' with no read in another state since, a row first read already so (an advance call-out, or one called out before the called-out test deploys) included; the scanner records it on its own alert-state rows, owed with it, never on a new `shifts` column, and a late read errs toward passing the person over. A Call out from the no-show flag keeps them passed over.
- **A standing no-show:** the no-show flag (ADR 0345 § Context › His fourteen picks › pick 6), raised once their row began more than the grace ago with no punch of theirs since, while they have made no floor act (check-in, seat, Paid, claim or take over) since it began and the table was assigned to them before it began.
- **Off shift,** pick 5's first case: no plan row of theirs in a presence state holds now, they have no open punch, and either a row of theirs in a service day not yet ended ended more than the grace ago, their live assignment on the table began before it ended, and they have made no floor act since; or their live assignment on the table began in an ended service day (dated as a punch's start is) and they have made no floor act in this one.

The grace is the house's `late_after_min` (default 10), the house's own figure for how long a table may wait. An acknowledge is not a floor act. Every read is the house's own: nothing in another house makes a person present, absent or reached here (`area-routing.service.ts:215`; `apps/api-gateway/src/notifications/producers/producer-ledger.service.ts:260`).

A waiter or cover not known to be absent counts as working, punched or not, with or without a plan row: the live assignment is evidence of presence (one from an ended service day only alongside a floor act in this one), and an empty schedule must never read as nobody working (`0218:40-43`, Locked; `apps/api-gateway/src/notifications/producers/roster.ts:12-16`).

For the manager step, working means an open punch opened since their live plan row began or, outside one, an open punch whose service day has not ended; else being inside a plan row (`onShiftAt`, `shift-window.ts:126-146`) with no Clock out or still-latest break punch since it began; and either way not on break by their own tap (above). `onShiftAt` returns team-member ids (`:143`), mapped to users by `team_members.user_id` (`supabase/migrations/20260805000000_baseline_from_production.sql:5630`, unique per house at `:11957`); a member with no `user_id` is not reached.

Residuals, accepted as risk: a waiter who works past their row and leaves without Clock out or a release reads as working until the service day ends (the late manager copy still goes and a Food-up climbs at `warn_after_min`); a present waiter who neither punches nor acts within the grace on tables assigned before their row, or has not acted today on an earlier day's tables, misses staff copies until their first act; a manager who forgets Clock out reads as working until the day ends (step 1 still pushes every other working manager; pick 6's forgotten clock-out flag is the remedy); a manager with no plan row whose punch starts before the day's last hour, for a service opening after that day ends (a bakery's 04:30 prep), stops counting then, so step 2 pushes every not-Away owner and manager until a plan row ends it; a manager working past midnight into a date they marked Away is passed over after midnight (ADR 0218's rule); and an alert counted against a holder for a cause other than a punch correction has no correction path here. Revisit each when a house runs covers or managers who never clock in, reports alerts going to people who had left, or a person asks for a figure to be put right.

### Not reached

Not reached means no web push was sent to any of their devices, or quiet hours deferred them (§ Decision › Quiet hours). Sent means the push service accepted it (`sendNotification` resolved, `apps/api-gateway/src/notifications/notifications.service.ts:184`), not that a phone showed it, so the floor says "sent to a device", never "reached". A person with push off for this house, or not a live member of it (`isLiveMembership`, `apps/api-gateway/src/common/tenant/live-membership.ts:39-54`), is not reached either. How floor pushes find devices is OD-238's.

### ADR 0218's manager step

The manager step follows ADR 0218's ladder (`0218:98-109`) over the house's live owners and managers (`houseMembersInRoles` with `OWNER_AND_MANAGER`, `live-membership.ts:62`, `:93-122`, no fallback). Working owners and managers who are not Away come first; when none is working or none is reached, every not-Away owner and manager, by push (step 2). When that reaches nobody (all Away, push off, quiet hours or a failed send), the floor writes a notification-center row with no push to every not-Away owner and manager or, when all are Away, to every owner (every manager in a house with no owner row), as step 3 does (`0218:107-109`, "never waits unseen"). Its `/floor` row then says no owner or manager was sent it, why, and which waiter or cover was, if any; it says nobody was reached only when the waiter, the cover and the manager step were all sent nothing. The ladder's step 0, everyone (`area-routing.ts:16-17`), is not used: the chain is modelled on the ladder, not routed through it.

Each read fails its own way, never read as nobody working, deferred or with push off. A failed member or role read fails the house's tick (no push, no row, no good-tick stamp). A failed Away read counts as nobody Away, as ADR 0218 rules for a register that only narrows an audience (`0218:121-125`): it is logged at error level, the tick sends and is stamped, and the manager copy and the `/floor` row say Away could not be read. A failed push-switch, quiet-hours, plan, punch, assignment, floor-act, Call-out-time or `floor_settings` read, or a house clock with no zone, sends no push and leaves the tick unstamped, but writes the last row once per alert, saying why. At a Food-up tap the same split holds: a failed read other than Away sends no push, still records the meal run, says why on the POST response and the `/floor` row and, unless it is the member or role read, writes the last row; the next good tick climbs that Food-up, unless acknowledged, to the manager step at once. A failed tick is shown: owed, the scanner stamps each house's last good tick, and once that is over two minutes old `/floor` shows managers "Floor alerts not sent since 21:00", so a stopped scanner never reads as a quiet floor (ADR 0020). Which functions it reads through is in § Decision › Which reads the floor uses, in part 2.

### Late tables

At `late_after_min` (default 10, `~/Projects/checker/supabase/migrations/0001_init.sql:31`) the late push goes to the waiter or cover and to the manager step together, as two copies. The staff copy names its house and the table with a state word, with no minutes and no other person's name or reason, since the waiter phone carries no figure (*"Nothing for now"*, AskUserQuestion asked 2026-10-31T03:03:50Z, Q3). The manager copy names its house, who it was sent to and why, and how long the table has waited.

As in the checker's late pass (separate copies, `lib/push.ts:272-285` at `5958689`), it repeats while the table stays late once a 15-minute cooldown has passed (`:101`, `:166-174`), keyed on (seating, `late`, the visit its lateness counts from, the 15-minute window since it turned late), so a visit starts a new episode and a re-run or a second scanner sends nothing twice.

An unassigned late table, which the checker leaves unsent (`:236-239`), goes straight to the manager step, and so does an unassigned Food-up, which is accepted: the checker's refusal (`app/meal-actions.ts:181-186`, working tree) is not ported. For this port the record departs from, and does not edit, the service-floor directive's and premortem's "the section or the expo screen" (`.planning/01-org/product/product-vision/teams/service-floor/service-floor-directive.md:37`, `:54`, `:71`; `service-floor-premortem.md:85`).

### Food-up

Any staff may raise one (*"Any staff (Recommended)"*, whose option text is "The pass, a runner or a manager taps it, as in checker.", AskUserQuestion asked 2026-10-31T03:13:20Z, Q2); the push sent at the tap is the chain's first, under the scanner's rules, quiet hours included. It runs its own clock from the tap that raised it: unless acknowledged by `warn_after_min`, it climbs to the manager step; an acknowledged Food-up never climbs. The acknowledge is the checker's `meal_ack` (`~/Projects/checker/supabase/migrations/0018_meal_run_alerts.sql:5`, `:12`, working tree), which came with *"Include it (Recommended)"* (AskUserQuestion asked 2026-10-31T02:52:37Z, Q4). Pick 5 names who an alert passes to on absence; whether a reached waiter's alert climbs is the question OD-236 put to him (16:57:03Z, Q2, "A Food-up or late-table alert goes to the table's waiter, then whoever is covering"), so this climb answers OD-236 and adds no case to pick 5. A check-in does not reset it; it climbs at once if the table is already late, rides a late push only when that push went to the manager step in the same tick, and is keyed on (meal run, threshold). No `floor_settings` column is added; that `warn_after_min` also sets the score's T and the reports' on-time share (`lib/waiter-score.ts:9`; `lib/reports.ts:182`, `:355`) is an accepted risk, to revisit when a house objects.

**Who may acknowledge.** A person it was sent to, or the table's waiter or live cover, while the floor does not read that person as absent, and any owner or manager, as the climb's target. Any other acknowledge, or one from a person read as absent then, is refused with the reason, the on-break waiter being told to tap *Back*, and does not stop the climb. A second acknowledgement is answered from the row.

### Quiet hours

No floor push passes quiet hours, whether the scanner or a person's tap sends it (a Food-up raised at the pass, a nudge), and whichever copy it is: a tap on the floor raises an alert that climbs to the managers, not a message to the team. A person quiet hours defer (`producer-ledger.service.ts:192-216`, its `ready` and `deferred` split, `:67-78`), or one it lists as away whom `isWithinQuietHours` puts inside their window (§ Decision › Which reads the floor uses, in part 2), is not reached; the chain passes over them at once, and the drop is logged. A floor push is dropped, not held as main holds a deferred item (`:47-55`), since a late or Food-up push after quiet hours is stale. When every step is deferred, the manager step's last row lands, so the alert never waits unseen. Quiet hours are off unless a person sets them (`:183-185`), so this binds only a person whose window covers hours they work, and the manager copy and the `/floor` row name quiet hours as the reason. A cover offer and a Call out are /team's own sends, whose class is OD-238's; outside floor pushes, whether one person's push may pass another's quiet hours is not ruled on main.
