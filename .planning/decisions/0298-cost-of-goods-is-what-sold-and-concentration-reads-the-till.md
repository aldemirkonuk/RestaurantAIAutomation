## Context

The owner-quarter sim's analytics walk on Tuzlu Rüzgar (2026-10-03, read-only) found three figures standing in for measures the POS feed can already give. Every cite is at `e2cbe426a`.

- **A-046.** `AnalyticsService.getFinancialSummary` called the sum of DELIVERED `procurement_orders` over 365 days `cogs` (`apps/api-gateway/src/analytics/analytics.service.ts:428`). It called menu price × bottles on hand `revenue`, with the basis "POS-revenue proxy" (`:434`, `:538-539`). /reports printed the first as "Cost of goods (365d)" and the second as "Sell-price valuation" (`apps/web/src/pages/reports/next/rp-registers-house.tsx:202-209`, `apps/api-gateway/src/reports/exports/report-export-cuttings.ts:547-548`). Gross margin took the on-hand value at cost as its cost (`analytics.service.ts:458`), so no ratio described a sale. Tuzlu was shown $39,302.50 of purchases as cost of goods. The ledger's count-based cost for July and August is about $47,914 (procure-summary B17), and the shelf value was shown as $28,889.
- **A-018.** `getRiskProfile` computed the revenue Gini and SKU HHI over `unitPrice * qty` (`:725-726`), which measures where the shelf value sits. It gave 0.7775 for Tuzlu. The rule `revenue_concentration` fires above 0.6 with "Revenue rides on very few wines… raise their service level to 98%" (`recommendations.service.ts:536`). The walk's truth figure, from its own sim data rather than this code, is a Gini of POS line sales per imported row (116 rows, Jul 1–Aug 30, the 4 rows with no sale weighed as 0): 0.5651 over all rows and 0.5091 over wine. Both are below the line, so the advice was produced by the stand-in. The house has 134 active rows, not 116. The 18 extra are menu rows (tea, ayran, cocktails) that no POS mapping points at (A-013), so the till can never sell them.
- **AW16.** `METRIC_REGISTRY` (`metric-registry.ts`, served at `GET /analytics/metrics`) marked all 33 entries `computed: true`. Seven have no served field at all: year-on-year growth, an early-payment APR, a newsvendor order, price elasticity, an optimal markup, a CUSUM break, and a drawdown that is 0 by construction (`v3.0-TECH-DEBT.md` defect 4). Others described inputs they do not read: the Gini as "per-SKU revenue", the COGS ratio as "Beverage cost as a share of wine revenue", and turnover, DIO and GMROI as if they used an average inventory and a year of cost.

The till already holds both halves of the truth. `pos_checks.items` carries each line's price and quantity, with `inventory_id` stamped at ingest. The POS writes the stock it moves to `inventory_transactions` as source `pos`: a `sale` of −qty for a whole bottle, a `return` of +qty for a void, and one `sale` of −bottles_opened for glass pours (ADR 0285).

## Options considered

For **cost of goods**:

1. **POS bottles out × the recorded unit cost (chosen).** This is theoretical, sales-only cost of goods. It uses POS `sale` rows net of POS void `return` rows, with glass pours counted as the bottles they opened, times `resolveUnitCost`'s answer (`inventory-cost.ts` stays the only place a cost is decided; OD-100 is untouched). It reads only what is recorded today and needs no counts.
2. **Delivered purchases (the status quo).** What was bought is not what sold. A month of heavy buying would read as a month of heavy selling. Rejected.
3. **Opening + purchases − closing, from counts.** This is the accountant's figure and the one the truth used. Counts exist on three dates, and C02's back-dating is still in build, so for now it would be a number only on those dates. Rejected for now. It is the right cross-check once counts are regular.
4. **`wine_consumption_log` units × cost.** A glass is not a bottle (AW02), and turning glasses into bottles needs a bottle size that ADR 0124 measured as known on 51 of 206 inventory rows. Rejected. **[Added 2026-10-08: [[0297-a-glass-is-not-a-bottle]] has since given the consumption log a labelled 750 ml stand-in, so the bottle size is no longer missing. The rejection stands for a different reason: cost of goods here prices bottles *opened* (the ledger's `sale` rows, ADR 0285), while ADR 0297's figure is bottle-equivalents *poured*. They are different quantities, and this ADR chooses the first.]**
5. **Write-time FIFO cost on each depletion row** (`lot_id` and cost on the ledger row). This is the long-term answer. It is ADR 0115 D3/R6's ledger design (PR #589, unmerged), and it would collide with lane glasspour's `record_glass_pour` rewrite. It is named as the follow-up, not built here.

For **an item that sold without a recorded cost** (the founder's fork): (a) withhold, as ADR 0051 does for the cellar value, and say N of M (**his pick**); (b) cost a sold-out item at its last lot cost, which adds a valuation branch that OD-100 has not decided; (c) show the costed part as a labelled floor, against ADR 0053's "a total is null, not a partial sum"; (d) record FIFO cost at write time, which is option 5.

For **which items weigh in the concentration**:

1. **Every active item a POS mapping of the house points at, with 0 when it sold nothing, plus every item that sold in the window (chosen, round 1).** An item can only sell through the till if a mapping names it, because `inventory_id` on a line comes only from `pos_item_mappings` (`pos-hub.service.ts` `resolveWine`). A mapped item that sold nothing is a real 0, a dead seller. An active row no mapping points at has a 0 that is not a sales fact.
2. **Every active item, with 0 for one that sold nothing (the first build, rejected in round 1).** Adding k zeros to n weights gives G' = (nG + k)/(n + k). Tuzlu's 18 unmappable rows would lift the truth's 0.5651 to about 0.62 ((116 × 0.5651 + 18)/134), over the rule's 0.6 line. The rule would keep firing, and the cause would be rows the till cannot sell. The round-1 verifier confirmed the formula on synthetic data.
3. **Only items that sold.** This drops the mapped dead sellers, so it reads a house as more even than it is. The truth figure counted its 4 zero rows. Rejected.
4. **Items with any sale or POS ledger move in the window.** It has the same gap as 3, and it adds void-only items as zero weights. Rejected.

For **sales**:

1. **POS line price × qty for lines naming a stock item, on closed, non-voided checks (chosen).** This is net of tax and surcharge, as AW17 rules. Check-level discounts are not apportioned, and the basis says so.
2. **Menu price × units sold.** It ignores the price the line actually carried (happy hour, a comp priced at 0). Rejected.
3. **Check totals.** These are gross, and they include food and lines naming no stock item, so they could not sit over a cost of stock items. Rejected.

For **where the aggregation runs**:

1. **One SECURITY INVOKER SQL function returning one jsonb value (chosen).** PostgREST's `max_rows` cuts rows, not values (ADR 0292), so a year of checks cannot be truncated. The output is bounded by the house's item count, and a new partial index covers the `closed_at` window.
2. **`.from('pos_checks')` in the gateway.** It is capped at 1,000 rows and ships every check over the wire. Rejected.

For **who reads the till's figures** (decision 9, added 2026-10-09):

1. **Withhold for a caller without sales, by the token's role, failing closed (chosen).** It is the mechanism ADR 0290 §5 already uses under ADR 0145, so this PR adds no new rule, and it keeps the non-sales figures (stock value, dead stock, delivered purchases) on the same routes for staff.
2. **Merge now with the widening disclosed (rejected).** This was the head at `fd0a57669`: an owed tech-debt entry and an open claim. It would ship real till money to staff on role-open routes where ADR 0145 (Locked) gives sales to the owner and manager only. The coordinator's call is that this PR must not widen a locked ADR's exposure, and disclosure does not change who can read the figure.
3. **`RolesGuard` with `@Roles("owner", "manager")` on the routes (rejected here).** It refuses the whole route, so staff would lose the stock figures they may read, and it refuses `admin`, which `ROLE_POLICY` reads as the owner row: the two locked readings differ for `admin`, and this PR keeps `admin` as the code treats it.
4. **Gate open by default, closed only on the routes (rejected).** Any caller that forgets to pass a gate (the digest, the producers, a future reader) would read the till for everyone it serves. Closed by default makes a missed caller show a dash, not a leak.

**Relabel only** (rename the two figures and change nothing else) was rejected. The scope asks for the real measure where the feed is connected, and the walk showed the feed is connected.

## Decision

The till's figures replace the stand-ins, and the stand-ins keep their own names.

1. **`cogs`** = POS bottles out × recorded unit cost over the trailing 365 days. It is null when the till read fails, when the window holds no closed check and no POS stock move, when the till traded but **no item moved stock** (round 1), or when **any** item that sold carries no recorded cost (the founder's fork). The no-move case was a sum over no rows, so it printed $0, a 100% margin and a GMROI of 30 for a till that sold 500 of stock and moved none. That is an unknown cost printed as nothing (ADR 0051). `cogsCoverage` says how many of the items that sold are costed (the founder's "N of M"), how many bottles went out, how many are costed, and how many items net-returned (B19 void artefacts). **Recorded cost** means what `resolveUnitCost` reads: the invoiced lots' WAC, else `last_purchase_price` above 0. An item that moved stock but is no longer active (deleted on /inventory, a soft delete) is costed from its own row through the same `resolveUnitCost`, as an active one is, with no new valuation branch (OD-100 untouched). `itemsNoLongerActive` counts these items, and the basis says they "read all the same". That second read runs only when an item that moved stock is missing from the active rows. An id the POS ledger moved that has **no** `restaurant_inventory` row of this house (`inventory_transactions.inventory_id` carries no foreign key) is **no longer in the books**. `itemsNotInBooks` counts it, and it withholds like any item with no recorded cost. The basis, the page and the export name it apart from "no recorded cost". If that second read fails, `itemsCostUnread` counts the items it was for, and the figure is withheld as unknown. (Until the 2026-10-05 rework, an inactive item counted as uncosted, so one deleted item that had sold withheld the whole figure.)
2. **`revenue`** = POS line sales for stock items, net, over the same window. It is null when the read fails, when there is no closed check, or when a line names a stock item but cannot be read (a total would be a floor). `salesCoverage` gives the counts of checks, lines, lines naming no stock item and their sales, unreadable lines, and items that sold without moving stock. Such an item adds sales and no cost. It can be a glass from a bottle opened before the window. It can also be a mapped line flagged not-stock, which pos-hub skips for depletion (`pos-hub.service.ts`, the `if (!it.is_wine) continue;` skip), or a sale the ledger refused (ADR 0285). The basis, the page and the export each name the count.
3. **The margin ratios** (`grossMarginDollars`, `grossMargin`, `cogsRatio`, `primeCostRatio`) divide that cost by those sales. They no longer depend on the on-hand valuation. `primeCostRatio` still adds only the labour the caller passes (`?labor=`, the MCP `labor` argument), and the registry says so.
4. **Turnover, DIO and GMROI** annualise the cost of the observed span (from the first POS sale in the window, at most 365 days) against today's inventory at cost. They are null under 28 days of POS history and null unless every on-hand row is costed (ADR 0053). `cogsWindow` gives the span. The value is today's, not an average, and the text says so. The span starts at the earlier of two clocks: the first closed check in the window (where sales start) and the first POS ledger row (where cost of goods starts). `cogsWindow` carries both (`firstCheckAt`, `firstMoveAt`). When they are a day or more apart, the basis says which clock starts first and how many days later the other does, counted from the two instants, and which side holds nothing for those days. It names no date: a date is a day of the house's clock (ADR 0296), and this reader has no zone. [Corrected 2026-10-08, lane `proxies`, after the BLOCK at `d1384598d`: until then this sentence said the basis "names both dates", which the code did up to `0ed2abc18` and stopped doing at `d1384598d`; the Decision body had not been changed with the code.] How far that moves the margin, turns, DIO and GMROI is not measured.
5. **Revenue concentration** (`gini`, `hhi`) uses POS sales per stock item over 90 days, over the items the till can sell (option 1 above). An active item a mapping points at weighs 0 when it sold nothing. An item that sold in the window counts whether or not it is still active or still mapped. An active row that no mapping points at and that sold nothing is left out. So is a non-active item that sold nothing (a void-only return, or a mapping left on a retired row). An item whose voids outweigh its sales in the window (net sales below 0) weighs 0 and is counted. Before the clamp, the Gini dropped it (`giniCoefficient` skips values below 0) and the HHI dropped it (`herfindahlIndex` skips values not above 0), while `itemsWeighed` counted it. It is null, never "well-distributed", when the read fails, when there is no closed check, or when no line sold a stock item. `itemsWeighed`, `itemsWithSales`, `activeItemsNotWeighed` and `basis` say what was weighed and what was left out.
6. **The old figures keep their true names**: `deliveredPurchases` and `shelfValueAtMenuPrice`. They feed no ratio. /reports and the export print "Sales of stocked items, net (365d)" and the sell-price valuation under its own name.
7. **A registry entry is `computed: true` only where a served field computes it.** Seven entries go to false. The descriptions of the rest say what they read. A test maps every `true` to a field on a real service call, or, for the five entries it does not call (vendor lead time and vendor price trend, sales correlation, the anomaly z-score and `recommendations`), to the source line that computes each.
8. **`public.pos_item_sales(house, since)`** does the aggregation: one jsonb value, SECURITY INVOKER, STABLE, executable by `service_role` only. Each item carries `mapped`: a mapping of this house points at it, and it is this house's item. A mapped item that neither sold nor moved is listed with zeros. A payload item without the flag is a failed read. A line whose price, quantity or item id is not a number or a uuid is counted in `unreadable_lines`, not raised. So is a line that is not a JSON object (the SQL test's T4 pins it); the gateway's ingest writes only objects, and if one is written directly the sales basis still calls it a line that "names a stock item", which says more than such a line does. A line the gateway's ingest wrote cannot raise either, because `pos-adapters.ts` coerces price and quantity to finite numbers. A numeric string or product beyond `numeric`'s range, written to `pos_checks` directly (service role), does raise, and it fails the whole call; the gateway reads that as a failed read, so the figures go null (ADR 0067). The SQL test pins both halves. A second per-item reader of the till should call it rather than aggregate `pos_checks.items` again.
9. **The till's figures reach only a caller who sees sales** [added 2026-10-09 after the CI gate BLOCK at `fd0a57669`, PR comment 6085530568]. This is the coordinator's call under the founder's 2026-10-07T20:04:10Z delegation, not the founder's pick, and he can overrule it: *"Do not ask me questions, I allow and approve for you to decide on your own. If its a decision question then research deep, find answers. While you can change decisions, you cannot change any feature we decided unless it breaks everything then only you can, but before that you should research."* The rule is ADR 0145's `ROLE_POLICY` (Locked) as ADR 0290 §5 applies it on the dashboard: `withSales = policyFor(role).sees.includes("sales")` (`apps/api-gateway/src/ask-readings/reading-data-classes.ts`). Owner and manager see sales. `admin` reads the owner row, as `ROLE_POLICY` already treats it. Staff and an unknown role read the staff row and do not. For a caller without sales, `getFinancialSummary` and `getRiskProfile` do not call `pos_item_sales` at all, so `cogs`, `revenue`, `cogsCoverage`, `salesCoverage`, `cogsWindow`, the margin ratios, turnover, DIO, GMROI and the concentration are null, `salesWithheld` is true, and each basis says the figure was withheld, not unread. Both methods default closed: a caller that passes no gate is withheld. The routes pass the token's gate (`salesGateFor`, `analytics.controller.ts`) to these two methods wherever they reach them: financial, risk, overview, consult, recommendations, and goals create, list and progress. The gate covers only what this ADR moved onto the till. The same routes still carry sales that predate it: the insights inside consult's evidence pack and overview, and the check-based goal metrics (Consequences, Owed). A `days_of_inventory` goal is not scored for a caller without sales, and says so (`DAYS_OF_STOCK_WITHHELD`, `goals.service.ts`). Two callers pass the gate open because their audience is already an owner or a manager: the MCP tools (a key is minted only by an owner or a manager, `organizations.service.ts` `assertManagerOrOwner`) and report exports (`@Roles("owner", "manager")` with `RolesGuard`, `report-exports.controller.ts:55-56`). The recommendation digest and the goal-reached and ceiling-held producers pass the gate open only for owners and managers of the house, read from `user_restaurant_access` (Consequences, "Who hears a till figure"). /reports names the withheld figures as "Sales are shown to owners and managers" (`rp-registers-house.tsx`).

**Not in these figures, and named in their bases:** waste, breakage, count corrections and sales outside the till (theoretical cost, not actual); check discounts (not apportioned); and food lines (no recipe cost, so a dish is in neither side).

What carried it: every number on the page should describe the event its label names. The till records sales and the stock they moved, so the honest figure was always available. Where part of it is not (a cost nobody recorded), the founder's ruling is the ADR 0051/0053 one: say how much is missing rather than print a smaller number under the same label.
