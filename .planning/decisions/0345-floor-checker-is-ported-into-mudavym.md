## Options considered

1. **Port in-SPA (chosen).** The checker's features are rebuilt as a Mudavym software: `/floor` (the manager board), `/floor/builder`, a chrome-free waiter screen at `/floor/me`, a NestJS floor module, and new migrations on Mudavym's Supabase. It reuses Mudavym's people, tables, push, realtime, audit and flags (the reuse map below). The checker is retired once the port runs. Cost: the largest build of the four. The checker's Supabase realtime has to be replaced by the gateway's socket.io. The checker's history is not carried over.
2. **A subdomain (`floor.mudavym.com`) on the Mudavym backend.** It uses the same gateway and database, with its own origin and install identity. It was rejected for now, for these reasons:
   - **Per-origin sessions.** The web app keeps the access token in `localStorage` (`apps/web/src/stores/authStore.ts:94`, `apps/web/src/contexts/AuthContext.tsx:814`), which a second origin cannot read. The subdomain therefore needs a second sign-in, or a new handoff-token auth surface that needs its own security ADR.
   - **Push devices.** `notification_push_devices` has no app or origin column (`supabase/migrations/20260925160500_a_preference_is_kept_once_per_person_per_house.sql:96-120`; the key is `UNIQUE (user_id, endpoint)` at `:119`), so a device row cannot say which surface it came from.
   - **Realtime.** The socket's CORS list would need the new origin (`apps/api-gateway/src/websocket/websocket.gateway.ts:174-190`).
   - **Not ruled out.** The founder's round 1 answer named a subdomain as a possibility. It stays open as OD-237.
3. **A separate subdomain app with sync.** The checker stays a Next.js app on its own Supabase, and the two systems sync. Rejected:
   - **Identity.** The checker allows one house per user (`0001_init.sql:35-37`), where Mudavym lets a person hold several houses and choose one (ADR 0164).
   - **Roles.** The checker's roles are `manager`/`waiter`. Mudavym's are `owner`/`manager`/`staff` (`supabase/migrations/20260902200000_team_access_role_is_a_known_role.sql:61-63`).
   - **Two numbers for one waiter.** Two databases would compute the same waiter's figures twice.
   - **Push.** It keeps a `push_subscriptions` table, which ADR 0027 says *"does not exist and must not be created"* (`0027:119`).
   - **Design.** It keeps its own design layer, and ADR 0149 (Locked) makes Mudavym's design the only one. That layer is `apps/web/src/components/mudavym`, 80 entries.
   - **Push devices.** The `notification_push_devices` objection from option 2 applies here too.
4. **Do nothing.** The checker keeps running alone. Its cost:
   - ADR 0001's "then unified" never happens.
   - Analytics never sees the floor; the task asked for exactly that.
   - DASH-G6 has no data.
   - The checker's reports stay mock (`lib/reports-mock.ts:4`).

## Decision

**Floor Checker is ported into the Mudavym web app and gateway, as a dark-launched software. Every one of its features is carried over in Mudavym's design, on Mudavym's data, and the checker is retired after the port.** Five points he did not rule are decided as the executor's calls under the founder's 2026-10-31T16:57:09Z delegation (*Executor's calls*, D1–D5).
