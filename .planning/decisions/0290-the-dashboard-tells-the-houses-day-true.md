## Context

The owner-quarter analytics walk (Tuzlu Rüzgar, 2026-10-03) found the dashboard month — `GET /dashboard/calendar-revenue/:id`, drawn by `SalesCalendar` — wrong in three ways at once:

- **C18.** The deliveries read selected `procurement_orders.wine_name`, a column the table has never had (`check_read_columns_exist.py` carries it on the debt list). PostgREST refused the whole read, the method did not read `error`, and every day showed $0 paid to vendors — Oct 2 drew $0 against $39,302.50 delivered.
- **AW12.** The events read was `select('*')` with no paging. PostgREST answers at most 1,000 rows (`supabase/config.toml:18`, `max_rows = 1000`), so a busy month lost its last days without a word, and the page received columns it never draws.
- **AW21.** The page is titled a *sales* calendar and had no sales source: every figure was vendor spend. The founder's AW17 ruling (below) fixes what "sales" means here.

Underneath all three, days were cut on the UTC prefix of a timestamp (`startsWith(dateStr)`), not on the house's clock (F-086): a delivery at 20:00 in Los Angeles landed on the next day's square.

## Decision

The dashboard month is filed on the house's own days, reads every row it needs or fails, and carries net sales — to the people who see sales.

1. **The house's day.** Every figure is bucketed with `localDateIn(instant, zone)` in the house's zone, and the month is the window `[localMidnight(first), localMidnight(first of next month))`. The zone is the one `houseFrame` returns from `restaurants.timezone` and `restaurants.country` (`common/house-frame.ts`, ADR 0207 question 6): the house's own zone when the server can read its name, else its country's only zone, else none — the rule #616's `readHouseZone` reads (ADR 0296). [Amended 2026-10-05, rework: the zone was `resolveZone(restaurants.timezone)` alone, so a Turkish house with no zone stated read as no zone here while #616's `readHouseZone` reads `Europe/Istanbul` for it.] A house with **no zone** (none stated or readable, and a country with several zones or none recorded) gets every day row with every figure `null`, events kept (`event_date` is already a house date), `zone_unset: true`, `timezone: null`, `today: null` — and neither deliveries nor checks are read, since nothing could be filed. The page draws "—" and one line, *"This house's time zone isn't set, so no day's figures can be filed yet"*, linking `/settings?tab=time-zone`. (F1 (a).)
2. **Net sales: count and say** (netsales F1). Net sales per day are the sum of `pos_checks.subtotal` over the checks with `voided = false` that stated one; `total` and `tip` are never read. A missing, blank or non-numeric subtotal is unstated: never 0 and never the total. Each day carries `net_checks`, the checks that stated a subtotal, beside `checks`, every check; the month carries `monthly_net_checks` beside `monthly_checks`. A day with checks none of which stated a subtotal has net `null`, and the page says "not recorded" there (the cell, its spoken label, the month line and DayDetail), not "—" and not $0; a day with no checks is a measured 0 (§4). When 0 < N < M the page says "from N of M checks" beside the figure. The fold is `foldNetSales` over `netSalesOf`, a local copy of lane netsales' helpers (`analytics/net-sales.ts`, ADR 0295, #615) with the same rule and return shape; whichever of #615 and this branch lands second deletes the copy and imports the shared one. [Superseded 2026-10-05, rework: the first rule 2 read "A day on which any check states no subtotal has net `null` (not the sum of the others), and so does the month."]
3. **The business day of a check** is the day it closed, else the day it opened — the rule `analytics/goals.service.ts:915` and `calendar/recorded-days.service.ts:91-99` apply — taken in the house zone. It is `houseDayOf`, a local copy of #616's `common/house-day.ts` helper (ADR 0296, the founder's "Midnight, by close") with the same rule and signature; whichever of #616 and this branch lands second deletes the copy and imports the shared one. The read starts 24 h before the month (`CHECK_LOOKBACK_MS`) so a check opened late on the last night of the previous month and closed after midnight is found and filed on the 1st.
4. **No register is not zero, and neither is a day before it or after it.** Whenever the month reads sales (the caller sees them and the house has a zone), two probes run. [Amended 2026-10-05: this read "Two probes run every time", broader than the code, which skips them for a withheld caller and a house with no zone.] Each reads one row on the `(restaurant_id, opened_at)` index: the house's FIRST check ever and its LATEST, voided or not (a voided check still means the register was sending). If the house has never sent a check: `pos_connected: false`, net and checks `null`, and DayDetail says "No register connected". Otherwise the register counted the days from the first check's house day to the latest check's. The latest check's day is the day it closed, else the day it opened; if one of the month's own checks is filed on a later day, that day is used. Within that range, a day with no check is a measured `0`. Outside it the day is `null`:
   - **Before the first check.** The register was not there to count it, so "no sales" would be a fabricated zero.
   - **After the latest check.** No check came, and nothing records that the register was still sending, so a register gone dark and a closed house read alike. This covers today until its first check lands, and every day ahead.

   The founder ruled this bound on 2026-10-05: *"Dash after last check (Recommended)"*.

   **The month total: sum, say N of M days.** The month's figures are over the days of the month that have begun on the house's clock (every day, for a past month). The month sums the begun days the register counted (`monthly_net_sales`, `monthly_checks`, `monthly_net_checks`, each day folded as in §2) and states `monthly_days_counted` and `monthly_days_begun`. The page says "net sales $61,240 · from 29 of 31 days" when only some begun days were counted, and adds "· from N of M checks" when only some counted checks stated a subtotal. No counted day states no figure ("—"); counted days whose checks stated no subtotal state none either, said "not recorded"; a month not yet begun states none. [Superseded 2026-10-05 by the founder's *"Sum, say N of M days (Recommended)"*: this paragraph first read "They are stated only when every one of those days is known: one unknown day leaves them `null`, never a partial sum".] (Found by the lane's re-verify, 2026-10-04: the first build asked the first-check probe only when the month was empty, so a month that began before the register drew its early days as quiet zeros. Fix round 2, 2026-10-04: the independent verifier found that with the first bound alone, Tuzlu Rüzgar, whose POS feed ends 2026-08-30, would draw every September and October day, the current month included, as "$0 · 0 checks". The latest-check bound is the outage fork's path (b), ruled 2026-10-05; see the rulings above.)
5. **Who sees sales.** The controller passes `withSales = policyFor(role).sees.includes("sales")`, the ask-readings `ROLE_POLICY` (`reading-data-classes.ts:179-182`: owner and manager see `sales`, staff do not; `admin` reads the owner row; anything else reads the staff row). The role is the role in the token's house (`jwt.strategy.ts:68`). For anyone else `pos_checks` is **not read at all**; the payload says `sales_withheld: true` and the page draws no sales line. Gating *vendor spend* for staff stays with #579 (its W22 `calendarForRole`).
6. **Complete or a 500.** Deliveries, events and checks are read through the keyset pager `readAll` (`providers/vendor-menu-supply.ts`, ordered by `id`, 1,000 per page, throws on error); the zone read and the two register probes throw on error. Events name their five drawn columns (`id, title, event_type, event_date, event_time`) — `status`, `description` and `color` are no longer sent — and every status is still kept. A failed read is a 500, never a month of zeros (ADR 0020). A malformed `year`/`month` is a 400.
7. **Vendor spend is unchanged in method:** `ORDER_SPEND_STATUSES` (`order-status.ts:69-72`, DELIVERED and COMPLETED, its docblock's "understating is the safer error"), `total_cost` else `final_price`, `bottles_total` else `quantity` — now filed on the house's day and stated `null` with no zone.
8. **The cell (F2).** With net sales shown, each cell headlines the day's net sales and is shaded by them; the "N orders" delivery mark stays; the header reads "net sales $X · paid to vendors $Y · N bottles in", with "· from N of M days" and "· from N of M checks" after the figure when they apply (§2, §4); a partial day's cell carries "from N of M checks" under its figure, and a day whose checks stated no subtotal reads "not recorded"; DayDetail shows Net sales (with its "from N of M checks") and Checks beside Paid to vendors. Withheld or no register: the cell carries vendor spend as before, labelled so. "Today" and "future" are the house's (`today` in the payload); the browser date is used only by a gateway older than this ADR. The footer no longer says every figure is procurement. It says net sales add up the subtotals register checks carry, voided checks left out, that the subtotal is before tax and surcharge only "when the register sends it that way", and that a check that carries none is counted, never guessed. The generic webhook and CSV import adapters pass `subtotal` through as sent; Square's maps `net_amounts.total_money`, Toast's `amount` and Clover's `null` (`pos-adapters.ts:65`, `:113`, `:203`, `:159`). So the page does not promise a basis the adapter may not keep.
9. **Sized by its containers, never the viewport.** [Added 2026-10-05, fix round after the HOLD at `3d29bc298`.] Inside the app shell (rooms rail 232 px; counter 320 px open from a 1280 px window, else a 52 px strip) a calendar cell is 38.6 px wide at a 1024 px window, 36.9 px at 1280 and 59.7 px at 1440, against 40.7 px on a 375 px phone. A desktop cell can be narrower than a phone's, and `dashboard-next.css`'s 720 px breakpoint never sees it, so a 12 px "$4.2K" drew as "$4.2". DayDetail's panel is 294 px at 1024, 282 at 1280 and 442 at 1440. (Measured on the local fixture harness with the shell's widths as spacers.)
   - **The cell** is an inline-size container. Its side padding is `clamp(3px, 25% − 6px, 7px)` of its own width. Its headline is `min(12px, k cqi)`, one size for the month, with k chosen so the month's longest headline fills the cell's inner width (JetBrains Mono at 0.59 em a character after the cell's −0.01 em). So a figure never clips: "$29.0K" draws at 8.0 px at 1280, 8.3 at 1024, 8.6 at 375 and 12 at 1440, and a month whose longest is five characters ("$7.4K") at 9.6–10.3 px. Its words ("N orders", "from N of M checks", "not recorded") are `min(9px, 28.5cqi)` and wrap; "from 90 of 97 checks" breaks only between words ("from / 90 of / 97 / checks" at 1280). "not recorded" still breaks inside "recorded" in every cell measured up to 49.4 px wide (inner width 34.7 px): "not / record / ed" at 1024, 1280 and 375, "not / recorde / d" at 1100 and 1360. It reads "not / recorded" in every cell measured from 59.7 px (inner 43.7 px), as at 1440. Widths between the two were not measured.
   - **The words stay the founder's.** The cell keeps "from N of M checks" and "not recorded" legible by wrapping rather than taking a shorter form. A shorter cell form ("90 of 97") would change the words netsales F1 ruled, He ruled it on 2026-10-05 (AskUserQuestion): *"Keep the words, wrap (Recommended)"*. The full words also stay in the cell's spoken label and in DayDetail.
   - **DayDetail's figures** take `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(max(8rem, (100% − (n−1) gaps) / n), 1fr))` with n half the figures, rounded up: a track is never under 8rem and a row holds at most half the figures. The six figures draw two across at 1024 (139 px each), 1280 (133) and 375 (146.5), and three across at 1440 (136.7); "$39,302.5" needs 100 px. `lg:grid-cols-6` had given each about 36 px at 1024, 34 at 1280 and 60 at 1440 (the panel less five 1rem gaps, over six), and the figures ran into each other. The four no-zone figures draw two and two. The four lists below sit side by side only where each gets 16rem, so at all four widths they are one column. `md:grid-cols-2` had squeezed a delivery's name to 0 px at 1024 and 1280 (the quantity and money took the row); it now gets 50 to 90 px there, cut with an ellipsis.

The payload change is additive: `daily[].net_sales`, `daily[].checks`, `daily[].net_checks`, `monthly_net_sales`, `monthly_checks`, `monthly_net_checks`, `monthly_days_counted`, `monthly_days_begun`, `timezone`, `zone_unset`, `today`, `pos_connected`, `sales_withheld`. The frozen route and its legacy keys stay; legacy figures become `null` where unknown instead of `0`.

## Options considered (rejected)

1. **#579's silent UTC fallback** (`houseZone || "UTC"`). Files every figure on a clock the house never stated, and says nothing. DASH-G2 rules against it.
2. **Bucket in UTC, labelled "days in UTC"** (F1 (b)). True, but useless to a house in UTC-7 and still against G2's "unknown".
3. **A SECURITY INVOKER SQL function per house-day.** A second fold rule in SQL drifting from recorded-days' TypeScript fold; applies to production on merge with no CI job that runs SQL; over the 15-file budget.
4. **Reuse `RecordedDaysService`.** It folds gross `total` on the UTC date, and is owned by the caltakings/cap lanes.
5. **No longer rejected — chosen by netsales F1 (2026-10-04), built here 2026-10-05.** "Sum the known subtotals on a day with a missing one" was first rejected here for "unknown taints the day", with a Clover house (`subtotal: null`, `pos-hub/pos-adapters.ts:159`) reading "—". The founder's netsales F1 *"Count and say (Recommended)"* chose it, with the count said beside the figure so a partial sum never reads as the whole (§2). A Clover house now reads "not recorded" with its checks counted.
6. **Cap events at 5 plus a count.** Needs an "N more" UI that paging makes unnecessary.
7. **Show sales to staff, or a figure-less line.** The ROLE_POLICY table already says staff do not see `sales`; reading `pos_checks` for them at all would put the figure on the wire.
8. **One unknown begun day blanks the month total** (this ADR's first §4 rule, built in fix round 2). Replaced by the founder's *"Sum, say N of M days (Recommended)"*: it left Tuzlu Rüzgar's August with no total over one dark day (Aug 31), and every current month blank each morning until the day's first check.
9. **Outage path (a): a quiet zero after the first check, said "no check received".** No schema change, but an outage still prints "$0": Tuzlu's September and October would read "$0 · 0 checks". Not picked: the founder chose "Dash after last check".
10. **Outage path (c): a register heartbeat now.** The POS hub would stamp each sync, so a day counts only if the register was heard from that day. It is the only path that also catches a gap in the middle of the month, but it needs a migration and a pos-hub write. Not chosen now (the 2026-10-05 ask).
11. **Viewport breakpoints for the cell and the panel** (`lg:grid-cols-6`, the 720 px cell rule). The defect §9 fixes: on a desktop the shell takes 284 to 552 px of the window, so the window's width does not say how wide the page is.
12. **One smaller fixed size everywhere** (say an 8 px headline). Fits the narrowest cell measured, but draws 8 px where a 1440 cell holds 12, and still clips if the shell narrows the page further.
13. **Clip with an ellipsis** ("$4.2…"). Hides the figure the cell exists to show.
