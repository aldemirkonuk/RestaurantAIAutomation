## How to read the verdicts

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **KEEP** | The redesign lands. Build from it. |
| **KEEP+** | Lands, but the founder named specific additions before it is done. |
| **MERGE** | Neither today's page nor the redesign wins outright — combine them. |
| **REWORK** | The redesign is rejected or judged insufficient; needs new sketches. |
| **HOLD** | Explicitly not to be touched yet. |

narrow: *"I didn't like any of them — maybe the calendar only. Maybe the design
foundation. Maybe the dev sandbox."* Everything positive below arrived page by page
afterwards. Read the general verdict as **the set does not yet cohere as a product**,
and the per-page verdicts as what survives inside it.

## Flagships (the three-direction bake-off)

### `/` — Dashboard · **KEEP+ / REWORK the blocks**
Likes the current page's bones and the KPI cards, but calls the whole thing
*"old-fashioned"*. From the directions: **Editorial's "Good evening / before service"
opening** and **Federation's "Waiting on you" panel** plus its **"Nothing on the
calendar this week"** empty state are all named as liked.

**The one big ask: a TradeZella-style sales calendar.** A month grid where each day
carries its own result, and clicking a day opens everything that happened on it. The
founder called this *"an important thing for me"* — it is the headline feature of the
page, not a widget on it.

Everything else on the page is up for replacement: *important days, recent orders, low
stock alerts, one-tap actions / quick actions.* Direction A's argument (the dashboard
is a queue you clear) is not rejected but is not what was asked for either — the
day-grid is.

### `/inventory` — **KEEP the dropdowns · REJECT Federation**
*"I really like the way it is for the dropdowns, especially the detail in the
dropdowns… it shows everything you need to see."* The row-expand / detail dropdown is
the thing to preserve and extend. **Federation's inventory is explicitly rejected.**

**Named gap:** the dropdown needs *more* detail for the **receipt and invoice actions**
— this is where inventory meets `/receipts`, and the founder wants it to be
differentiated work, not a generic expander.

### `/notifications` — **REWORK**
Clarified 2026-08-29: **`/notifications` needs re-transformations.** Neither drawn
direction is the answer; the page needs new work, not a merge of the two.

What survives as *inspiration only*: Federation's density — it shows more of what is
actually happening — and Editorial's way of **subduing the already-handled** items so
the page quiets down as it is worked. The handling of the problem was called *"really
good"*; the execution was not enough.

### `/settings` — **KEEP (Editorial) · needs more**
*"I kind of like the Editorial. I think that's the best way to go — or Federation, it
doesn't matter. But I feel like there should be more."* Editorial wins on preference;
the section is under-specified and needs more substance before it is built.

### `/sommelier` — **HOLD**
*"Do not touch /sommelier yet."* No direction chosen. Nothing to do here.

## Where I was asked to think

The founder asked for deeper thinking on three of these. Written here as argument, not
built as sketches, per *"do not do anything yet."*

### `/receiving/:orderId/door` — "brainstorm me here, think deeper"

What the door screen already gets right is that it is **one job, one hand, one
minute**, with a photograph and a box count. The deepening should not add screens; it
should make the single screen carry the reconciliation that today happens later, badly,
somewhere else. Six candidates, in the order I would argue for them:

1. **Match against the PO before the driver leaves, not after.** The screen already
   knows the expected lines. Show the delta as the count is entered — *14 of 16, two
   short* — and make the short-ship a fact captured **at the door with the driver
   present**, which is the only moment it is cheap to resolve. Today it becomes an
   email three days later.
2. **The photograph should do work, not just exist.** One photo of the pallet plus one
   of the invoice is enough for the platform to read the invoice lines and pre-fill the
   count. The receiver's job then becomes *confirm or correct*, which is a 15-second
   job instead of a 5-minute one. This is the same extraction that `/receipts` needs —
   build it once.
3. **Three outcomes, not two.** Accepted · short-shipped · refused. Refusal (wrong
   wine, broken case, temperature) is the state that currently has nowhere to go and is
   the one that costs the most money.
4. **The credit request should already be drafted when the count is saved.** `--calm`,
   unsent, with the driver's name and the photo attached. The receiver never writes an
   email; the manager approves one later.
5. **Offline is the default assumption, not an error.** A loading dock has no signal.
   The screen should queue and say so plainly — *"saved on this phone, will send when
   you're back inside"* — rather than failing.
6. **Signature or initials, and who received it.** Not for ceremony: when a case goes
   missing between the door and the cellar, the only useful question is who signed.

The one thing I would *not* add: a full line-item editor. If a delivery needs
line-by-line editing, that belongs on `/receipts` at a desk, and the door screen should
hand off to it rather than grow into it.

### `/team` — "three more suggestions"

The redesign was called spot on, so these are additive, not corrective:

1. **Coverage gaps as the page's first object** (the founder's own candidate). Not a
   column that happens to be empty — a named, countable thing: *2 unfilled, Saturday,
   line*, with the platform's suggested cover beside it and one control to assign.
   This is the shift-manager's actual job, and today it is inferred by scanning.
2. **Cost as you build the week.** A running labour figure that moves as shifts are
   assigned, against the week's budget, with overtime thresholds visible *before*
   publish rather than discovered in payroll. Pair it with the existing
   40-hour/overtime markers.
3. **Certification and compliance as a blocker, not a badge.** The redesign already
   shows an expired food-handler card blocking four shifts. Make that generic: any
   credential that expires inside the scheduling window blocks the shifts it touches,
   with a renewal request as one control. A schedule that cannot legally be published
   should say so while it is being built.

A fourth, if there is room: **who actually turned up.** The gap between the published
schedule and the worked schedule is where labour cost really lives, and nothing in the
product currently closes it.

### `/providers` — combining "small buckets" with the new grid

The founder liked today's page for being **less crowded** and the redesign for being
richer. Those are reconcilable: today's virtue is that a vendor is a **small, closed
card** you can scan; the redesign's virtue is the **digital twin** — what the platform
has learned about how that vendor behaves. The combination is a quiet grid of small
vendor buckets where the learned facts live *in the sheet*, not on the card, and the
card carries at most three: delivery days, open orders, and one behavioural fact
(*confirms in 6 hours*, *ships Tuesdays*). Crowding comes from putting the twin on the
card; the fix is not a denser card but a card that promises less.

## What this changes about the canvas

- **Two outright rejections** — `/wines` and `/login` (with `/register` alongside it).
  Both need new work from today's page rather than a revision of the redesign.
- **Seven REWORKs** needing new sketches — `/notifications`, `/receiving`, `/documents-reports`,
  `/recommendations`, `/onboarding` (five sketches asked for), `/documents-reports`
  (three asked for), `/recommendations/catalog` (more asked for, though liked).
- **Four MERGEs** where today's page holds something the redesign lost —
  `/providers`, `/communications`, `/reports`, and the dashboard's block set.
- **One HOLD** — `/sommelier`.
- **`/cellar` becomes a parent surface** with `/wines` as a child.
- **The single largest new build** is the dashboard's **TradeZella-style sales
  calendar**, which has no equivalent anywhere in the product today.
- **The whole set has been re-skinned** to İznik + Warm Charcoal
  ([[0042-iznik-seal-and-warm-charcoal]], applied 2026-08-29). Judge it in that palette.
