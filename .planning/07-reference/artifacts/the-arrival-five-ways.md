    <h2><span class="letter">A</span>First evidence</h2>
    <p class="idea">The house's first ledger row is written before its first setting: one photograph of the last invoice through the door becomes the lines, a vendor, a price and a currency, and every register is then read from what that paper said and confirmed in place. Nothing is asked that the paper already answered; what it did not answer stays open, never defaulted.</p>
    <div class="cols">
      <div><h4>What it asks, in order</h4><ol>
        <li><b>Two doors</b> at <code>/register</code> — open a house (six fields, no currency question) or join one (the code resolves to house, city, inviter and role first).</li>
        <li><b>The letter</b> — "open it on the phone you will photograph with".</li>
        <li><b>The door</b> — photograph the last invoice; second road, the wine list. The 390 px frame is a manager doing this standing up.</li>
        <li><b>What the paper answered</b> — currency, vendor, terms, what the house pours, the low-stock line; each grey until confirmed; "Not now" recorded as a skip. One seal: "Enter it in the book".</li>
        <li><b>The book, opened</b> — one row, every register with its provenance; the nine producers offered with defaults kept; the assistant offered for what the paper left open.</li>
      </ol></div>
      <div><h4>Two roads not taken inside A</h4><ul>
        <li><b>Keep the currency question at <code>/register</code> too.</b> Safer when an invoice prints no currency, but it puts a setting before the evidence — the one thing A refuses. The address stays as the stated fallback.</li>
        <li><b>A seal per register.</b> Six ceremonies. The wax is rationed to the commitment that does not reverse in one click; only the row entering the book qualifies.</li>
      </ul></div>
      <div><h4>Honest gaps (drawn on the screen in mono)</h4><ul>
        <li>The skip writer does not exist — no route writes <code>configuration_step_skipped</code>.</li>
        <li>Payment terms are typed, not inferred: the extractor has no such field.</li>
        <li>Nothing matches the paper's vendor name to a provider; the person is the match.</li>
        <li>Register inference reads house items, never document lines — so "what it pours" can only be proposed after the lines become items.</li>
        <li>A house item is still a wine on this tree (ADR 0115 decided, not built); stock and cost differ by tree; the assistant's batch is not built.</li>
      </ul></div>

    <h2><span class="letter">D</span>The invitation</h2>
    <p class="idea">The house is resolved before the account exists: whoever was invited arrives already inside it, with everything the house has said about itself in view; whoever was not invited is founding a house and presses the seal once.</p>

        <li><b>The letter</b> — founders only; the invited are vouched for.</li>
        <li><b>The owner's arrival</b> — first evidence, then the four registers each in a sheet, each skippable as a recorded fact; then issue an invitation, which closes the loop.</li>
      </ol></div>

        <li><b>Bind the invitation to an email and pre-fill from it.</b> Would make "expecting you" literally true, but <code>targetEmail</code> is optional and unchecked today and the sharing model is the founder's open call.</li>
        <li><b>Ask "what it pours" inside the founding record.</b> The write needs a tenant and a verified session; a register read off nothing is a guess, which ADR 0108 refuses.</li>
