## What each PR does, and the forks it decided

Each fork was the session's call under the 2026-11-07 /goal.

### #1616: Spend pacing starts at the first purchase order

New `purchasing-span.ts`:
- The span runs from the earliest of the house's first order `created_at` and first `delivered_at`.
- The pace needs 60 days of records (two full 30-day windows). Under that, the pace and "the 30 days before" are null, never 0.
- The Holt projection runs on complete weeks inside the records, newest-aligned to yesterday, and needs 4 of them.
- If the first order cannot be read, the whole window is counted, and the sentence says so.

Also in this PR:
- The export's withheld reason for "Paid out, the 30 days before" was reworded.
- A pre-existing verbatim-test failure from #1567 (the export's window line for the reading) was fixed, and the PR body discloses it.

Rejected alternatives:
- The 30-day pace over a partial window, scaled up. It invents spend for days with no records.
- Starting at the first window day that has any order. A quiet stretch inside an old house would then shorten that house's window.

### #1624: the house registers say where to start

New step sources in `rp-next-step.tsx`, carried from #1605 and extended:
- `goods`: Inventory.
- `margin`: Menu and Orders.

Changes in `rp-registers-house.tsx`:
- New branch for a restock register with goods but no consumption line. It says no good has a demand to reorder from, with the consumption step: Settings, till, and Inventory.
- The margin-quadrants empty sentence carries the `margin` step.

The gateway restock export gets the same no-consumption sentence.

Merge note: an add/add conflict on rp-next-step.tsx with #1605. Keep the superset, which is this PR's file.

### #1631: the week's shape counts only weekdays it saw

New `weekdays-recorded.ts`:
- A weekday with no line on any of its days in the window is left out of the weekday profile.
- Inside a measured weekday, a day with no line still counts as 0. A weekday whose lines read 0 bottles still counts as measured.

The basis names the weekdays left out.

The no-movement test in `absent-not-zero.spec` now expects an empty profile and the EXTREMES_TOO_FEW sentence.

Merge note: a new conflict on absent-not-zero.spec with resolve/r33-cost-1464-on-cost-meet-v2. Keep ours.

The PR body holds the port recipe for Train A: build the recorded-days Set from `houseDayOf`.

Note: #1639 (fix/seasonality-says-which-weekdays-have-no-line, opened about a minute after #1631 by another session) marks itself "Superseded by #1631. Do not land this PR."

### #1641: a new good reads from the day it was added

`loadInventory` now also reads `restaurant_inventory.created_at`. The column is in the baseline schema at line 35 of the CREATE TABLE. It is dated the way consumption lines are dated.

New `good-window.ts`:
- A good added inside the house's window starts on its added day, still divided by at least a week.
- A line before the added day means the row is younger than the good (a re-created or re-imported row), so the house's window stands.
- With no added day, the house's window stands.

Each sku gets `demandSince`. The demand working counts the goods read from their added day.

Rejected alternatives:
- The good's first line in the window. It inflates slow movers.
- min(added day, first line). It inflates the same way after a re-created row.

Mutant G1 (dropping `if (!addedDay) return house`) is equivalent: a null day falls through to `observedWindow(day:null)`, which returns the house's window.
