## What each PR does
- **#1611**, a line printed only in another money, on the plan:
  - The summary counts these lines apart from blank prices: "N line(s) is/are priced on the menu only in another money, so no price is taken from it/them and the house keeps its own; nothing converts (flagged)".
  - Each line's words in Every line say "no price taken: the menu prints it in another money; the house keeps X (flagged)" or "…and the house has none (flagged)".
  - A `menu-plan-other-money` list shows each held line with its flagNote.
- **#1617**, CSV and Excel price columns say their money:
  - The new `parsers/price-mark.ts` reads the money mark.
  - Precedence: the cell's own mark, then the Excel numFmt, then the header ("Şişe Fiyatı (EUR)").
  - The line's first money becomes `currency`. A certainly different second money goes to `also_priced`.
  - Nothing converts. Family marks ($, £, ¥) are never treated as certainly different.
- **#1634**, a kept menu can be put away:
  - Migration 20271118141300 adds `set_aside_at` and `set_aside_by` (FK to users, RESTRICT).
  - CHECK: a menu that is put away is never the current one.
  - CHECK: a put-away menu always says who put it away.
  - New endpoints `POST /menus/:menuId/set-aside` and `POST /menus/:menuId/bring-back`, for owners and managers only.
  - Make-current on a put-away menu answers 409 "This menu is put away. Bring it back from Put away first, then make it current. Nothing was changed."
- **#1640**, on Menus kept:
  - A "Put away" button on every non-current menu (managers only).
  - A folded "Put away (N)" list with "Bring back".
  - Putting away the menu whose plan is open closes the plan.
- **#1647**, after a discard the page now says the line is off the current menu.
  - For a drink with a house price, it names that price and says it "stays on Inventory: change it there under Your price, or the next menu read without it offers to take it off sale".
  - The footer says the same in one sentence.
