## Decisions (the session's call, under the 2026-11-07 /goal)

1. **Carry many sends one POST per line, through a pool of 4.**
   - Rejected: a bulk gateway route. It is a new contract, and an all-or-nothing answer would lose the per-line result.
   - Rejected: serial sends. Too slow for 100 lines.
   - A 409 is said as "already carried", so a retry can never add a name twice.
2. **Names already on the list are left out on the device.** The server's 409 stays the backstop.
   - Rejected: letting the server answer alone. That costs a request per known name, and each one would read as an alarm.
3. **The batch picks g, ml or each, never kg or l.** This follows ADR 0070 (stock in whole small units) and avoids #1150. A par may still be typed as "5 kg".
4. **Carry many's entries are Tools and inside Carry this, not the empty-page card.** #1568 owns that card.
5. **Gap 2 is fixed in words, not with a kg order unit.** The order-unit CHECK has no weight, and #1110 refuses weights by name. #1367 and #1444 book nothing for measured goods at the door or on "Mark delivered", and R8 (#1108→#1110) books weighed invoice lines.
   - A kg order unit before those land would book nothing, or the wrong amount.
   - A weighed good is now sent the way it really comes in: the vendor, a count here, and the invoice on Receipts.
6. **BottleLeaf reuses `iv-failure`.** Rejected: a cellar copy, which would drift.
   - The order failure is said once, by the hold.
   - Neither write promises "trying again is safe": the order dedupe fails open, and bring-in's 409 is a read-before-insert, not a constraint.
7. **USDA: document the key, not a fallback.**
   - Rejected: `DEMO_KEY`, which is shared and rate-limited.
   - Rejected: bundling figures, which would be a download nobody decided on and numbers with no source.
   - The deployer must still get a free api.data.gov key.

### #1646 Carry many, route `/inventory`, role manager (then staff)

1. Sign in as manager in a house with at least one food on the list, for example "Salt".
2. Open **Tools**. Expected: a button **Carry many at once** after **Carry a food or supply**.
3. Press it. Expected:
   - a sheet titled "Goods the library does not hold";
   - a textarea labelled "The list";
   - selects **Kind** (Food) and **Kept** ("By weight (kept in grams)");
   - the button reads "Paste or type a list first", disabled.
4. Paste these four lines:
   ```
   Bread flour, 5 kg
   Lemons
   Salt
   lemons
   ```
   Expected: the rows say:
   - "Will be carried, par 5 kg.";
   - "Will be carried, no par.";
   - "Already on the list; left out.";
   - "Listed twice; carried once.".

   The button reads "Carry 2 goods".
5. Change line 1 to `Bread flour, 5`. Expected: that row asks "Say which: 5 g or 5 kg.", and the button reads "Put right 1 line first". Change it back to `5 kg`.
6. Press **Carry 2 goods**. Expected:
   - the rows read "Carried.";
   - the summary reads "Carried 2 goods, with nothing on hand until a delivery or a count. Left out: 1 already on the list and 1 listed twice.";
   - Kind, Kept and the list are disabled;
   - the button reads "Nothing left to carry".
7. Close the sheet. Expected: Bread flour and Lemons are on the list with nothing on hand.
8. Reopen the sheet and paste `Lemons`. Expected: "Already on the list; left out."
9. Open **Tools → Carry a food or supply**. Expected: "Have a list? Carry many at once". Pressing it closes that sheet and opens Carry many.
10. Sign in as staff. Expected: no **Carry many at once** in Tools.
