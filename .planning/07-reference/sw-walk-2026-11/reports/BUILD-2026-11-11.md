## Built

- **Charts** (#1019):
  - A forecast draws as one bar series with dashed projected cells, instead of two grouped bars that drew the seam day twice.
  - A null is a gap, never 0. The till fills every house day.
  - A heat cell with no figure reads "nothing recorded".
  - Charts have role="img" with a label.
  - MAPE and R² are said in words (ADR 0104), and the model name is no longer printed.
  - Full screen keeps its bars-and-line pick when the drawing is switched.
- **Currency** (#1020):
  - Money prints through Intl in the house's ISO code.
  - The currency is read in three states: stated, unstated and unread. A sentence and a link to Settings cover the last two.
- **Gateway reads and refusals** (#1033):
  - `loadOrders` and `loadDeliveredOrders` use readWholeWindow, selecting by created_at OR delivered_at.
  - `lensFailure()` keeps a 503 across seven routes.
  - Web `failureOf` prints the gateway's sentence without the SQLSTATE detail.
- **House days** (#1037):
  - `houseZoneFor` and `houseDaily` put cashflow and seasonality on the house's day.
  - A missing zone withholds spend and weekday but still states committed orders.
  - The Holt projection runs on 25 complete weeks.
  - `basis.uncosted` is added.
  - The exports writers say when the zone is missing.
- **Zone wording** (#1038): one zoneUnsetSay(), used by the till, pacing and the week. The bench gets a zone note and loses the ambiguity note.
- **Bench** (#1051):
  - New `AnalysisSpec.sources` and `readsRegister()`.
  - Raw payloads are cached by URL, so pacing and the bench share one cashflow request.
  - A goals list that could not be read is no longer "no goal running".
- **Till window** (#1052): a 365-day option.
