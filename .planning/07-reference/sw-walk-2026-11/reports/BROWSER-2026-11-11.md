## Defects

### Fixed
1. **Narrow keyboard arrange (the brief's bug).** At 2, 4 and 6 columns the announcements used the 12-column slot, and every arrow answered "did not move". Fixed in #1057.
2. **A phone drag shrank the desktop.** A phone drag saved 4-column slots into the one saved sheet. Fixed in #1057.
3. **Up/Down never moved anything, at any ruling.** Compaction undid a one-row step. Fixed in #1057 ("past the neighbour").
4. **Phone static sheet in the wrong order.** It read the left column first; Through the till sat at y=1872px. Cause: one 12-column layout was handed to every breakpoint, and for static items `correctBounds` pushes clamped cutting down instead of compacting. Fixed in #1058. Screenshot 03.
5. **Tablet Up answered "did not move"**, the order was not saved, and Escape kept the reordered list. Fixed in #1058.
6. **Clashing chart labels.** Margin, Restock, Servers and Goals were affected. Fixed in #1059. Screenshot 04.
7. **Week shape dropped weekday ticks** at card width. Fixed in #1059. Screenshot 04.

### Not fixed (and why)
- **"You're 0% of the way to 'Wine revenue to 60k'"** (screenshots 01, 02). This comes from the backend insight generator (`computeGoalsFamily` reads the stored `current_value=0` and ignores direction). It is gateway code, outside this web lane; it needs its own PR.
- **"Show the working" prints engineering words** (`wine_consumption_log`, `null`). This is copy in the register's working text, across many registers. It is a copy pass, not a chart bug.
- **Forecast tooltip "9.599 bottles"**: a count is formatted as a decimal. Small, not done.
- **No "leave without saving"** in arrange mode, other than Escape per cutting and "Put it all back". This is a product fork; I did not open it.
- **The grip overflows its card by 6px** in arrange mode (Margin card, 194 vs 188). Cosmetic.
- **Shell: the sidebar does not fold on a live resize from 900 to 390** (the sheet is squeezed to 74px). A fresh load at 390 is fine. This is shell code, not /reports.
- **Shell: the theme dialog comes back on every reload** after "Not now" on this fresh DB. Seen several times here. Whether "Not now" is meant to be per session is not checked.
- **Shell console**: 404/401 on shell endpoints that are not wired on the local stack. These are not /reports requests.
- **Known limit** (#1058 body): at 6 columns, two 3-wide cuttings side by side cannot be reordered visually.

## Sweep results
- **1024:** every cutting × Draw as × Show instead drew a chart, with no blank or stale chart after a swap. A few overlap flags were measured mid-transition and could not be reproduced once settled, so they are unconfirmed.
- **Full screen:**
  - At 1440, Margin full screen drew 1376×529. Switching it from Scatter to Bars in full screen gave 10 bars and 10 distinct ticks.
  - At 390, five cuttings (till, margin, week shape, spend pacing, what's coming) each filled 390×844 with a 358-wide chart, with no horizontal scroll. "Back to the sheet" closed each one.
- **First render:** about 1.36 s to the full sheet at 1024 (Vite dev, not a production build). The 9 analytics GETs took about 10–90 ms each.
- **Console:** the only /reports error seen was `shortLabels is not defined`. That was a mid-edit HMR moment of my own. The served module has the import, and the page renders.
