## Built

- **No silent loss at intake.**
  - An unnamed 23505, or a bytes race whose winner cannot be re-read, is an error in words; the race winner is returned as a duplicate.
  - A foreign or unreadable vendor/order id is dropped from the link (the paper is kept) and reported in `linkRefused`.
  - The vendor-name read is house-scoped.
  - The door stores `suggested_qty` and `suggestion_accepted` (ADR 0059 L3).
- **Each emailed attachment is read once.**
  - `ingest_state` per attachment, with an outcome for every row: ingested, duplicate, failed (with reason), not_a_document or backlog.
  - Retries are capped at 3, and every download counts against the run's limit.
  - `GET /procurement/documents/arrivals`.
- **The e-invoice (UBL-TR) is read as written.**
  - A strict XML reader (DOCTYPE refused) and a bounded zip reader.
  - A field-by-field UBL-TR reader covering ETTN, VKN/TCKN, units, KDV, tevkifat, IADE and e-irsaliye.
  - It is routed before EDI and the model.
  - The same ETTN is the same paper, enforced by the `uq_pd_restaurant_ettn` index with the race handled.
- **/receipts takes a paper.**
  - Drop or choose a photo, PDF, .xml or .zip; each file gets its own outcome in the gateway's words.
  - An "Arrived by email" list shows each emailed file's state and reason.
