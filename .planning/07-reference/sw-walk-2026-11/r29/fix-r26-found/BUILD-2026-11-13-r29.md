## The session's calls (full rationale is in each PR body)
- **Order's door, volume or weight good: refuse and book nothing; do not convert.** The order's door has no pack size for a measured order: `bottles_total` echoes the quantity, and one box could be one 5 L tin or four 1.25 L tins. Any conversion would be a guessed multiplier going into live stock (ADR 0011). The event is kept as evidence. Weight goods are included because they have the same defect: 1 box was booked as 1 g.
  - Rejected: convert by the order's liters or the container size; a 400 that writes nothing (it loses the door's evidence); a web-only refusal (the outbox replays old clients); volume only.
- **Menu advice: the page formats from the gateway's figures.** The web's locale rule (`house-number.ts`, locale from the house's money) exists only on the web. The gateway's `house-frame` takes the locale from the country instead.
  - Rejected: format in the gateway (a second rule that can disagree); rewrite the numbers inside the sentence; drop the margin and target clause.
- **Get-started: the stored house money wins over the country box once it has been read.** It uses a plain effect, not react-query (no GetStarted test mounts a QueryClient). `createHouse` is unchanged.
- **Pour focus: the panel returns focus**, because the opener unmounts while its box is open.
- **Theme prompt: defer the deep-link scroll until the shared scroll lock is released.** Rejected: scrolling under the modal; changing the prompt's rule; a timeout.
- **Plural: reuse `unitWord`.** Rejected: a local "s" suffix ("eachs", "split_cases").
