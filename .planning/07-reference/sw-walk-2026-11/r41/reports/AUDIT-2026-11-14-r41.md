### Request chain on a cold /reports load (from code, Train A tip)

1. `index.html` → entry JS (275–830 kB gz).
2. `GET /auth/me` (`AuthContext.tsx:424`). `ProtectedRoute.tsx:29` renders the loader until it answers, so the **route chunk is not requested before this**.
3. In parallel: the `ReportsNext` chunk (59–171 kB gz), `GET /organizations/branches` (`AuthContext.tsx:545`, which sets `activeRestaurantId`), and `GET /users/:id/preferences`.
4. Once the chunk has mounted and the house is known: `GET /auth/me/role` (`AuthContext.tsx:603-632`), plus **11 GETs in parallel** for the default sheet: insights, pos-revenue, goals/progress, cashflow, seasonality, goals?status=active (the bench's three sources, two shared through `readRaw`), forecast, menu-engineering, financial, house currency, and the exports list.
5. Registers with `readers` start only after step 4's role read answers (`useReportsNextData.ts:386` `heldBack` while `activeRole === null`).
6. On the server, each register is itself a chain: `readWholeWindow` pages 1,000 rows at a time in sequence (`common/read-whole-window.ts:185`), with an exact count on every page (`:216-226`).

Client serial depth before the first register byte: 3 round trips plus the route chunk. For role-gated registers: 4. Server work per default-sheet load: 5 consumption-window chains (financial 90 d, menu-engineering 90 d, seasonality 90 d, forecast 120 d, the pos-revenue breakdown), 2 order chains (financial 365 d delivered, cashflow 180 d), 1 check chain, plus up to 6 per-goal metric windows.

### Database (from #786's measurement, not re-run; the rules forbid starting a stack)

#786 timed a synthetic house-year locally: a 90-day consumption chain of 12 pages took 366 ms. Each page's exact count is a `Seq Scan` of the **whole table** (1,196 buffers per consumption page, 3,025 per check page). Five chains in parallel took 724 ms. The network was not measured.

## Gaps NOT fixed by any open PR (ranked by impact on "smoothly, fast loading")

### 1. Every register waits for the route chunk, and the chunk carries all of recharts

- **What the reader sees.** On a cold load, nothing on the sheet starts reading until the whole route chunk has downloaded and mounted, because the data hook lives inside it (`useReportsNextData` is imported by `ReportsNext.tsx:79`, and the route is `lazy` at `App.tsx:96`). The chunk is requested only after `/auth/me` answers (`ProtectedRoute.tsx:29`).
- **Measured.** Under the perf chain the chunk is 162.4 kB gz, about 112 kB gz of it recharts. That makes it the heaviest route chunk in the app. Merged with Train A it is about 171 kB gz.
- **Fix shape.**
  - Lazy-load the plotting module (`rp-plot.tsx` and its recharts imports) behind the cuttings, so the sheet frame, words, tables and skeletons ship in about 60 kB gz, and the register reads overlap the recharts download.
  - Optionally, warm the route chunk for the current URL in parallel with `/auth/me`.
- **Open PR:** none. #1239/#1287 fix the shared `ui` chunk, and on purpose put chart code into the pages that draw charts (#1287 `vite.config.ts` note). Nothing splits it inside /reports, and no PR prefetches a route chunk.

### 2. Once the staff trains merge, an owner's first look is "Your role at this restaurant is not known yet" on five cuttings, and those reads start one round trip late

- **Evidence:**
  - `activeRole` is null until `GET /auth/me/role` answers (`AuthContext.tsx:603-632`, Train A tip).
  - A spec with `readers` is held back while the role is null (`useReportsNextData.ts:386-395`, tip). It renders as a **refusal**, not a skeleton (`rp-format.ts:327-331`: "Your role at this restaurant is not known yet, so the … is not read until it is. Nothing below is claimed.").
  - The staff trains put `readers: ['owner','manager']` on till, pacing, bench, quadrants and ledger (#1456 at `rp-registers-trade.tsx:170,336`, `rp-registers-bench.tsx:83`, `rp-registers-house.tsx:46,193`). That is 5 of the 9 read cuttings on the default sheet.
  - The exports shelf flashes the same sentence (`useReportExports.ts:124-128`).
- **For staff.** An unknown role gets the house sheet (`rp-readable.ts:18`, #1558 on Train A). When the role answers, it is swapped for the staff sheet (`decodeSheet(…, activeRole)` at `useReportsNextData.ts:360-361`). Every cold load therefore re-lays the sheet out, and the fills start reading one round trip later.
- **Result.** Owners see refusal text that then flips to skeletons and then to charts. Staff see a layout jump. The gated reads sit at serial depth 4.
- **Fix shape.**
  - Expose "role in flight" from AuthContext. Render a held register as `Waiting` while the role is in flight, and hold the sheet's role-dependent layout behind the outlines until the role is known.
  - Optionally, remember the last role per house locally and start the reads at once. The gateway still enforces `@Roles`.
- **Open PR:** none. The sentence is correct when the role read fails. It is wrong while the read is in flight.

### 3. Each page of a whole-window read re-counts the whole table, and the window columns have no index

- **Evidence:**
  - `readWholeWindow` requires an exact count on every page and refuses as "unstable" without one (`read-whole-window.ts:216-226`). The callers send `{count:"exact"}` (`advanced-analytics.service.ts:155`, `analytics.service.ts:218,256,358`, `goals.service.ts:1077,1082` on the tip).
  - #786 measured each such count as a `Seq Scan` of the entire table, every house and every day.
  - `wine_consumption_log` is filtered on `(restaurant_id, created_at)`, but its only window index is `(restaurant_id, recorded_at DESC)` (baseline `:8793`).
  - `procurement_orders` has only `(restaurant_id)` (baseline `:10459`).
  - The new first-record reads add two more `ORDER BY created_at LIMIT 1` reads on the same unindexed pair: #1641 `observed-window.ts:124-136` (called twice per load, at `analytics.service.ts:718,1032`), and #1616 `firstOrderAt` (`advanced-analytics.service.ts:255`).
- **Result.** A /reports load costs about (pages × table size) in every chain. Today there is one tenant, so this is latent. It grows with every house on the platform, not with this house's window.
- **Fix shape.**
  - Count on page 0 and once at the end, not on every page.
  - Add `(restaurant_id, created_at, id)` on `wine_consumption_log` and `procurement_orders`, with a migration version past everything on main and the open PRs.
  - Re-run #786's bench with two houses.
- **Open PR:** none. All 976 open heads were scanned:
  - only #1278 and #1320 add an index on these tables, `pos_checks (restaurant_id, closed_at)`, for /logs;
  - #874 changes only the cursor column;
  - #786 is docs only.

### 4. Switching the till window blanks the till (and, after #1632, the ledger) to a skeleton

- **Evidence.**
  - The query key carries `tillDays` (`useReportsNextData.ts:411` on the tip, `:277` on main), with no `placeholderData: keepPreviousData`.
  - #1632 makes the ledger `takesWindow` (`rp-registers-house.tsx:329`), so it re-keys too.
  - #786 measured 850 ms locally for `?days=365`, without the network.
- **Result.** Each click on 7/30/90/365 swaps the drawn chart for skeleton bars, then draws the new one. The goal asks for "no bugs when switched".
- **Fix shape.** Keep the previous data while the new window loads, with a quiet "reading the last N days…" note on the cutting.
- **Open PR:** none.

### 5. Coming back to the tab after a minute re-runs the whole sheet

- **Evidence.**
  - `refetchOnWindowFocus: true` is the app default (`App.tsx:161` on the tip), and the registers use `staleTime: 60_000` (`useReportsNextData.ts:404,413`).
  - The figures behind them change hourly (insights are daily by default, r36 gap 4) or per sale.
- **Result.** Every return to the tab after 60 s fires about 11 GETs. These cost 8 whole-window chains, plus up to 6 goal metric windows and 6 `analytics_goals` UPDATEs (gap 6).
- **Fix shape.** On this page, set `refetchOnWindowFocus: false`, or a 5-minute stale time for the window lenses, and keep "Refresh" explicit.
- **Open PR:** none.

### 6. Goal progress re-reads each goal and writes to it on every GET

- **Evidence.** `listGoalsWithProgress` lists the goals, then `getGoalProgress` runs per goal (`goals.service.ts:418-453` on the tip). Each call:
  - re-reads the row it was just listed with (`:488-491`);
  - reads its metric window (`:510`);
  - awaits an `UPDATE analytics_goals SET current_value` (`:519-522`; the same on main, `:482-522`).

  The bench separately lists the same goals (`rp-registers-bench.tsx:90`, `/goals/:rid?status=active`). Two revenue goals each read the same check window the till already reads.
- **Result.** Up to 6 by-key reads, 6 window reads and 6 writes per viewer per load or focus, and the GET's latency includes the write.
- **Fix shape.**
  - Pass the listed row to `getGoalProgress`.
  - Share one window read per metric across goals.
  - Write the reading only when it changed, or off the request path.
- **Open PR:** none (#1116 and #1133 change what is written, not how often).

### 7. The cold load shifts twice before any figure lands

- **Evidence:**
  - While preferences are pending, four outline boxes are drawn: 322 px high, in an auto-fill grid with a 320 px minimum (`reports-next.css:59-69`; `ReportsNext.tsx:689-698` on the tip).
  - The sheet that replaces them has different geometry. The first row is 7/12 by 418 px plus 5/12 by 418 px (`DEFAULT_SLOTS`, `rp-sheet.ts`). The default sheet is about 2,600 px tall against about 660 px of outlines, so the exports shelf and footer drop by about 2,000 px.
  - The opening line goes from one line, "Reading the registers…", to the top insight sentence, which is often two or three lines. `.rp-voice` has no min-height (`reports-next.css:617-623`), so the whole sheet moves down again.
- **Fix shape.**
  - Draw the outlines on the default sheet's own slots, through `packSlots` or `DEFAULT_SLOTS`.
  - Reserve two lines for `.rp-voice`.
- **Open PR:** none.

### 8. The till route reads its breakdown only after the check window

- **Evidence.** `getPosRevenue` awaits `getPosRevenueWindow` (zone and history, then the check pages), and only then starts `getPosConsumptionBreakdown`'s consumption pages (`analytics.controller.ts:1196-1225` on main; the same on the tip).
- **Why it is serial for no reason.** The breakdown needs only `from`, `to` and the zone, which are known after the first key read.
- **Result.** The two page chains run end to end instead of side by side. At 365 days that is 21 check pages plus the consumption pages in sequence (#786).
- **Open PR:** none (#1145 makes the checks read whole, not parallel).

## Fit once the trains merge

- **Two "first record" helpers with two day conventions:**
  - Train A: `purchasing-span.ts` (#1616), on house days.
  - House train: `observed-window.ts` and `good-window.ts` (#1631, #1641), with `dayOf = toISOString().substring(0,10)`, and `readFirstRecord` cutting `.substring(0,10)` of a UTC timestamp.
  - After merge, "since D" on pacing and on restock or forecast can name different days for the same house. Already owed per r40 ("Train A port … needs the houseDayOf form"), so it is not ranked again. The fix should fold the two into one helper, not port twice.
- **`rp-next-step.tsx` add/add** (#1605 and #1624): already noted by r40. Keep the superset.
- **Bench sources.** Train A's bench is `path: null` with `sources` (`rp-registers-bench.tsx:86-90`). The staff train puts `readers` on the same spec (#1456 `:83`). The merge must keep both fields, or the bench is either fetched for staff (a 403 per source) or never fetched. Nothing is broken today; this is a resolver note.
- **Chunking.** #1287's `vite.chunks.test.ts` asserts chart code lives with the pages that draw charts. Gap 1's split must keep that test true: a recharts chunk imported only from /reports passes.
- **No duplicate formatter or contradicting wording was found** between the tips read this round beyond what r36 gap 6 lists. Not exhaustive: only the reports trains above were diffed.

## Checked and found sound

- **The exports list polls every 2.5 s only while an export is queued** (`useReportExports.ts:131-141`). The server fails a queued export older than 10 minutes on the next read (`report-exports.service.ts:29,126`), so polling cannot run forever.
- **Fonts.** Fraunces normal and JetBrains Mono are preloaded (dist `index.html`). The italic used by `.rp-voice` is a `swap` face, so the cost is one reflow, not a block.
- **Payloads.** The inventory-science `skus`, menu-engineering `items` and pos-revenue `consumption` are bounded by the house's goods count. Insights are capped at 40. Not measured on the wire.
- **The bench shares its sources.** On Train A, the bench's cashflow and seasonality share one request with pacing and week (`readRaw`, `useReportsNextData.ts` tip).
- **The insight cold-start recompute** is fixed by #1606 (r36 gap 8).
- **The sheet never draws the house sheet as the reader's own** (#851/#1258): it is fixed on Train A with outlines. Gap 7 is only about the outlines' geometry.
