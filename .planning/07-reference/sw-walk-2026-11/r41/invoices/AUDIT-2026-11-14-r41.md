## Measured

- **Bundle** (`vite build` on main, chunk map from a generateBundle plugin): /receipts first paint pulls the entry chunk
  `index` 1,963 KB raw / 487 KB gz, plus `ui` 1,026 / 290 gz and `vendor` 164 / 54 gz (both modulepreloaded), then
  `ReceiptsNext` 53 / 16 gz. /documents/:id adds `CanonicalDocumentPage` 65 / 18 gz; the door `DoorNext` 28 / 10 gz;
  `/orders` 100 / 29 gz. The entry carries `data/wineops_basic_v1.json` (393 KB rendered), eager `/inventory` pages
  (`App.tsx:76-77`), `Register.tsx` and libphonenumber. **Fixed by open #1239** (entry 3.1 MB to 1.2 MB, lazy
  /inventory and sign-up, drops the `ui` manualChunk), with #1287 on top. Not re-listed below.
- **Cost of goods SQL** (PGlite, minimal schema, 2 houses): house A with 200 sales, house B with 200,000.
  `rows_ LEFT JOIN inventory_transaction_settled_cost` for house A ran **4,354 ms** and seq-scanned all 200,200
  transactions, all 200,200 draws and all 100,148 settlements (plan: Hash Right Join over HashAggregate of every draw).
  PGlite is slower than Postgres; the plan shape, not the ms, is the finding. Script:
  scratchpad `pgl/view-plan.mjs` (not kept).

## Ranked gaps NOT fixed by an open PR

### P1. Opening one paper downloads the whole inventory, again for every paper.
- `CanonicalDocumentPage.tsx:160-163` runs `getInventory()` under key `['canonical-document-items', rid, id]`: the
  document id is in the key, so each paper opened refetches it; it is only used to name linked lines (`:164-176`,
  a linear `.find` per line per render).
- Server side `GET /inventory/:rid` is four unranged reads per call (`inventory.service.ts:256-261`):
  `restaurant_inventory` joined to `master_wine_library` (`database.service.ts:42-50`), lot rollup, location
  breakdown and analytics (`:178-245`).
- Open PRs make it heavier, not lighter: #1077 feeds the ItemChooser from it, #1268 refetches it after an add,
  #801 pages it past 1,000 goods. None changes the key or the read.
- Fix: one house-scoped key (`['inventory', rid]`, shared with /inventory), or a names-by-ids read for linked lines
  and a server search for the chooser.

### P2. /receipts list rows ship the whole parsed paper and the EDI text, which the page never reads.
- `documents.controller.ts:1363` is `select("*")`. That includes `extracted` (the full parsed document, every line;
  `document-intake.service.ts:1016,1043`) and `raw_payload` (up to 500,000 chars for EDI/SFTP, `:1039-1041`).
- First paint asks three lists of up to 100 rows (`useReceiptsNextData.ts:147-166`). `grep` over `apps/web/src`
  finds no reader of `.extracted` or `raw_payload` outside the legacy `ReceivingWorkspace.tsx:426,445`.
- #688 and #1039 touch this handler; neither narrows the select. Payload size not measured on real rows (no
  production query); it grows with line count per paper.

### P3. Each list re-reads every line of every listed paper to recompute tie-outs, in serial pages.
- `tieOutsAsRuledNow` (`documents.controller.ts:1199-1260`) reads `procurement_document_lines` for up to 100
  documents in 1,000-row OFFSET pages awaited one after another (`:1222-1245`), ordered by
  `(document_id, line_no)` with only `idx_pdl_document (document_id)` to serve it.
- First paint runs it three times (queue, clean, verified). 100 verified invoices of 40 lines = 4,000 rows = 4
  serial pages behind the document read: depth 5 DB round trips for the verified lane alone, for verdicts that a
  verified paper can no longer change.

### P4. Verifying one invoice reloads every /receipts read.
- `ReceiptsNext.tsx:1034` invalidates `['receipts-next']`: all three lists (P2+P3 each), the door strip, credits,
  credit stats and memos, and every opened paper's detail. Ten verifies in a row = 30 list rebuilds.
- Fix: invalidate the queue and verified keys and the one paper; patch the list cache from the verify response.

### P5. Table side: cost of goods aggregates every house's draws and settlements, for all time, on each read.
- View `inventory_transaction_settled_cost` (#1004 `20270615100000_a_settlement_keeps_six_places.sql`; earlier copies
  #919, #932, #949) LEFT JOINs two GROUP BY subqueries over the whole `inventory_lot_draw_settlements` and
  `inventory_lot_draws` tables, unfiltered by house or date. `pos_cost_of_goods` (#1025
  `20270705110000_what_sold_cost_is_its_draws.sql`) joins it per sale row; Postgres cannot push `txn_id` into the
  aggregate, so the PGlite plan above scans everything.
- Called once per analytics base load (`analytics.service.ts:305` at #1632 `loadTill`), i.e. /reports and the
  financial summary. Cost grows with the platform's whole sales history, not the window.
- Fix: inline the two aggregates in `pos_cost_of_goods` filtered by `txn_id IN (SELECT id FROM rows_)`, or make the
  view's subqueries LATERAL on `t.id`. Indexes exist (`idx_inventory_lot_draws_txn`, `..._settlements_txn`).

### P6. The door strip silently loses the oldest uncounted deliveries.
- `listUnverifiedCapped` reads the 500 newest receipt events (`receiving.service.ts:1022`) and computes `capped`
  (`:1028`), but `listUnverified` returns `.rows` only (`:993-994`) and the controller never says it
  (`receiving.controller.ts:422-430`). Past 500 events the oldest case counts, the "overdue" ones the endpoint exists
  for, drop out and the strip reads as caught up: absence read as health.
- #1096 adds the document-door half with `doorCountedComplete`; the receipt-event half still drops `capped`.

### P7. Two open PRs collide on the invoice line unit words; the natural resolution loses the US units.
- #1289 deletes `UNIT_WORDS` from `components/documents/canonical-format.ts` and re-exports `unitWord` from
  `lib/unit-words.ts`. #1549 (r36 R4) adds `kg, g, lb, oz, gal, floz` rows to the deleted table.
  Merged, the file conflicts; taking #1289's side drops them and the sheet prints raw codes ("floz").
  `lib/unit-words.ts` at #1289 has no weight or US unit.
- Wording contradicts across sides: the door refusal says "pieces" (#1366 `door-count-lines.ts`, "Count whole
  bottles or pieces"), #1289's web table says "item(s)", main says "each"; #1289 spells "litre", main "liter".
- Fix: when #1549 restacks, put its rows in `lib/unit-words.ts`; pick one word for `each` on both sides.

### P8. Cold load shifts the page; no skeletons.
- While reading, the queue section renders an empty bordered `div` (`ReceiptsNext.tsx:1922-1945`), then the
  "read cleanly" lane (`:1948`) and the verified lane (`:1974`) are inserted as their reads land, in any order,
  pushing what is below. The header line carries "Reading the queue…". No reserved height or skeleton rows.

### P9. Opening one paper is about 8 requests with a 2-deep client waterfall and a 7-to-9-deep server chain.
- Client: detail + canonical + inventory + mappings in parallel; then the order (`ReceiptsNext.tsx:866-871`, waits
  on detail's links) and the delivery event and proposals (`CanonicalDocumentPage.tsx:211-235`, wait on canonical).
- Server `GET :id/canonical`: document, lines, corrections, resolveLines (order items, mapping proposals, user names),
  parties (provider then restaurant), vendor resolution, each awaited in turn
  (`canonical-document.service.ts:255,296,334,346,363,375`), then the controller re-reads the same document row
  with spine and log (`documents.controller.ts:550`) and signs the original (`:590`). Batched, not N+1.

### P10. Low: the manager receiving queue reads vendor names and good names one after the other.
- `receiving.service.ts:1257-1286`: `providers` then `restaurant_inventory`, both depending only on the
  orders read; one avoidable round trip on /receiving.
