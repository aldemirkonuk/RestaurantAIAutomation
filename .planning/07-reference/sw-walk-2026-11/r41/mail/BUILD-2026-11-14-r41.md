## Decisions (the session's calls, recorded in each PR body)

- **Gap 1: drop and recreate `mail_letter_rows` under the same name.** A shell wrapper was rejected: the #954→#1032 stack CREATE OR REPLACEs the function and would bypass a shell without any error. Unsent letters are folded into thread states. Also rejected: dropping `search_path`, and a table kept up to date by a trigger.
- **Gap 2: one read for the counter.** The 15 s per-house memo was rejected for three reasons:
  - Mail's header would be stale for up to 15 s after the house's own act;
  - the counter and Mail use different zone keys, so they would not share entries;
  - a memo kept in one process is not shared across gateway instances.
- **Gaps 4 and 5: the lists follow the summary, with a 2-minute fallback.**
  - Rejected: `maxPages`, which would drop pages the person loaded.
  - Rejected: refetching the first page only. v5 has no `refetchPage`, and the pages would contradict each other.
  - The fallback stays because the summary cannot see a refused or pulled-back letter, or a colleague's letter in a house with no zone.
- **Gap 3: fold where the page draws the letter.**
  - Rejected: stripping the quote at the reader, which would destroy the record.
  - Rejected: folding text that matches an earlier letter, which costs O(n²) and is fragile.
  - Deferred to slice 2: the gateway sends the new words and serves the quote on demand.

   - a letter with no quote shows no button;
   - a forwarded letter shows whole.
