## Findings (not fixed here), with fix briefs

- **F1: a menu of 120 or more new wines cannot be imported.**
  - The gateway answers 500 "Failed to read back provisional library wines: URI too long". In `wine-submissions.service.ts` (~l.930), `.in("signature_hash", signatures)` puts every hash in the GET URL. It fails at 120 lines and passes at 60.
  - The failed import still leaves a kept menu: menus of 126, 206 and 430 lines were left behind.
  - Brief:
    - read back in chunks of 50 (the local patch did this, and 430 lines then imported in 0.43 s);
    - do not keep the menu row when the read-back fails, or say it was kept unread;
    - add a spec with 120 signatures that asserts more than one `.in` call.
  - No open PR covers it.
- **F2: Check its prices contradicts Carry again (#1376/#1377).**
  - The plan lists prices set after the menu was *read* as "would replace", but the carry keeps every price set after the menu was *chosen*. In the walk, the plan said 3 become TRY 501.00, and the carry set 0 and kept 3.
  - Brief: the plan must use the carry's own cut-off (`chosen_at`). Share one predicate between plan and carry, and add a test where a price set between read and chosen is reported as kept.
- **F3: /inventory lists "0 of 1,000 titles" past 1,000 goods.**
  - With 1,105 goods, Negroni could not be found from /inventory.
  - Brief: page past the 1,000-row cap, or search on the server.
- **F4: the country box does not search aliases.**
  - `CountryCombobox.tsx:34` filters `COUNTRY_NAMES` only, so "Ivory Coast" and "Türk" find nothing, although `countries.ts` records both as aliases.
  - Brief: filter `COUNTRIES` on the folded name plus every alias, and show the name.
  - Place it on the combobox stack: #1295 (its base), since #1316 and #1372 rewrite the same file. Not on #1364, which would conflict.
  - Test: "Ivory Coast", "turk" and "CI" each list their country.
- **F5: menu and dishes money in a lira house uses English digits.**
  - "TRY 45.50" and "TRY 1,500.00" on /menu; "₺600.00" on /dishes beside "%16,7"; "₺12.50" on /cocktails.
  - This belongs to the house-number lane (`lib/house-number.ts`, #1303 and the like).
- **F6: the no-answer sentence carries raw axios text** ("timeout of 120000ms exceeded").
  - Brief: say "no answer within 2 minutes" in the house's words.
- **F7: the plan says "prices for wines new to the house" for beer and cocktail lines too.**
  - Brief: name the line by its kind (cf. #1220).
- **F8: the currency select on /get-started has no accessible label, and the "Not known" hint shows before any country is chosen.**
