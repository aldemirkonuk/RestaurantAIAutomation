## Decisions (the session's call; each is in its PR body)

1. **Which menu lines are cocktails.** A line counts only when its section names cocktails: `cocktail` or `cocktails` as a word, or a word starting `kokteyl`.
   - Mocktails are left out, because the menu chain (#1392) counts them as soft drinks.
   - A cocktail word beats a kitchen hint: "Dessert cocktails" is a drink, and `menu-dishes.ts` no longer offers it as a dish.
   - *Rejected:* the cellar ledger's wide list ("sour", "spritz", "signature"), which would turn a "Whisky sour" under "Whisky" into a recipe.
2. **Pricing a menu cocktail.** The glass price comes first, then the bottle price, because a recipe is one serve. Lines of one name that disagree give no price, and `pricesDiffer` says so.
3. **Writing the menu cocktails.**
   - They go in one insert, all or nothing, with `source: 'menu'`.
   - *Rejected:* batching the dish offer's inserts here. Its spec covers per-row races and stop-at-failure, so that rewrite deserves its own PR.
   - The route sits on the recipes controller, because #1544 (r37) is editing the beverages controller.
4. **The drinks offer component.** It is a separate `MenuDrinksOffer` that reuses `someNames` and `PER_RUN`, rather than a `kind` prop on `MenuDishesOffer`. The words differ in almost every sentence, and #1504's files stay untouched.
5. **Gap 9, opening the nutrition card.** The card opens in place by scroll and focus, with no hash and no route, because the rows already use `?open=`. Only the Energy and Pure alcohol cards open it.
6. **Gap 9, the margin card.** It repeats the pour cost's tax caveat. Sold count and target are not built, because they need a gateway join from till names to drink ids.
7. **Gap 10, the wave width.** `WAVE = 4`.
   - *Rejected:* unbounded fan-out; a larger `ID_CHUNK` (longer URLs); a new SQL view (a migration for a latency fix).
