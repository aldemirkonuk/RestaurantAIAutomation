## Browser checks (walker, local stack, never prod)

Stack order: each PR sits on #1571, so check each one on its own branch, or on a local merge of #1571 plus the three heads. #1588 and #1602 both touch RowDropdown.tsx, on separate lines.

### #1582 count many, route `/inventory`, role staff
1. Sign in as staff. Open `/inventory` in a house with at least 3 goods, one of them a food kept in g.
2. Open Tools and press **Count the listed titles**. Expected: a sheet titled "Count the N listed titles", one box per title, and no stock figures shown.
3. Type `5` into the food's box. Expected: "Say which: 5 g or 5 kg." and the button reads "Put right 1 amount first".
4. Change it to `2.5`. Expected: "Reads as 2.5 kg."
5. Type a count into one drink's box and leave another blank. Hold "Hold to record 2 counts". Expected:
   - each counted line says "Counted …; the book said …";
   - the summary starts "Recorded 2 counts:" and ends "1 left blank was not counted; a blank box is never read as 0.";
   - the list shows the new figures after the sheet closes.
6. Tick two rows and press **Count selected**. Expected: only those two titles are listed.

### #1588 drawer reads, route `/inventory`, roles owner then staff
1. Sign in as owner. Open devtools → Network and filter `inventory-ledger`.
2. Click a wine's row to open its drawer. Expected: no `/inventory-ledger/transactions`, `/procurement/documents` or auction-lot request.
3. Click the **Cost** card. Expected: one auction-lot request, and a `/inventory-ledger/transactions?…transactionType=purchase` request only if the good has no settled delivery price.
4. Click **All the evidence**. Expected:
   - the ledger request, then one `/procurement/documents?orderId=…` per ordered purchase;
   - the Paperwork table fills.
5. Sign in as staff, open the same drawer and click Cost. Expected: no ledger or lot request, and "Cost is shown to owners and managers."

### #1602 use from counts, route `/inventory`, role owner
Seeds:
- a wine created 2 days ago with 12 bottles and 2 sale rows today;
- a food in g with two counts 10 days apart.

1. Open the wine's drawer and click the Usage card. Expected:
   - "2 bottles sold in the last 2 days, from the stock ledger, less voids.";
   - a usage of about 1.0 a day, not 0.29.
2. Open the food's drawer and click Usage. Expected: "… between the counts of <d1> and <d2> (10 days): the first count, plus what was received, less the last count. What was written off or found short between them is part of it."
