## Audit

- **The cause, confirmed.** On the chain-B tip (#1265) and on `resolve/r21-menu-chains-meet`, `resolveAndPersistItems` is identical. It sends every non-kitchen line to `resolveLibraryWinesBatch`. `addToInventory` then finds house rows only by `.in("master_wine_id", …)`. Nothing compares the line with the house's goods.
- **A second defect under D1.** `addToInventory` and `computePlan` keyed only on `wine_library_id`. Linking a line to a `house_declared` good (null `master_wine_id`) would therefore have stayed "not linked" in the plan and never been carried. Fixing D1 needed that path too.
- **Schema.**
  - `menu_items.inventory_item_id` exists and has no FK (baseline:3797), so no migration is needed. A dangling link is said on the line.
  - `restaurant_inventory` has `kind` and `display_name`, both NOT NULL, and `bottle_size_ml`.
  - The vintage is only on `master_wine_library`.
  - The library's `bottle_size_ml` is 750 by default on every row, so it is not used.
- **Base.** Chain B (#1252 is the newest chain-B PR on `menus.service.ts`), stacked on its tip #1265. Chain A's #1256 is the newer PR number on the file, but its `resolveAndPersistItems` lacks the kitchen-line split.
