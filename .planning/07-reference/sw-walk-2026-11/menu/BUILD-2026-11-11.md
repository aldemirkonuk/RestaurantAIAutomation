## Decisions (the session's call under the 2026-11-07 /goal; each is in its PR body)

- Background read: in-process continuation plus a lazy 30-min sweep (gateway = long-lived Railway container). Rejected: a cron-claimed queue, and SSE/long-held requests.
- A failed scan is kept as a version that says why; this overrides #793's scan 422 for scans only. Rejected: deleting it.
- The read state lives on the version row, with a DB CHECK that only a done read can be active. Rejected: a jobs table, or a guard in the service only.
- Several photos are joined into one PDF on the gateway. Rejected: a multi-source column.
- MU-11 (a): the same bytes reopen the earlier read (an amendment to ADR 0193 item 7). Rejected: always re-reading, or refusing the duplicate.
- Web polls with backoff. Rejected: SSE, or raising the axios timeout.
- Photos are shrunk and converted on the device. Rejected: converting server-side (HEIC needs a native dependency, and the 15 MB body limit is too small).
- Scan buttons: the direct add-to-inventory goes; the hosts' props are kept; the cellar is handed only unmatched lines.
- Kitchen test: a known drink section wins, and hints match as whole words. Rejected: dropping 'dessert' from the hints, or keeping substring matching.
