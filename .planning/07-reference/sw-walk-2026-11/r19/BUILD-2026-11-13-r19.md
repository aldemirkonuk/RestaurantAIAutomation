## Decisions (the session's call under the 2026-11-07 /goal; each is in its PR body)

- **Auth: AuthProvider is the single owner, and the store is its copy.**
  - Rejected: making the store the owner. That means rewriting a context of about 1,200 lines.
  - Rejected: a shared promise with two owners. The two copies still drift, and that drift is the sign-out leak.
  - Found along the way: before this, the store was EMPTY after any in-app sign-in or the dev bypass, and it still held the last person after sign-out on a shared device.
- **framer-motion: async LazyMotion with domAnimation.**
  - Rejected: synchronous features (only about half the saving).
  - Rejected: domMax (nothing uses layout or drag).
  - Rejected: CSS only (loses exit animations).
  - Risk: an `m` element shows its initial frame until the 14 KB-gzip chunk arrives, and stays on that frame if the chunk fails on a cold load.
- **Sentry: defer, buffer, client-chosen event id.**
  - Rejected: keeping it in the entry.
  - Rejected: dropping early calls.
  - Not kept: the SDK's automatic breadcrumbs before it starts, and buffered calls if the tab closes first.
- **Rail units: each figure scales on its own, as the list and Lately do.**
  - Rejected: forcing both figures into the minimum's unit.
  - `each` keeps its "units" word on the rail, a deliberate difference from Lately's bare count.

## Left / not verified

- **The /auth/me rate limit still bounces a signed-in person to /login at about 10 loads per minute.**
  - Cause: RateLimitGuard allows 10 per 60 s per IP. On a 429, AuthContext keeps the tokens but `user` stays null, so ProtectedRoute sends the person to /login.
  - Reproduced on /reports at two reads per load (6th load). One read per load only halves it.
  - Fix belongs in AuthContext/ProtectedRoute (a 429 means "still signed in, retry") or in the guard's budget. Not built.
