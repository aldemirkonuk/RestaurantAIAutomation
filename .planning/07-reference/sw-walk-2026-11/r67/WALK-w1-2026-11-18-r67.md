## Findings

1. /auth/me bucket too tight for the page's own double load (not changed; gateway rate-limit policy is an auth rule). Fix brief: (a) in apps/web, make AuthContext.loadUser and authStore.checkAuth share one single-flight GET /auth/me so a load costs 1 request, and/or (b) give `GET /auth/me` its own `@RateLimit({limit: 60, windowSeconds: 60})` in apps/api-gateway/src/auth/auth.controller.ts (the bucket is per IP and route, so one restaurant wifi with several staff reloading hits 10 quickly). Also a 429 from /auth/me sends the page to /login with tokens kept; a protected route could wait and retry instead of redirecting.
2. Houseless user: GET /users/:id/preferences 403 on every load (twice). Skip the call when the session holds no house.
3. Calendar at 375 under the terms modal: modal hid the grid in screenshots (terms fixture not accepted); DOM measures used instead.
