## Findings

F1 (#1872, calendar record tag at 375): the month cells are too narrow for any uppercase 11 px word of the tag. Fix brief: at max-width 480 px do not draw the tag in a month cell (keep it as visually hidden text so the reader and the title still say what the number is) and add one quiet legend line above the grid ("Under each passed day: covers recorded", the net-sales variant when that is what the house shows). Alternative: tag text on one line with `white-space: nowrap` and `font-size: 11px; letter-spacing: 0`, cells 46 px cannot hold "recorded" (about 58 px). Update typeFloorOverflow guard (it asserts break-word) accordingly. Not fixed here: a design choice on a floor-guarded page.

F2 (get-started, houseless user, not a #1871 defect as far as measured): a verified user with no house and tokens in localStorage lands in a reload loop between /get-started and /login (console: 403s, WebSocket re-init, then the gateway 429s /auth/me after a few dozen loops).
