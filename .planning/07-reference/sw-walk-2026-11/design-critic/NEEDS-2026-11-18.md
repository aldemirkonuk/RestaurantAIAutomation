# NEEDS, 2026-11-18 (marketing-UI critic, high temperature, merged)

Sources: CRIT-front, CRIT-system, CRIT-ops, CRIT-insight (all 2026-11-18, base origin/main c459b0a12, production build on the local stack, never prod). Duplicates merged; the source id(s) are in brackets. I write no code and open no PRs: each "Brief" is one line a Sonnet builder can act on. Priorities: P0 looks or works broken, P1 cheapens, P2 delight.

Voice note: I am the adversary. I do not care what it costs to build. The ledger look is the brand and it is already the most considered editorial UI in a restaurant tool. It moves like a PDF, and half of it falls apart in the dark.

## The 10 things that would most change how Mudavym looks and feels

1. **Finish the dark theme in one token pass (N-01).** Ground on html/body, fields off pure white, coach banner and the hard-coded `#fbfaf7` flipped. Today charcoal has cream slabs in it, and it is the first thing a night-shift owner sees.
2. **Never state a conclusion before the house is resolved, and make loading a layout, not a sentence (N-02, N-15).** "No restaurant is active" and "the last read failed" flash on cold load. Replace them with skeletons that develop into content.
3. **One route transition for the whole app (N-03).** `startViewTransition`, 90 ms out and 220 ms in on the house curve, page title as a shared element, theme change as a circular reveal. One wrapper makes every screen feel connected.
4. **Raise the type floor and cut the paragraphs (N-04).** 8.5 to 11 px text is everywhere (/connections, /cellar, charts). Floor of 11 px for mono kickers and 14/22 for sentences, with explanations behind disclosures. The words for developers go too.
5. **Fix chart axis collisions, then make charts answer (N-05, N-17).** Rotated titles sit on tick labels on every chart. After that, bars draw, tooltips follow a crosshair, and the forecast gets a band.
6. **One front door (N-06, N-07, N-08).** Put forgot-password, verify-email, invite, no-access and /get-started on the open-book spread, add a real 404, and turn the first-sign-in 429 into a calm retry instead of a logout.
7. **Collapse empty things instead of explaining them (N-09).** The 7-row "none waiting" Counter rail becomes 5 dots, and the empty registers get a 96 px line drawing with one action and one honest sentence.
8. **One design system underneath (N-10).** Kill the `on ? mdv : legacy` fork, the five warm whites and the three radius languages. Use an OKLCH ramp, tinted shadows and motion tokens, and ban `transition-all`.
9. **A staff home that is one thumb deep (N-11).** Three big actions, swipeable low-stock rows, a 4-item nav, and 2-line bottle cards on a phone. The person on the floor is the daily user.
10. **Give every control physics (N-12).** Sliding pills on every segmented control, `grid-template-rows` 0fr to 1fr for inline panels, a press state (`scale .98`, 90 ms) and the existing 300 ms sheet spring borrowed everywhere. The team already built the spring. Use it.
