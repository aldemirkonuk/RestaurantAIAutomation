Verdict in one line: the paper theme is already quiet and expensive (Fraunces headlines, warm neutrals, teal accent); the product is a beautiful printed ledger that has no motion at all, a half-finished dark theme, and prose where a glance should be.

## P0 (looks broken)

1. Dark (Charcoal) has a light slab in it. On /wines, /inventory?view=map and others, below the footer line a cream rectangle (about 395 x 60 px at 1440, x 130 to 620) fills the rest of the main column; it grows when the Ask rail opens. Evidence: body is `rgb(250,247,245)` while `.mdv-shell` and the page are dark, `html.className` stays "light", and the shell is 901 px in a 900 px viewport. Why: the body/root ground does not follow the theme, so any short page shows the bare body. Needs: ground on `html` and `body` from the same token as the shell (`--ground`, charcoal `oklch(0.17 0.01 70)` = rgb(21,19,15) already in use), `color-scheme: dark` on `:root` so scrollbars, selects and the overscroll flip too.
2. Every input is a pure white slab in dark. Computed: palette search, Ask box, Profile fields, inventory search, zone/type selects: `rgb(255,255,255)` background, `rgb(31,41,55)` text (a Tailwind grey, off palette), 8 px radius, on a charcoal page. The command palette is the worst: a 51.5 px blinding bar on a calm dark card. Needs: field fill `oklch(0.22 0.01 70)`, 1 px hairline `rgba(239,231,217,.14)`, text `#efe7d9`, placeholder at 45% of that; focus ring teal `oklch(0.72 0.09 205)` 2 px with 3 px offset. Same for the white "Inventory insights" pill, the white empty-map card and the white "SELECT" checkbox.
3. The first-run tour banner is a light card on dark ("Dashboard / Inventory ... Take tour | Later | Don't show again"): near-invisible grey body text on pale grey, the one light object on a 375 px dark screen. Needs: the token flip like the rest of the shell, and a 1 px top inset highlight so it reads as a card, not a hole.
4. The theme question appears again on every full load until you press Save. "Not now" does not persist: I dismissed it on /, reloaded, and it was back (three times, owner and staff). It also opens on top of the content with a scrim at first paint. Why: a first-run decision that returns like an advert. Needs: "Not now" writes a 30-day snooze; ask once, after the first successful task, not at paint; and when the OS is in dark, offer System first.
5. Cold-load flashes a false alarm. /wines on a fresh load showed for several seconds "No restaurant is active on this account, so there is no building to open a cellar for. The book is unread, not empty" and the counter said "The last read failed", then silently became the real register. Needs: the page must not render any conclusion before the house is resolved; show the loading shape (see 6) until then. A wrong sentence that vanishes is worse than a blank.
6. Loading is plain text. "Reading the locked prices...", "Reading the menus this house has kept...", "Opening the book..." with the rest of the page empty. A `mdv-skel-pulse` keyframe is in the CSS and I could not find a place it is used on these pages. Needs: skeletons that are the real layout: header line 240 x 14 px, table rows 44 px at 5 rows, opacity 0.5 to 0.9 on 1.4 s ease-in-out, and when data lands the skeleton cross-fades 160 ms (no layout jump); text "Reading..." only after 800 ms as a status for assistive tech.

## P1 (cheapens the product)

7. No route transition at all. Clicking Receiving from Orders: `location` and the H1 changed within 40 ms, `main` opacity 1 throughout, no animation. Only one element carries a view-transition-name. Needs: `document.startViewTransition` on router navigation: outgoing content opacity 1 to 0 in 90 ms, incoming `translateY(6px)` to 0 + opacity in 220 ms `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`, the sidebar active bar and the page title as shared elements (title morphs, 260 ms). 11 reduced-motion rules already exist, so the guard is there.
8. Inline panels pop with zero animation. "Add a cocktail" form: `getAnimations()` empty 60 ms after the click, the page body shoves down 190 px instantly. The inventory row expand (three-column "where the count comes from / how fast it pours / what it has cost") is also a snap. Table to Cellar map swap: 0 animations. Needs: height auto via `grid-template-rows: 0fr` to `1fr` over 280 ms `cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1)`; content fades 120 ms later; FLIP the rows below; for table/map a 200 ms cross-fade with the toggle's pill sliding (shared underline).
9. 534 words and 10 to 11.5 px type on /cellar. Paragraph sizes measured: 10 px (4 paras), 10.5 px (4), 11 px, 11.5 px (8), 12.5 px; line-height about 1.5; plus the mono kickers. It reads as a legal page in a very good font. Needs: body 14 px / 22 px minimum (Plus Jakarta 14 at 65 ch); cut the page to the three numbers it shows ("56 bottles, 6 titles, 2 need you") and put the explanations behind a "How is this counted" disclosure; the tiles above the prose should be the page.
10. The dashboard month grid is a graveyard when empty. Of 35 cells 27 are grey "-" boxes (the whole month, "This house's timezone isn't set, so no day's figures can be filed yet"). Needs: when there is no data, collapse to a week strip (7 x 64 px) with one clear action chip "Set the time zone" in teal, and let the month appear on first data; the month reveal could stagger cells 12 ms each from today outward (compositor-only opacity + translateY 4 px).
11. KPI tiles are inert and quiet in the wrong way. IN THE CELLAR 56 / RUNNING LOW 2 / WAITING ON YOU 0 sit in flat beige boxes (about 8 px radius, 1 px hairline); only "2" is teal. Needs: tabular numerals counting up from 0 over 600 ms `easeOutExpo` on first view only; "2" gets a 2 px teal underline that draws left to right; hover = lift 2 px with a shadow `0 14px 28px -18px rgba(33,28,22,.35)`, 160 ms; every tile that has a destination is a link (running low goes to the filtered inventory, it already has `?` filters). Zero values should be at 55% ink, not the same weight as live ones.
12. Menu upload is a 2005 file input. /menu shows the browser's native "Choose File / No file chosen" (311 x 25 px, 12.5 px) in a form row with "How often it changes" and "Date" fields as 18 px high selects; the primary "Read this menu" is a disabled 0.55 opacity chip. Needs: a drop zone 180 px tall with a dashed 1.5 px hairline, a page-turn icon, "Drop a menu, a photo, or a PDF"; while a file is held the zone breathes (scale 1.01, 1.6 s), and on read the file thumbnail slides to a "reading" state with a 3-dot pulse; the two optional fields collapse behind "Add details". On a phone the camera is the primary action (full-width 56 px button).
13. /house/menu is a stranded page: bare serif H1 "No menu has been read yet." and a 10 px link, no sidebar, no header, 1440 px of empty cream. Needs: either the shell, or this is a deliberate full-bleed moment: a ruled page with a big "Read a menu" button, a line drawing of a menu card, and the same drop zone as item 12.
14. Empty states are sentences, not scenes. /orders ("The book is open and empty - no active orders"), /receipts (left "Nothing awaits review" and right "Choose a document from the queue", two text lines in 1000 px of cream), /vendors ("No vendors yet"), /cocktails, the empty Cellar map ("No storage locations yet" in a white card), /team coverage. Honest (ADR 0020) but the house's most-seen screens when it is new. Needs: one 96 px line illustration per register in the ink of the logo, one primary action, and the honest sentence in 13 px under it; the receipts split pane should show a ghost document (skeleton lines at 6% ink) so the product's job is visible.
15. Dense 5-pill filter row on /inventory ("All 6, Out 1, Below par 1, Reconcile 0, Count due 6, Dead stock 2, Price signals -") has no active-state motion: the selected chip is just a fill. Needs: a single shared pill that slides under the chosen chip (spring stiffness 400, damping 32), counts tick on change, and zero-count chips drop to 40% opacity.
16. Table typography on the inventory register. Mono "0 / 0 - 2" (live/shadow/par) at 10 to 11 px next to 14 px names, hairline dividers only on the group bands, "- btl - - gl" and "no target set" repeated per row as 9 px mono. Eight of ten columns say a dash. Needs: hide empty columns by default behind a "Columns" control, 56 px row height, a tiny 6 px par bar under the live count (it exists as a grey line, make it teal/amber/red against par and animate its width 400 ms on mount), status as a coloured dot plus word, not a word alone.
17. Staff gets the owner's whole desk. Sam sees 17 nav items (Dashboard to Help), the same dashboard with two money tiles swapped for "Deliveries today" and "Bottles in / month", the same calendar graveyard. On a phone that is a 2,400 px page where the 5th KPI orphan leaves a hole. Needs: a staff home that is one thumb deep: three big actions ("Count", "Receive", "Ask"), the low-stock list as swipeable rows (swipe right = recount, 56 px rows), and the nav reduced to four. This is the one place I would be loudest: the person on the floor is the daily user.
18. Mobile (375 px): zero horizontal scroll (good), the bottom tab bar (Counter, Rooms, Search, Ask) is lovely, but 57 of 62 interactive targets are under 44 px on /inventory (filter chips 32 px, selects 18 px high on desktop, tour links). Table rows become label/value stacks of 9 lines per bottle, so 6 bottles are about 2,000 px. Needs: a 2-line card per bottle (name + status dot, then "12 live - par 4" + price), tap opens the same expansion as a bottom sheet; chips as a horizontal scroll-snap strip.
19. Developer words reach the owner. Receiving: "ONE EVENT - THREE RENDERINGS - THIS ONE IS OWNER"; wines: "Stock moves arrive on the socket (stock:updated -> restaurant:<id>) and are written straight into the row"; cellar: "restaurant_inventory" in a sentence; counter log: "read 17:28 - again in 60 s - on focus"; profile: "no workspace connected, nothing agreed to act as you". Needs: one plain line each ("This updates as bottles move."), the mono kicker only for place names.
20. The Ask rail reflows the page. Opening it docks 330 px on the right and the register table rewraps ("2020 Synthetic Estate Local Check Cabernet Napa Valley" goes to two lines) with no animation. Needs: Ask as an overlay panel over the existing right counter rail (already 330 px wide and mostly empty "none waiting" rows), slide 280 ms, no reflow underneath; and when idle the counter rail's seven "none waiting" lines should collapse to one line "Nothing waiting" with a green dot.

## P2 (delight, daring)

21. Command palette: today a flat list at 14 px radius, no blur (`backdrop-filter: none`), white input in dark, opens in about 160 ms. Make it the signature: `backdrop-filter: blur(14px) saturate(1.2)` on a 55% scrim, the card scales 0.97 to 1 with a spring (stiffness 520, damping 34), results stagger 14 ms each, the selected row has a shared highlight that glides between rows (layout animation), recent items with tiny thumbnails, and "Ask Mudavym" as the last row when nothing matches.
22. Counter rail as a living thing: the 7 registers (Seal, Verify, Reply, Decide, Mudavym proposes) could be seven 6 px dots in the header that pulse once (1.2 s) when a register changes; the full list slides out on click. The ink-and-stamp language already exists on the front; a "seal" stamp 320 ms (scale 1.15 to 1, rotate -4deg, soft thud shadow) when an order is sealed would be the best micro-interaction in the product.
23. Theme switch as a reveal: today it is a hard repaint (and in one mid-frame the dark ground arrived while the ink was still dark, about 1.1:1). Use the View Transitions API with a circular `clip-path` reveal from the toggle, 480 ms `cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)`, `::view-transition-old(root)` z-order flipped. Add the System option's moon/sun as a 2-state icon morph.
24. Numbers deserve kinetic type. `Fraunces` for headlines is the right call; for figures use the mono you already have with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums slashed-zero`, `font-variation-settings` weight sliding 400 to 500 on change, and a 160 ms digit roll (translateY 100% to 0, clipped) when a stock figure updates over the socket. Right now live updates presumably swap silently, which is the least trustworthy way to change a number on an inventory screen.
25. Sidebar: the active item is a teal left bar + pill; add a 220 ms slide of that bar between items (shared element), and make "Tuck the rooms" collapse to a 56 px icon rail with tooltips at 120 ms delay 300 ms. The grouping labels (THE FLOOR, THE DOOR, THE CELLAR, THE BOOKS, THE PEOPLE) are a beautiful idea: turn them into sticky mini-headers that fade as you scroll the rail.
26. Hover and press states. Buttons transition at 0.16 s `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` (good), but there is no press state: add `scale(.98)` 90 ms on :active, a 1 px inner highlight on hover. Focus: the measured outline on a focused nav link is `none / 3px / rgb(192,182,165)` with `:focus-visible` false on a script focus, so I could not prove a ring; check it with the keyboard (Tab) in dark specifically, the grey 3 px token has about 2:1 on charcoal. Needs 2 px teal ring, offset 2 px, at 3:1 on both grounds.
27. Light mode H1 scale: dashboard greeting is 38 px, "Inventory." 40 px, "Receiving" similar, all Fraunces 420 with -0.95 px tracking and a teal full stop. Nice and consistent; add optical sizing (`font-variation-settings "opsz" 144`) on the greeting and a 400 ms ink-in (letter-spacing 0.02em to -0.02em, opacity .6 to 1) on first load only. Greeting could change with time ("Good evening, Olive." is already right at 17:2x).
28. The gorgeous mobile receiving screen shows what is possible: a teal mono "≥$0" at about 48 px in a charcoal card. Bring that scale to the dashboard KPI tiles (value 40 px mono, label 11 px) instead of the current 28 px values, so the one number per tile owns the tile.
29. Daring: turn the dashboard's empty calendar into a "day strip" ribbon that scrolls horizontally with scroll-snap, today pinned centre with a 2 px teal needle, and bottle arrivals/orders as small stamps on their days. Scroll-driven animation (`animation-timeline: scroll(inline)`) fades the days away from today to 40%. Nothing in this category of software looks like that.
30. Daring: the cellar as a place. The Cellar map is a white "No storage locations yet" card; draw the zones as a plan view in line art (Main Cellar, VIP Reserve, Bar Stock as soft rounded rectangles), bottles as 6 px dots grouped by par state, tap a zone to zoom (layout transition 360 ms) into its list. For a wine person this is the screenshot that sells the product.
