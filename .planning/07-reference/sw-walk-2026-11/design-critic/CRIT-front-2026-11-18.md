# CRIT front tour, 2026-11-18 (marketing-UI critic, high temperature)

Base: origin/main c459b0a12, production build (vite build + preview), local stack only. Landing, /story, /l/day, /privacy and the auth pages were first viewed with no gateway (build pointed at a dead port), then the signed-up path on the local stack (4410/3410). Stack held 17:15 to 17:20 by the machine clock, then `down`. Viewport back to desktop, tab closed.
Seen: / , /l/day (file is /l/day/index.html), /story, /login, /register (+?type=new, ?type=join), /forgot-password, /reset-password (no token), /verify-email, /invite/:code (gateway down), /no-access, /privacy, an unknown URL, then a real new-owner run: register -> verify (email_verified set in the LOCAL db, mail is mocked) -> sign in -> /get-started steps 1 to 3 -> /house, /house/menu.
Not seen: /terms (same shell as /privacy), the 6 other /l/* pages (only /l/day exists in this tree), the menu upload and photo path (no storage locally), light/dark of /story. Screenshots of the browser pane lag one frame after an action; every "blank" frame below was re-checked with a second screenshot or a JS read.

Verdict in one line: the landing is already clean-girl. Everything after you click Start free is a different, cheaper product.

## P0 (looks or works broken)

1. /get-started and /house go half-dark in dark mode. Evidence: with ui-storage theme=dark the header turns near-black but the page wrapper is a hard-coded `bg-[#fbfaf7]` (DIV.min-h-screen), so "Welcome, there." renders cream on cream (about 1.1:1), inputs become black slabs, role chips black boxes. Why: one hex bypassed the token system, so tokens flip and the literal does not. Needs: the wrapper on `var(--paper-0)`; dark paper `oklch(0.17 0.01 70)` (the app already resolves body to rgb(21,19,15)); sweep for other `bg-[#` literals.
2. First sign-in as a brand-new owner bounces back to /login. Evidence: after the password step the URL went /get-started, then /login. Gateway log shows `Rate limit exceeded ... /api/v1/auth/me - 10 requests per 60s` (the sign-in fans out several /auth/me calls); next click on step 1 returned "Too many requests. Please try again later." in small red-brown text. Why: the most important first minute ends in an unexplained logout. Needs: de-duplicate the bootstrap calls; and if a 429 happens, a calm inline state ("One moment, setting up your house", auto-retry with backoff, 3 dots at 1.2 s), never a redirect to the front door. (Caveat: local gateway, one tab; I could not prove it is the same on production.)
3. Unknown URLs have no 404. `/this-page-does-not-exist` and a mistyped `/l/day` silently become the sign-in screen. Needs: a real page in the book language: a ruled page, a torn-off corner, "That page isn't in the book", one link home. 200 ms fade, nothing else.

## P1 (cheapens the product)

4. Three brand grammars in the auth family. /login and /register are a two-page open book (excellent). /forgot-password, /reset-password, /verify-email, /invite/:code, /no-access are a bare 300 px card floating in 1440 px of cream with 9 to 11 px mono labels. /get-started is a third thing: system-serif H1, square corners, white borderless inputs. Needs: put every one of them on the book spread (verify-email = the page being sealed, a wax-seal stamp animation 320 ms; no-access = a locked page). One wordmark: today the page chrome shows a badge tile, an N-M line mark and a bare wordmark.
5. /get-started H1 is not Fraunces. Computed: `ui-serif, Georgia, Cambria, Times New Roman`, weight 700, 48 px, -0.96 px tracking; the landing H1 is Fraunces 500, 73.6 px. Fraunces is loaded, so the class was never applied. Needs: Fraunces 500, optical size 144, `font-variation-settings "SOFT" 50, "WONK" 0`, 56 px desktop / 36 px at 375.
6. Fields on the onboarding screens do not exist visually. Name input: bg #fff on #fbfaf7 (about 1.04:1), border-bottom 0, 32 px tall, 24 px serif text. Mobile input: lavender-grey placeholder, off palette. Needs: 1 px hairline `rgba(33,28,22,.22)`, 52 px height, 16 px sans text, focus = underline grows from the left over 240 ms `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`, label floats up 8 px.
7. Placeholders look like entered values on /register. Placeholder colour rgb(102,93,80) at opacity 1 on cream, same family and size as the typed ink rgb(33,28,22); "John Smith" and "••••••••" read as pre-filled. Needs: placeholder at 45% ink (about 3.2:1 is fine for a hint), italic Fraunces, and never dots in a placeholder.
8. No inline validation, no in-flight state. "Create account" is a washed teal (disabled, white on about #9aaaa9 = 2.4:1) with no reason; "Min. 8 characters" stays grey when the password is 5 characters; mismatch is not flagged; after submit the button does not change for about 2 s. Needs: the button text becomes a 3-dot pulse (ld-breathe 2.4 s already exists), a checklist under the password that ticks one dot at a time (120 ms stagger), a field-level shake of 4 px / 180 ms on mismatch only.
9. The book spread is mute. Login to register to password step are state swaps; there is no page turn. Needs: View Transitions API `document.startViewTransition`, the right page `rotateY(-160deg)` about the spine, `perspective 2200px`, 520 ms `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`, a moving shadow gradient on the gutter. The password step should slide the email into a chip (it already becomes a chip with "Change"; animate the shared element with FLIP, 280 ms). Daring version: the left page's 112 px watermark tiles parallax 6 px against the cursor.
10. The orphaned jewel: /l/day. A 3D book that opens onto the real login page, line-art restaurant, live POS/website split. It is noindex, nothing in src links to it, and on the current site `/l/day` (no index.html) falls through to /login. Needs: make it the hero of the product page or the "See it work" target, not a hidden URL. Its own bugs: (a) display headline "A day in the life of a house." has collapsed word spacing ("inthelife"), negative tracking too tight at 44 px and above, add `word-spacing .08em`; (b) at the final dark section the fixed nav wordmark and "Sign in" are dark ink on near-black (unreadable), switch the nav to the on-dark token when the stage is dark; (c) mobile nav loses the wordmark; (d) 25,967 px (desktop) and 23,594 px (375) of scroll for one story, give a chapter rail with a progress line like /story has.
11. Landing hero reveal blocks the LCP. Every hero element starts at opacity 0; headline words are staggered 95 ms each from 175 ms, 760 ms each, the CTA is at full opacity only at about 1.6 s after paint. (FCP read 3.46 s on this busy machine, so I am not quoting an absolute.) Needs: render the H1 visible and animate only `translateY` + blur 6px to 0 (no opacity 0); total choreography 700 ms; keep the word stagger at 40 ms.
12. Mobile landing carries two identical "Start free" buttons on screen at once: the header pill and the 56 px bottom dock, plus 11 targets under 44 px. Page is 20,171 px tall at 375. Needs: header CTA hidden while the dock is shown; dock safe-area padding kept; the platform grid as a horizontal snap carousel (scroll-snap-type x mandatory, 86% cards) instead of 6 stacked cards.
13. Landing dark mode: forced light. With the OS in dark the page keeps `class="light"` and the only dark thing is the "Coming soon" island, which is gorgeous. Needs: either a real dark theme (paper-0 `oklch(.18 .01 70)`, hairlines at 14% ink, the same teal at L .72) or an explicit "light only" decision; today the product has dark on the app side and none on the front.
14. Primary button hover is a flat colour swap (`.ld-btn:hover {background: seal-deep}`) and a 3 px arrow nudge. There is no press state. Needs: `transform: translateY(-1px)` + a 1 px inner highlight on hover, `scale(.98)` 90 ms on :active, a soft teal glow `0 10px 24px -10px rgba(26,94,107,.55)`; arrow nudge 4 px with a spring (stiffness 520, damping 28).
15. Onboarding steps share one frame but swap through a blank. Step 3 "Type a few lines": two consecutive frames were empty (header gone, drop zone gone) before the table appeared, about 2 to 3 s end to end. Needs: shared-layout transition: the drop zone shrinks to 96 px while the table grows beneath, 360 ms; no frame may be blank. Progress ("01 · YOU", "02 · YOUR RESTAURANT") is 11 px mono top right; give it a 2 px progress hairline under the header that eases to the next third.
16. Developer words in front of an owner. Step 2 says "Places search needs VITE_GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY. Type the address by hand until it is set." (env var name); login says "Google sign-in isn't configured on this deployment yet." Needs: one plain sentence ("Address search is off for now. Type it in, it works the same.") and no Google line when Google is off at all.
17. Step 2 details: country list opens alphabetically from Afghanistan although the placeholder promises "United States, Turkey, UK…" (pin those three on top, type-ahead already works); the "Find your restaurant" box mirrors what you type into Address; timezone inferred as America/Detroit for a San Francisco address (shown as grey 11 px text with no edit affordance). Needs: pinned countries, one address field, timezone chip with a pencil.
18. The name is asked again. Registration already collected "Critic Test"; /get-started step 1 shows YOUR NAME · REQUIRED empty. Needs: prefilled, collapsed to "Welcome, Critic." (the headline already updates live as you type, that is the best micro-interaction in the flow; keep it and add a 120 ms crossfade on the name token).
19. /house is the first-run payoff and it is a to-do list. One column, text only, "Later" blocks at about 0.4 opacity (about 2:1), no motion, no image, no sense of a house taking shape. Route change also keeps the previous scroll offset (arrived at scrollY 224). /house/menu empty state is a headline and an 11 px link on 90% blank.
Needs: a house card that fills in as it learns (a line-art room that gains a table per supplier, see /l/day's illustration style), skeleton rows that morph into content, a 6 px "Later" fade-in rather than faded type, and `scrollTo(0,0)` on route change.
20. Labels are not tied to inputs. Accessibility tree names the register fields by their placeholder ("John Smith", "••••••••"); the login Continue button has no accessible name. Needs: for/id on every label.

## P2 (delight, divergent bets)

21. Hero feed as a living ledger: new rows currently drop in with `ld-ev-in` (translateY -18 px, 620 ms). Add a quiet haptic: the "Waiting for you 1" counter should count up with a tabular-nums roll (odometer) at 240 ms, and the row that needs a yes should breathe a 1 px teal ring once.
22. Scroll-linked story spine on the landing: a single teal thread (SVG path, `animation-timeline: scroll(root)` already supported here: `CSS.supports` true) that draws from the hero feed through "It arrives, is read, is checked..." and ends in the Start free button, which lights as the line arrives. The existing 2 px top progress bar is the shy version of this.
23. Kinetic type for "running itself.": the italic teal phrase could set with variable-font `opsz`/`SOFT` axes animating from 9 to 144 over 900 ms as it arrives (Fraunces has both), so the word literally settles into focus. Respect reduced motion (the file already gates on no-preference).
24. The before/after toggle ("Without / With Mudavym") cross-fades rows; mid-transition both texts are visible overlapping on the same line ("Vendor mail" row). Needs: stagger out 120 ms, in 220 ms, `position:absolute` swap so lines never overlap; make the pill a sliding thumb with a spring (stiffness 400, damping 32).
25. Easing monoculture. One curve (`--ld-house` = cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), an expo-out) is used for arrivals, hovers and dock. It is lovely for arrivals and mushy for presses. Needs: a second token for press (ease-in 90 ms) and one real spring for shared-element moves (use `linear()` easing with a 12-point spring fit, 520 ms).
26. Empty/error states are text: "Invitation unavailable. We could not load this invitation. Please try again." with a "Try again" link is honest (good, a failed read says it failed) but flat. Needs: a half-open book with a bookmark ribbon that droops, and a primary "Try again" button with a 600 ms spinner ring; keep the plain sentence.
27. The "Coming soon" dark island is the best section on the page. Make it the opening act of the dark theme (item 13) and let the flavour map points drift 2 px on a 6 s sine so the chart is never dead.
28. Stamp the brand: the badge mark appears 16 px in the nav and 112 px as a repeated watermark on the auth page. A 40 px version should "press" (scale 1.06 to 1, 140 ms, with a 1 px ink-bleed shadow) when the book opens on /login.

## What is already right (keep)
Fraunces plus Plus Jakarta Sans pairing; Fraunces italic teal accent phrases; one confident accent (#1a5e6b); the "sample" labelling on every invented number; the scroll progress hairline; `prefers-reduced-motion` gating; the "Your week, in hours." sliders; the book metaphor on /login.
