# CRIT insight tour, 2026-11-18 (marketing-UI critic, high temperature)

Base: origin/main c459b0a12, production build (vite build + preview), local stack only, held 17:41 to 17:48 by the machine clock, then `down` (exzueerziesmczwlhomd containers untouched, 6 still running), viewport back to desktop, tab closed. Owner account only (dev bypass tokens). Because every register was empty I seeded made-up local rows (3 vendors, 36 delivered orders over 150 days, 420 consumption rows, 300 POS checks; `design-critic/seed-crit.sql`), so the three charts that can draw did draw.
Seen: /reports (every card, scroll top to bottom, Arrange the sheet mode, keyboard grip, 1440 paper and charcoal, 375 charcoal), /communications (page, "Write a letter" sheet at 1440 and 375, Counter rail), /connections (whole 6,000 px page), /settings, /vendors (Book, Scorecard, vendor sheet), /orders (list, receipt sheet, 1440 and 375), the bell popover, cmd+K palette, Rooms bottom sheet at 375, the login at 375.
NOT seen, and why: a "Mail tab" on Communications does not exist at this base (Communications is one page; the sender/mailbox register lives on /connections and the mailbox gate sits in the letter sheet), so there are no Mail sheets to tour. Toasts: I could not make one fire (the palette "default landing" action showed nothing in 150 ms and Sonner never mounted), so toast comments come from `house-toast.css`/`AppToaster.tsx`, not from sight. Pointer drag in Arrange mode: a synthetic drag did nothing; I drove the keyboard grip instead (it works, see 4). The "full screen" chart: there is no full-screen affordance on any chart (none to tour, which is finding 3). Staff and manager views: staff/manager seeds are not in this fresh DB (login returned empty tokens), so owner only. Floor, mail threads, receipts detail: not in this leg.
Caveat on every motion number: the browser pane throttles and screenshots lag by a frame (I re-shot several times), so durations below are `getAnimations()` timings read at 30 to 60 ms, not felt frame rates.

Verdict in one line: this is the most considered editorial UI I have seen in a restaurant tool, a printed ledger with a real point of view (Fraunces headline, mono kickers, one teal), and it moves like a PDF: the one spring it owns (the sheet, 300 ms with a 1.0093 overshoot) proves the team can do it, and then nothing else uses it.

## Credit first (so you know where the bar is)

- Sheets already use a proper spring: `linear()` easing sampled at 59 points, 300 ms, peak 1.0093 (that is stiffness about 520, damping about 34, a gentle 0.9 percent overshoot). Scrim `rgba(23,19,15,.28)`. Right sheet 640 x 900 on desktop, bottom sheet with a grab handle and mono kicker on phone. Keep it; make everything else borrow it.
- Mobile shell is right: 4-item bottom bar (Counter, Rooms, Search, Ask), Rooms as a bottom sheet, communications at 375 in charcoal is the best screen in the product.
- Reduced-motion is respected in the toast CSS; the 11 guards the ops leg found exist.
- Roman-numeral index on Settings (I The house ... VII The record) with a sticky left rail is genuinely luxe editorial.

## P0 (looks broken)

1. Chart axis titles collide with tick labels, on every chart that draws. Measured on /reports: each SVG has 2 to 3 overlapping text pairs; the rotated y-title ("paid to", "spend/day") sits on top of "$3.4K / $2.5K / $1.7K" (visible at 375 in the screenshot: "paid to" glued onto "$3.4K"). Tick type is 9.5 px, axis titles 9 px and 8.5 px. Why it fails: it reads as a rendering bug on the page that sells "the books". Needs: move the y-title to a top-left caption above the plot ("Spent per day, $", 11 px, 60 percent ink) and delete the rotated one; ticks 11 px minimum, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`; reserve a 44 px left gutter computed from the longest tick; hairline gridlines at 6 percent ink, no axis line.
2. 8.5 px type is in the product. /connections has 71 text nodes at 8.5 px, 80 at 11.5 px, 31 at 10.5 px, 2,849 words on one page (6,065 px tall). Chart ticks are 9 to 9.5 px. Why: below the 11 px floor for mono caps and illegible on a 1x phone; it is a legal document in a beautiful font. Needs: floor of 11 px for mono kickers (tracking 0.08em), 14 px / 22 px for any sentence, and the page cut to its verdict: the "Two things the house has attached. None may spend money or call a tool." panel plus a collapsed row per connection; the EDI 832 / MSS5 paragraphs go behind "How this is measured".
3. The sheet inputs are white slabs and under 16 px. The "A letter from the house" sheet at 375: five fields at 12 to 15 px with `rgb(255,255,255)` fill in charcoal, 30 to 39 px tall. iOS Safari zooms the whole page on focus below 16 px and these are below the 44 px target. Needs: 16 px field type on touch (`@media (pointer: coarse)`), 48 px field height, fill `oklch(0.22 0.01 70)` in charcoal, 1 px hairline, teal focus ring 2 px with 3 px offset. (The ops leg found the same white fills on the palette and Profile; this is the same root token.)
4. Light coach banner on dark. "Reports Sales, costs ... Take tour | Later | Don't show again" is a cream card on charcoal on every page, 130 px tall at 375 (16 percent of the viewport), sitting between the header and the title. Needs: the token flip, a one-line mobile variant (icon + "Tour" + x, 44 px), and it must go away after "Later" for 14 days, not per page load.
5. Cold paint tells a lie. Loading /reports flashes "No restaurant is active yet, so none of these registers is addressed to anyone" under a title that says "Reading the registers...", then fills in; /communications shows "the last read failed" in the rail for a moment before "none waiting". Whole app boots on a bare cream screen with a 12 px grey "Loading...". Needs: no sentence of conclusion before the house is resolved; a shell skeleton (sidebar + title bar) in the first paint; 160 ms cross-fade into content.

## P1 (cheapens the product)

6. Reports is eight identical beige boxes. /reports is 2,995 px with the same card grammar for "Goals" (empty), "Against ourselves", "Spend pacing", "The week's shape", "What's coming", "Margin", "Figures of record". Only 3 are SVG charts (bars, bars, a line); the rest are lists of numbers and prose. Goals card has a 250 px void under one sentence. Needs: a hero (the engine's one sentence "sales fell 3% vs the previous week ($5.9k vs $6.1k)" set at 40 px Fraunces with the two numbers as a 160 x 48 px sparkline pair), then a bento with 1 x 2 and 2 x 1 modules, so the page has a rhythm, not a form. Empty cards collapse to a 72 px strip.
7. Charts are mute. Bars have no values (the numbers live in a table beside them), no hover, no tooltip, no crosshair, bars drawn in a flat teal; the forecast line is 1 px with a dashed projection and no band. Needs: on hover a 1 px crosshair + tooltip card (radius 10, shadow `0 14px 28px -18px rgba(33,28,22,.35)`, 120 ms); bars draw from the baseline over 480 ms `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` staggered 40 ms, once, when 40 percent in view; value label above each bar in tabular mono; forecast gets a 10 to 90 percent band at 12 percent teal and the "today" divider drawn as a 1 px vertical with a 4 px dot. Daring alternative: a "pour" chart where bar height fills like wine in a glass (a clip-path wave, 900 ms), which is the product's own metaphor.
8. "Arrange the sheet" is a clever idea with no choreography. The 12-column grid appears (nice), the keyboard grip works (Move arrows, Narrower/Wider/Shorter/Taller, Place it / Put it back), but: moving a cutting changes its x by a full column with no FLIP, `transform` 200 ms exists on cards but the neighbours do not part, the left-over gap stays as a hole, and the arrange controls are 11 px mono buttons ("MOVE THE READING", "TAKE OFF") on every card at once, which makes 14 buttons shout. Needs: spring layout (FLIP, stiffness 400, damping 32) so the neighbours slide to close the gap; the dragged card lifts (scale 1.015, shadow `0 24px 48px -24px rgba(33,28,22,.45)`) with the destination drawn as a teal 2 px dashed slot; grips appear only on hover/focus of a card (not all at once); a one-line "Layout saved" with an undo. Both native `select`s ("Show instead", "Draw as") should be a small segmented icon control (table / bars / line / area), animated with a sliding pill, because "Draw as" is the delight of the feature and it is hidden in a 12 px dropdown.
9. No route transition, no content stagger. Navigation changes the H1 within one frame; the only motion at load is a 420 ms `rp-head` ease on the title (cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), a good curve). Needs: View Transitions API on route change: outgoing 90 ms, incoming `translateY(8px)` to 0 + opacity 220 ms `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`; the page title as a shared element (260 ms); cards stagger 36 ms from the top-left (opacity + 6 px, compositor-only), capped at 8 items. Daring: the Counter rail's seven "none waiting" rows tick in like a departure board (split-flap, 18 ms per char) the first time a number changes.
10. The Counter rail is 320 px of "none waiting" x 7. Open by default at 1440 on every page; seven registers, all empty, labelled SEAL / VERIFY / REPLY / DECIDE / MUDAVYM PROPOSES, then "THE HOUSE SAID this sitting - 0 / Nothing yet this sitting" and a polling line "read 17:46:52 - again in 60 s - on focus / Read now". Needs: collapse to a 56 px rail of 5 dots when all zero (dot ring 6 px, teal fill when something waits, a 1.6 s breathing halo on the one that needs the owner), full width only when non-zero; the polling line is a 10 px "synced 12 s ago" with a tiny arc spinner, not a sentence.
11. Empty states are paragraphs. /communications: "The book is open and empty - no vendor exchanges yet." /orders drafts: "Nothing here reaches a vendor until someone who may send it approves it. No drafts waiting. When the house writes to a vendor, it stages the letter here first." /reports "Through the till": 3 lines. Honest (ADR 0020) and wordy; the first-week owner sees ten of these. Needs: one line-drawing (96 px, ink of the logo) per register, one primary action, one 13 px sentence; keep the long truth under a "Why is this empty" disclosure.
12. A full-height sheet for one sentence. Orders row click opens a 640 x 900 right sheet containing "RECEIPT / This order / No receipt has been attached to this order yet." Needs: a 360 px popover or an inline expand under the row (height 0fr to 1fr, 280 ms), reserve the sheet for real content; at minimum a ghost document (6 percent ink skeleton lines) so the product's job is visible.
13. Vendor cards and scorecard are text. /vendors: three beige cards with name in Fraunces then "Open orders 0 / Lead time - / Contact never contacted / Did - 90 d no expected dates recorded"; the Scorecard tab is 15 cells reading "not collected / prior 90 d - not collected" in 10 px mono. Needs: a 40 px monogram disc per vendor (initial in Fraunces, tint from `oklch` hue derived from the name), a 12-week order sparkline, the one status as a dot + word; scorecard cells become small bars (on time as a 6 px arc) and "not collected" collapses to a single "Collecting since Nov 18" row; the Book/Scorecard toggle gets a sliding pill (spring 400/32, 220 ms) instead of an instant swap. The "Currencies stated" amber panel above the cards leads the page with a warning; it should be a chip row inside the header, resolved by one inline select per vendor.
14. /orders list rows lead with "—". 36 delivered rows, each: em dash, vendor name, DELIVERED chip, $ amount, chevron. First column carries no item (in this seed the order has an inventory id but no line name) so the eye lands on a dash 36 times. Needs: a lead column that never renders an empty glyph (vendor name 14 px/600 first, then the date), the status chip as a coloured dot, tabular amounts right-aligned with a 2 px share-of-month bar under each. The KPI strip (PENDING 0, APPROVED 0, ORDERED 0, DELIVERED 36, RECURRING 0) is a flat row; make zero values 45 percent ink and let DELIVERED count up 0 to 36 in 500 ms `easeOutExpo` once.
15. Mobile /orders is cramped. At 375 the primary button "Write a new order" wraps to 3 lines in an 83 px box beside "Write down an agreement" (also 3 lines), the five KPI tiles sit in a 3 + 2 grid with an orphan hole, and row names wrap ("Bosphorus / Imports"). Needs: primary full-width 52 px, secondary a text link below, KPI as a horizontal scroll-snap strip (tile 112 x 72) with the active tile edge-peeking.
16. Bell popover is a single line. "0 UNREAD / Notifications / Nothing unread. The bell is quiet. / Read the whole book" in a 4-line popover that covers the top of the Counter rail, no arrow, no entrance beyond a fade. Needs: arrow 8 px pointing at the bell, scale-from-origin entrance (0.96 to 1, 160 ms), and a quiet "All caught up" with a small checkmark that draws (stroke-dashoffset 360 ms).
17. Reports toggles are tiny: Through the till "7D / 30D / 90D" is a 22 px high segmented control with mono 9 px caps; the pill is a fill, not a slide. Needs: 32 px segmented control with a sliding pill and a number that tweens when the window changes.
18. Palette is competent and plain. 620 x 566, input 51.5 px at 17 px on a pure white fill (charcoal too), results in groups with an icon and a mono kicker, recommendation sentences leak into results ("Place the order today - reorder point is 113 bottles. If the vendor is slow, split the order..."), shadow `rgba(0,0,0,.5) 0 24px 60px -30px`. Needs: 14 px radius kept, input becomes borderless with a hairline under it, highlighted row animates as one sliding 8 px-radius pill (not per-row fill), results cap at 2 lines with ellipsis, a small "Enter to open / Tab for actions" footer in 11 px. Daring: show a live preview pane on the right (the vendor card, the order sheet) so cmd+K is a teleporter, not a list.

## P2 (delight)

19. Wordmark typing on the login: at 375 the hero shows a lone "]" then "Mu" before "Mudavym" (a letter-by-letter reveal). It reads as a glitch if you catch it mid-way. Needs: either complete it in under 450 ms with a clip-path wipe (one element, no partial glyphs), or fade the full word in at 280 ms.
20. Theme change is an instant flip. Clicking Charcoal in Profile swaps the whole palette in one frame (no transition). Needs: `document.startViewTransition` with a circular reveal from the clicked chip (clip-path circle, 520 ms `cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)`), the classic that sells dark mode; `color-scheme` switched so scrollbars follow.
21. Charcoal logo mark is dark on dark. The header "M" glyph at top-left (x 28, y 28) nearly vanishes in charcoal (grey on `rgb(21,19,15)` about 1.5:1). Needs: swap to the cream token or add the 1 px light rim the paper one has.
22. Mobile "The writing desk" card hard-clips a paragraph mid-line at 375 ("A cutting is written up under the sheet, in 'Written up': the gateway writes it to a CSV ... and") with the next line sliced by the card edge. Needs: line-clamp 3 with an ellipsis and a "More" or just fit the content; never clip a text line horizontally through its x-height.
23. Settings: the page is a 14-register document; the index is lovely but the right pane fades in from 0 over about 320 ms with nothing leading the eye. Needs: when an index item is chosen the new heading slides up 8 px while the previous one fades out in 90 ms (cross-fade, same slot), and the index item's active bar animates its height (200 ms).
24. Daring screens the product is begging for: (a) a "Morning at the house" full-bleed opening screen after the first sign-in of the day: one sentence in 56 px Fraunces ("Sales fell 3 percent. Two things need you."), the number sets counting up, then the tool collapses into the dashboard with a shared-element move; (b) the Reports page as a vertical scroll-linked story (scroll-timeline: view()) where each cutting is pinned for one viewport and its chart draws as it enters; (c) the vendor sheet with a cinematic header: monogram disc scales from the card (shared-element, 320 ms) into the sheet; (d) the "Hold to accept" gesture (HoldToApprove) is the best micro-interaction in the product (a 420 ms pour fill, press and hold) and appears once, on terms; give it to every irreversible action with the same fill and a haptic-style 8 px spring on completion, and a 2 px teal ring that fills around the button on keyboard hold.

## Numbers I measured (for the builders)

- Sheet spring: 300 ms, linear() 59 stops, overshoot 1.0093; scrim 28 percent; desktop right sheet 640 x 900; phone bottom sheet 375 x 471 (Rooms) and 375 x 747 (letter).
- Route change: 0 view transitions; load animation `rp-head` 420 ms `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`; card hover transitions 160 ms; arrange cards `transform` 200 ms.
- Chart SVGs on /reports: 3 (317 to 434 px wide, 130 to 163 px tall), tick type 9.5 px, axis title 9 px, 3 text overlaps each; no `role`/`aria-label` on any chart SVG (a11y: they are unnamed graphics beside a table).
- /reports main 1156 x 2995 px at 1440; /connections 2,849 words, 8.5 px x 71; fonts in use: Plus Jakarta Sans, Fraunces, JetBrains Mono, DM Sans (four faces; DM Sans looks like a stray, it appears on the orders amounts/KPI labels).
- Header controls at 375: 24 x 28, 38 x 29, 33 x 33, 60 x 36 px; Rooms rows 32 px high (target 44).
- Palette: 620 x 566, input 51.5 px, 17 px, `rgb(255,255,255)`.
- Toast (from CSS): Sonner top-right, `visibleToasts={3}`, radius 10, `box-shadow: none`, 13 px title, 11.5 px body, `animation: none` under reduced motion. No shadow means a toast over a beige card has no edge; give it `0 18px 36px -22px rgba(33,28,22,.4)` and a 3 px left accent in the status colour.

## The ten-second version for the founder

The ledger look is the brand; keep it and add physics. Do four things and the product feels 2x more expensive: (1) fix the chart axis collisions and the 8.5 px type, (2) give every route one 220 ms spring entrance plus a view-transition title, (3) make charts draw and answer hover, (4) collapse empty things (rail, cards, sheets) instead of writing paragraphs in them.
