The calls:
- **Counted use is `first count + arrived − last count`**, read from `stock_counts.counted_qty`. "Arrived" means purchase, initial, transfer, any `source='order'` row (door corrections at delivery-stock.service.ts:286 and reversals at :478 are `adjustment`), and a `return` with quantity < 0 (to the vendor). Rejected: netting every non-count type, which reads door corrections and vendor returns as use.
- **Counts that found more than arrived** leave the good unmeasured (NULL), not "0 used, dead".
- **Counts are used only for food and supply**, and only when the last count is within 30 days.
- **Cover and the reorder suggestion for a counted good** read on hand − max(velocity × days since the last count − booked out since, 0). Without that, the book stays at the count-day figure, so cover went stale between counts. The drawer copy says so.
- **A separator before exactly 3 digits** ("1,500" or "1.500") is asked about in the par field and in count, write-off and transfer: dot or comma could be either reading in a Turkish house. "0.035" and "2,25" are still read as decimals.
