# r24 house-number-style — BUILD 2026-11-13

Problem (r23 walk, TRY house): one card wrote money in Turkish form ("₺12,00", after #1298) and the
share beside it in English form ("POUR COST 13.1%"); /menu plan "11.00", get-started hints
"14.00/68.00/24.00", /house/menu "1250 bottle" with no currency. Two decimal marks on one card mean the
reader cannot tell a decimal from a thousands mark.

## Invariant
Every number on a house's page (money, share, ratio, price hint) is written in ONE locale: the locale of
the country that owns the house's money (the `moneyLocale` rule from #1298: TRY -> tr-Latn-TR, GBP ->
en-Latn-GB; EUR/X-codes/unknown/failed read -> the reader's locale), Latin digits only. Two readers of one
house see one figure. A box hint is in a form the box's own reader accepts (never grouped).
Untouched: CSV/XLSX exports, chart coordinates, gateway-written sentences.

## Session's calls (founder /goal 2026-11-07 delegated)
1. One helper `lib/house-number.ts` that is self-contained on main (countries.ts + currencyMinorUnits only),
   not an extension of #1298's currency.ts. Rejected: building on #1298 (every consumer train would need
   #1298 first); per-page `Intl` calls (the rule would drift, as tm-format's country rule already has).
2. Locale keyed on the house's CURRENCY, not its country. Rejected: country (tm-format.ts houseLocale):
   a house with a country but an unreadable money would write numbers in one form and money in another.
3. Percent in Intl `style: percent` -> "%13,1" in Turkish. Rejected: keep "13,1%" by hand — that is not
   how tr-TR writes it, and it would be the same two-rules problem one level down.
4. Price hints: money's minor digits, `useGrouping:false`, and ONLY on boxes read by readTypedPrice /
   parseMenuPrice. A `type=number`/`parseFloat` box (#1295's) keeps "0.00". Rejected: grouped hints
   ("1.250,00") — the box refuses grouped input as ambiguous.
5. Carry byte-identical copies of the helper into each train (`git checkout <#1303 branch> -- file`)
   instead of merging #1303 in. Rejected: merging — the trains are 6-8 commits behind main and the merge
   pulled 50 files / 262K into a menu PR. Identical additions merge clean later.
6. `useHouseCurrencyCode` reads `useAuthStore`, house in the query key. Rejected: `useAuth()` throws with
   no AuthProvider (menu plan mounts in onboarding too); a key without the house leaks one house's money
   into another's page.
7. HouseMenu reads currency in a plain effect, not react-query: its tests and mount have no QueryClient.
8. The money side calls the helper (#1319) so there is one rule, not two equal copies.
