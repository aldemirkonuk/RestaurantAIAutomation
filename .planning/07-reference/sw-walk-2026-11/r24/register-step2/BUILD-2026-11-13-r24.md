## Audit

- **The brief's framing was wrong.** On main, `pathBStep` starts at 1 (Register.tsx:91). Its only setter, `setPathBStep(1)` at :1121, is inside step 2, so the brief's line references are correct. But step 2 is dead code rather than a lost step. ADR 0213 (#455, `ddc5e094b`, "account-only signup") made the account step call `registerAccount` and go to /verify-email. The house, with its country, opens at /get-started after verification through `createFirstHouse`, which already has the labelled combobox (#1295). Making step 2 reachable would open a house before verification and ask for the place twice. The fix is to delete step 2 and make the one real step effortless.
- **Owner of Register.tsx:** the #691 → #1104 → #1106 → #1107 chain. That chain conflicts with main in the import block only. `git merge-tree` shows 1 hunk, and it still shows 1 hunk after this stack.
- **The account step on #1107:** Create account stayed greyed and silent until all fields were valid; Enter with a gap returned silently; labels read "Full Name *" (star in the accessible name); no `required`; the hint was not tied to its box.
- **Add-a-location clip:** CountryCombobox's list is `absolute top-full max-h-56`. The Sheet body is `flex: 0 1 auto; overflow-y: auto` (sheet.css:408) and as tall as its content, so it clips the list at its bottom edge. The legacy Radix dialog (`overflow-y-auto`) clips it the same way. Edit location uses the same Sheet and box. Owner of CountryCombobox: #1295. No open PR touches AddLocationDialog or EditLocationChainDialog.
- **Two pre-existing guard breaks this stack had to handle:** OD-105's claim (CLAIMS.jsonl:115) pinned 17 labels / 16 htmlFor and already failed on #1107 (15/14). `AuthContext.houseOpening.test.tsx` read `handleCreateSubmit` from source.
