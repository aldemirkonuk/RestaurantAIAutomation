## Gaps NOT fixed by any open PR (ranked by impact on the goal)

1. **A sale of a drink or dish takes nothing from stock.**
   - Evidence: no code outside `src/recipes` reads `recipe_lines`. The only additions are on the recipe stack (#863-#1304).
   - No PR adds a POS line -> cocktail_id/dish_id link (pattern grep empty).
   - The UI admits it: `apps/web/src/pages/inventory/next/ItemUsedIn.tsx:82-84` DRINKS_DO_NOT_DRAW.
   - Impact: no theoretical usage, no variance against a count, and no margin by volume. The "goods relationship" is display-only.
2. **Recipes are not connected to the uploaded menu. Every drink and dish is typed again, with a second price.**
   - ADR 0366 defers the link: `.planning/decisions/0366-...md:56`.
   - Only the recipes module reads `dishes`. `menus.service.ts` writes no `dishes`/`cocktails` row. Menu prices land on `restaurant_inventory.menu_price_current` (`20260922230800_a_house_can_hold_a_price.sql:476`).
   - Pour/food cost divides by `cocktails.price` or `dishes.price` (`DrinkCards.tsx:225`, `DishesPage.tsx:50`). Neither is the menu's price.
3. **"Cost a serve" blends estimates into the invoice cost, and still says every line is priced.**
   - `apps/api-gateway/src/analytics/inventory-cost.ts:142-158` takes the rollup `wac` whenever ANY live lot is invoiced.
   - The rollup averages every priced non-sample lot, including estimated and provisional ones (`20260902150000_lot_cost_truth.sql:493`).
   - "partial" compares `wac_qty` (all priced lots) to `live_qty`, not `invoice_qty` (`:501-502`).
   - Example: 10 invoiced units at 100 and 90 estimated at 60 read as 64, "invoice_lot_wac". The sentence is "all N ingredients priced" (`DrinkCards.tsx:61`).
   - r24 named this and left it (`r24/price-agreement/BUILD:55`). No PR changes the branch.
4. **Pour/food cost is taken over a price that may include VAT.**
   - `DrinkCards.tsx:92` POUR_COST_BASIS says so.
   - The founder answer of 2026-10-12 (build the per-house tax basis) is unbuilt. #784 is docs-only.
   - Impact: Turkish list prices carry KDV, so the % reads low against any published band.
5. **No waste or trim yield on a line.** `recipe_lines` has no factor (`20270425100000_...:38-50`). A trimmed fish, vegetable or citrus is costed at its usable weight's price, so food cost reads low.
6. **No prep or sub-recipes** (house syrup, sauce, batch).
   - `owner_kind IN ('cocktail','dish')` (`20270803100000_a_dish_has_a_recipe.sql:52`).
   - A syrup line is "not linked" or untracked unless the house invents a priced shelf good for it.
7. **No unit bridges: pieces <-> weight, and volume <-> weight.**
   - Cost: a piece only meets uom `each` (`recipe-cost.ts:101-102`). ml10 against a g good is a unit_mismatch (`:89`), and mg against an ml good likewise (`:96`).
   - Nutrition: a piece never counts (`recipe-nutrition.ts:90`). Volume against per-100 g figures and weight against per-100 ml figures are both refused (`:95`, `:98`).
   - Foods are forced into g/ml (`house-item.service.ts:19,32`).
   - So "2 eggs", "1 lemon" or "2 tbsp flour" never price or count. Most dish nutrition cards will read "at least".
8. **A recipe line with no shelf match cannot add the good from the sheet.**
   - `ShelfPicker.tsx:71-73` only says "Nothing on your shelf matches".
   - The owner must leave for /inventory, add the good, and come back. The draft survives only in the session store.
9. **/cocktails has no cost or pour-cost column.**
   - `CocktailRegister.tsx:290-298` shows only Price and Kept. /dishes has Cost a plate and Food cost (`DishesPage.tsx:306-308`).
   - The gateway already computes drink figures (`dishes.service.ts:136` `figures(..., "cocktail")`).
   - The owner cannot scan pour costs without opening every drink.
10. **Pour cost is not judged against any target.**
    - `DrinkCards.tsx:109-115` shows a bare %.
    - The "Hold pour cost" goal has `metricKey: null` (`analytics/goal-scenarios.ts:437-440`).
    - The house target margin (#1345, settings) is not read. There is no "price to hit target".
11. **Nobody is told when an invoice moves a recipe's cost.**
    - Nothing outside `src/recipes` reads recipe cost: no reports, insights or notifications (grep: only `item-used-in.service.ts` and a comment).
    - /reports has no recipe or menu-engineering view.
12. **The cocktail price box is `type="number"`** (`CocktailRegister.tsx:221-225`).
    - "12,50" where the browser rejects the comma saves silently as unpriced.
    - A cleared price sends `undefined`, so an amend cannot remove a price.
    - #1250 fixed this exact defect for dishes only.
13. **/dishes shows a partial cost with no mark** (`DishesPage.tsx:50-54`). It reads `complete` only, while `partial` comes from `recipe-cost.ts:135`. This was r21 item 5 and is still open.
14. **Turkish case fold.**
    - `dishes.service.ts:35` `foldName` uses `toLowerCase()`, so "RAKI" never proposes "Rakı".
    - Shelf search uses `ilike` (`recipes.service.ts:686`).
    - The dish-name unique index uses `lower()` (`20270803100000_...:41`).
15. **Numbers are not in the house locale.**
    - `DrinkCards.tsx:113` and `DishesPage.tsx:54` use `toFixed(1)%`, giving "18.7%". The drawer reads "%18,7" after #1312.
    - `units.ts:76-79` `qtyText` prints "22.5" after "22,5" was typed.
16. **Two names for one figure.** The sheet and /dishes say "Cost a serve" / "Cost a plate" (`DrinkCards.tsx:55,180`; `DishesPage.tsx:307`). The drawer says "Cost per serve" / "Cost per plate" (`ItemUsedIn.tsx:111-112`, #1302).
17. **Slow paths.**
    - Opening a sheet makes 5 serial round trips. `readPricing` and then `whatIsIn` are awaited in turn though they are independent (`recipes.service.ts:324,327`).
    - The cost preview makes one `recipe_qty_base` RPC per distinct qty/unit, up to 60 (`:431`). The function is IMMUTABLE and could be one call.
    - The drawer's "used in" reads every historical version's lines for the good (`dishes.service.ts:278-285`), so it grows with every save.
18. **Staff cannot read a spec on the phone.** apps/mobile has no recipe or cocktail screen (grep empty). The bartender's spec card is web-only.
19. **Versions say less than they could.**
    - A past version is priced only "at today's prices" (`RecipeSheet.tsx:283`).
    - The cost at save time is not kept, and there is no diff between versions.
20. **A zero invoice cost reads as a real zero.** `inventory-cost.ts:144` accepts `wac >= 0`, so a lot invoiced at 0 (a parse miss or a free line) prices its lines as free and complete.
21. **The legacy recipe writer is still live.**
    - `PUT /cocktails/:rid/:id/ingredients` (`beverages.controller.ts:230`).
    - `useCellarNextData.ts:1056-1090` keeps `setRecipe`/`useCocktailRecipe`. Nothing calls them after the merge.
    - This is a second recipe table, shown only while no version exists (`recipes.service.ts:159-182`).
22. **"1 each" of a bottle-counted good is a unit_mismatch** (`recipe-cost.ts:101-102`). The picker has no bottle or can unit, so "1 bottle beer" must be typed in ml and needs a stated size.
