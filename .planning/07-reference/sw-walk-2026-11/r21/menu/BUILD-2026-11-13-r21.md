Scope: menu upload and updates. That covers scan, upload or typed entry, then review, then make current, then later updates and price changes. It covers food and every drink kind, dates in the house's time zone, and plan math matching the result. All PRs are DRAFT. Nothing was checked in the browser: the task rules held the local stack for the walk agent.

## Browser walk (for the walk agent: owner or manager, a house with a current menu that has a wine and a dish)
1. **#1252/#1253.** On /menu, click "Change price" on a wine row.
   - Glass and Bottle boxes open and the button says "Save price".
   - Type `45,50` in Bottle and click Save. Expect under the row: "<wine>: the bottle price is now …45.50 on the menu, and the house's own bottle price is …45.50 too." The row shows 45.50, not 4,550.
   - Change price again with `1,250`. Expect an alert: "…the bottle price was not changed ("1,250" is not a price: it could be 1250 or 1.25…". The boxes stay open.
   - On a "From the kitchen" dish, set Bottle to `11`. Expect "…the price is now …11.00 on the menu. A dish has no house price and no stock, so nothing else changed."
   - As staff, there is no Change price button.
   - If a lock is held on a wine's glass price, change the glass price. Expect "…but the house's glass price is locked at …, so it was not changed."
2. **#1256/#1257.**
   - In Settings, set the house's time zone at least 3 hours from the browser's (for example Europe/Istanbul on EST). On /menu under "Menus kept", the read names ("the menu read YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM") and "current since" use the house's hour and day. There is no "on this device's clock" sentence.
   - With no zone set, the list says "Dates and times here are on this device's clock: the house has no time zone set. Set the house's time zone in Settings."
   - In the plan ("Make … current"), "locked by … on" and "set by hand by … on" use the house's day.
3. **#1261.** On /get-started step 2 ("Type a few lines"), enter "Lamb shank" and choose What it is = Food.
   - One Price box appears, with "The dish's price stays on the menu; no stock is made for it."
   - Enter 24.50, import, and make the menu current. On /menu under "From the kitchen", the dish shows 24.50 in Bottle.
4. **#1265.** Read a file that leaves out one drink of the current menu and includes one dish, then click Make current.
   - The plan says "1 drink on the current menu is not on this file and keeps its price".
   - The result says "1 kitchen line, on the menu and carried nowhere".
