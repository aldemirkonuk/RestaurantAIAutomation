## Browser walk (the test house's own login, local stack only, TRY house)

1. **Cold load.** Open `/reports` with a slow network (DevTools, Slow 3G).
   - Expect outline blocks and the text "Reading how you arranged this sheet…".
   - The **Arrange** button is disabled until the owner's own layout appears.
   - The house default must never flash and then save.
2. **Arrange, then rule off.** Click Arrange, move a cutting, then click **Rule it off**.
   - Expect the button text "Ruling off…".
   - While it is saving, "Take off" and "Show instead" are gone.
   - Then "Ruled off." appears.
   - Reload: the order is kept.
3. **Failed save.** Block `PATCH`/`PUT` to preferences in DevTools, then rule off.
   - Expect the alert "The sheet was not saved. Try again."
   - The draft stays on the paper and arranging remains open.
4. **Combo pick.**
   - On "The week's shape", set Draw as to **Bars and a line**, then set **Line shows** to a second series (for example "Days seen").
   - Reload: the same pick is still drawn.
   - Arrange and move that cutting, then rule off and reload: the pick moves with it.
   - Click "Show The week's shape full screen": the dialog draws the same bars and line.
5. **Every cutting, every drawing.**
   - For each cutting, go through every Draw as option and every "Show instead" option, then open full screen and press Esc.
   - Expect no blank charts, no "cannot be drawn as", no `$`, no `USD`, and no camelCase or snake_case text, including on hover tooltips.
6. **Tooltip edge (G16).** Hover the right-most bar of a narrow cutting. Check whether the tooltip is clipped by the cutting edge.
7. **Till chips.** Check that they read 7d/30d/90d/365d, and note it as G-chips.
