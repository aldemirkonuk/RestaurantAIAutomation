# receipts-floor lane, r21 (2026-11-13)

## The real problem
The brief said table-side sales come from the Floor Checker. **They don't.** The Floor Checker gateway train (#958-#972, `git diff origin/main...origin/feat/floor-gw2-14-docs`) never calls `apply_stock_movement` or `record_glass_pour`, and never reads `inventory_transactions` or any cost. Automatic Paid from the till (ADR 0353) is not built.

Table-side counts reach stock and cost only through the till: pos-hub, then `apply_stock_movement` / `record_glass_pour` / `return_glass_pour`. That is cost-math proposal F8 (`cost-math/proposal.md:233`), re-checked on the gw2 tip. So there is no floor-to-cost path to fix, and adding one would deplete twice for a house that has a till.

On the till path, the live gap was **by-the-glass**:
- `record_glass_pour` (#809) and `return_glass_pour` (#805) move lots themselves and wrote no lot draw.
- So `cost_total` / `unpriced_qty` stayed NULL, and `pos_cost_of_goods` (#1025) withheld cost of goods for any house that sells glasses.
- #949 wrote the owner contract for this (`.planning/tech-debt.d/2026-11-07-a-void-gives-back-its-cost.md`). `grep -c stamp_lot_draws` gives 0 on both #809 and #805.
