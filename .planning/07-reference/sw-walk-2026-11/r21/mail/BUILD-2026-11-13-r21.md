## Browser walk (for the walk agent; no stack was started here)

Use an integration of the #1212 tip, #1269 and #1270. Add #1075 and #1271 for steps 9-10. Sign in as an owner.

1. `/communications` (Letters): the page foot has **no** "Who is writing" section.
2. Click the **Mail** tab (`?tab=mail`). Under the people list, or under the empty-list sentence, a section headed **WHO IS WRITING** shows with a one-line summary and **Show**. The quiet line above the list reads: "Vendor mail is listed here by person and thread. A stranger's letter, and a letter held because its sender could not be confirmed, are under Who is writing, below. Mail from your team and guests is not read yet."
3. Open `/communications?senders=open` cold. The **Mail** tab is current, and "Who is writing" is expanded (its button reads **Hide**).
4. From step 3, click **Letters**. The URL becomes `/communications`, with no `senders`.
5. On Mail, open a vendor (`?with=vendor:…`), a thread, and **Mail setup**. "Who is writing" is absent in all three.
6. Run the page tour (help) on `/communications`. It has 3 steps. Step 1, "Mail and letters", mentions "Who is writing: strangers, and letters held because their sender could not be confirmed". There is no 4th step.
7. **Switched off:** a house with a `gmail_read` grant and `enable_house_inbox_read=false`. Mail status reads "Connected, but reading is switched off for this house."
   - Click **Set up how mail reaches the house**. The setup shows: "A mailbox is connected for reading, but the house's “Read this house's mailbox” switch in Settings is off, so nothing is read from it." followed by **Open the switch in Settings**.
   - Click it. You land on `/settings?tab=features`, scrolled to Features, where the row "Read this house's mailbox" is shown.
   - As staff, the same line ends "An owner or a manager can turn it on." and has no button.
8. **#1270:** open Mail setup, or "How mail reaches the house" → "Which address letters leave from".
   - With a send grant only, the sentence starts "Letters leave from <address>, so a vendor sees…". It contains "on the Connections page". It never contains "Half of it", "enable_house_inbox_read" or "/connections".
   - With a read grant only, it contains "Order letters leave from notifications@mudavym.com".
9. **#1271**, needs an inbound letter on the local stack: a stranger's letter. Its notification links to `/communications?senders=open`, and clicking it lands on Mail with "Who is writing" open. The message says "on the Mail tab under Who is writing".
10. **#1271:** a known vendor's reply on a thread with no order. The notification opens `/communications?tab=mail&with=vendor%3A<id>&thread=gm%3A<gmail thread>`, which is that thread in Mail. A reply on an order still opens `/orders?order=<id>`.
