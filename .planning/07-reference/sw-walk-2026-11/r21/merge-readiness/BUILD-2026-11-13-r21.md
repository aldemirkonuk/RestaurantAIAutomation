# r21 merge-readiness build: 2026-11-13

The lane's job was to make the open draft trains landable. For each conflict the task named, it
built a resolution with the fix already applied, and it wrote a merge order for every train based
on trial merges. The full order and the remaining conflicts are in `MERGE-ORDER.md`, next to this file.

## Audit (measured)
- **605 open PRs, 522 of them draft.** They form 111 trains with 152 tips, starting from `origin/main` `c459b0a12`. 27 new PRs (#1250-#1276) appeared during the session, and five of them were stacked on tips this lane had already resolved.
- `tipsim.py` lands every tip in order with merge-tree and does not use a worktree. With the resolutions applied, it reports 80 clean, 14 union-only, 3 already-landed and **55 conflicting** tips.
- `gh` showed `mergeable` as stale or UNKNOWN on the stacked PRs, so this lane did not rely on it.
- **#1013's own tree fails `check_migrations_single_home.py`.** The cause is `supabase/tests/20270512091500_a_good_says_what_is_in_it_test.sql`, which is missing from the inventory list. The nutrition train has this defect whether or not anything else merges.

## Decisions (each is the session's call; the rejected alternatives are in MERGE-ORDER §1)
1. **Land with a merge commit, tip by tip.** Squash and rebase-merge were rejected: the squash trap, the identical-content conflicts, and 522 serial CI runs. If a squash is ever required, use the `-s ours` catch-up.
2. **Resolutions are pushed `resolve/r21-*` branches, not PRs.** Each one carries two trains, so it is far over the 15-file / 30K cap. To land one, fast-forward the head branch of the tip that lands second to the resolution commit, then merge that PR.
3. **The money rule in reports.** An unknown cost is never $0. The cost train's withholding beats #843's `basis.uncosted` "adds nothing to spend".
4. **Menu wording.** #1220's per-kind counts win. For kitchen lines and lines missing from the file, #1265's "drinks, not wines" words win.
5. **Cocktail.** The recipe side of `CocktailRegister.tsx` is kept, and the RecipeNutrition cocktail case is dropped.
6. **Registers.** `sql_outside_migrations.txt` is regenerated with `--update`, never hand-merged. README, OPEN-DECISIONS and CLAIMS are merged as a union.
