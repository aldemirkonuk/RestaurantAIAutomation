## Audit: defects and gaps, assuming the open PRs merge

Ranked by owner pain. **FIXED** = fixed in this round.

1. **FIXED #1254: a litre line was booked ×1000 wrong into a volume good.**
   - `delivery-stock.service.ts` `shelfQuantity` (#1126 tip, about :188-215) returned `round(n)` for `uom 'liter'` into an item kept in `ml`.
   - `lot-unit-cost.ts` `resolve` (about :100-200) priced it "per liter, booked as-is".
   - Worked example: 5 liter @ 120 became 5 ml at 120 a ml. A 30 ml pour then cost 3,600.
   - A sized bottle line (12 × 200 ml tonic) booked 12 ml at a bottle's price.
   - A 30-liter line into a keg item booked 30 kegs.
2. **FIXED (#1268): the new-goods side was not possible from the paper.**
   - `ItemChooser.tsx` (#1077) listed only goods the house already carries, and said "No item matches. Add it on /inventory first".
   - An unpaired line is "NOT booked" (`delivery-stock.service.ts` "this line names no item on the shelf"), so a new vendor's first cheese or oil never reached stock or cost until someone left the paper.
3. **OPEN, tax basis (ADR 0359 C2/C3, proposal lane 3.5a): built only as a pure rule.**
   - `paper-landed-cost.ts:58` `houseTaxForLotCost()` returns `{tax_basis:'unset', reclaims_input_tax:null}` for every house.
   - `paper-landed-cost.ts` feeds `price_tax_basis: null, vat_rate_percent: null` for every line, and `charges_tax_basis: null` for the header.
   - No column holds them: `procurement_document_lines` has no VAT column, and `restaurants` has no `tax_basis`.
   - Effect: every lot is "costed as printed, what its cost holds is not stated". A KDV-inclusive receipt (a market fiş) costs a reclaiming house 10-20% high.
   - Founder's picks 2026-10-12 (F1/F2/F3) say build it. Brief below.
4. **OPEN, order path costs from client-typed figures (proposal lane 1.3, skipped since wave 2).**
   - `procurement.service.ts` `verifyReceipt` (#1127 tip, about :6440-6460) builds the match from `body.invoiceUnitPrice` and `body.allocatedCharges` sent by the page.
   - It does not read the order's linked invoice document on the server.
   - The math is right when the person types right. Nothing checks the typed price against the paper.
   - It conflicts with #787/#820/#821 and #1127 in the same method, so it needs its own lane after those merge.
5. **OPEN, an order for a volume good.**
   - An order is in containers (`ORDER_UNIT_TYPES`) and carries no size.
   - An ml item ordered "6 bottle" still books 6 ml through `verifyReceipt` / the agreed price.
   - #1254 covers only the document paths (door count and VERIFIED from invoice lines).
6. **OPEN, table side: Floor Checker carries no sale lines.**
   - The floor port (#958-#972, `apps/api-gateway/src/floor/`) records seatings, visits, Paid and marks.
   - It writes no `pos_item_sales` and calls no `apply_stock_movement` (`git grep` on `feat/floor-gw2-14-docs`: no hits).
   - Consumption and cost of goods come only from POS (pos-hub/Toast/Square/Clover → #845 draws → #1025 `pos_cost_of_goods`).
   - A house without a POS has no table-side depletion. That is a product fork (an order pad in Floor), not a defect to patch here.
7. **OPEN (known), cost of goods stays withheld for houses with voids or glass pours until #805/#809 merge.**
   - Recorded in `cost-math/BUILD-2026-11-11.md`. No new finding.
8. **CHECKED, not a defect: TL/₺ invoices.**
   - `invoice-currency.ts` `filingCurrency` falls through a non-ISO `"TL"` to the order's or the house's currency.
   - The `GLYPH_CODES` table maps `TL`/`₺` to TRY, so a TRY house's TL invoice files as TRY and is not held.
   - Only a house with no stated currency holds every paper (#933, by design).

## Session's calls (2026-11-07 /goal; also in the PR bodies)

**#1254, convert by the line's `format_ml`, and refuse without it.**
- Rejected: assume 750 ml or 1 l. That is a guess posted as a cost.
- Rejected: refuse every container into an ml item. The paper usually prints the size.
- Rejected: keep the door's plain-count rule for invoice lines that carry a size. That books 12 ml for 2,400.
- A plain count with no size keeps the door's rule, as #1109 did for weights.
- A litre line into a bottle or keg item is refused, not converted.

**#1268, carry through the existing `POST /inventory/:rid/house-items` and the existing line link, from the page.**
- Rejected: a new gateway route that creates and links in one write. It would be a second writer of `restaurant_inventory` beside #862, to save one round trip.

**#1268, propose the unit from the line, and let the person change it.**
- Rejected: no default. People pick "each" for cheese billed by the kilo, and that line is held at VERIFIED.
- The form says a misfit in the gateway's own words before the good exists.

**#1268, a wine goes through the library.**
- Rejected: a house row for a wine. It loses producer, vintage and size, and the library match.

**#1268, a 409 pairs with the good the house already carries.**
- It never adds a twin, and the note says which good it paired with.

**Tax basis not built this round.** It needs a migration on `restaurants`, VAT columns on `procurement_document_lines` plus the readers that fill them, and a Settings field: three layers and at least three PRs. A half-wired basis would stamp lots with a claim no column backs. Brief below.
