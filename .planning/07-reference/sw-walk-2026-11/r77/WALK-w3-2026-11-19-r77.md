Gateway read side is vendor-only: GET /mail/people returns kind "vendor" hardcoded and storedKinds ["vendor"] (conversations/mail.service.ts:151 and the list_mail_people RPC); /mail/threads and /mail/thread know nothing of kind/from/to. With a live gmail_read_all grant and a team and a guest row in procurement_conversations, the owner's Mail tab says "This tab shows every conversation in the mailbox: vendors, team and guests." and, directly under it, "Mail from your team and guests is not read yet."; the Team chip says "No conversation with team members yet." although the row is in the book. A false empty (ADR 0020). PR bodies already say this read side is missing; it means #1917+#1916 together cannot deliver "the Mail tab shows ALL conversations" and the line READS_ALL_LINE is untrue until it lands. Fix brief: (gateway, own PR on #1917) list_mail_people + list_mail_threads + getThread take the non-vendor rows (key `team:`/`guest:` + address, name from from_address), withheld for staff via p_withhold_house_letters, storedKinds from what exists, messages carry kind/fromAddress/toAddresses; (web, #1916 stopgap if the gateway lags) show READS_ALL_LINE only when storedKinds contains a non-vendor kind, otherwise "The house files every conversation, but this tab lists vendor mail only for now.", and kindEmptyWords for Team/Guests then says the same instead of "No conversation ...".
