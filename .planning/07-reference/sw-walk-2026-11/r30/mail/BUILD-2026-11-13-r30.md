## What each does

- **Gap 4: a letter can need no reply.**
  - Table `mail_letter_no_reply_marks`, keyed by letter. RLS is on and only `service_role` can reach it.
  - The mark settles letters up to the named one in (at, id) order, not up to now(). A letter that arrives later waits again.
  - `owes` = `owes_raw AND NOT automatic AND NOT duplicate AND NOT settled`.
  - The thread page gets `no_reply_marked_at` and `thread_waiting`. The functions `mail_letter_mark_no_reply` and `mail_letter_unmark_no_reply` are in a new migration, `20271013143000`.
  - Routes: `POST` and `DELETE /mail/letters/:id/no-reply-needed`. Only an owner or a manager may use them; anyone else gets 403. A bad id is 400, another house's letter is 404, a letter the house sent is 409, and any database error is 503.
  - Web: while the thread waits, the act "No reply needed" marks the newest received letter that is not automatic. The marked letter says "Marked as needing no reply, <when>." The newest marked letter also offers "It needs a reply after all".
- **Gap 10: connect Gmail from Mail setup and come back to it.**
  - When the catalogue offers it, an owner or a manager gets `/authorize/gmail_send` or `/authorize/gmail_read` with `returnPath=/communications?tab=mail&setup=1`.
  - On return, the page says only what the provider said. A "connected" return points at the page's own lists and claims nothing. An unfinished one gives its reason, in the words of the exported `REASONS`.
- **Gap 11: forwarding advice.** The page now says that order letters already ask vendors to reply to the address (Reply-To is set in `sendProviderEmail`). It also says that a letter forwarded by hand is from the forwarder, is not filed as the vendor's, and is kept only when it has an attachment or reads as an offer.
- **Gap 15: staff.** Staff now see "See how mail reaches the house", not "Set up …".
- **Gap 6, the line under the chips.** It now says that a plain letter from someone Mudavym does not know is not kept.

## Browser checks (local stack only, after the stack is merged into a local build)

1. **No reply needed.**
   - Owner or manager, `/communications?tab=mail`. Open a vendor thread whose newest letter is the vendor's, received and not automatic.
   - Expect the button "No reply needed". Click it.
   - The vendor's letter then reads "Marked as needing no reply, <date time>." with a link "It needs a reply after all". The "No reply needed" button is gone.
   - Back on the list, that vendor has no "waiting on you" mark, and the header's waiting count is one lower.
2. **Undo.** Click "It needs a reply after all". The marked line goes and "No reply needed" comes back. The vendor is waiting again.
3. **Staff.** Log in as staff and open the thread from check 1 while it is marked. The line "Marked as needing no reply, …" is shown, with no undo link. In any waiting thread, there is no "No reply needed" button.
4. **A later letter.** After marking, have the vendor write again (local seed or a test letter). The thread waits again and "No reply needed" targets the new letter.
5. **Connect from setup.**
   - Owner of a house with no Gmail grants, on a deployment whose catalogue offers gmail_send and gmail_read. Go to `/communications?tab=mail&setup=1`.
   - Expect "Connect your Gmail to send from it" under "Which address letters leave from".
   - Expect "Let vendor replies in your Gmail land here", with the sentence about the "Read this house's mailbox" switch.
   - Click the first. The address becomes `/authorize/gmail_send?returnPath=%2Fcommunications%3Ftab%3Dmail%26setup%3D1`.

   - Open `/communications?tab=mail&setup=1&integration_status=error&integration=gmail_send&integration_reason=denied`. Expect "Sending from your Gmail did not finish, so nothing was connected. 7. **Forwarding advice.** On the same page under "How mail arrives", for a house with a Mudavym address:
   - Expect "Give this address to your vendors. The order letters Mudavym sends already ask vendors to reply to it."
