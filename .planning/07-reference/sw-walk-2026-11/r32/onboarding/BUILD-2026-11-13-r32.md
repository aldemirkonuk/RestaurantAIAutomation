## Decisions (each is the session's call under the 2026-11-07 /goal)

- **#1451: redirect to /house after the menus read.**
  - Rejected: holding the entry until the read returns. That meant rewriting 8 test files of sync assertions.
  - Cost: the menu step shows for one round trip before the redirect.
  - The standing comes from `listMenuVersions`, not `progress.menu_current`, because that field does not exist on the GetStarted chain.
  - A failed read gives 'unread' and leaves the page as it was. It never guesses "current".
- **#1457: fit the photo in the browser, with a 2048 px long edge and a 3 MB limit.**
  - The 2048 px edge sits above the model's 1568 px, with headroom.
  - Rejected: resizing in the gateway. The upload itself is the slow part on a phone, and #762 owns the gateway.
  - Rejected: the model's exact 1568 px. That would add a second, lossy scaling.
  - A HEIC the browser cannot open is sent as is, so the gateway's own words (#778) apply.
  - A PDF is never touched.
- **#1460: show the next steps after setup only where the sidebar runs setup (`runsSetup`), and point "App usage guide" at /help.**
  - Rejected: always showing the steps. A staff panel would nag about owner work.
  - The HelpNext card on /help is left alone because #570 edits the same lines.
- **#1465: the clock label reads "City · Generic name, UTC±N".**
  - Rejected: "City (Generic)". It doubled the parentheses next to "(from the country)".
  - It falls back to the raw id when a city or offset cannot be named.
- **#1469: say "vendor".** It matches the sidebar, the checklist and /vendors. Each refusal class (unconfirmed write, 409, 400/422, 401/403, other) gets its own sentence and an "Open Vendors" link.
- **#1475: search lives in a new `countrySearch.ts`.**
  - Rejected: editing `countries.ts`, because #1364 edits it.
  - CountryCombobox changes only its `filtered` expression, so it merges with sibling #1316.
  - Ranking: a whole code or alias first, then names that start with the text, then names that hold it.
  - The display name is still what is picked and saved.

## Browser checks for a walker (local stack only, never production)

Stack: `local-stack.sh up <wt>` on the tip of each train. #1465 is the tip of #1451 → #1457 → #1465. #1460, #1469 and #1475 each need their own tip.

1. **#1451, a house whose menu is current.**
   - Role: owner of a house with a current menu.
   - Open `/get-started` directly, or from Learn & Help → "App usage guide" on a build without #1460.
   - Expected: the page lands on `/house` (URL replaced). "One last step · Your menu" is not left on screen.
2. **#1451, a draft only.**
   - Role: owner of a house whose menu was read but never chosen (a version with lines, status not archived, and `current` null).
   - Open `/get-started`.
   - Expected: the menu step shows the status line "A menu read earlier is kept as a draft. Choose it on Menu, or read another here." "Menu" links to `/menu`.
   - A house with no read shows no such line.
3. **#1457, a dropped phone photo.**
   - Role: a new owner on `/get-started`, menu step. Drop a JPEG over 2048 px on its long edge (a 12 MP phone photo) on "Drop the menu here".
   - DevTools → Network → POST `/menus/import`. Expected:
     - `data.imageBase64` starts with `/9j/`, not `data:`.
     - It is under about 4,000,000 characters.
     - The read returns lines.
   - Repeat through the photo box on `/menu` (MenuScanUpload). Expected: the same bare `/9j/` body, and the proof crop image draws.
4. **#1457, a transparent PNG.**
   - Drop a PNG with a transparent background and dark text.
   - Expected: `imageBase64` starts with `/9j/` and the proof shows the text on white, not black.
   - Drop a PDF. Expected: the body starts with `JVBERi0`, unchanged.
5. **#1460, Learn & Help after setup.**
   - Role: owner with setup run but steps left (for example, no vendor yet).
   - Open the sidebar's Learn & Help.
   - Expected: a "To see your costs" list with the unfinished steps. With every step done, the list is absent.
   - Click "App usage guide". Expected: the URL is `/help`, not `/get-started?tab=use`.
6. **#1465, the clock line.**
   - Role: a new owner on `/get-started`, place step. Choose Turkey.
   - Expected: "Clock · Istanbul · Türkiye Standard Time, UTC+3 (from the country)". The generic name is whatever Chrome's Intl gives; the test only pins "Istanbul · …, UTC+3".
   - Choose United States and open the clock choice. Expected: options such as "New York · Eastern Time, UTC−5" (UTC−4 in summer, with a true minus sign) and "Indianapolis, Indiana · Eastern Time, …".
7. **#1469, a refused vendor add on /house.**
   - Role: owner on `/house` with no vendor yet. Expected: the ask "Who is your first vendor?" with the button "Add this vendor".
   - Add a vendor name that already exists (409). Expected alert: "<name> is already on your vendors." plus an "Open Vendors" link to `/vendors`.
   - Stop the gateway and add again. Expected: "We could not tell whether <name> was saved: the connection dropped. Look on Vendors before adding it again."
   - On success: "<name> is saved as your vendor." After a reload: "Your vendor is saved. See vendors".
8. **#1475, the country box.**
   - Route `/get-started`, place step, Country box. Expected:
     - "Türkiye" offers exactly "Turkey"; ArrowDown then Enter fills "Turkey".
     - "uk" offers "United Kingdom" first and "Ukraine" after it.
     - "USA" offers "United States" first.
     - "England" offers "United Kingdom".
   - The same box on Settings → Locations → Add location behaves the same way.
