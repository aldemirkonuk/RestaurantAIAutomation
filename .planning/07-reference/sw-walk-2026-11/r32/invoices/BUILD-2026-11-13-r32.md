## The session's calls (2026-11-07 /goal)

- **The stamp is a separate RPC after finalise.** Rejected:
  - Redefining `finalise_delivery_cost`: the cost-of-goods chain redefines it twice (20270502100100, 20270615100000), and a third redefinition would conflict with both.
  - A trigger that infers the stamp, because it cannot know the paper.
  - A gateway UPDATE, which would skip the house check.
- **The stamp names the cost actually posted.** It is never the pre-rounding landed figure; a test uses 100.0000004 against 100.
- **The paper basis comes from arithmetic only.** "Tax inside" needs printed bases. Without them, a pre-tax total in the total box looks identical, and the wrong reading costs a reclaiming house too low. Rejected: guessing from the language of the paper, a country rate table (C3), and tax ÷ total as a rate.
- **A rate is set only when exactly one is printed and it ties out.** The tolerance is tighter than landedCost's own check, so the rule never holds a paper back over a rate this code gave it. A multi-rate fiş gets no rate, and the reason is stated.
- **The reclaim answer is required for a VAT-charging house, and a no-VAT house is stored as false.** Sending true from a no-VAT house is refused. A basis without a reclaim answer would cost the same as unset.
- **The settings route has its own controller.** Five specs build SettingsController positionally.
- **The web block is self-contained, like TargetMarginSection.** A 17th register would touch `st-format`, `useSettingsNextData`, the tally and the counting tests, which are owned by #1342, #1345, #1134 and #1135.
- **No backfill (founder F2).** `since` is set by the trigger, and the page says deliveries already costed keep their cost.

## Browser checks needed (local stack, never production)

Build a local branch that merges #1459, #1461, #1468, #1473 and #1481 (with #1399's chain). Then run `local-stack.sh up <wt>`, with migration 20271022140500 applied.

1. **Unanswered house (owner).** Go to `/settings` and scroll to The house.
   - The block `st-section-tax-basis` is headed "Does tax sit in its prices?".
   - No choice is pressed, and Record is disabled.
   - `st-tax-sentence` reads "Not answered yet. Until it is, every delivery is costed as its invoice prints it, tax and all."
2. **Answer: reclaims (owner or manager).**
   - Press "Our prices include tax". The sentence reads "Say whether the house reclaims the tax it pays on its purchases. Its stock is costed before tax only if it does."
   - Press "Yes, we reclaim it". The sentence reads "Record will write: This house reclaims the tax it pays: a delivery whose invoice shows its tax is costed before tax. This counts from now on. Deliveries already costed keep their cost."
   - Press Record. The sentence becomes "That is what is already recorded." The page shows "First answered <today>. Deliveries costed before then keep the cost they were posted at." and "SET BY · <name>".
   - Reload: the same choices are pressed.
   - In the local DB, `restaurants.tax_basis_since` equals `tax_basis_set_at`.
3. **Switch date holds.** Change to "No, we keep it as a cost" and Record.
   - The "First answered" date does not change.
   - The local DB shows `tax_basis_since` unchanged.
4. **No-VAT house.** Press "We charge no VAT".
   - The reclaim question disappears.
   - The sentence reads "Record will write: This house charges no VAT, so it reclaims none: a delivery whose invoice shows its tax is costed with the tax in it. This counts from now on. Deliveries already costed keep their cost."
5. **Manager vs others.**
   - As a manager, the block is editable.
   - As a member whose role is unresolved (`canManage` false), the choices are greyed, there is no Record, and the block reads "Only an owner or a manager can answer this."
   - Staff are sent to "Ask a manager" and never see the block.
6. **Trail.** `GET /settings-audit?register=tax-basis` (manager token) returns the `house_tax_basis_changed` rows, each with `fields.tax_basis` and `fields.reclaims_input_tax` holding from and to values.
7. **Cost, reclaiming house (owner).** Set the house to include tax and reclaim yes. Upload a SYNTHETIC market receipt: 10 × 110.00 with KDV %10 inside, total 1100.00, tax 100.00, and a tax row with base 1000.00. Map it to one item, count it at the door, then agree and verify.
   - In the local DB, the new `inventory_lots` row has `unit_cost = 100` and `cost_tax_basis = 'net'`.
   - The verify answer's `finalised[0].basis` / `taxSaid` reads "Its prices are read as including tax, since its lines add up to its total of 1100.00 and the tax it prints, 100.00, is on a base of 1000.00. Every line is at 10 %, the one rate it prints, which makes its tax of 100.00."
8. **Cost, keeping house.** Repeat step 7 with reclaim set to No.
   - `unit_cost = 110` and `cost_tax_basis = 'gross_cost'`.
9. **e-Fatura file (only if the e-invoice reader #1042/#1043 is in the build).** Use a SYNTHETIC e-Fatura: 10 × 100 + KDV %20, total 1200.
   - Reclaiming house: 100, `net`.
   - Keeping house: 120, `gross_cost`.
10. **A paper that proves nothing.** Use a receipt whose lines add up to its total but which has no printed tax base.
    - The cost is as printed, and `cost_tax_basis` is null.
    - The note includes "Whether its prices include tax is not read: its lines add up to its total of …, but it prints no base for its tax, so its total could be before tax."
