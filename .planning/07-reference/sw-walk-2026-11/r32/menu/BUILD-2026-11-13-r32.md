## The session's calls (founder delegated forks, 2026-11-07 /goal)

- **G11: where the house price shows.**
  - Chosen: show it on the current menu only, read live from `restaurant_inventory` (in the house, in chunks of 150). A difference counts at 0.005 or more. A printed kind with no price of its own is said; a kind the menu does not print is left to the price flag.
  - Rejected: saying "changed after this menu". No timestamp shows when Your price was set relative to the menu, so the claim could be false.
  - Rejected: failing the whole read when prices are unreadable. Instead the menu still shows, with one alert.
- **G21: how a line that fits two goods gets resolved.**
  - Chosen: link on kept, not-current menus only. A current menu is refused with 409, because its lines were already carried.
  - Chosen: accept any live good of the house, without re-running the matcher. The manager is the authority.
  - Chosen: show the candidates on the read reply only, without persisting them.
  - Rejected: persisting the candidates. It needs a column and migration, and the read reply already carries them at the moment of choice.
  - Rejected: letting make-current pick the first candidate. It silently prices the wrong good.
- **G19: where the money format happens.**
  - Chosen: format at display (`flagNoteInHouseMoney`). The gateway writer is unchanged.
  - Rejected: changing the writer. It would leave every stored note in the old form and mix locale into stored text.
- **G16: what to do with the 25-line flag.**
  - Chosen: stop writing the flag.
  - Rejected: gating the flag on `subscription_tier`. Every house is pilot, which resolves to core, and no plan holds a 25-line cap (pricing is OD-23, undecided). The gate would enforce a plan that does not exist.
  - Old rows keep their note, and `menu-line-view` still maps it.
