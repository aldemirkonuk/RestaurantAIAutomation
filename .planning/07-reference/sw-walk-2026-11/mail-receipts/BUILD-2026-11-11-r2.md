## Built

### D6 and D7 (#1093, #1094)

**One reply count.** "Replies · 30 days" in Letters now comes from Mail's server count. The book's inbound rows no longer feed it.

**One zone rule.** "Today" counts in the house's own zone. When the house has no zone set:
- a single-zone country falls back to its zone, for example TR to Europe/Istanbul;
- a multi-zone country, such as the US, sends `p_zone: null`, and Today says no zone is set instead of counting in UTC.

### R10 (#1096, #1097)

**The cause.** The document door (`door-count` with `createDelivery`) writes a delivery and a `door_count` document but no receipt event. So its deliveries never reached the "Counted at the door, no paperwork yet" strip.

**The fix.** `readDoorCountedWithoutPaper` reads the open deliveries that have a door count and no vendor paper.
- Reads are tenant-scoped and capped. A failed read throws; a capped read says the list is only a floor.
- Keg, liter and weight lines are named apart and never added into bottles.

**Where it shows.** `/receiving/unverified` returns these deliveries as `doorCounted`. The receipts strip and the sorting office list them.

### R8 (#1108, #1109, #1110)

**Cost (#1108).**
- `UOMS` gains `kg` and `g`.
- `perBottleLotCost` costs a weighed line per one of its item's units: 3 kg at 450 into an item kept in g gives 0.45.
- It refuses a weighed line priced per container, a dimension mismatch, and a missing unit.

**Door and verify (#1109).**
- `shelfQuantity` converts a weighed line into the item's own unit. It refuses a non-whole result, a cross-dimension unit and a missing unit, and never rounds (ADR 0070).
- Finalise feeds landed cost in item units.

**Schema and extractor (#1110).**
- The migration `20270727120000` widens the two line uom CHECKs.
- `normalizeUom` maps KGM, kgs and kilo to `kg`, and GRM and gr to `g`. The extractor prompt is updated to match.
- The order door, order units and agreed prices refuse non-container units.

### Item 5 (#1112, stacked on #1087)

**The check.** An order booked 6 bottles unpriced at the door; 2 were sold through POS; then the invoice was verified at 25 in the legacy `/inventory?verify=` workspace.

**The ledger was correct.** The lot went to 25 invoice final. A settlement row was written: 2 bottles, null to 25, total 50.

**The house was told nothing,** for two reasons:
- (a) The answer carried no cost information.
- (b) `useNotificationStore` toasts are never rendered anywhere, so "Receipt verified" and "Verification failed" were both silent, as were six other store callers.

**The fix.**
- The answer now carries `costCorrection`.
- The store forwards its toasts to sonner's AppToaster.
- The toast adds: "A sale made before the invoice arrived now carries the invoice cost. The 4 bottles still on the shelf are costed at it too."

**The re-check passed** on the same stack: the answer carried `costCorrection {25, 4, 1}`, and the drawn toast text was captured from the DOM.

**The brief was framed wrong.** There is no verify button on `/orders`. On the next design, `/inventory?verify=` redirects to `/receiving?order=`, which never calls verify-receipt. The legacy workspace is the only verifying UI.

- **`procurement_orders.invoice_quantity` is never written by verify.** Seen during the item 5 check; not touched.
