## The math bug (item 6)

Plan "add 2 prices" vs result "1 price set" was a unit mismatch:
- The plan counted kinds: a bottle and a glass are 2.
- The gateway tallies one outcome per line (`carryMenuPrice` + `set_house_menu_price`: locked if every named kind is held, else changed if any named kind changed, else unchanged).

#1125 had moved the changed and new counts to wines. #1216 moves the rest. A test restates the gateway rule on its own and checks all 28 one-line kind combinations, an unmatched line, and 784 two-line menus. The plan cannot predict `stale`; that is unchanged.
