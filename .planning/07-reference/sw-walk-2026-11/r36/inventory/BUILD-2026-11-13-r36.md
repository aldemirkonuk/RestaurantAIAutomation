## Decisions (the session's calls, recorded in each PR body)

- **Gap 1:** "never moved" is read from the ledger plus lots, because a lot is deleted at 0, through a read-only RPC.
  - Rejected: a `first_moved_at` column kept by a trigger. It writes on the hot path and needs a backfill.
  - Rejected: editing `apply_stock_movement`. Open PRs redefine it.
  - Rejected: gateway ledger queries. They hit the 1000-row cap, or take N queries.
  - Only `true` changes the standing. Null or absent keeps "Out".
- **Gap 4:** refuse a cost from staff (403) rather than drop it silently. A costed batch is refused whole. The role is read lazily, only when a good would be reactivated.
- **Gap 5:** hide the cost acts from staff, and show them the food card with a sentence. Rejected for now: a cost-free staff grid.
- **Gap 9:** never fall back to the ledger on a failed figures read.
- **Gap 12:** a pool of 4.
  - Rejected: `Promise.all` over every chunk (up to 50 requests at once).
  - Rejected: a POST with the ids in the body (a new route and a contract change).
