## Gaps NOT fixed by any open PR (ranked by impact on the goal)

1. **HIGH: day 1 says every new good is "Out".**
   - The empty page promises that a scanned title "comes in with no count until someone counts it. It is never set to zero." (`InventoryNext.tsx:692`).
   - But `restaurant_inventory.stock_live` is `integer DEFAULT 0 NOT NULL` (main `baseline_from_production.sql:3264`), and house items keep that 0 (`house-item.service.ts:59`).
   - `standingOf` reads stock 0 as `'out'` and never looks at `lastCountedAt` (`useInventoryNextData.ts:150-152`).
   - Effect: after a menu scan, or after carrying 30 foods, the read sentence says "30 are out" (`:554`). Every row is filed under Out in "Needs you first", for goods that sit on the shelf uncounted.
   - Fix shape: when stock is 0 and nothing was counted or received, read the standing as "not counted yet" rather than out.
   - Variant check: standingOf is identical on all 776 heads that carry the file.
2. **HIGH: a weighed good (a food's default unit) cannot be bought in through the app.**
   - The order units are bottle, case, keg, pack, split_case, each and liter (main `order-units.ts:63`). There is no kg or g.
   - "Order more" on a gram good opens a sheet that refuses and says "Place it on Orders, where the vendor's own unit is written down" (`InventorySheets.tsx:735`, `iv-amount.ts:154`). Orders cannot state a weight either. #1110 says so outright: "no order is placed by weight".
   - "A delivery without an order" is wine-only: the rows are Master Library wines or wine drafts (`BatchReceiveGrid.tsx:23`).
   - #1444 makes Mark delivered book nothing for a measured good.
   - CarryThis defaults a food to grams (`CarryThis.tsx:30`); #1150 makes the default kg, still a weight.
   - Effect: a flour, meat or vegetable good gets stock only from counts, and a cost from no screen I could find. So Value reads "No cost yet" and dish cost reads "Not known".
   - Not verified: whether a receipts-desk path books an order-less kg invoice line to a food with its cost. I found no web caller.
   - No open PR adds a weight order unit.
3. **HIGH (lands with recipe-draw slice 4): a food used in both cocktails and dishes would read a fraction of its real use.**
   - `record_recipe_draw` books as `p_transaction_type => 'sale'` (#1480, `20271020110000_a_recipe_sale_books_its_draw.sql:187`).
   - `inventory_analytics` picks `'sales'` over `'counts'` whenever `sold_90d > 0` (`20270802100000_days_of_stock_from_real_use.sql:129`).
   - Dishes draw nothing. #1396's brief says "Dishes join once #1222 lands", and no slice or brief exists for that.
   - So the first Margarita switches lime's pace from the counts (all its use) to the cocktail draws alone. "Days left" can then be too long by many times, below-par warnings stop, and the card says lime was "sold" (`ItemCards.tsx:247-248`).
   - Fix before slice 4: prefer counts when the good is in any current dish recipe, or combine the two sources (sales draws plus the counted remainder).
   - Extends r28 gap 1. None of #1396/#1421/#1478/#1480/#1483 mentions the usage source.
4. **MED-HIGH: staff can write what the house paid, while every read hides it from them.**
   - "Start an auction lot" (the hammer price) and "A delivery without an order" (a cost column, plus "sample" = cost 0) are not role-gated on the empty page or in Tools (`InventoryNext.tsx:666,685,1006,1009`).
   - They post to `POST /inventory/:rid/items` and `/items/bulk`. Those routes gate only menu prices (main `inventory.controller.ts:182,220`), then book an `initial` lot at the typed cost (main `inventory.service.ts:996,1098`).
   - The same create path reactivates a good that a manager marked inactive (main `inventory.service.ts:969`). That is the act #1363 forbids staff by PATCH.
   - Effect: a staff typo sets WAC, Value and dish cost, and #1355/#1356/#1411 then hide that figure from the person who typed it.
   - #1363 and #1386 do not cover these routes or buttons.
5. **MED-HIGH: a food-first house has no way in from the empty page.**
   - The empty state reads "No bottles on the books yet" and offers Add a bottle (/wines), an auction lot, a wine-only delivery and a menu scan (`InventoryNext.tsx:641-706`).
   - "Carry a food or supply" exists only under Tools on a non-empty list (`:990-994`).
   - #1482 (recipes lane) adds "Add X to your shelf" from a recipe line, but only after the owner finds /dishes.
   - No open PR changes the empty state (variant check: the text is identical on every head).
6. **MEDIUM: counts are the only measure of a food's use, and they are entered one good at a time.**
   - "Export count sheet" (`InventoryNext.tsx:1013`) has no import.
   - Counting means a drawer and a CountSheet per good. A weekly count of 80 kitchen goods is 80 sheets.
   - Until dishes draw stock, NOT_DRAWN_BY_SALES (`ItemCards.tsx:224`) makes this path load-bearing for "Usage a day" and "Days left" on every food.
   - Extends r28 gap 15 (which was framed as a camera count of a shelf). No open PR adds a list count mode or a count-sheet import.
7. **MEDIUM: every drawer open fires reads the drawer does not show.**
   - RowDropdown always calls `useRowDetail` (`RowDropdown.tsx:214`), and the drawer always mounts one for its acts with `sections={[]}` (`ItemDrawer.tsx:245`).
   - So each open also reads the purchase ledger (50 rows), up to three order-document reads after it (a waterfall) and the auction lots. That is on top of figures, nutrition, used-in, dishes and drinks: 7–10 requests where 5 are needed.
   - Staff, who are never shown cost, still fetch the purchase lines.
   - Fix shape: read the row detail only when an evidence panel that needs it is open. No open PR does this.
8. **MEDIUM: the raw-ingredient nutrition lookup is off by default and nothing turns it on.**
   - USDA FoodData Central runs only when `USDA_FDC_API_KEY` is set (#1013, `nutrition-lookup.service.ts:26`). No env template or doc on any branch sets it; only EXTERNAL_CONNECTIONS names it.
   - Open Food Facts is a packaged-goods source. So flour, tomato and lemon, the goods the KPI → nutrition card most needs, get no draft, and the owner types every figure.
   - Not verified: the deployed env (production is never queried).
9. **MED-LOW: two PRs combine to show absence as health in the Cost evidence.**
   - #1515 passes `settled` only when figures are `ready` (`ItemDrawer.tsx:285`). While reading, or after a failed figures read, it falls back to the ledger (`RowDropdown.tsx:798-807`).
   - A good received at the door has costless ledger rows, so it reads "Last bought: none on the ledger". The Cost card above it says "What each delivery cost could not be read".
   - Fix shape: when figures are not ready, say "reading" or "could not be read", and do not fall back.
10. **LOW-MED: day-1 and day-7 pace is diluted, and the sentence misstates the window.**
    - `sales_days` is floored at 7 (`days_of_stock_from_real_use.sql:69`). Two bottles sold on a house's first day read 0.29 a day, and "Days left" reads up to 7× too long.
    - The card says "Sold over the last 7 days" when the house is 2 days old (`ItemCards.tsx:247`).
11. **LOW: the counts-based use includes write-offs, but the sentence implies they were taken out.**
    - `counted_used = first count + arrivals − last count` (`…real_use.sql:73`), so waste is inside "Usage a day", and the Waste card shows the same waste again.
    - The sentence reads "the two counts, with what was received, written off and found short between them" (`ItemCards.tsx` usageSaid). Say "including what was written off", or subtract waste.
12. **LOW: #803 reads the wine library one chunk after another.**
    - `wines.ts:76` loops `await` per 100 ids, so 1,000 wines take 10 serial round trips after the list. The Type filter and the producer and grape columns wait on them.
    - Fix: `Promise.all` over the chunks, which keeps the URL-length fix.
13. **LOW: the item's recipe lines are read twice per open.**
    - The dishes route and the drinks route each run `currentUses` (#1304, `dishes.service.ts:326,373`), which reads every `recipe_lines` row for the item and then the pointers.
    - One route answering both groups would halve it.
