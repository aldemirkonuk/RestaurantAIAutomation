## Ranked gaps not fixed by any open PR

### N1 (high, money). A menu's prices are taken as the house's money, whatever money the menu prints
- `parseMenuPrice` drops a leading or trailing `₺ $ € £ TL TRY USD EUR GBP` and keeps the number (`apps/api-gateway/src/menus/menus.service.ts:476-481`). The /menu Change price and Add boxes go through it.
- `WineExtractItem` has no currency field (`apps/api-gateway/src/menus/wine-extract-item.interface.ts`), and the photo prompt says nothing about currency or about a menu printing two price columns (`parsers/scan-parser.service.ts:79-121`).
- #761's own `menu-price.ts` header says it: "No currency is taken or converted (`₺450` reads 450 …); resolving by the house's currency is MU-9, not done here".
- **What the owner sees:** an Antalya house whose menu prints "€12" (or "₺450 / €12") gets ₺12 as the house's bottle price on Inventory, and every margin built on it. Nothing says the money was not the house's.
- **Open PR:** none.

### N2 (high, upload and updates). A read menu cannot be corrected, or a line dropped, before it is made current
- The only web callers of `reviewMenuItem` are `pages/menu/next/MenuNext.tsx:507` (current menu, prices only), `pages/HouseMenu.tsx:154` (first proof, section only) and `components/onboarding/MenuReviewScreen.tsx:125`, which nothing mounts (only its test imports it).
- There is no web client for `GET /menu-versions/:menuId` (exports of `services/api/menus.ts`), so a kept menu's lines are shown only in the plan's read-only "Every line" table, drinks only (`MenuPlan.tsx:473-500`).
- **What breaks:** a misread name makes a twin good at make-current; a misread price goes to Inventory; a drink read as a kitchen line (or the reverse) cannot be moved. The only paths are to make the menu current and repair afterwards (prices only), or to read the file again.
- **Open PR:** none (no diff adds a kept-line edit or discard).

### N3 (high, day 7+ upload). The /menu read box sends a phone photo whole, and a too-big photo is told to wait
- `ReadMenuForm` base64s the file as picked (`MenuVersions.tsx:178-185`, `:211`): no fit, no size check. The body cap is 15 MB (`apps/api-gateway/src/main.ts:47`); the reader takes at most 5 MB per image (#1457's note).
- Any failed model call is answered "Menu scan service temporarily unavailable" (`scan-parser.service.ts:537`), so an oversize photo is told to try later, and fails again.
- #1457 fits photos on /get-started only (MenuCsvUpload, MenuScanUpload, GetStarted). Chain B (#1065/#1066) makes this same `ReadMenuForm` the reader behind the /inventory scan buttons, so after the merge the gap spreads there.
- **Open PR:** none for /menu. (The 30 s axios cut on a long scan read, `client.ts:67`, is chain B's #1061–#1063, not in the train.)
- Not measured: real phone photo sizes or reader timings.

### N4 (medium-high, PRs contradict once merged). Kitchen or drink is decided two ways
- /menu and the carry use `isKitchenLine(category, raw, line)` (`menus.service.ts:103`, `menu-line-view.ts:179-191`): drink fields and the raw line count.
- The recipe lane calls `isKitchenLine(category, null)` (#1414 `recipes/menu-price-match.ts`, #1503 `recipes/menu-dishes.ts`) on `CURRENT_MENU_LINE_COLUMNS` (`menus/current-menu-lines.ts:40`), which has no producer, vintage or raw line.
- **Probe** (ts-node on the tip's `menu-line-view.ts`):
  - dessert wine with producer and vintage (photo category `dessert`): /menu kitchen=false, recipe lane kitchen=true
  - "Moscato d'Asti" under Desserts: false / true
  - CSV dish known only from its raw row: true / false
- **What happens:** /dishes (#1504) offers a Sauternes as a dish to add, and /cocktails' Menu column matches it as a dish; a CSV dish /menu sets apart is never offered.
- **Fix brief:** use the `kitchen_line` that `lineForViewer` already computes, or add the columns to the shared read.
- **Open PR:** none. #1503 chose `null` and says it matches "the Menu column".
