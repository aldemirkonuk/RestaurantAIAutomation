## Decisions (the session's call, under the 2026-11-07 /goal)

**N1: a price printed in another money is held, never converted.**
- A house with no named money (`restaurants.currency` null) takes the price as read.
- A line that names the house's money, or none, is taken.
- A line in another money takes the house-money column (`also_priced`) when the menu prints one. Otherwise it is held: no price is taken, and `price_flag = 'other_currency'` is set with a note naming the money.
- A typed price with a foreign mark is refused (add line, review line).
- `$` and `£` are families of monies, not one money.
- Rejected:
  - converting at a rate, since the house-money rule (`20260905120000_a_house_names_its_money.sql`) says nothing converts;
  - writing the euro figure into `bottle_price`, since every reader of that column takes it as house money;
  - a new currency column per line, since the existing `price_flag`/`price_flag_note` already carries "no price, here is why".

**N8: only the same printed line read twice at a page break is dropped.**
- The key is producer|name|vintage|category|glass|bottle.
- Text is folded with NFKD, marks stripped, and every Unicode letter and digit kept.
- Only the last 3 lines of one part are compared with the first 3 of the next. A failed part ends the seam.
- Rejected:
  - keeping the global dedupe with a Unicode fold, which still drops a dish printed again pages later;
  - overlapping chunks, which would change what the reader sees.

**N3: classify the reader's refusal, and fit and refuse in the browser.**
- On the gateway, a 413, or a 400 whose message is about the file, is a 422 that says what to do. Everything else stays "temporarily unavailable".
- In the browser, the photo is fitted with #1457's `fitPhoto`. Non-photos over 10 MB are refused at pick, and a photo still over 5 MB after the fit is refused at Read.
- Rejected:
  - the gateway alone, since a 15 MB+ body never reaches it;
  - a second menu-only resizer.

**N5: offer the discarded drink off sale at the next menu; do not clear its price on discard.**
- Rejected: clearing the price on the discard tap. That tap is undoable and is not marked as a price change.
- Also rejected: a confirm dialog at discard, since `MenuNext.tsx` is being edited by the r34 round.

## N2 builder brief (Sonnet, bounded)

Goal: on `/menu`, a read menu that is not current can have a line corrected or dropped before "Make current". Today the plan is read-only, and the only fix is after it is current.

Facts (checked on the #1517 tip):
- `PATCH /menus/items/:id` (`menus.controller.ts:100`, `reviewMenuItem`) edits any line of the caller's house.
  - The fields are from `EDITABLE_MENU_ITEM_FIELDS` (`dto/review-menu-item.dto.ts:3`): name, producer, category, vintage, region, grape_variety, by_glass_price, bottle_price.
  - A price field needs an owner or manager. On a non-current menu, any other field is open to any member (r35 G14 rule in the service).
  - Web client: `reviewMenuItem` in `apps/web/src/services/api/menus.ts:214`.
- `PATCH /menus/:restaurantId/items/:id/discard` (`menus.controller.ts:147`) is owner/manager only. The service (`menus.service.ts:1132`) does not check that the menu is current, so it works on a read menu. Web client: `discardMenuItem` at `menus.ts:309`.
- `GET /menu-versions/:menuId` (`menus.controller.ts:212`) returns the lines. There is no web client for it yet. `GET /menu-versions/:menuId/plan` is `getMenuPlan` (`menus.ts:535`).
- Drink or dish is `category: "food"`.

Build:
1. `menus.ts`: add `getMenuVersion(menuId)` for GET `/menu-versions/:menuId`, typed from the service's `getVersion` return.
2. `MenuPlan.tsx`: for a menu that is not current and a viewer who can manage, each plan line gets an Edit action.
   - Edit opens inline fields: name, section (category select from the existing vocabulary plus "food" labelled "Dish"), producer, glass price, bottle price.
   - Save sends one `reviewMenuItem` per changed field, then invalidates the plan and versions queries.
3. Each line also gets "Leave this line out", which calls `discardMenuItem` and refetches. A left-out line is not in the plan, because `computePlan` reads the chosen menu without discarded lines; only the replaced menu is read with them (#1561).
4. A staff viewer sees no Edit. Prices stay owner/manager only, as on the gateway.
5. Words, in the house's terms:
   - "Correct a line before this menu is made current."
   - "Left out of this menu. It will not be carried."

Tests:
- vitest in `MenuPlan.test.tsx`: Edit sends the right field and value; Leave out calls discard and the line goes; staff sees neither; a current menu shows neither.
- Mutate: drop the not-current check, drop the manager check, and send the wrong field.

Do not: re-read the file, mount the onboarding `MenuReviewScreen`, or edit `MenuNext.tsx` (owned by the r34 round). Stack on the newest open PR owning `MenuPlan.tsx`.
