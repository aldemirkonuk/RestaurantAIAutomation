## Gaps NOT fixed by any open PR (ranked by impact on the goal)

### 1. A recipe's cost disappears when its good runs out, and the sheet then says something false
- **Cause:** the invoice cost view only reads live lots with `qty > 0` (`supabase/migrations/20270920100000_a_serve_is_costed_from_invoices_only.sql:52-53`, #1385). The agreed-cost view does the same (`20270502100200_an_agreed_cost_is_a_known_cost.sql:32-33`).
- `mergeInvoiceLotCost` marks the good as read so it never falls back to the rollup (`apps/api-gateway/src/analytics/inventory-cost.ts:217`). `last_purchase_price` has no writer on main or in any open PR; only test fixtures set it.
- **Result:** the moment the last bottle of Campari or prosecco is drawn (wine POS sales already draw lots; counts and waste do too), every drink using it reads "Not known".
  - /cocktails pour cost becomes a dash (#1403).
  - The line says "No price yet: it will cost once a delivery of it is checked in." (`apps/web/src/pages/cellar/next/recipe/LineRow.tsx:174`, #1499).
  - Meanwhile the drawer's Cost card says "Last bought … settled 1 Nov" for the same good (#1510/#1515).
- **Day 30 impact:** pour cost vanishes exactly when the owner is about to reorder. The cost of the last settled delivery is known, and nothing reads it.
- **Open PR fixes it?** No. No PR touches the view's `qty > 0`, and no recipe code reads #1510's settled points.

### 2. One good, two unit costs: the drawer and the recipe on the same page disagree again
- **Background:** #1304's own body names this defect: "Same good, same moment, two answers". It fixed it by making recipes read what the inventory list reads.
- **What brings it back:** #1385 then made recipes alone read invoices only, opt-in (`recipes.service.ts:298,315`, #1526).
- **What the others still read:**
  - the inventory list and drawer read the raw rollup `wac`, which blends estimates (`apps/api-gateway/src/inventory/inventory.service.ts:197-199`, #1515);
  - analytics and margin advice read the rollup through `resolveUnitCost` without the view (`analytics.service.ts:180`, `advanced-analytics.service.ts:102`, `pricing/margin-advice.service.ts:253`).
- **Example:** a good with 10 invoiced units at 100 and 90 estimated at 60 reads 64 on /inventory and in margin advice, and 100 (partly invoiced) on its dish card in the same drawer (#1255 cards).
- **Open PR fixes it?** No. r28 left "analytics callers keep the rollup's answer until they opt in", and nobody opted in.

### 3. Day 1: the menu's drinks never reach /cocktails; they become phantom shelf goods instead
- **Dishes have a path, drinks do not.** #1503/#1504 offer the menu's dishes onto /dishes (`GET/POST /recipes/:rid/dishes/from-menu`, `dishes.controller.ts:41-56`, #1503). No PR offers the menu's cocktails onto /cocktails: a grep for `from-menu` hits only #1503 and #1504.
- **Instead,** making a menu current adds each cocktail line as a `restaurant_inventory` good of kind `cocktail`, uom `each` (#1392; its body says "Linking a cocktail to its recipe is not done here. It belongs to the recipe software").
- **Effects:**
  - **The owner must retype every drink** on /cocktails by hand, with its price.
  - **A phantom Negroni sits on the shelf** with no stock. The recipe ingredient picker offers it as an ingredient: `shelf()` filters nothing by kind (`recipes.service.ts:768-784`, #1526; ShelfPicker shows the kind only as a word).
  - **The same page contradicts itself after merge.**
    - The ledger says the menu and till lines "are shown apart rather than merged: joining them to a row of the list would need a name match this page is not entitled to make" (`apps/web/src/pages/cellar/next/CocktailRegister.tsx:549-559`, #1512).
    - The Menu column on the same page does exactly that name match in the gateway (#1414/#1420, "₺11.00 differs").
- **Open PR fixes it?** No.

### 4. The recipe draw would take a whole case per lime
- **The draw's rule.** A piece line (`each_milli`) draws one unit of any container count: `CONTAINER_COUNT = bottle, case, keg, pack, glass, shot` (`apps/api-gateway/src/pos-hub/recipe-draw.ts:124,151`, #1396). The SQL twin does the same: `if p_uom in ('each','bottle','case','keg','pack','glass','shot') … base_per_unit := 1000` (`supabase/migrations/20271020100000_a_recipe_draw_keeps_its_remainder.sql:128-131`, #1478).
- **Effect:** "1 lime" linked to Limes kept by the case draws one case per serve. "2 eggs" against Eggs kept by the pack draws two packs.
- **The cost side refuses the same pairing on purpose:** "1 lime linked to a case of limes would cost the case" (`recipe-cost.ts:142-146`, #1526).
- **So cost and stock disagree in both directions:**
  - a bottle beer as "1 each" is a cost unit_mismatch but a correct draw;
  - a lime against a case is no cost but a wrong draw.
- **Status:** latent until slice 4 calls it (see 5), but it is in SQL that ships with #1478.
- **Open PR fixes it?** No.

### 5. Even with the whole recipe-draw stack merged, a sale draws nothing, and a dish never can
- **No caller.** No TypeScript in any open PR calls `rpc('record_recipe_draw')`; `planRecipeDraw` and `drawArgs` have only spec callers (#1483). Slice 4 ("pos-hub wiring") exists only as a brief in #1396's body.
- **No control.** No web PR lets a manager name a till button's recipe. A grep of web POS/till/mapping files for `cocktail` finds nothing. The link (#1421) is API-only.
- **No dish link.** The link is `pos_item_mappings.cocktail_id` only (`20271015100000_a_till_button_names_its_drink_recipe.sql:29-30`). Dishes have no till link in any PR; #1396 says "Dishes join once #1222 lands".
- **Also open, already noted by r32/r35:** the draw ignores `usable_pct` (#1525) and `piece_grams` (#1458). A line the sheet prices completely therefore makes the whole sale wait (I2, all or nothing).
- **Open PR fixes it?** No. This is new detail on r28 gap 1 (inventory lane).

### 6. The nutrition card can never be complete for a drink with ice, water or a garnish
- **How a line becomes untracked.** The paste reader makes ice, plain water and any garnish untracked (`parseSpec.ts:97-99,138,152`, #1499).
- **What untracked does to the card:**
  - every figure becomes "at least …, unknown for Ice" (`recipe-nutrition.ts:100,167-168`, #1526);
  - no allergen can be "free" (`:128,137`), so the card says "No allergen can be ruled out: Ice does not say what it contains" (`WhatIsInAServe.tsx:84-86`).
- **This is by design** in #1012 ("free only when every line, untracked too, states it free"). An untracked line can never state anything, so almost every cocktail has a permanently incomplete card.
- **The trade-off the owner is forced into:** link Ice as a stocked good and the cost goes "Not known" (unpriced ice), or keep it untracked and the nutrition goes "at least". Ice and still water are known zeros.
- **Open PR fixes it?** No.

### 7. After the tax-basis PRs land, the pour-cost sentence is false, and cost mixes net and gross
- **What the tax PRs add:**
  - #1459/#1461/#1473 add `restaurants.tax_basis` ('prices_include_tax' etc.) and `reclaims_input_tax`, set from Settings (`20271022140500_a_house_says_if_it_reclaims_tax.sql`);
  - they stamp each lot's `cost_tax_basis` as 'net' or 'gross_cost'.
- **What the recipe side still does:**
  - every pour or food cost card says "whether that price includes tax is not recorded" (`DrinkCards.tsx:93`, #1499);
  - `invoice_wac` averages net and gross lots together.
- No recipe file in any PR reads `tax_basis` (grep empty).
- **Open PR fixes it?** No. r28 gap 4 said "waits on the tax basis"; the basis now exists in open PRs and nothing reads it.

### 9. A recipe's KPI cards stop at cost and pour cost
- **What is missing:** a margin a serve in money, a sold count, and a target.
  - The cards are Cost, Price, Pour cost, Ingredients, Energy and Pure alcohol (`DrinkCards.tsx:233-239`).
  - The /cocktails ledger below already shows Sold and Taken per menu or till line by name (`CocktailRegister.tsx:566-591`). The owner sees a Margarita's pour cost in one table and its sales in another, unjoined.
- **The goal's "KPI card → nutrition card" is not a click.** The Energy card is inert text, and the nutrition section is simply below it.

### 10. /dishes and /cocktails list figures are serial waterfalls
- `figures()` reads pointers in chunks of 20 ids, one after another, then versions and lines in chunks of 20, one after another (`apps/api-gateway/src/recipes/dishes.service.ts:36,153-214`, #1526).
- 120 dishes means 6 + 6 sequential waves, plus pricing, before the page shows one cost.
- #1424 fixed only the sheet.

### 11. Nothing leads a new owner to recipes
- Learn & Help's steps go to /menu, /get-started, /vendors, team, /inventory, mail and /receipts, never to /dishes or /cocktails (`apps/web/src/guidance/components/LearnPanel.tsx:85-175`, #1460).
- /cocktails is not a room; it is reachable only through Cellar (`apps/web/src/lib/mudavym/rooms.ts:111`).
- /dishes sits in "The cellar" (`rooms.ts:68-72`).
- There is no single "Recipes" home.

### 12. The no-price sentence gives the wrong way out
- "it will cost once a delivery of it is checked in" (`LineRow.tsx:174`; AddToShelf repeats it). This is wrong in two ways:
  - a delivery checked in at the door without an invoice or agreed cost gives no price (#1385 reads invoices, then agreed cost);
  - the hand-price path (a manual stock entry with a cost, booked final and read through the agreed view, #1304) is never named.

### 13. A retired cocktail keeps its till button
- Retiring dates the row and never deletes it (`apps/api-gateway/src/beverages/beverages.service.ts:630-640`, #1512). So `on delete set null` (`20271015100000…sql:21,30`) never fires, and its comment "a recipe removed from the house leaves its button unmapped" does not hold.
- The house check runs only when the mapping is written (`pos-hub/cocktail-link.ts`).
- **Status:** latent until slice 4.

### 14. "Add the menu's dishes" makes up to 500 inserts one after another in one request
- `dishes.service.ts:540` (#1503) awaits `createDish` per dish.
- A long menu is a long request with no progress shown and no batching.
