## Gaps NOT fixed by any open PR (ranked by impact on the goal)

### 1. Days before the house had any record are counted as zero sales (days 1–90 of every house)

- **What goes wrong.** These lenses read a fixed window and zero-fill every day before the first consumption line or check:
  - restock: 90 days;
  - menu velocity: sold ÷ 90;
  - seasonality and trend: 90 days;
  - forecast: 120 days;
  - cashflow: 180 days.
- **What the reader sees in week 1** (measured above):
  - Reorder points are about 4× too low, and days of cover about 13× too high (154 against 12).
  - The bench trend reads "+16.1% a day" for flat sales.
  - "What to buy back" stays silent while a good runs out.
  - Menu engineering files every new good as a slow mover.
- **Evidence:**
  - `apps/api-gateway/src/analytics/analytics.service.ts:289-307` (`toDailySeries` zero-fills `sinceDays`), `:652`, `:677`, `:651`;
  - forecast `:930` (sinceDays 120);
  - `advanced-analytics.service.ts:266-280` (`houseDaily` zero-fills), `:342` (`/ sinceDays`), `:662`, `:676` (trend28), `:771` (cashflow);
  - all at decc7d4af. The same code is on every train tip (`toDailySeries(rows, sinceDays)` and `/ sinceDays` grepped on d42565bd8, 06789c0bd, b5b69bb9f, caae4f4dd and e9b94f12b).
- **Fix shape.** Start each window at the house's first observation: the first `wine_consumption_log` row or the first `pos_checks` row, whichever the lens reads. Divide by the days actually observed. Say "over N days since D" in the basis. When N is less than about 14, say the rate is too young to plan from instead of plotting it.
- **Open PR:** none.

### 2. The merge resolution silently drops goal units on the page bench (r28 gap 6 comes back)

- **What goes wrong.** #1382's units arrive only through `getOverview().activeGoals[].unit` (b5b69bb9f `advanced-analytics.service.ts:967-970`).
  - Train A's bench, which the r33 merge plan keeps ("the page bench keeps its three sources", r33 MERGE-ORDER §1 item 5), reads `/analytics/goals/:rid?status=active`.
  - That route returns `presentGoalRow(dbRow)`, which has no `unit` (f0b6a30f4 `goals.service.ts:277-293`, `:213-219`).
- **Result on the merged build:**
  - a money goal prints "60.0K" with no ₺;
  - a percent goal, stored as a share since #1488, prints "0.6";
  - a days goal prints "30".
- **Why the tests pass anyway.** The resolution's test fixture adds a `unit` that the gateway never sends (f0b6a30f4 `rp-registers-bench.test.ts:19-22`; the reader is `rp-registers-bench.tsx:103,147`).
- **Fix shape.** Attach `unit: GoalsService.SUPPORTED_METRICS[metric_key]?.unit ?? null` in `listGoals` (or `presentGoalRow`), so every goal route carries it. Change the fixture to the real gateway shape.
- **Open PR:** none. The resolve/ branches are not PRs, so whoever lands second inherits this.

### 3. Staff's first page is mostly refusals: the default sheet and layouts ignore the role

- **What staff see once the staff trains merge:**
  - `DEFAULT_ON` (`rp-sheet.ts:130-141`) lays down till, bench, pacing, quadrants and ledger. All five are `readers: ['owner','manager']` (06789c0bd `rp-registers-trade.tsx:170,336`, `rp-registers-house.tsx:46,193`; f0b6a30f4 `rp-registers-bench.tsx:107`).
  - So 5 of the 9 readable cuttings say "written for owners and managers", and goals is partly withheld too (#1498).
  - The house layout called "service" (`rp-sheet.ts:223`: reading, till, week, seats, service) is 3 of 5 refused for the role it is named for.
  - While arranging, "Add a cutting" and "Show instead" offer the held-back analyses with no mark (`ReportsNext.tsx` offSheet; `Cutting.tsx` swapOptions).
- **Fix shape.** Build `defaultSheet(role)` and layouts from cuttings the role can read. Mark held analyses as "(owners and managers)" in both pickers.
- **Open PR:** none.

### 4. "The reading" claims hourly freshness, never shows its age, and heads the page

- **What the page says:**
  - The window line reads "the stored insight feed, recomputed hourly" (`rp-registers-trade.tsx:49`).
  - The empty sentence says it "computes hourly and on demand" (`:72`).
- **What the code does:**
  - The default cadence is **daily at 06:00 on the server's clock** (`insights/insight-scheduler.service.ts:117` uses `now.getHours()`; `:126` defaults to daily at hour 6, and `:146` checks that hour), not the house's local hour.
  - The page never asks for `refresh=true` (path `:50`).
  - `analytics_insights.computed_at` exists (baseline migration :2194-2209), but `select` drops it (`rp-registers-trade.tsx:53-66`).
- **Why it matters.** The page's opening line is the top-scored stored sentence, verbatim (`ReportsNext.tsx:401`). It can be up to a day older than the live registers under it, with no date. r33's R-F3 ("0% of the way" against "$50.1K of $60K") is one case of this. #1114 fixes the goal sentence itself, but no other category is dated.
- **Fix shape.** Read `computed_at` and print "as of HH:MM" on the reading and the opening line. Change both sentences to the real cadence.
- **Open PR:** none (#1114 dates goal sentences only).

### 6. Two number forms on one page in a non-English house

- **What goes wrong.** After #1447 and #1518, money is in the house's form ("₺114.865,43", `rp-format.ts:81` at caae4f4dd). Every other figure stays English:
  - `figure`, `figureTo`, `countOf` (`:153`, `:165`, `:206`, through `lib/utils.ts:49` `toLocaleString('en-US')`);
  - `ratioPct` and `pct` (`:179`, `:197`, `toFixed` with a ".").
- **Result.** A Turkish reader sees "₺1.234" beside "2.667/day" and "59.0%". In the first, "." groups thousands. In the second, "." is a decimal point.
- **Open PR:** none on /reports. This is the same class as r33 w1 F4. It may overlap r34's "TRY number forms" lane, so check before building.

### 8. A house with nothing notable pays a full insight compute on every page load

- **Evidence.** A stored read returns only when `stored.read > 0` (`analytics.controller.ts:540`). Otherwise it runs a live `generate({persist:true})` (`:549-552`).

  - So each /reports load (staleTime 60 s) and the ⌘K feed rerun the whole bundle: whole-window consumption and checks (`insight-generator.service.ts:857`, `:888`).
- **Not measured.**
- **Open PR:** none.

### 9. A shared full-screen link opens nothing for a reader without that cutting

- **Open PR:** none.
