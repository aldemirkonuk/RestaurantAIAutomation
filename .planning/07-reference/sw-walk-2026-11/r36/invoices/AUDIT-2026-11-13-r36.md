## Ranked gaps (most harm to the goal first)

### R1. Stock that arrives without an invoice can never be costed, and FIFO sells it first, so cost of goods is withheld from a new house's first sale. NOT fixed.
- **Day 1.** The owner brings the shelf they already have. Stock enters through three paths:
  - add-to-inventory: `inventory.service.ts:1091-1103` on main;
  - re-activation: `:985-1000`;
  - bulk receive and menu scan: `:1456-1475`.
  
  In all three the cost is `resolveLotCost` (`:914-925`), which is null whenever no price was typed. `apply_stock_movement` books that lot as `cost_state = 'provisional'`, provenance `estimated`, `unit_cost NULL` (the newest definition is `#1380 20271002100000_…sql:109-113`).
- **No way out.** Only a delivery's lots are ever finalised (`finalise_delivery_cost` and `finishProvisional`, `delivery-stock.service.ts:792-826` at #1500). `revalue_lot` is called only by the /orders verify path (`procurement.service.ts:6138` on main). Nothing on web or gateway lets a person say what opening stock cost: there is no route, and no title among the 856 PRs.
- **Day 7.** The negative branch draws oldest first (`ORDER BY received_at ASC`, #949 `20270520100000_…sql:266-268`), so the first till sales take the uncosted opening lots. `pos_cost_of_goods` counts those bottles as uncosted, and #1036 withholds the figure: "cost known for N of M" (`cost-of-goods.ts:232-235`). Inventory value is also withheld (`analytics.service.ts:524-532` at #1036).
- **Goal harm:** the owner sees no cost of goods until all pre-Mudavym stock is sold, and with R2's window, for about a year after that.
- **Brief:** add `POST /inventory/:id/lots/state-cost` (owner or manager) that revalues the provisional, non-delivery lots of an item at a stated per-unit cost with provenance `manual`, writes the ledger, and settles prior draws the way #919 does. On web, put "What did these cost?" on the item sheet when it has uncosted non-delivery lots.

### R2. The cost of goods window is a fixed trailing 365 days, and nothing names which items hold it back. NOT fixed.
- **Where:**
  - `TILL_WINDOW_DAYS = 365` is hard-coded (`analytics.service.ts:506,513` at #1036).
  - `loadTill` passes only `p_since` (`:249-256`).
  - The SQL takes no end date (`20270705110000_what_sold_cost_is_its_draws.sql:69,84-85`).
- **Failure:**
  - Day 30: there is no "this month" and no "October". The owner cannot read food or beverage cost % for a period.
  - Withholding is sticky. One uncosted bottle sold in the last 365 days withholds the headline for a year. The causes include R1, R7, an unknown pack (r28 G1) and a rejected lot.
  - The payload carries per-item `uncosted_out`, but `TillFigures` keeps only counts (`cost-of-goods.ts` CogsCoverage). The page shows "N of M" with no list and no link to the deliveries or goods to fix (`rp-registers-house.tsx:158-215` at #1036).
- **Open PR:** none. The reports PRs (#1113, #1115, #1204) build on #1036 and keep the window.

### R3. A house that only scans invoices has no spend anywhere; the dashboard says 0. NOT fixed.
- **Where:**
  - Every spend reader reads `procurement_orders` only:
    - `dashboard.service.ts:839-880` on main (`getProcurementSpendSummary`);
    - `getStats`, `getCalendarRevenue` and `getSalesChart` (`:1052,:1216,:1339,:1601` at #1175);
    - `analytics.service.ts:215-237` at #1036 (`loadDeliveredOrders`: purchases, vendor concentration, the purchases goal).
  - Intake only *reads* orders (`document-intake.service.ts:844,2091` and `documents.controller.ts:1416` at #1500). A scanned paper with no PO never becomes spend.
  - The one reports read of `procurement_documents` is a count (`reports.service.ts:270-280`).
- **Failure:** In the founder's main flow (scan the paper, count at the door, verify), "Paid to vendors this month" reads 0 for an empty order list, not "unknown". That is absence read as health, and it contradicts the goal's "invoice scanning applying to costs".
- **Open PR:** none. #1175, #1326-#1328, #1353, #1354, #1173 and #1132 all harden the order-based figure. None of them reads papers. Title search for spend, purchase, paid and bought found none.

### R4. US weight and volume units (lb, oz, gal, fl oz, dozen) do not exist, so a US food invoice books the wrong quantity at the door. NOT fixed.
- **Where:**
  - `UOMS` holds metric units only (`document-types.ts:79-89`, `MASS_UOMS` `:105`, `GRAMS` kg/g/mg).
  - The reader is told uom is one of bottle…kg, g (`document-extractor.service.ts:112`). An unknown unit falls back to `each` (`:344-350`).
  - The door's plain-count rule books `each` into an item kept in g as that many grams (`delivery-stock.service.ts:171,206` at #1431). Door papers default to `bottle` (`door-count-paper.ts:119`, `door-count-lines.ts:59`, `document-intake.service.ts:218`).
  - Cost does refuse a printed glyph that is not `each` (`lot-unit-cost.ts:171-174`).
- **Failure:** "10 LB chicken" goes on the shelf as 10 g, and its lot stays provisional forever, which feeds R2. If the model writes `kg` for "LB", the booking is 10 kg against a real 4.54 kg, and costing is silently 2.2x wrong, with no refusal anywhere. The founder's market is US/SF and East Lansing.
- **Open PR:** none. No head's `document-types.ts` has a lb or pound case (grep over every PR that touches a unit file).
- **Brief:** add `lb`, `oz` (mass) and `gal`, `floz` (volume) to the line vocabulary, `normalizeUom`, `GRAMS` and `MILLILITRES`, and the line CHECK. Items stay metric (ADR 0070). A conversion that is not a whole number of grams or ml rounds to the item unit and says so. Do not refuse it, because 1 lb = 453.59237 g never comes out whole.

### R5. The glass-pour cost stamp cannot land as numbered. Merged together, it breaks the migration chain or is silently overwritten. NOT fixed.
- **Where:**
  - #1263 `20270811100000_a_glass_pour_carries_its_cost.sql:24-41` raises unless `record_glass_pour` from `one_pour_size_for_the_house` already exists.
  - #809 moved that migration to `20271113190000` today (a125d8fe6, "move the one-pour-size migration past every open version").
  - #1263's PR base is the non-PR branch `stack/glass-pours-meet-the-draws`. That branch still carries #809's old copy at `20270301180000`.
  - Probe: `fn.py` / `needs.py` over all 81 migration-touching PRs printed `1263 20270811100000 a_glass_pour_carries_its_cost NEEDS one_pour_size_for_the_house [('20271113190000', '809')]`.
- **Failure:**
  - A fresh reset with both merged dies on the guard.
  - A production push of #1263 before #809 dies too.
  - If both copies land, the later #809 body replaces #1263's `stamp_lot_draws` lines, glass pours go unstamped again, and every by-the-glass house's cost of goods is withheld for good (the defect #1263 exists to close).
  - The claim `A-GLASS-POUR-CARRIES-ITS-COST` globs for exactly one `*_one_pour_size_for_the_house.sql`, so with two copies it fails, and with one it does not check the order.
- **Fix:** renumber #1263 and #1264 past 20271113190000, retarget them onto #809 (or main), and drop the stack copy.

### R7. A foreign-currency invoice is never costed, and there is no way to give a rate. NOT fixed.
- **Where:** `paper-landed-cost.ts:215-230` at #1500: "no exchange rate is recorded, so a cost in EUR is not booked as TRY". No table, route or field records a rate, and no PR title matches exchange, fx or döviz. The landing page promises "Nothing is converted at a guessed rate" (`Landing.tsx:1029`), but offers no stated rate either.
- **Failure:** An Antalya house buying imported wine on a EUR-priced invoice keeps provisional lots forever, which feeds R2. An e-Fatura in a foreign currency prints its own TRY totals and rate, but the reader does not keep them.

### R8. The cost of shrinkage, waste and count corrections is not reported anywhere. NOT fixed.
- `pos_cost_of_goods` reads `source = 'pos'` sale and return rows only (`what_sold_cost_is_its_draws.sql:84-90`). The basis line says "Waste, breakage, count corrections and off-till sales are not in it" (`cost-of-goods.ts:259`).
- Count and waste movements do stamp draws, but no reader sums them. The page has no actual-versus-theoretical figure and no "what went missing cost". For "right math" at month end, the owner cannot see the gap between what was bought, what sold and what is left.

### R9. Door booking and verify posting are serial per line, which is slow on a real invoice. NOT fixed.
- **Door:** `bookAtTheDoor` awaits one `move` RPC per line (`delivery-stock.service.ts:577-601` at #1431). After that it runs a research-queue call and a close call per item.
- **Verify:** `finaliseAtVerified` awaits `post` per item (`:736-763` at #1500), and since #1461 `post` adds a second RPC, `stampTaxBasis` (`:925`).
- **Cost:** a 40-line wholesaler invoice is about 120 sequential round trips at the door and about 80 at verify. r28 G10 is the till import, not this path.

### R10. /receipts tells staff to "Agree and verify", and the page they land on then refuses them. NOT fixed.
- **Where:** `cost-next-step.ts:93-99` (#1416) has no role input. #1406 hides Agree and Verify from non-holders and says an owner or manager does them. The two PRs are on different stacks.
- **Failure:** A staff member follows a link to a dead end. The step should read "An owner or a manager agrees and verifies this delivery" for staff, with no link.

### R11. A vendor price rise is never surfaced. NOT fixed.
- `market-price.producer.ts` only says "selling below its 30-day average" (drops). No producer compares a newly verified invoice price with the last price of the same good, and none names the dishes or drinks whose cost moved.
- For "applying to costs" this is the one signal an owner acts on. Low effort, because `vendor-comparison.service.ts` already reads `procurement_documents`.

### R12. Analytics inventory and lot reads are unranged (the 1000-row cap). Already in QUEUE:635, still open.
- `analytics.service.ts:139-166` at #1036 reads `restaurant_inventory` and `inventory_lot_rollup` with no range.
- Past 1,000 goods (foods included), items lose their lot cost and inventory value is withheld under the wrong reason. #1007 left ">1000-row paging" open.
