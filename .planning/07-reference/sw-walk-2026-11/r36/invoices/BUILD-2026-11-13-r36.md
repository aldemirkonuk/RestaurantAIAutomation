## Decisions (the session's call; each is recorded in its PR body)

**R4 units (#1549, #1550)**
- A US line rounds only into g, mg or ml. Into kg or l it is refused unless the result is whole. A metric line is never rounded.
- The paper's listing is rounded the same way the door books it.
- `oz` is a weight and `floz` is a fluid ounce. `gal` is the US gallon.
- The stored-row repair (`withStatedUnit`) applies only to weight or volume glyphs.

**R10 (#1555)**
- The page's role rule is mirrored, admin included, instead of importing #1406's `settlesDeliveryMoney` across stacks.

**R1 (#1574)**
- It restates only lots with no delivery, no order and no settled cost. A final cost is never corrected here.
- Earlier draws are settled in the same transaction.
- A cost of 0 is refused.
- The route is item-scoped: `POST /inventory/:restaurantId/item/:itemId/opening-cost`, owner or manager only.
- Rejected: restating every provisional lot (a guess would overwrite a delivery's estimate); restating the lots only (the earlier sales would stay uncosted).
