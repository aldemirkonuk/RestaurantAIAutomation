## Fix made in the walk
**#1342 `fix/settings-fits-a-phone` — bdbc98eae (pushed, still a DRAFT).**
- The bug: /settings was still 756px wide at 375px. To find why, I hid the page's elements one at a time while watching `scrollWidth`. The cause is the vendor-terms table header in `VendorTermsSection.tsx`. Its "Record terms" screen-reader label is `position:absolute`, and the table's `overflow-x:auto` wrapper was not positioned. The label was therefore placed against an ancestor outside the scroller, ended up at x=756, and widened the page. None of the rows the lane fixed were the cause.
- The fix: the scroller is now `position: relative`. A new test in `SettingsNext.test.tsx` ("fits a phone" block) pins the wrapper's overflow and position.
- Evidence:
  - The test passes: 5 passed, 62 skipped.
  - Mutant (dropping `position: 'relative'`): killed, 1 failed.
  - After the rebuild, /settings at 375 measures scrollWidth 375, with 0 rows, 0 controls and 0 selects past the edge.
- The session's call: fix it where the label's containing block is wrong, on the lane PR that claims the 375 fix. Rejected alternatives:
  - Removing the label: it would cost screen-reader users the column name.
  - `overflow:hidden` on the page: it would hide the problem, not fix it.
  - A new PR: #1342 is the open DRAFT that owns this check.
