# r26 staff-cost — BUILD 2026-11-13

Lane: B1 from r24/WALK-2026-11-13-r24.md. Staff were reading the inventory "Value at cost" column and the header total, and `GET /inventory/:rid` was returning `wac` / `unitCost` to them.

## Audit (evidence)

- **Web (on #1233).** These all showed cost to every role:
  - `useInventoryNextData.ts` `readSentence` ("At cost the cellar holds …");
  - `InventoryTable.tsx` "Value at cost" column, and `costCell` (a food or supply's cost a unit in "Your price");
  - `InventoryNext.tsx` sort option "Value at cost", "Export valuation", and the count-sheet WAC and Value columns.

  Only the drawer was gated (`ItemCards.tsx:194`, "Not shown").
- **Gateway (main).**
  - `inventory.controller.ts` has no role on any read.
  - `mapInventoryItem` spreads the raw `restaurant_inventory` row, so `last_purchase_price`, `negotiated_price`, `target_price`, `max_price`, `custom_price`, `margin_percentage`, `markup_ratio`, `markup_classification` and `total_revenue` reach staff, plus `wac` and `locations[].wac`. #1007 adds `unitCost` and `costBasis`.
  - `inventory-ledger` transactions carry `unitCost` and `totalCost`.
  - Auction lots carry hammer price, premium, rate and unit costs.
- **Already gated, not touched:**
  - `item-detail.controller.ts` on the #860 chain (`moneyView`);
  - `/dashboard` (`amounts-for-role.ts`);
  - recipes and dishes (`seesHouseAmounts`).

## Calls (the session's, under the /goal)

1. **Gateway: one interceptor per controller** (`WithholdCostFromStaffInterceptor` on `InventoryController` and `InventoryLedgerController`). It sets cost fields to null at any depth, keeps the keys, and leaves an owner's or manager's body untouched.
   - Rejected: per-route wrapping (more lines in files owned by #809 and #1024, and a new route can forget it).
   - Rejected: stripping in the service (it has no role, and internal callers need the real cost).
2. **Role test: `seesHouseAmounts(user.role)`.** `user.role` is the house role from the access row; admin reads as owner, an unknown role as staff.
   - Rejected: a per-request `readRestaurantRole` database read.
3. **Selling prices stay for staff.** They sell at them, the list already showed them to staff, and DASH-G6 points toward waiters seeing invoices.
   - Rejected: hiding every price, as `/dishes` does. This leaves a cross-lane inconsistency, noted below.
4. **No `costReadable:false` for staff.** That field means a read failed.
5. **Web drops the Value column for staff** instead of filling it with "Not shown". The drawer drops its value card the same way. The read sentence says nothing about cost, because "no cost on the books" would be false.
6. **Gateway PR based on main, not stacked on #809.** Its controller hunk is disjoint and it carries an unrenumbered migration. Trial merges are clean apart from a `sql_outside_migrations.txt` conflict those branches already have with main.
7. **Legacy page:** a withheld auction-lot price is left out rather than printed as "null".
