## The session's calls (founder delegated forks under the 2026-11-07 /goal)

1. **A printed line rate is trusted only through the printed tax total.** Rejected: trusting the rate as read, because one misread digit would move cost silently. Also rejected: a looser tolerance than landedCost's, which would let two checks disagree on one paper.
2. **A multi-rate paper with a charge or discount is refused** and falls back to the single-rate path with the refusal sentence appended. Rejected: spreading a charge pro rata across rates. Turkish practice and UBL put a charge at a rate the paper often does not print, so we would be guessing tax.
3. **Without printed bases, "tax inside" holds only when the inside reading ties and the before-tax reading does not.** Rejected: defaulting to inside for a receipt-shaped paper. That is the exact guess the ADR 0359 C3 lane removed.
4. **The rate field lives on the parsed line and is split into its own PR** (#1501 reader, #1507 e-Fatura). The cost side (#1500) reads the snapshot. This was split because the combined diff was 36.9K, over the 30K wall. Rejected: a new column. The snapshot already carries the lines, so no migration is needed.
5. **The door hint shows whenever a booking exists, failed bookings included.** A failed booking still records the count. Rejected: showing the hint only on success, because it hid the replace-not-add rule exactly when the house was confused.
