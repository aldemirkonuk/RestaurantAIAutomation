### G10: choosing a menu can take the drinks it leaves out off sale (#1494, #1497)

- **Gateway.** `make-current` accepts an optional `takeOff: string[]`.
  - Every id must be a `notOnThisFile` entry of the plan that the house holds. Any other id gets a 400 before the switch.
  - After the carry, the named drinks' bottle and glass prices are cleared through the one writer, `set_house_menu_prices`. The write uses source `manual`, is dated by the choice, names the menu, and gives the reason "taken off sale: not on the menu made current". If the batch writer is missing, it falls back to one write per row.
  - A lock still holds (L16). A price set later still stands (L14).
  - The reply carries `takenOff[]`.
  - `notOnThisFile` no longer lists a good the new menu carries under another line.
- **Web.** On the plan, an owner or manager gets a tick per dropped drink. Staff see no tick.
  - The plan sentence counts what is taken off and what keeps its price.
  - After the choice, MenuVersions prints `takenOffSentence` at both call sites.
- **Decision (the session's call).** Clear the price only; the row, the stock and the kind stay.
  - Rejected: deactivating the good, which loses its stock and history.
  - Rejected: an on-menu flag, which needs a migration and a new meaning.
  - Rejected: retiring the drink automatically, which breaks L12 ("silence changes nothing").

### G21: a reload loses "this line fits two of your goods" (#1506)

- The plan matches unlinked lines of a non-current menu against the house's live goods. It runs `matchHouseGoods` on the plan's own inventory rows, so there is no extra read.
  - It returns `houseCandidates` per line.
  - The candidates go into the fingerprint (`fits`). A good added or removed means a different plan, and make-current answers 409.
- **Web.** A `PlanFits` block says "<line> fits N of the house's goods." / "<line> fits the house's <good> (<kind>)."
  - Managers get "It is <good> (<kind>)" buttons, which call the r28 link route and then re-read the plan.
  - Staff see chips.
  - The plan sentence names how many lines are carried as a good of their own.
- **Decision.** Re-match each time the plan is read.
  - Rejected: storing the read's candidates, which needs a migration and goes stale as goods change.
  - Rejected: linking automatically at make-current, which would guess between two goods.

### G14: staff could change the current menu and nobody on staff could undo it (#1517)

- `POST /menus/items` on the current menu is owner/manager only, priced or not. A kept, non-current menu stays open to every member (the D2 review step).
- `PATCH /menus/items/:id`:
  - The controller resolves the role for every field.
  - For anyone else, the service reads the line's menu, scoped to the house, before writing anything. On the current menu it answers 403. On a kept menu the line is corrected and the house's good is not renamed.
  - A failed menu read is said, and nothing is changed.
- **Web (`/menu`).** Staff get no add form and see a note instead. The empty-menu text drops "add the first line above" for staff.
- **Decision.** Gate the act.
  - Rejected: letting staff discard their own adds, which needs `added_by` and a migration and still misses the rename.
  - Rejected: a suggestion queue, a new flow nobody asked for.
  - A role that cannot be read counts as not a manager, as the priced-add gate already does.
