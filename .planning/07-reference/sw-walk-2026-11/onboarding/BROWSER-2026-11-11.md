## /onboarding/progress 404

- **Why.** `MenusService.getOnboardingProgress` throws NotFound when there is no `user_onboarding_progress` row.
  - Rows are inserted only by `registerRestaurant` and `register-with-house`.
  - **No insert happens at invite acceptance**, so every invited member gets a 404, as does every seeded local user.
- **What breaks.**
  - Nothing crashes: the web maps 404 to null.
  - SetupNudgeBanner is hidden for those users.
  - VerifyEmail sends an in-house session with no row to /get-started, whose entry rule then routes it on.
  - The cost is console noise and a nudge that never shows for invitees.
- **Not changed.** The GET semantics belong to #848's lane. The fix would be an insert at invite accept, or a GET that answers an all-false row.
