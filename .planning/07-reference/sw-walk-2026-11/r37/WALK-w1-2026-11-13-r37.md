## Findings and fix briefs

- **M-F1 (#1506). The two "fits" buttons are identical.**
  - Problem: "It is House White (wine)" ×2, both with aria-label "Link House White to House White". A manager linking a price to one of two same-named goods cannot tell which is which. That choice is the point of G21.
  - Gateway brief: `houseCandidates[]` (plan and read) gains what tells twins apart, all already on the rows the plan reads (`byInventory`):
    - `bottleSizeMl`
    - the house bottle/glass price
    - on-hand quantity
    - `linked: 'library' | 'catalogue' | 'by hand'` from `identity_provenance`
  - Web brief: `PlanFits` buttons read "It is House White (wine, 750 ml, $30.00, 4 on hand)". The aria-label carries the same detail. When even that is identical, add "(added by hand)" / "(from the wine library)".
  - Specs: two goods whose names fold equal get different button names. Mutation-test the detail builder.
- **M-F2 (#1497). The plan sentence counts a part-locked drink as "taken off sale, its price cleared".**
  - Problem: Chianti's bottle is locked. The result says correctly "glass cleared, bottle held", but the plan sentence before the choice says "its price cleared".
  - Brief: in MenuPlan's sentence, when a ticked drink has a locked kind, say "1 is taken off sale: its glass price is cleared, its bottle price stays locked" (by kind, from `n.locked`). Add a test next to `MenuPlan.test.tsx:223`.
- **M-F3 (pre-existing, menus CSV). A header "Glass price" / "Bottle price" (with a space) is not read.**
  - Problem: `csv-parser.service.ts` HEADER_MAP keys only `glass_price` / `bottle_price`. A CSV with the common spaced headers imports every line with no price, and the import does not warn.
  - Brief: normalise headers by lower-case, trim, and turning spaces and dashes into `_` before the lookup. Add a spec: "Bottle Price" and "Glass price" are read. Check open PRs touching `csv-parser.service.ts` first.
- **M-F4 (pre-existing, MenuVersions). Rows of menus read on the same day share one accessible name.**
  - Problem: "Make the menu read 2026-11-14 current" appears ×2.
  - Brief: add the line count or the read time to the aria-label.
- **R-F11 (still open from r34, now also #1512).** /dishes at 375 keeps the open sheet in the table's scroll box, and the menu-price offer is clipped. Owner: the /dishes page (#1400/#1420 chain). The brief is r34 F11's: render the open sheet below the table under 600 px, or make the cell sticky at the wrapper's width.
