## What was built

  - `insight-verbalizer.ts` prints the house mark: ₺181, £1.0k, CHF 181. A USD house is unchanged.

  - The generator reads `restaurants.currency` per `generate()` call through the bundle, never through singleton state. It re-renders each record in that currency and stamps `evidence.currency`.

## Decisions (the session's call; full text in the PR bodies)
1. **Stored `$` rows are recomputed through the version bump, not re-rendered on read.**
   - Re-rendering on read would mean two sources of truth, with no stored template and six readers depending on a guess.
2. **A later currency change is caught on read through the `evidence.currency` stamp, not invalidated on write.**
   - `restaurants.currency` has several writers, and invalidating on write would add a settings-to-analytics dependency.
3. **Version 17, not 14.** The sim branches hold 16, and a gap is better than a collision.
4. **The currency is passed per call and never held on `this`.**
5. **Unreadable is not "not stated".** Money sentences are withheld rather than printed bare.
6. **Cocktails is scoped to /cocktails only.** The shared cellar `money()` has 37 call sites across 9 files, all owned by other stacks.
