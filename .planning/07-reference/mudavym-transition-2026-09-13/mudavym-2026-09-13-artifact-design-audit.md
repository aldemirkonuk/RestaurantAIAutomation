## Product language established by the collection

Mudavym presents a restaurant as a house whose records can be read, questioned and signed. The ledger is functional: a figure opens to its sources, a delivery is separate from an invoice, a vendor promise is separate from recovered credit, and an AI draft is separate from a sent message. The recurring idea is provenance at the moment a person makes a decision.

Paper and warm charcoal are complementary grounds, with teal/Iznik acting as the seal/accent. Serif display typography and restrained monospaced metadata provide character; rules, quiet spacing and rows provide density. Error, unknown and loading states should not masquerade as healthy zero-valued metrics. Icons and words carry meaning independently of color.

Motion reinforces a state change. A row can settle, an unknown value remains an em dash, and approval culminates in the mark. The canvas is exploratory: its many candidate gestures do not all belong in the application. Later go-live forks explicitly ration the seal, reduce travel, limit shimmer and address keyboard/reduced-motion behavior. The old 150px swipe hero and the later proposed 96px implementation are separate dated designs. Paper and charcoal are historical complementary palette grounds; current-main PageGate and DashboardNext explicitly force Warm Charcoal for the new app pages, while legacy pages retain user theme behavior.

The identity artifact says the previous bowtie-like mark was withdrawn and a raster slab monogram selected, with variants still unspecified in that old artifact. Later ADR 0047 supersedes that gap: the founder selected the trued A+M interlock, the wordmark Mudavym. and the locked Iznik palette; its status records PR #172 merged and production verified on August 31. Geometry is seven polygons plus four counter ticks, a 24px minimum, with ticks reading from 32px; smaller use takes the tile treatment. The warm candidate exploration remained open, without replacing the selected mark. The ADR's old body still says main held, but its later dated status and review trail record that gate opening. A current physical/device small-size pass was not repeated here.

## Arrival: what was preferred and what was unresolved

Direction A, First evidence, derives configuration from the first invoice or menu instead of asking everything up front. The saved note asks for simpler registration with explicit open/join choices and a flyleaf visual, broadens the fallback from a wine list to a full menu, and retains later evidence/review screens.

Direction B, The interview, turns a conversation into structured configuration rows beside it. The saved note shows limited enthusiasm. Direction C, The house's book, is stronger: the flyleaf, contents and readable staged setup were liked, with requests for simplicity, carefully rendered rules and distinctive motion. The verification book remained visually promising but unresolved.

Direction D, The invitation, resolves the house and role before registration; specific mobile and arrival frames were retained in the notes. Direction E, Set the table, shows all configuration as one organized spread; its voice-led arrangement was liked. Later ADR 0143 records the narrower decision: on-device speech, retain structured rows only, and only AI-proposed values wait for the seal. Typed human values do not need the same ceremony.

All five sketches identify real gaps: recording offered-and-skipped versus never-asked states, batch proposal/application receipts, reliable inference and signup routes. A visual microphone is not evidence of local-only recognition. MDN's [SpeechRecognition documentation](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/SpeechRecognition) explicitly notes that some implementations send audio to a remote service; support for local processing must be verified and enforced before making the privacy promise.

## Overlays and document architecture

The census gives objects a right-hand sheet, questions a centered panel, and choices a popover. It prevents a seal from sitting in a popover; a choice may open the panel where the consequential action is reviewed. Width, focus, Escape, dirty state, dismissal, denied state, ground propagation and stacked surfaces form a shared component contract. Merely replacing modal CSS misses that contract.

The collection distinguishes a document reading surface from reports/analytics, receipt settlement and communications. The Sorting Office groups countable records into drawers and borrows the Reading Desk's detail pane. File-to and cross-filed provenance avoid silent duplicate ownership. The Signal Press is an alternative that summarizes important events while retaining the full feed; it is not proof that generated reports are already produced by a worker.

The Go-Live Board reports 14 unanswered forks, some described as answered by a build. Those include sidebar migration, motion behavior when reduced motion is requested, wax rationing, rejected orders, swipe travel, wide sheet exceptions, dead consent controls and guards that actually cover the relevant paths. Latest handoff still lists motion/overlay ports as outstanding. Do not treat the board's branch-local LANDED labels as proof those ports are on current main.

## Simulation evidence and its practical value

The first simulated restaurant exercise used 53 inventory items,135 POS buttons and44 closed checks. It demonstrated double-close refusal and identified breaks across identity, volume, price, tenant scope, valuation, notification delivery and analytics. These numbers are historical fixtures, not current account totals or real customer usage.

The second venue tested lira and a broader drinks/menu catalogue. It exposed currency presentation, food misclassification, generic product identity and inbox scope errors. Subsequent sections describe fixes and rechecks; the old findings list is not a current bug backlog.

The document/delivery scenario used explicitly synthetic invoice fixtures, including Turkish taxes/deposits, California freight/CRV/sales tax, and a money-free delivery note. The gallery states extraction was supplied through a guarded route because the gateway model had no credit; it does not prove the production model itself parsed those PDFs. Later receiving tests record stock at the door and cost at verified agreement, keeping unknown cost provisional. Those distinctions should become acceptance scenarios for future changes.
