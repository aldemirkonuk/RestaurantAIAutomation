## 101–103: the contract includes the failure and exit

101's strongest contribution is the amendment to its own idea. A single “done” check is false when a multi-service apply partially fails; a stated value cannot quietly join an assistant batch; and a proposed value retains its provenance after acceptance. This directly requires displaying **the target, current value/source, proposed value/source, per-row outcome, and undo deadline**. Two vendors' identical field names must remain distinguishable. A menu row must read as a menu item, not a UUID label.

The current Arrival build also distinguishes **unconfirmed** writes when a call may have reached its owner but the final receipt could not be established. That is an implementation honesty refinement, not a claim from sketch 101. Retrying blindly would be less safe than saying the outcome is unknown. The receipt and audit must survive reload; process-local arrays are not the book's memory.

102 and 103 add concrete acceptance criteria:

- A sheet preserves the source list's context. A dirty close must state whether words survive, and resuming a stub must restore the right draft. The founder's 103 amendment makes the engine's draft grey under the field; accepting it does not erase its attribution.
- A spindle is a named navigation chain, capped at **three** with breadcrumb; the phone uses detents. The founder's canvas amendment says an outside click collapses the spindle. That gesture must be reconciled with retained draft state; “close” cannot silently destroy it.
- A panel's accessible name is the contract: what it asks, writes, and what leaving costs. The hold reads back what was bound, not merely a celebratory stamp.
- A peek remains non-modal until promoted. Space, arrows, Enter and Escape have defined jobs. A non-modal helper is not a place to hide a form or commitment.
- Proposed changes stay distinct from recorded ones; ticks choose, the seal commits. A queue state is not a server acknowledgement, and an acknowledgement is not the final sealed record.
- Advanced ceremonies must be tied to current authority and backend support. The old passcode demo is explicitly permissive example data. Its presence does not prove current passkey enrollment, dual authority or tamper-evident ledger availability.

The census count merits precision. Its prose says 141 scanned JSX sites folded into 117 overlays with additions, but also reports 121 rows. The acquired JSON has **143 entries**: built 32, no-shape 3, retire 42, owed 12, delete 15, migrate 10, target 7, pattern 22. Excluding the 22 research patterns gives **121**. Use those labels together; do not call 143 shipped overlays, or infer an implementation count from an old screenshot.

**103 export limitation.** `canvas.dc.html:6` requests `./support.js`; `:722` contains `<script type="text/x-dc" data-dc-script ...>` and host props. The acquired 103 directory has only `README.md` and `canvas.dc.html`; no support script or documented independent bootstrap was found. README says the founder's own copy lives outside the repo but gives **no artifact URL**. Root's raw `{{ clock }}`/`{{ pHint }}` observation is therefore consistent with missing host runtime. The source is usable as a contract and layout reference; interactive validation requires its real host, not an invented bootstrap.

Speech needs a technical correction to the historical rationale without changing the founder's privacy decision.
