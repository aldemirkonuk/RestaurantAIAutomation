## §0 What it is

Floor Checker tells a manager, live, which tables have been checked on and which are
waiting too long. It lets a waiter tap a table when they visit it and get a buzz on
their own phone when the food for that table is up. Today it is a separate app (Next.js,
its own Supabase, its own Vercel project). This note is the plan to rebuild it inside
Mudavym, with Mudavym's look, its sign-in, /team's staff and the till's tables, and then
switch the old app off.

### §1b The fourteen picked features

Numbered in the rulings record's order, as ADR 0345 numbers them. Every "pick N" in this
note uses these numbers. The labels are the record's, unquoted there.

1. **Paid tap.** The waiter taps Paid; the table stops nagging; a guest who stays on is not counted against the waiter. **PR3** (state), **PR8** (endpoint), **PR11** (button), **PR10** ("paid 12 min ago", manager only).
2. **Seating timeline.** Seated, first visit, paid, left; the shift report splits each turn into time to greet, time to pay and time from paying to leaving, by section. **PR13**.
3. **On-break handoff.** "On break" passes my tables and alerts to the cover until "Back": the cover on `floor_table_assignments` (PR3). After Back, a manager records the shift's closed breaks, in one tap in a Turkish house, as ADR 0345 D1 sets it out (`0345:278`, `:282`), through /team's own `PATCH shifts/:shiftId` (`apps/api-gateway/src/team/team.controller.ts:252-253`; `updateShift`, `schedule.service.ts:711`), which writes `recorded_break_min` and the re-priced `labor_cost` in one UPDATE (`:771`, `:820`, `:835-841`; `supabase/migrations/20261201110100_a_shift_over_four_hours_has_a_break.sql:13-15`). The tap is the pick's option text, "The manager can record the break on the shift in one tap" (`0345:282`). That it is the one path from a punch to `labor_cost` is the executor's call under the founder's 2026-10-31T16:57:09Z delegation (OD-235, §0; `0345:281`); Clock in (PR5b) and PR9a's Call out and Offer cover, which /floor also offers, go through /team's own routes, which price a shift as /team does. **PR8**, **PR9**, **PR10** and **PR11**.
4. **Reassignment proposal.** When a waiter leaves, calls out or runs late, the board proposes who takes which tables. Nothing moves until the manager taps Apply. **PR2** (a pure function) and **PR10**.
5. **Alerts always reach someone.** The research describes it (evidence §2 item 3; ADR 0345 D2, `0345:300-310`, replaces this description where they differ): a Food-up or late alert goes to the table's waiter, else to whoever is covering, else to a working manager, and the manager sees who it reached. **PR9** builds the chain as ADR 0345 D2 decides it, the executor's call under the founder's 2026-10-31T16:57:09Z delegation (OD-236, §0, `0345:296-322`), and **PR9a** adds its no-show test. The pick is designed to be met in full once PR9, PR9a, PR10 and PR11 are built, on D3's last-step row (`0345:327`) and PR10's manager and PR11's staff push opt-ins; until PR11 the waiter and cover steps reach nobody, so every alert goes to the manager step. [2026-10-31: the OD-238 judge round and judge round 3 matched this pick's "in full", its last step's trigger and its all-Away branch to D2 and D3, and added PR9a and PR11.]
6. **No-show + forgotten clock-out.** A shift that has started with no punch shows Call out and Offer cover, which run /team's own routes (`apps/api-gateway/src/team/team.controller.ts:271`, `:281`, `:291`).
