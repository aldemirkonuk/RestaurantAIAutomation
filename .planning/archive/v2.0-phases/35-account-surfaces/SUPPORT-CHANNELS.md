# Support Channel Taxonomy (Phase 35 backlog)

Out of scope for Phase 35 implementation beyond P0. Each future channel gets its own classification when promoted — different IA per channel.

| Tier | Channel | Importance | Classification notes |
|------|---------|------------|----------------------|
| P0 | Email | Critical | Direct 1:1; SLA-friendly; shipped on Help |
| P0 | Slack | Critical | Real-time team support; invite URL env; shipped on Help |
| P1 | In-app docs / Notion KB | High | Searchable product help; per-surface deep links |
| P2 | Ticket form | Medium | Needs triage queue + status; not a chat widget |
| P2 | Intercom / Crisp | Medium | Live chat; requires agent coverage hours |
| P3 | Discord | Low | Community, not support SLA |
| P3 | Status page | Low | Incident comms; separate from Help IA |
| P3 | WhatsApp Business | Low | Regional preference; compliance-heavy |

Do not merge into one mega-widget. Promote via `/gsd-review-backlog` when ready.
