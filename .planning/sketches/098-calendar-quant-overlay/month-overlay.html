What it may do alone

Create, move, resize or annotate an entry · set or clear a reminder · write a note onto a day · exclude a day from the baselines · draft an in-house notification. All of it reversible, all of it inside the house, every one written to the settings ledger with the utterance that asked for it.

What it may never do alone

Anything that leaves the house: mail a vendor, place or amend an order, push an entry to a connected external calendar that other people read, invite an outside attendee. Each of those is a proposal with a hold-to-approve seal on it — the same gate the procurement and communications families already pass through.
