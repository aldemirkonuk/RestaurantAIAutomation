The strip, and how it is worked

click a day → the book below becomes that day
click it again or Esc → back to everything standing
shift-click → a range, Fri to Sun
← → move a day · ↑ ↓ a week
hover an entry → its hairline lights on the strip
double-click a day → take it out of the analysis

The last one is the point of drawing days at all: the exclusion store already exists, and today it is a list in a side panel. On a strip it is the object itself — you rule out the Tuesday you were shut by striking the Tuesday.

What is drawn here that is not built

proposed The per-day money. The gateway returns each entry’s money inside its sentence, not as a field, so a strip that totals dollars per day needs a structured moneyAtStake on the recommendation. Nothing else on this page would change.
proposed Falls due. Snooze wake dates exist (snooze_until); goal deadlines exist on the goals endpoint; vendor cutoffs do not exist anywhere. The strip can draw the first two today and must draw nothing for the third.
proposed The hairline. It needs firstSeenAt, which the feed already attaches — but capped at 40 keys, and null for any rule with no impression row. An entry with no first-fired date draws no hairline and says so; it must never draw one starting today.
