Change a rule

nothing behind this yet

The founder’s fifth heading, and the one this page cannot fill. No rule can be tuned from anywhere in the product: the thresholds are constants in recommendations.service.ts, and the only feedback the engine takes from a manager is a dismissal — which silences a finding rather than moving a threshold. Drawn dark here rather than left out, because the absence is the interesting part: the refusals this page already collects (“not relevant” × 6 on one rule this month) are exactly the evidence a tuning surface would run on.

Not built: no rule store, no threshold history, no proposal-and-seal. DESIGN-FOUNDATION §6 calls this “the refusal is as valuable as the act” and rates it need-it-now.
Where the act and the hand disagree

Entry 07 — the Wednesday shortfall. Its act is brief the floor; its hand today is /reports, because the mapping in rec-format.ts keys on the rule’s category (sales → Reports), not on what a person physically does. The docket needs its own act mapping — nine rules, one word each — and it is the only thing this direction needs that does not exist. Everything else here is a regrouping of fields the feed already returns.

Entry 09 — the pairing. Filed under price it because a menu insert is a pricing decision, though its hand is Promotions. Judgement, stated, rather than a rule.

proposed The money per section. Seven of nine rules state a figure inside their sentence; two state none and show an em dash rather than a zero.
