Direction 084 · brand · OD-106 document-only

Mudavym. Anatolian

The name is Turkish, so the house is too. İznik glaze and tile geometry doing structural work — grid, seam, data, loading — never wallpaper. Brass and copper from the working kitchen. The generosity of a laid table, rendered with software precision.

§01Premise — the müdavim's own home

Rooted, not decorated

Every other direction dresses a Turkish name in international clothes. This one goes home. The reference is a great İstanbul meyhane or a Gaziantep kitchen: abundance, craft, warmth, and a wall of tiles that is load-bearing — it is how the room is built, not what was hung on it. Ornament is the failure mode. Geometry is the system.

star-and-cross · the layout grid, the divider, the loader, the empty state
müdavim(Tur., from Ar. dāʾim, “constant”) — the regular. The one who returns. The house knows the table, the bottle, the hour. Mudavym treats the operator as its own müdavim: it learns the house, and has the tray ready before you ask.
Structure before ornamentThe star-and-cross tiling sets the bento (one star tile, three cross tiles), the 8px module, the seam-as-divider, and the tile-wall data bar. Delete the decoration and the layout still stands — that is the test.
Glaze is the materialDepth comes from three optical layers — meniscus highlight, body gradient, pooled bottom edge — not from drop shadows. Two hairlines and one gradient per surface. Never neumorphism.
The tray, never the plateTurkish hospitality is generous and never imposing. Every AI proposal arrives the way a meze tray arrives: everything offered, nothing forced, one gesture to take it. That is the whole interaction model.
§02Logo — three resolved marks

Marks built on a grid, not drawn by hand

All three come out of the same construction: an eight-point star from two overlapping squares (outer radius R, inner radius R·cos45°/cos22.5° = 0.7654R), and a square tile split by a seam. Each is shown light, dark, and at real favicon sizes — a mark that dies at 16px is not a mark.

A · Mühür — the eight-point sealThe formal mark. Documents, contracts, the sign-off.
Construction Two squares overlapped at 45°, R = 26u, inner r = 19.9u. The eight inner vertices are filled solid --glaze; the star outline stays open in --ink-1 at 1.7u — a glazed octagon core inside a bisque star. Wordmark starts at 3R, cap-height optically matched to the star's inner octagon. Clear space = one tile module (8px) on all sides. Minimum size 16px: the outline thickens to 4.4u so the octagon still reads; below 16px use the octagon alone.
B · Dört — four tiles, one seamThe app icon. Recommended primary.
Construction A 64u square split by a 4u seam cross into four 30u tiles, corner radius 2.5u. Three tiles carry the house's work at three glaze depths — --slip-2 bisque (queued), --glaze-lit (in hand), --glaze-deep (done) — and the fourth is --bolus: the one thing waiting on a person. It is the product's model, drawn. The bolus quadrant is a live surface: it goes bisque when nothing needs you, so the icon itself is the badge. Survives 16px because it is four rectangles and no strokes but one.
C · Sofra — the set tableThe hospitality lockup. Signature, invoice footer, front door.
Construction Wordmark centred; beneath it a --brass rule at 1.6u with 9u end-ticks — the table's edge, drawn the way a sini tray sits on a stand. Centred on the rule, an octagon (r = 15u, the star's inner form) knocked out of the rule with a 3u slip-coloured keyline so the tray reads in front. The --bolus dot at centre is the one dish meant for you. At 16px the wordmark drops and the tray-on-rule alone carries — the only concept whose small form is a different composition, stated rather than hidden.
§03Slogans — eight, English, one Turkish idea each

What the house says at the door

Structure borrowed from the balanced-antithesis pattern: a duty and a discipline in one breath. None of them says “AI”, “platform”, or “powered by”. Every one of them could be said out loud in a dining room.

01

Mudavym. The house knows you're coming.

The literal definition of müdavim, turned toward software: the system has prepared before you arrive — drafts written, counts done, tray ready.

02

Hospitality is a duty. So is the count.

In Turkish culture hosting is an obligation, not a mood. Pairing it with the count says warmth and rigour are the same commitment — the whole mandate in six words.

03

Set the table before the guests arrive.

Names the product's actual behaviour — proactive drafting, reordering, scheduling — in the one image every restaurateur already lives by.

04

Nothing is asked of the guest. Everything is asked of the house.

The service posture, stated as a design constraint: the software carries the burden; the operator only decides. It also reads as an engineering promise.

05

Bring the tray. Never push the plate.

The meze-tray model of AI: offer everything, force nothing, one gesture to take it. It is also the literal product rule — Mudavym drafts vendor mail and never sends it unapproved.

06

Made of the same clay as the kitchen.

Material kinship instead of a benefit claim — brass, copper, fired clay. Signals “built by people who work a service”, and quietly disqualifies generic SaaS.

07

Every tile counted. Every guest welcomed.

The two halves of the brand in the tile grammar: inventory precision on the left, hospitality on the right, one seam between them.

08

A regular is not a report.

Anti-dashboard line for the sales page. The relationships — vendors, staff, the Thursday regulars — are the asset; metrics only describe them.

§04Voice — the host's grammar

Generous in the noun, exact in the number

A host is warm without being familiar and precise without being cold. Eight rules; then the five house strings rewritten.

  1. Offer, never impose.Every AI action is a tray. “Ready”, “drafted”, “waiting for you” — never “we've sent”, “confirm now”, or a countdown.
  2. The house is “we”, the müdavim is “you”.“We drafted it. You decide.” The work is always ours; the decision is always theirs. Never “let's” — that blurs who is responsible.
  3. Name the thing, not the record.“Rioja”, not “inventory item”. “Marta”, not “vendor contact”. Specificity is hospitality; category nouns are how software forgets people.
  4. Warmth in the noun, precision in the number.Adjectives are banned. Warmth comes from concrete nouns — table, door, morning, tray — and from numbers being exactly right, with their units.
  5. Empty is a sentence, then a door.Never a shrug. Say plainly what is not there, then offer exactly one way in. Never render a zero we did not measure.
  6. No exclamation, no applause.A good service is normal. The house does not congratulate itself or the user. Warmth arrives quoted — from Marta, from the floor — never self-declared.
  7. One Turkish word, earned.Müdavim appears where it explains itself — onboarding, the about page, the empty state that welcomes you back — and nowhere else. No decorative Turkish, ever.
  8. Verdict first, arithmetic after.The sentence that decides something is set in the display face and comes first. The working — quantities, prices, deltas — follows in mono, always available, never in the way.

§05Palette — glaze on slip

Turquoise, bole red, brass — no startup hues

Not indigo, not violet, not emerald. The accent is Armenian bole — the raised iron-oxide red of İznik ware, #C04A2E — and it carries exactly one meaning across the whole product: a person decides here. That unifies the primary button and the alert chip on purpose: both mean “this is waiting on you”. The house's own colour is glaze turquoise; cobalt draws information; brass and copper are metal, used on rules and metadata, never as chroma.

Glaze depth, in three layers

meniscus · body · pool

A fired tile is not a flat fill. Top: a 1px inner highlight where the glaze meets the rim (--meniscus). Middle: a body gradient, lit → hue → deep. Bottom: the pooled darker edge where glaze ran (--pool). Two hairlines and one gradient — that is the entire material. No drop shadows on data surfaces, no neumorphism, ever.

Data is a tiled wall

Stock, capacity, coverage and progress all draw as a wall: glazed tiles for what is there, bisque for what is not, and bolus only where a decision lives. One glyph, one rule, forty pages — the grayscale-data discipline in the tile grammar, and readable at a glance from across a pass.

§06Type — three voices

A display face with a hand in it

Fraunces is the point of difference: a variable old-style with SOFT and WONK axes, so headlines carry brush pressure and a slightly wonky y — hand-painted, not hand-drawn. §07Motion — setting, not sliding

A tile is placed, then it settles

The physical model is a tile-setter's hand: the piece is placed with a small overshoot as it beds into the mortar, then it stops dead. Nothing floats, nothing wobbles, nothing travels more than 10px. One emphatic exception — glazeFire, reserved for the moment a person's decision fires a tile.
