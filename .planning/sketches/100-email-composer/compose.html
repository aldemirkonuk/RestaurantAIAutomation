Compose — the house writes, with its workings shown.

Every figure in this letter came out of a register, and every one of them says which register, over what window, and when it was last computed. A figure that could not be measured goes in as words, never as a number.

The unit of insertion is a sentence the engine already computed — not a figure this page scrapes back out of one.

That is not a preference. rec-forward.ts:16-21 already states the rule for the sibling page: an insight sentence carries “numbers formatted for reading, not for arithmetic”, so a target scraped back out of the sentence is a figure the page invented. The same trap applies to a letter, and it is worse there, because the vendor keeps the letter. So a merge field inserts the engine's own sentence with its provenance attached; a bare figure field exists only for things that are literally a stored column — an order total, a PO number, a delivery date.
