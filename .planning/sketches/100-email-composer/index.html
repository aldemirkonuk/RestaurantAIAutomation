Honesty

What Send does, and what it does not

  • It doesSend one letter, now, from the identity the house has set, threaded onto the vendor's last message so their reply lands in the same conversation — and write it into the conversation book as yours, marked ai_generated: false. The house's next AI draft reads that row, so a letter you write by hand is counted in the round limit instead of being invisible to it.
  • It does notQueue, schedule, batch, or send later. It does not send to a list. It does not attach a file — there is no attachment path on the manager-written route today, and this sketch does not pretend there is.
  • It is not the AIThe vendor-reply AI is a different thing and stays exactly as it is: it drafts, and enable_ai_autonomous_send is off unless a literal stored true says otherwise — no row, a read error or a thrown client all fail closed. When it is on, a guardrail-clear reply is staged for two minutes and can be cancelled from the sidebar. Nothing in this composer changes any of that.
  • What is storedThe letter body, its subject, who sent it, when, the vendor, the thread and message ids, and — new — the rule key and computed-at of every inserted sentence. That last part is the point: six months later you can ask what the house believed when it wrote this, and get an answer.
  • What is never claimedThat a figure was measured when it was withheld. That a letter was sent when the send failed. That the AI wrote something a person wrote, or the reverse. And — the one this pass adds — that a letter came from the house when it left from a mailbox the house does not own.

The order

What to build, in what sequence, and why that sequence

  • First — the identityNothing else is honest without it. A composer on top of a shared deployment mailbox produces letters that are from the house and do not look it, and no amount of provenance in the body fixes the line above it. Option A is the smaller build and unblocks the houses that already have a domain; option B is the product decision and the revenue line.
  • Second — the two migrationsinserted_insights on the conversation, and the four columns on the template. Both are additive, neither touches an applied file, and doing them before the UI means the chips are real from the first letter rather than retrofitted onto rows that lack them.
  • Third — the composer on the route that existsShip it against manual-reply first, so the first version writes only letters that belong to an order. That is most of them, it needs no new endpoint, and it proves the provenance chip end to end before a new route is designed around it.
  • Fourth — the letter with no orderThe new route, the CHECK-constraint migration, and the composer's “no order” path together. By this point the shape is known rather than guessed.
  • Fifth — the libraryTemplates last, because a template is a letter you have already written twice. Building the library first produces seven guesses about what a house wants to say.
The fork

What only the founder can decide

  • 1 · What does Send cost the writer?This sketch puts the seal on it — a deliberate hold — because a letter cannot be recalled and, on option B, one house's letter affects every other house's deliverability. The counter-argument is real: a manager writing eight letters a day will find the ceremony tiresome, and the house rations the seal on purpose. The alternative is a plain button plus a short undo window, which is what the AI path already uses.
  • 2 · May a letter be sent to someone who is not in the book?Typing a free email address is how a composer normally works, and how a letter escapes the vendor book, the guardrails and the round count. This sketch allows only a provider or a member. That is a restriction, and it is the founder's to lift.
  • 3 · What does the Mudavym address cost, and who may take one?Pricing is undecided (OD‑23) and the page says so rather than showing a number. Related and sharper: does a house on a free plan send at all, or does the sending identity become the paid line?
  • 4 · May the composer send SMS?The SMS route was deleted on 2026-09-02 for being unguarded and untraceable, and the typed SmsService methods that remain each know what they are sending and to whom. A free-text SMS composer would re-open exactly what that deletion closed.
  • 5 · Does a staff broadcast belong in this composer at all?It is the one template with no vendor, no guardrail and a different legal footing — it is the row that could drag the sending domain into bulk-sender territory. It may deserve its own surface rather than a row in a vendor-shaped library.
