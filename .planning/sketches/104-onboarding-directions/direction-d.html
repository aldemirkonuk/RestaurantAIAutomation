Why this is the standing-up step

An owner founds a house at a desk, once. A manager joins one from wherever the message reached them, and the join is the only moment in the whole arrival where a person is likely to be standing. So it is the one drawn at 390: everything the preview knows is above the fold, everything it does not know says so, and there is a single press.

What was left out on purpose

No code entry: the link carries it, and typing eight characters on a phone is the door's failure mode. No confirmation after the press: the join is reversible from Settings (POST /auth/me/leave-restaurant, auth.controller.ts:321). No verification letter: the invited are vouched for.

What happens after the press

Frame 03, on the phone: the house's answered registers as record, the unanswered as not yet answered, the list already in the books. The manager can read Hasan's house before Hasan has said a word to her.
