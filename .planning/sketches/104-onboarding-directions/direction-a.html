Inside A

Two roads not taken

Road 1 · keep the currency question at /register
Ask it twice: once from the address, once from the paper.

Safer for a house whose first invoice prints no currency. Rejected because it puts a setting before the evidence — the one thing this direction refuses — and because the paper’s sighting is stronger evidence than a country table. The address stays as the stated fallback when the paper is mute.

Road 2 · a seal per register
Six ceremonies instead of one.

Every confirmed value would carry its own wax. Rejected because the wax is rationed to the real commitment, and a currency, a vendor name and a threshold are records that reverse in one click. The row entering the book is the only thing here that does not.
