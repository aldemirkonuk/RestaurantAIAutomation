The phone shows the interview’s smallest unit: one question, the guess it starts from, and the sentence that turns a guess into an answer. Confirmed registers are ink; inferred ones stay dashed and italic until tapped or spoken to; the drawer at the bottom is the proposal, folded. Nothing on this screen writes. The seal is the same hold as on the desk, and a manager who prefers to finish standing can.

The two grey chips are the whole argument for provenance at phone width: a house that walks away here leaves whiskey and non-alcoholic recorded as guesses, and the desk shows them grey tomorrow.
