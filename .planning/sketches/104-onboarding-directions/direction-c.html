The arrival, drawn as opening the ledger

The house's book.

A new house is a new set of books. The arrival is its opening entries: five ruled pages — Currency, What we pour, Whom we buy from, What we hear about, The assistant — each carrying its own state, ruled off with the double rule when it is answered, left open in grey when it is not. The owner opens pages in any order. The book remembers.

Double rule
The page is ruled off: answered and posted. Reopening is a correcting entry, never an erasure.
Single rule
A subtotal: the page is partly stated and stays open.

The apply verb is never exposed: the seal in a first-party client is its only caller. Voice and typing (ADR 0113 Q1): no route takes an utterance today. Three rules the page obeys
A stated value never enters the batch — fo. 1 is already Defne's, so it is contested at most, never overwritten (rule 3). Each line posts through its own owning service and reports on its own — the receipt says written, refused or not attempted, never one "Done" (rule 4). The batch stays revocable as one unit for seven days, and the date is on the receipt (rule 4a).
