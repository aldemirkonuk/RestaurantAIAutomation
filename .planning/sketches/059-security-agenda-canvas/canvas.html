Open to the founder · and what stays locked

  1. INTEL-F4 — merged or split? Still merged. New: the written split trigger is now six routes and one CI script away, so the live question is whether we pre-commit the split to the day S1 goes green.
  2. The six undeclared auth routes — declare or leave? Recommend declare. Correct by absence is not correct by intent, and CI cannot tell them apart until the intent is written down. Last of OD-19.
  3. Tokens in localStorage. Open it as a platform change, or accept it in writing with an owner and a date. Both legitimate. Silence is not, and it has been silent since 08-24.
  4. fail_open_defaults 4 → 1. The value genuinely moved — three JWT fallbacks became one fail-closed resolver. Confirm we may republish it at 1.
  5. GET /events/metrics is public. Deliberate, or a decorator that outlived its reason? delete is an available verdict and not ours to take alone.
