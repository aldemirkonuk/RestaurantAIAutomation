# Sketch 119 · Two more shells, past 106

## What this decides

Sketch 106 answered ADR 0149 row 5 with three shells and recommended **A with B's phone**.
The founder's note on 2026-09-17: *"1-2 more with the best quality"*, at the bar he set the
same day — *"the most sota with best user experience, (do not care about cheapest rebuild)
I need full quality"*, people-facing pages in the register of the Wave Four gallery
(REPORTS and CALENDAR singled out) and *The Arrival, Five Ways*: fewer words on the
surface, one clear primary act per view, generous spacing, honesty kept but quiet.

Two directions here go past 106 by giving the shell a **second organ** besides the rooms —
something an owner or a floor manager actually lives *in*, not just navigates *by*:

- **D — The counter.** The work comes to the person. A right-hand column holds the acts
  waiting on *this* person (Seal · Verify · Reply · Decide · Judge · Mudavym proposes), and
  each act is done in place, in a side sheet over the page you are on.
- **E — The day.** The time comes to the person. Line two of the header is the house's
  service arc for today, read from its own hours, with today's fixed points as ticks that
  open to their records; the page gets all the width.

Both keep everything already decided: charcoal ground, declared paper surfaces only
(ADR 0138, 0149 row 6); the floating "Wine Agent" button gone, `/ask` and the ⌘⇧K panel as
the two doors (0149 row 33, ADR 0145); `support@mudavym.com` (0149 row 8, ADR 0143); the
built header (`apps/web/src/components/mudavym/HouseHeader.tsx`, minus `ThemeMenu` at
`:229`) and the eight surviving ADR 0112 overlays reused or, where superseded, superseded
on purpose and said so. Internal tools (`/studio`, `/simpos`, `/dev/truth`, `/dev-sandbox`
— `App.tsx:189-273, 347, 413`) appear in neither rooms table.

Both also draw, this time, the three overlays 106 left as "built elsewhere": the palette
open (⌘K), the bell open, the account menu open.

## The two directions

### D — The counter (`direction-d.html`)

**Idea.** The rooms stay a quiet rail of words in 106 A's grammar (The floor · The door ·
The cellar · The books · The people; *Ask Mudavym.* first; Settings, Connections, The desk,
Help in the foot; tucks to a 28 px strip on ⌘\). The new organ is **the counter**: a 320 px
column on the right that stays across every page and holds what waits on the person who
is signed in, grouped by the verb they would use —

| Verb | What it lists | Register it reads today |
|---|---|---|
| **Seal** | orders awaiting the seal | `GET /procurement/orders/pending` (`procurement.controller.ts:169-181` is the count; the list is the sibling route) |
| **Verify** | receipts at the door, credit notes promised | `GET /procurement/receiving/unverified` (`receiving.controller.ts:347`), `GET /procurement/credits` |
| **Reply** | vendor threads waiting on the house | `GET /conversations/pending/list` (`conversations.controller.ts:327`) — **see the trap below** |
| **Decide** | invitations not yet accepted; identity candidates | `GET /restaurants/:id/invites` — owner/manager (`members.controller.ts:28,45` is `JwtAuthGuard` only, but `getInvites` calls `assertMembership(…,'owner\|manager')`, `members.service.ts:129`, which throws for staff at `:60-62`); `GET /vendor-intel/identity/candidates` — owner, manager **and staff** (`vendor-intel.controller.ts:460-461`, `:489-490` override the controller's own owner/manager class guard with a handler-level `@Roles`, and `roles.guard.ts:12-14` reads the handler first) |
| **Mudavym proposes** | the assistant's proposals, not yet applied | `GET /ask-ai/actions` (`ask-ai.controller.ts:124`; class guards only at `:76`, so every member reads it — confirming is owner/manager, `:152`; ADR 0113 — the assistant proposes, the seal applies) |
| **Judge** | a price or market action | `GET /house/market-actions` — **placeholder, 501 until the quant lands** (specified below) |

Each act opens **in place**: a side sheet (ADR 0112 `Sheet`, 440, `tuck`) over the page
you are on, with the act's whole record and — where the act is a seal — `HoldToApprove`
inside it (`pour`, 620 ms). Closing the sheet lands back on the page exactly as it was.
An owner clears a morning without leaving Orders; a floor manager sees what waits without
leaving Receiving. The counter's head prints how many **registers answered**, computed
from the registers actually rendered and their outcomes, never from a constant: *5 of 6
registers · 1 not built* for an owner (a 501 is a real answer — the register said *not
built* — but it is named on its own, never folded into the *n* that answered with
content: counting it as ok is absence reported as health), *3 of 6 registers · 1 not
built · 2 not read* when two failed, *4 of 7 registers · 3 refused* for staff (frame 03:
Decide splits into two registers for the role check, so seven render — Seal, Reply,
Identities and Mudavym proposes answered — Identities because `vendor-intel`'s queue and
its decide route hand staff a role the controller's own class guard would otherwise
refuse, and Mudavym proposes is readable by every member but not hers to confirm; Verify,
Invitations and the market refused). Never *n of n* when a register refused, was not
read, was not built or was not shown; never a sum of acts; each section's
eyebrow prints its own register's count. A register that did not answer is a **hollow ring with the failure
named** (*Receipts & Credits · not read · 503 at 14:02:11 · Read again*); a register the
role may not read says **refused** in words; an unbuilt register says **not built**; a
register still being read shows a static hairline, never the ring. Below the acts, **The
house said** — the session's own log (what the house confirmed, wrote here, sealed,
refused, kept as a draft), newest first; the bell's book is the house's record, this is
yours. The foot is the cadence: *read 14:02:11 · again in 60 s · on focus · Read now* —
the bell's staircase (`useBellBook.ts:81`, `BELL_POLL_MS = 60_000`, focus at `:288`).

Toasts **land at the counter's head**, in flow (the first act is never covered), and after
8 s fold into *The house said*. Five kinds, one anatomy: eyebrow (what the house did ·
when) · one sentence · what is not claimed · Undo or Open · a drain. *Written here* is
dashed and carries no Undo and no "saved"; *Refused* changes nothing and says so; *Draft
kept* is not sent; *Sealed* carries the wax and opens the receipt. With the counter tucked
(36 px strip, a dot that is never a sum) toasts dock under the header at the right.

The header gains one control: the counter's toggle, with a dot when acts wait. The bell
(`HouseBell.tsx`, popover 350) and the account menu (`HouseUserMenu.tsx`, 274) are reused
unchanged; the palette (⌘K, the house `Panel`, 620) reads the same rooms table as the
rail and adds **On the counter** as its first section, then Rooms, Records, and *Ask
Mudavym.* with the typed text carried across (⌘⇧K).

Offline: the strip under the header; the counter freezes and dates itself (*6 registers ·
from 14:01:48*, for an owner — staff, once Decide splits, would freeze at *7 registers*);
the queue appears as a held row at its head (*Written here, not sent ·
3*), opening the four-rung ladder (written here · sent · received · sealed by the house —
ADR 0140's door outbox, generalised); every record stays on rung 1 until the house
answers; a seal is never queued. Failure: the boundary sits **under** the shell, so the
rooms, the counter, the bell and the search survive a page crash; the readings (error id,
build, route, house, when) are copyable; the address is `support@mudavym.com`; the
stale-chunk case reloads once and says so. Loader: 0–400 ms nothing but the hairline;
to 3 s the page's own skeleton; after 3 s a mono line; at 12 s a sentence and two acts.
404: the address kept, the nearest room by edit distance over the rooms table, the rooms
listed with `/sommelier redirects here` under Mudavym (ADR 0145 fork 5), `/no-access` as a
different door; no HTTP status claimed (`vercel.json` rewrites to `index.html`, 200).

At 390 the counter is the **first of four doors** on a bottom bar — Counter (a dot, never
a sum) · Rooms (a left sheet with the house and role at its head) · Search · Ask Mudavym
(a tab, because ⌘⇧K has no phone equivalent) — and opens as a bottom sheet at a **mid
detent**, sized to its content, the page visible above it and the four doors visible
under it (F9); the act sheet is a bottom sheet the same way; toasts are cards above the
bar; the legacy mobile
top bar (`DashboardLayout.tsx:79-92`) is deleted and the house header is the only bar.

**Optimises for:** the act-in-place loop — the thing that saves an owner an hour a day;
provenance per verb (every section answers for itself); one place the assistant's
proposals and a market action can land without a new page; a chrome whose every claim is
a register's own answer, dated.

**Costs.** New: `HouseCounter.tsx` + `house-counter.css` (a shell region, not an overlay —
it does not trap focus, does not scrim, and is never a form; the sheet it opens is the ADR
0112 primitive); `HouseRail.tsx` as in 106 A; the act sheets (seal, verify, reply, decide,
judge) as `Sheet` bodies — the seal sheet is the one that must carry `HoldToApprove` and
send the order, i.e. it is the `/orders` seal flow re-mounted inside the shell, which is a
policy call (fork 3). **One read**, `GET /house/counter`, per-register outcomes (shape
below) — six reads exist today, the aggregate does not; and it has to retire the
absence-reported-as-health catch on the one count it inherits live
(`services/api/dashboard.ts:29`, `.catch(() => 0)`). A staff gate on receipts
(`credits.controller.ts` **and** `receiving.controller.ts:228-229,347` are both
`JwtAuthGuard` only — Verify reads both; 106 found the same on credits alone) — drawn as
if it existed. **Width:** rail 232 + counter 320 leaves the page 888 px at 1440, and `/reports`
wants 1280 (`reports-next.css`, `.rp-page__inner`); the honest fix is a per-page default
(the counter tucked on `/reports` and `/inventory`, the rail tucked on ⌘\) — fork 10.
**Height:** with six registers and three log entries the drawn counter runs ~1180 px at
1440; on a 900 px window its body scrolls and the foot pins (frame 01 is drawn unclipped
so the whole thing can be read; frames 02–14 are drawn at 900 and clipped, as a window
would).

### E — The day (`direction-e.html`)

**Idea.** No rail. The built header keeps line one, and the page's name becomes the door
to the rooms — a chevron beside it opens one anchored popover (ADR 0112 `Popover`, `ink`,
widened to 560 for five groups + the house; this **supersedes** `mdv-hdr__page`'s plain
span deliberately, one edit to `HouseHeader.tsx:209`) with letter chords (`G` then a
letter — `CommandProvider.tsx:165` already captures `g`). Line two is **the day line**:
the house's service arc for *today* drawn from its own hours (`restaurants.operating_hours`,
migration `20260902210000`, ADR 0093 D1; `GET /restaurants/:id/operating-hours`,
`operating-hours.controller.ts:38`) — Prep · Doors · Close as bands, *now* marked — and
today's fixed points as **ticks that open to their records**:

| Tick | Register | Drawn as |
|---|---|---|
| a delivery expected | no source today — a new capability, not a re-key (see Costs below) | a seal-ring mark; the popover names the window as *the vendor's word* |
| a delivery that arrived | `GET /procurement/receiving/unverified` | ruled off — the calendar page's double rule, reused |
| a calendar event (the rep's tasting, a reservation) | `GET /calendar/today` (`calendar.controller.ts:575`) | an ink mark |
| a shift boundary | `GET /restaurants/:id/team/week` / `my-week` | an ink mark |
| the count due at close | a calendar reminder (`calendar/today`); no count schedule exists (`stock_counts` is a record, ADR 0078, not a schedule) | an ink mark; **not gated** — `calendar.controller.ts` carries only `JwtAuthGuard` and `getTodayEvents` (`:582-591`) applies no role filter, so this reads the same for every role; absent on the sim house only because none is scheduled today, not because staff are refused |
| a market action, when built | `GET /house/market-actions` | a mark **at the hour the action stops holding** (the quote's close) — frame 09 |

Same-time records merge into one mark with both names (*16:00 Kermit Lynch · Daniel
Reyes*). An order with no window (*Revel Wine — sent, not received by the vendor*) has
**no tick**: missing is not a time. The bands come from the hours; when the house has not
set them the line says so in words, where the bands' words would be (*Hours not set — no
prep, doors or close today · Set the hours*), and the fixed points still draw on a
bandless rule (frame 05, left). The right end carries the read, computed the same way D's
counter head is (never *n of n* when a register is unbuilt, refused, or was not read) over
the same six registers as the Tick table above — delivery-expected, delivery-arrived,
calendar, shifts, reminders and market: *read 14:02:11 · 5 of 6 registers · 1 not built*
for an owner with the market unbuilt; *5 of 6 registers · 1 refused* for staff (the market
is the one gated register — `GET /house/market-actions` is owner/manager — reminders is
not gated and reads the same for every role); *2 of 6 registers · 1 not built · 3 not
read* when partial, in amber, with the calendar, shift and reminder ticks absent (reminders
rides the same `calendar/today` read as the calendar tick, so it fails with it) and its cadence —
*again in 60 s · on focus* only after a read that succeeded, *will read on return*
offline, nothing while reading — the day's log (`+4`), and *Ask Mudavym.* (⌘⇧K).

Toasts are **moments**: a toast lands under the now mark **in flow** — the line grows by
one row for 8 s (`settle`, 320) and the page moves down with it, so the page's headline
is never covered — then becomes a small mark on the line — the day's own log — and `+N`
opens it (newest first, Undo where F10 allows). Nothing floats over the work on a
desktop; on a phone they are cards above the tab bar. Loader: the now mark hollow, the read says *reading the day · 0.8 s*, the
page's skeleton under it, the 12 s sentence. Offline: **the line stops advancing** — a
hatched gap from the last answer (14:02) to the wall clock (14:41), the now mark hollow
and labelled *14:41 · offline*, expected ticks dashed, the strip under the line, the same
four-rung ladder. Failure and 404 as in D, under the day line, which survives both.

At 390 the day line is the strip under the header (labels truncate with an ellipsis; the
now label never does) and the books are a **bottom tab bar** — Floor · Door · Cellar ·
Books · More — 106 B's phone; More holds Settings, the two role-gated rooms, Help,
Notifications and *Ask Mudavym.*, the phone's only door to the assistant. A tick opens as
a bottom sheet.

**Optimises for:** the floor manager — at 15:40 *Kermit Lynch at the door by 16:00* is on
every page, and the shift change and the close are where they will be; a shell that knows
what time it is in a restaurant (no back-office chrome does — Toast, R365, MarginEdge and
7shifts all carry inert chrome); the page gets 1440; the owner reads the whole day in one
line without opening the calendar.

**Costs.** New: `DayLine.tsx` + `day-line.css` with a **real label layout** — this sketch
assigns label rows greedily from estimated widths (`build.py`, `_layout`); a build
measures with the DOM and must keep the rule that no two labels overlap and nothing is
clamped off the line (a tick before prep draws before prep; a day that closes after
midnight puts the tail on the next calendar day — the TR house in frame 04 is the test).
The rooms popover and the chevron trigger in `HouseHeader.tsx`. **One read**,
`GET /house/day` (shape below), six registers: four exist today — delivery-arrived
(`receiving.controller.ts:347`), shifts (`team/week`, `my-week`), and calendar and
reminders, which both read the *same* `calendar/today` call and so cost no new endpoint,
only a second key in the aggregate's response; market is the fifth, unbuilt until the
quant lands (its own placeholder above). Delivery-expected is the sixth and, unlike the
other five, is not a re-key of anything live: `procurement_orders.expected_delivery_date`
is a `date`, never a time (`20260805000000_baseline_from_production.sql:3288,:4533`), and
no endpoint lists orders in transit — capturing the vendor's promised window is new work
this sketch has not costed. The aggregate itself does not exist yet.
A dependency the founder should measure
before choosing E: **how many production houses have `operating_hours` set** — unset hours
degrade the line to ticks only (frame 05), which is honest but is not the design. And a
standing honesty burden of a new shape: the now mark is the **device's clock** and the
ticks are the **house's readings**; the offline frame is where the two part, and the line
must never let a tick after the last answer look confirmed (dashed, frame 12).

## Where a price or market action surfaces, and what exists today

**What exists today, measured on this tree (2026-09-17):**

- **Sightings and the drop.** `GET /vendor-intel/below-average`
  (`vendor-intel.controller.ts:103`; owner/manager — `RolesGuard` at `:36-39`) with the
  pure arithmetic in `vendor-intel/price-below-average.ts` (the mean excludes the newest

  verified this session). One correction to the plan: its "the controller has **no POST at
  all**" (§10, correction block) is stale on this tree — `POST /commodity-index/exposures`

- **The act an action would open.** `one-tap-actions` (`GET /one-tap-actions/pending`,

  It opens the existing one-tap order draft … Nothing is sent, nothing is approved, nothing

- **The org side.** ADR 0048 folds domain quant into Research & Math ("food into math");
  ADR 0054 (Proposed) is the price series an order writes.

**What is missing** — the thing the founder is calling *price actions / market actions*:

1. **No register of judged actions.** Nothing turns a signal into *for this house, this
   act* — buy now, hold, switch vendor, reprice, stock up — with an expected value, a
   confidence and the working. `market-signal.ts` yields a notification; the commodity
   rule yields a dark alert; neither is an act.
2. **No join to the house's own consumption and cover.** A saving is only a saving against
   what the house will actually pour; the plan's §5 (pass-through is partial and measured
   small) and §9c (the storability clause has no input — no shelf-life column) are the two
   blockers it names, and neither has moved.
3. **No profitable cadence has been found yet** on the monthly public series
   (`cadence-value.ts`), and no egg series is recorded, so the quant's own verdict today is
   *no threshold*.
4. **No surface** but the two boxes on `/notifications` and the legacy `/vendor-prices`.

## The two other new reads

**`GET /api/v1/house/counter`** (D) — one read, six registers, per-register outcomes:

```
{ readAt, house: {id},
  registers: {
    seal:     { state: "ready"|"refused"|"failed"|"unbuilt", count, readAt, ms, query, rows: [ …first 5 ] },
    verify:   { … }, reply: { … }, decide: { … }, proposed: { … }, market: { … }
  } }
```
For staff, `decide` itself carries two keys — `identities` and `invitations` — because the
role check differs per source (the Decide table row above), which is why the staff head
counts seven registers, not six. `refused` is the role gate (403) printed in words; `failed` is a 503 or a timeout, drawn
as the hollow ring with *Read again*; `unbuilt` is the 501 above. A failed register never
collapses to a zero — which means retiring `dashboard.ts:29`'s `.catch(() => 0)` on the
one count the counter inherits live, not repeating it. **Trap found on this tree:**
`GET /conversations/pending/list` calls `getPendingConversations()` with **no restaurant**
(`conversations.controller.ts:327-333`; the service's parameter is optional,
`conversations.service.ts:622`), so read as-is it would put another house's threads on the
counter. The aggregate must pass the token's house; the sketch draws the scoped read and
names this as a gateway fix the counter cannot ship without.

**`GET /api/v1/house/day`** (E) — one read, the bands and the ticks with per-register
outcomes:

```
{ readAt, house: {id, timezone},
  hours: { state: "set"|"unset", prep, doors, close, crossesMidnight },
  registers: {
    deliveriesExpected: { state, readAt, ticks: [ {at, id, label, window: "vendor"|"house"|null} ] },
    deliveriesArrived:  { state, readAt, ticks: [ {at, id, label} ] },
    calendar:   { … }, shifts: { … }, reminders: { … }, market: { … the 501 above … }
  } }
```
`hours` rides in the same read as the registers — a client must not draw the bands before
this response lands, the same rule that keeps a register's content off the page before its
own answer. The device supplies the wall clock; the payload never claims *now*. Expected
ticks after the last answer are drawn dashed while offline. `reminders` carries no role
gate of its own — it is read from the same `calendar/today` call as `calendar`
(`calendar.controller.ts:52-53` is `JwtAuthGuard` only; `getTodayEvents`, `:582-591`,
applies no role filter) — so staff receive it the same as an owner; `market` is the one
register staff never receive as content (refused, in words, in the line's read).

## Recommendation

**D — the counter — as the shell, with E's day line as a page element, not chrome.**

D is the one that changes what an owner's day is like: the acts that make a morning —
three seals, two receipts, two replies, an invitation — are done from one column without
leaving the page, and every one of them already has a register that answers for itself,
so the column's honesty is the registers' honesty, dated. It gives the assistant's
proposals and the price/market action a home that reads correctly *today* — a hollow
*Judge* row that never prints a zero — and the day the register answers 200 nothing in
the shell changes but the row. Its costs are concrete and bounded: one aggregate read
with per-register outcomes, a scoped conversations read (the cross-tenant trap above is a
gateway fix regardless of this sketch), a staff gate on receipts, and a per-page default
for the width it takes.

E is the more original idea and the better one for the floor manager, but its inputs are
thinner than D's on this tree — hours set on an unknown number of houses, delivery-expected
has no source at all today (Costs above), shift boundaries not yet read for the day — and
its honesty is a new
shape (device time against house readings) that would need its own rule before it could
carry the chrome. The day line should exist: on the dashboard and the receiving page, as
the page's own first line, where its reads are the page's reads and its hours can be
asked for in place. If the founder wants the floor manager's shell to differ from the
owner's (fork 8), E's strip on the phone above D's bar is the combination to draw next.

If the founder wants E anyway, the conditions are: measure `operating_hours` coverage
first; the one read (`GET /house/day`) with per-register outcomes; the DOM-measured label
layout with the no-overlap and no-clamp rules; and the offline rule that a tick after the
last answer is never drawn confirmed.
