Sysco's cutoff falls before you open. An order for Wednesday's delivery has to be placed on Tuesday before about 15:40, and the kitchen is not in until 17:00. No sender reminds anyone of it today: the recurring-order reminder is refused, and it fires at 05:00 in any case, twelve hours before the door opens. The bracket is inferred from 23 orders; confirming it, or Sysco stating a time, is what would let the calendar draw the deadline as a fact.
Monday is closed and carries the most machine. The audit reminder at 04:00 (proposed), the weekly report at 05:00, the event-prep check and the refused reminder at 05:00, the daily summary at 06:00, Sysco delivering. A closed day is not a day nothing happens; someone should be there for the delivery, and the calendar draws it as unplaced until a window is stated.
Five of the six scheduled senders fire inside your quiet hours. 04:00 to 06:00 here is 07:00 to 09:00 in New York, where the server's one schedule is set; only the delivery ETA at 14:00 falls in your day. Your quiet window does not hold them: it is read by the Python chooser (notification_agent.py:1487) for low-stock, negotiation and delivery alerts, and the six gateway senders never pass through it. The daily summary lands on the SMS door at 06:00 regardless. A per-house schedule does not exist — founder question 5.

Not on the grid, because it is not stated: the recommendations digest (hour, least urgency, recipient — the sender exists, the answer does not); Monterey Fish Co. and Chefs' Warehouse cutoffs and delivery days; Valley Produce's cutoff (its delivery days are stated); a delivery window for any vendor. On the grid, but not this house's: the six fixed-hour senders — daily summary, weekly report, delivery ETA, event prep, audit reminder, recurring-order reminder — run at cron constants in America/New_York (scheduled-tasks.service.ts:195–680), one schedule imposed on every house; they are drawn grey, converted, and are not editable here. Two senders have no hour: the low-stock alert fires on a threshold crossing and is drawn as the dotted rail; the custom-reminders check runs every fifteen minutes (:733) and sends only when a reminder a person wrote is due, so it is the eighth row of the table beneath and is not drawn as a mark.
