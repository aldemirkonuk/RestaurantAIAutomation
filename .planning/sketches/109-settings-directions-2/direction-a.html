3b · The second overlay — the hours editor on a week that is null opened from “Type the hours instead” — the seven-day editor, a right sheet, 440, because on a null week the object is the week (ADR 0112)

The contract has no partial week: all seven keys are required, [] is closed, null is the whole week unknown, and unknown is never turned into closed (operating-hours.ts:13-20). The editor therefore asks for all seven; Record the week stays off until every day is answered, closed or with ranges, and nothing is written before then.
