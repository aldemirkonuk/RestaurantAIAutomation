The idea. The unit of the page is the thing the finding is about — a wine, a vendor, a weekday, a server, or the house itself — and every subject gets an account: what stands against it, what was done about it and when, what has been silenced for it (with the scope), and what the engine could tell you about it. The rail is the catalogue's own dimension list, so the catalogue is not a second page but the second view of the same accounts: switch the view and the same rail filters 573 types instead of standing findings. A rule that names no subject is filed to The house and never coerced onto the nearest wine. Costed, not assumed: today only sales_below_weekday_baseline and weekly_demand_slide carry a subject the gateway can key on (recommendations.service.ts:163, :182); the other ten rules carry none, so the Barolo and Sancerre accounts drawn here need a subject (and periodKey) on each of them, and “wrote on” per account needs first-seen keyed per subject rather than per rule (attachFirstSeen, :527). That is B's gateway cost (README) and a founder fork.
