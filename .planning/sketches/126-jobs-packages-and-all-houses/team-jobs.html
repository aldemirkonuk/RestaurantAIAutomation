Already decided, drawn as decided

  • One row per kind of work: Receive deliveries, Count stock, Place orders, Set up zones. Being listed is the right from now on, per person and per house. No separate label list, no template (ADR 0253, round 7).
  • Staff can't do these until given (round 2: "Closed until given"). Owners and managers do all four by role.
  • A job carries its own right while it is open. That right ends when the job ends, never at the due time (round 7). Late is a notice, never a lock (round 2).
  • Set up zones is the switch already built for zones (ADR 0238). It moves here; nothing about who may set up zones changes.
