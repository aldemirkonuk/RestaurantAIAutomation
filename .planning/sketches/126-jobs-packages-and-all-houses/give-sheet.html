Sketch 126 · Give a jobpick the work, pick the person, say for how longexample dataall frames

The idea. One sheet hands someone a job and, if they need it, the permission to do it. The person list has two groups: the people who already do this work, and everyone else. For someone in the second group you choose For this job or From now on. Nothing is chosen for you, and the sheet says in plain words what the person will be able to do.

Already decided, drawn as decided

  • A job and the right to do it are given in one step (ADR 0253, Decision).
  • The dropdown groups "Does this" and "Everyone else". For someone not listed it offers For this job and From now on, neither preselected (round 7, settling round 2's "make it select, permanent and for this").
  • A job's right ends when the job ends, never at the due time. A late job: the person is reminded, then the giver is told once (round 2).
  • Managers give money or sending rights only where an owner has allowed it (round 6, "managers can give only if the owner accpeted to give access for those actions"; ADR 0175 D10 as amended). A Standard manager gives receive, count, order and zone work, not money (round 8).
