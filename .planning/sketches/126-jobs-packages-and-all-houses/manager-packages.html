The idea. A manager gets one of three packages: Full, Standard or Light. The package fills in their access once. After that you can add or take away single things, and Team shows it as, say, "Full, adjusted". A package changing later never changes anyone by itself: you are asked, person by person.

Already decided, drawn as decided

  • The three packages and what each opens: "Yes, as drawn (Recommended)" (ADR 0253, round 8). A manager with no package gets Light. Light's card follows round 11's F2, below.
  • Adjusting one person: "Yes, shown as adjusted (Recommended)". The package fills in once; single rights can then be added or removed (round 8).
  • Changing a package later: "No, you're asked (Recommended)". Managers keep what they were given; you apply the new version per person (round 8).
  • Staff: "Jobs list only (Recommended)". Packages are for managers; staff rights come from the Jobs rows and from jobs (round 8).
  • Every package turns on the house's money, Light too; Light's "no money" means it gives no money rights (round 11, F2: "Every package turns it on (Recommended)"). An owner, or a Full manager an owner allowed, may take it from one person, and Team shows the package as adjusted (F2; F4, "Owners + allowed Full managers"). A role change resets it to the new role (F9: "Resets to the new role (Recommended)").
  • Profit shows only to someone with both the house's money and pay; the pay switch is offered only to someone who sees money (F14: "Needs money and pay (Recommended)", amending ADR 0252 round 4). An owner's wage is never shown to a manager, pay access or not (ADR 0215 item 24).
  • An owner allows each money or sending action separately, and sets up to how much a manager may give, above the manager's own limit if the owner wants (2 Oct: "Yes, per action (Recommended)", "Owner sets the cap (Recommended)"). Standard's order work carries no amount of its own (2 Oct: "No amount of its own (Recommended)").
  • Full ticks "may give" for each money and sending action, with the cap you set, and you can untick any one (ADR 0253 round 13, on PR #566: "Full pre-ticks, you adjust (Recommended)").
  • There is one Place orders right. Each person's amount lives in the house's approval rule: orders under it go through, orders over it wait. Until approvals are built, every order waits for an owner or a manager (round 13: "One right, amount in approvals (Recommended)").

Each manager is on one of three packages. Pick one, then adjust a person if you need to. Staff don't have packages; they get work from the Jobs rows.

Full

Opens

  • All houses, with profit and pay
  • The house's money
  • Every job
  • May give each money and sending right: all ticked, with the cap you set; you can untick any one

Never

  • Owners' wages
Standard

Opens

  • All houses, with sales, goals and decisions
  • The house's money
  • Gives receive, count, order and zone work; order work carries no amount

Doesn't open

  • Profit and pay
  • Giving money or sending
Light

Opens

  • Their own house only
  • The house's money there
  • Runs jobs there
  • Gives receive, count and zone work there

Doesn't open

  • Pay
  • Giving money rights
  • All houses
  • Giving order work

A manager with no package gets Light. Like every package, Light sees the house's money; an owner, or a Full manager an owner allowed, can take it from one person, and a role change resets it.

Open, noticed while drawing

  1. Which money actions there are. The owner allows each one separately and sets the cap (2 Oct), and Full ticks them all (round 13). The 2 Oct questions named two, placing orders up to an amount and sending to vendors; round 13 made the first one Place orders with each person's amount in the approval rule. Whether there are more is the rights list (OD-208). Not listed on the card.
  2. One package per person, or one per house. Ayla manages two houses. Drawn as one package for the person, her untick applying in both. Whether a package, or an untick, can differ by house is not settled.
  3. Who may set a manager's package. Drawn as the owner. Managers may make someone a manager (ADR 0162); whether they may pick that person's package is not settled.
  4. Who changes a package's contents. Round 8 settles that managers are asked when a package changes, not who may change it: each owner, or only Mudavym.
  5. An adjusted manager when the package changes. If Full changes, does Ayla keep her untick on top of the new version, or start over? Not settled.
  6. Pay added to Standard, and All houses. A Standard manager given pay at one house, with the house's money, sees that house's profit (F14). Whether that profit also appears in their All houses, which Standard opens without profit, is not settled. Not drawn.
  7. A Light manager placing orders themselves. Light doesn't give order work. Whether a Light manager may still place orders by role is not settled.
