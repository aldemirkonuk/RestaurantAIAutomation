The shape of the gap. Mudavym is ahead of the field on the two hardest items — the consent screen written in our own words, and an outbound guard that parses an address to bytes and pins it into the socket. It is behind on the two cheapest — one list, and a log of who attached what. Every one of the nine absences is cheap; all three decisions are the founder’s alone.

What no competitor has. The em dash. Every product above will show you an empty connections list whether nothing is connected or the read failed. The rule that a failed read must say which register could not be read, applied to a connections page, is a differentiator nobody in this comparison ships.
