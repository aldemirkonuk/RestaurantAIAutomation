What moved off this page, and where it went

Three registers left. Each was about the house while living on a page that is about a person — and one of them was readable by every member of the house on their own profile.

  • How the house pays → Connections, Register II. The rows are keyed to the restaurant with no user column at all; the writes are manager-gated and the read is not.
  • Model context → Connections, Register I. Its own lead calls them "servers the house agents may call". Whether a row keeps a person's name on it is the one open fork.
  • The house (restaurant record, memberships, plan) → Connections' header, or /settings. It was never personal; it was on this page because there was nowhere else.

What stayed is what only you can answer for: who you are, what protects you, and what is attached to you. That is the same cut Stripe makes between Personal and Business settings, and the same one GitHub makes between a user's authorized apps and an organization's.
