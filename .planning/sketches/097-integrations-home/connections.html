Connections.

What acts for this house.

Fourteen things can act in Lokanta Müdavim's name. Seven are the house's own, three are set by the deployment for every house on it, three belong to one person who works here, and one is offered and untaken. Nothing here can spend money today — the payment provider has no key, and the one tool that could place an order is advertised, not granted. None may call a tool.

Every row below says whose it is, what it may do, what it last did, and how to stop it. A row you cannot stop from here says who can. Nothing is listed twice, and nothing that acts is missing — a connection with no row is a defect, not a feature.

Deployment setting.
Everything on this page is drawn by one row component. A live POS feed and an unconnected Excel grant get the same amount of design — what separates them is the chip, whether the control is live, and the sentence under it. The page therefore cannot flatter an empty attachment by drawing it richer than its evidence, and there is no control on it that can appear to succeed.

Four things on every row, and no fifth. Every row says whose it is (the house's · a person's · the deployment's · public), what it may do, what it last did, and how to stop it. A row that cannot be stopped from here names who can stop it. An unknown is an em dash — never a zero, and never a blank a save could write back.
