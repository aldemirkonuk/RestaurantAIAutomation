The idea. Each tier is its own row, so size never fights density: one hero band across the page, three large cards beneath it, then the rest as five-across compact tiles — twelve offers read on one 1440 screen. A bundle is a tray: one card with its bottles set into it as a small table, ranked by its own rolled-up worth and taking whatever tier that worth earns (here: large). A bundle whose worth is withheld drops to a compact tray.
