Frame 05 · The first proof · F5 A, F6 A, F7 A — interactive

The menu comes back typeset as the house’s own drinks list, at /house/menu — a page, not an overlay. Ink lines carry no label at all. Pencilled lines carry a margin stroke and Worth a second look; two lines with no candidate section wear an open ring and Needs a place. Tap any pencilled line: it opens in place with the crop of the original and one-tap sections — the guess dashed, never pressed, no “all”. Choosing erases the pencil, the line settles into ink (or moves to its section) and the counts tick. Show my original opens the split view. Empty sections are not printed; one line at the foot names them. Chivas is pre-opened for the shot. Drawn ahead of the service: the per-line pencil signal (R1) and per-line crops (R4) do not exist yet.
