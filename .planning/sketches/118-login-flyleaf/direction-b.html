The endpaper.

A hardback's first leaf is pasted down and decorative — the house's own mark, tiled like a printer's endpaper, standing in for the İznik wash today's card already wears. It never asks anything and never changes state; the working page sits beside it, on the recto, for every screen below. More object than direction A, still one clear act per page.
