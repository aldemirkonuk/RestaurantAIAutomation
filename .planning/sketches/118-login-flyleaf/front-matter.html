Front matter

Every house keeps a book.

Not the one on the shelf — the one kept in the walk-in at six, on the back of a delivery slip, in your head at closing. What came in the door. What is still in the cellar.

Mudavym keeps that book. It reads the invoice, keeps the count, notices the price that crept, the bottle running low. It drafts the reply, and sends nothing until a hand says yes — and every yes is sealed: who, when, how much.

You keep the room. We keep the book.

The endpaper lifts at its outer edge, curls, and lands on the right. Width: the leaf's back carries the first 340px of the 600px poem page; the right page crossfades to that same poem while the leaf is edge-on, so the leaf lands on a copy of itself and fades out.
