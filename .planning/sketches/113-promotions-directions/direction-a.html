1b · Fork 1, drawn as a choice — the same line's headline three ways, where the answers disagree ADR 0144 §4's sentence — "12% under your last landed cost" — names the house's most recent paid price from any vendor (1a). The grader's bestElsewhere (offer-grade.ts:332-376) is the cheapest other vendor's line over 540 days (1b). They differ whenever the latest landing came from a dearer vendor, or the cheapest other line is old. Miraval is that case: the house moved to Winebow in July and pays $18.60; Skurnik's $16.40 is from March. Château de Sours is the easy case, where the last landing is Empire's own and 1(a) collapses into the claim. The direction as drawn uses 1(b) as the headline and prints both in the working; choosing 1(a) or 1(c) is an amendment to ADR 0144 §4 (README, founder question 1).
