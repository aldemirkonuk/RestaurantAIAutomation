1 · Desktop, 1440 — Müdavim Brooklyn (USD, en-US) — signed in as Dan R., owner One slate: every bottle an open offer names, down the side; every vendor who has ever charged this house for it, across the top. A cell is a price with its date and its source. An offer is a cell that moved, and the big figure inside it is the offer against the lowest other vendor in the row — the only figure the mail did not already carry, with one short line beneath (against whom, when it ends). The vendor's own percentage, taken off the house's own price, is the claim, and stays in the sheet, not the cell — five lines of reasoning in a 118px column is the cell losing to its own footnotes. The offer's code and its copy act, and the "with a code" filter and export, are the legacy page's acts, kept. This direction also takes DESIGN-FOUNDATION §6 at its word — an offer with no end date is not an offer — and files an undated offer on the slate as a price, unhighlighted and uncounted.

Miraval: the house moved to Winebow in July and pays $18.60; Skurnik's $16.40 is from March. Choosing 1(a) or 1(c) amends ADR 0144 §4 (README, founder question 1).
1(a) · against your last landed cost — ADR 0144 §4 as written
−15.8%under your last landed — Winebow $18.60, 8 Sep, 8 days old

Says: what taking the offer changes against what you paid last. Hides: that Skurnik charged $16.40 in March. Fails: on a bottle whose last landing is Empire's own, the figure is the claim.

1(b) · against the lowest other vendor — as drawn on the slate
−4.5%under your lowest other — Skurnik $16.40, 3 Mar, 197 days old

Says: the figure the mail could not carry. Hides: that the house no longer buys from Skurnik and pays Winebow $2.20 more. Fails: never into the claim, but can understate a real fall.

1(c) · both, landed first
−15.8%under your last landed — Winebow $18.60, 8 Sep−4.5%under your lowest other — Skurnik $16.40, 3 Mar

Says: both, in ADR 0144's order. Costs: a second column on every row of the slate, and a "last landed from anyone" read the grader does not do today.
