The gap this canvas is really about. Fifty-three declared dependencies cross this department’s boundary and it has never published a readable artifact. That is the condition A1 and A2 exist to end — and the reason the truth board, not the engine, is the spine.

Locks — respected, not worked around

  • Pricing model deferred. Nothing here produces a price or a metric whose only consumer would be a pricing decision. Cost-per-consult is Finance’s to consume from Track A2; we do not pre-stage it.
  • Brand / landing visuals held. A3’s register governs wording and provenance only. It commissions no visual, no landing copy, no deck — and when the hold lifts it is the prerequisite that already exists.
  • No fork resolved here. INTEL-F3 / F6 / F7 are carried, not closed.

Findings — no card or loop can carry these

  • analytics.claim_published has no publisher. A weekly job cannot catch a deck written on a Tuesday.
  • Escalation to the Decision Office is a doc edit with no notification. B4 is the first live test of whether that path works at all.
  • One department agent, three teams with opposed incentives — it cannot arbitrate. Conflicts land at AB-3 (definition) or the founder (priority).
  • Two skills ship, not six: metric-claim-census, insight-candidate-reach.

Reach — graded, not scheduled as if earned

  • Versioned public metric contract at GET /analytics/metrics — buildable, unscheduled; it is a contract decision (INTEL-F7).
  • Counterfactual ledger for threshold changes — aspiration; needs storage nobody agreed to and touches RM-2’s boundary.
  • Insight → purchase-order attribution — the number that would matter most. Blocked on INTEL-F3: the operator has no subject_type.
  • insufficient_data as a designed surface — code already returns null; needs a founder yes and a Design render.
