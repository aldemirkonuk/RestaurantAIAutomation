What this direction costs

It spends the whole page on the one dimension every row already states in five characters (“3h ago”), and the axis is mostly empty. In exchange the shape of a day becomes visible — the 12:00 knot is a cron, not a crisis — and “ruled off” stops being a bucket and becomes what it is in a real book: a day that is finished.
