One-tap, honestly — identical mechanics to direction A

Two controls, never confused for each other. A tap (solid pill) is reversible, client-visible, with Undo, no seal. F10's own closed list is dismiss, archive a thread, remove a shift, a note, a door count within ten minutes — snooze, pin and mark-as-briefed are drawn the same way here, but adding them to F10's list is this round's proposed amendment, asked below, not something F10 already allows. A hand-off to another page (reprice, schedule, call a vendor) records nothing, or an honest "opened" — never "acted," since nothing has actually happened yet. A die (the ring) is for what moves stock or money — the same mechanism the one-tap desk already seals for a delivery (ADR 0107/0125): mint, hold, redeem, then the write. Nothing here claims an autonomous execution that does not exist — "Let Mudavym do it" stays off (dossier §1a); a tap always fires a write this product already makes, per ADR 0083.
