How this was calculatedthe engine's own notes, verbatim
  1. 1 observation flagged as an outlier by median-absolute-deviation and excluded from the consensus. It remains in the ladder so a bad parse is visible and fixable at source.
  2. Confidence blends how much weight mass survived (1.6 of 2) with how many distinct source types agree (4, capped at 3). It is a blend, not a probability of anything. 0.6·min(1, 1.6/2) + 0.4·min(1, 4/3) = 0.48 + 0.40 = 0.88
  3. Per-750 figures divide the quoted price by pack and by volume as the source stated them. A row whose pack or volume was not stated is not averaged in with a guessed value — none this time.
  4. The 7-day chip refused: 2 sightings in the window, and this register prints a change only from 5.
