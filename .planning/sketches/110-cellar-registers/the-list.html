No on-hand number on the row. The margin is the number, as a length, so the eye can compare depth without leaving the name. A bottle with no count has no bar. A bottle with no par has a bar against eight, which is a scale of convenience and is said here rather than drawn as a claim. Market price is not a column: it is the dash on every bottle this house has, and a column of dashes is crowding.
