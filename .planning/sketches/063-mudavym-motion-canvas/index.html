Mudavym. Motion canvas · OD-106

Every motion, one surface

A canvas for exploring movement on its own terms. Each demo runs real restaurant content, states its own curve, and replays on click or hover. Speed slows everything down so you can see what a 240ms spring actually does; Neutral skin strips the brand out so you can judge the movement without the colour arguing for it.

On reduced motion: every demo here is authored to collapse to its end state when the viewer asks for less movement. If your system has that on, the canvas will look still — that is the correct behaviour, not a broken page.
