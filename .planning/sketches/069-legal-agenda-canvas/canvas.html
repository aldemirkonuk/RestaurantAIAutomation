The agenda of a department with zero artifacts and one running question

One picture of legal-agenda-full.md: 16 tasks, three owners, six close-times. The spine is not the fifteen document types — most of them presume counterparties that do not exist. The spine is which surfaces can already bind us, and the paper that is already missing under work already done.

ADR 0013 is this department's proof of concept. It asked which runtimes could commit the company, counted instead of trusting two “ported verbatim” comments, and found 19 / 8 / 3 — with the runtime that could actually place an order running the weakest list. This agenda turns that one-off count into a standing census across every channel, and builds the DPA substrate before a counterparty’s DPA arrives without a warning window.
No agent signs, files, or sends. Nothing on this page is drafted legal text, and nothing here is legal advice — every output is a fact sheet, a register row, a specification, or a hold, addressed to the founder and then to counsel.

Gates that must exist before the thing they gate arrives

  • Counsel engaged — before the first one-way-door instrument. Every rule routes to “counsel reviews”; without one, R1 is a sentence.
  • Two-signature DPA gate — before the first enterprise DPA. There is no preparation window; the email arrives or it does not.
  • The register — before the second instrument. The first can be tracked by memory. The second cannot.
  • A number on the waiting period — the floor exists for the day it is inconvenient, which is the only day it does anything.
  • The merge condition, dated — 2026-11-27 and 2027-02-26. This org names split triggers everywhere and merge triggers nowhere.

  • F2 — the census has no mechanical home: run_card.py executes mechanical cards; the registrar is extraction. LEG-1 runs by hand until a card revision.
  • F3 — the register, the request path and the legal.paper_requested publisher are one missing object seen from three sides. LEG-10 closes a third.
  • F4 — CORP-F1 / OD-17 got sharper, not answered: 9 artifacts × 3 units, for a department whose register is empty.

What this agenda refuses

No template drafting

Not a DPA, not an NDA, not an MSA. R7 keeps clause language out of this vault and drafting is counsel’s. Program 2 builds the substrate an Annex is assembled from — a subprocessor list, a commitment→test map, an intake routine — and stops there.

No skill without a past instance

Six of the seven checkers stay specifications. The four skills admitted on 2026-08-28 each carried a real prior firing; these have none, because there are no instruments. Committing them now would be the same overreach in a friendlier costume.

No opinion on what a surface means

The census records channels, guard states and call paths. Whether a spoken “press 1” or a sent DEMAND_OFFER forms anything is counsel’s question, permanently. Legal prepares the fact; it does not preview the answer.
