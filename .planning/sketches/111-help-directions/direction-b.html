Sketch 111 · /help · Direction B

Is it me, the house, or the service.

The page is a triage ladder, not a document. A person who opens /help is stuck; the first thing they need is to know which of three things is at fault, so the page reads the same routes as Direction A but arranges them as four rungs — the service (the deployment), the house’s connections, what is waiting, and you — each carrying a state word (clear · fault · open · unread · refused · unproven), never a colour. The rule is one sentence: a rung is clear only when every row answered for itself, otherwise it carries the word for its worst row, and the first rung that is not clear is where the rail starts — a rule, never a judgement. The rail answers for that rung: which FAQ entries belong to it, the one act that clears it, and the mail to a person, which the reader sees before it opens. An unread rung is a place to start, the same as a fault; a recorded row is unproven, never clear.
