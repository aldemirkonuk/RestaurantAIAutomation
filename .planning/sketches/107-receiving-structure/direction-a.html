The idea

The delivery is the spine. The record hangs beneath it.

Every delivery is one event with eight stations — ordered · acknowledged · door · paper · verdicts · agreed · verified · paid — drawn as a spine across the whole desk (ADR 0103 D1, the founder's praised five-station orders spine extended to receiving). Time runs down beneath it as the record: every count, every reading, every proposal and every verdict is a mark on the same rail, in the order it happened, by name. A verdict that changes is a new mark that names the mark it supersedes; the old mark stays. The lines sit beside the record with their current state derived from the entries, never stored as a column.
