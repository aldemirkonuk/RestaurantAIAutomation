The idea

A queue of deliveries in lanes by clock state. The ruler is the page.

Every open delivery is on a clock, and the clocks are data (ADR 0103 D4: delivery_timers — door correction, response window, invoice issuance, objection window, payment — each with a state: open, nudged at 50 %, escalated at 80 %, blocked because it cannot be computed, or fired into LAPSED). The desk sorts deliveries into lanes by that state, soonest first, and draws each delivery as a card with a ruler: how much of its shortest window is gone, the two notches where the house nudges and escalates, and the sentence that says what happens at the end — the law deems acceptance, EFT debits, this clock asks. Owner and deputy are on every card, because a queue never falls back into an unowned backlog (D9). The lines and their append-only verdict ledger open in a side sheet; reassigning an owner is a popover.

The delivery sheet and the owner popover

ADR 0112: a sheet for one object (the delivery: its clocks, its documents, its lines and the selected line's ledger); a popover for a choice (who holds it). The popover carries no seal — the seal never appears inside a popover (ADR 0112) — and a holder row is read here as an assignment on F10's undo-after side, not a ledger row (founder question 8). The sheet's append is held (F10); the send lives on the claim.

What the staff phone is. The same queue, with the only clock that matters to a porter: the driver is here, or is not. The door-correction track has no fill: its window has no known length, so nothing is drawn as a proportion — the minutes are a number, the count is a count.

Nothing about money, no payment clocks, no lanes for the desk. "Came today" shows the porter's own entries by name and what the desk appended after — one ledger, read from the door's side. The porter's short (v2) is struck, not deleted, by the porter's own v3.

The manager's phone (second) is the delivery sheet in ADR 0112 F9's form: a bottom sheet with detents peek · half · full. At half the lines and their derived state fit; full reveals the selected line's ledger. The lanes keep their pulse behind the scrim.

Expected carries the pack arithmetic once, in the order's unit, and never re-multiplies it.
