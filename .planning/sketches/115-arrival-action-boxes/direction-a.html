The note in the margin.

The guidance is a gloss, not a box. Every act a new owner can take sits in the outer margin beside the very line it concerns, in the keeper's voice, with one verb and one alternative. The line is the record; the note tells you what to do to it. As a fact lands, the note beside it changes its mark, never its place.

To answerThe line is open and this is the act. One verb in seal, one quiet alternative.
