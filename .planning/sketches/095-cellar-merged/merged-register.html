What this column is

Everything this house has been charged for it, across every invoice. Open the cell for the ledger those lines make — what was bought, when, from whom, at what.

Read from procurement_document_lines.line_total, summed over invoice lines.

The gesture set, stated once. The header owns the column: right-click it, or press its caret, for this menu — sort, hide, and the column’s own account of itself (what it means, which table it is read from, how much of it is filled). Clicking the label sorts. The cell owns the row’s record: double-click or right-click any cell with a dotted underline and the series behind it opens below. It is the split every table tool in the study makes — Airtable’s field menu is schema, and TradingView is explicit that “you can click any row to open the chart” while a right-click on a column header customises the column.

A line belongs to this row when its label is the same words (exact), or contains this row’s label inside a longer till or invoice line (loose). Weaker than the register’s own rule, and every line above says which of the two found it.

Columns not drawn (4)

  • Style — pilsner, IPA, Gose. The founder’s first beer column, and the field agrees: Untappd makes Style one of only three required fields on a menu beer. type_attributes is {} on all 609 catalogue rows, so 0 of 57 beers carry one. The column needs no migration — it needs a writer.
  • IBU — bitterness, one of BJCP’s five Vital Statistics. 0 of 57. Carbonation (“gaz oranı”) and yeast (“maya”) are not offered at all: BJCP keeps both in prose — Mouthfeel and Characteristic Ingredients — and prose does not sort. They belong on the reading stand.
  • ABV — 0 of 609. A real column (20260817070000_beverages_table.sql:228) with no writer.
  • Format — 330ml can, 50l keg. The one field a beer register needs that a wine register does not. 0 of 609 on both volume_ml and package_format.

Every one of these is offered rather than deleted: an operator can turn one on and get the em dash with the measurement beside it, which is a decision, not a blank cell.
