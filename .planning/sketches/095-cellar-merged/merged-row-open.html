SKETCH · 095 · the expanded row, both grounds · example data, not a tenant

The Cellar · Beer · Kadıköy Meyhane

One row, opened in place.

The founder singled out /inventory’s row expansion — “it shows everything you need to see” — and asked whether this wave can have “that dropdown, all in one with essential details”. This is that shape on a cellar register: the row opens in place, the rows below move down, and the record is laid out as cards.

The two differences from /inventory, and both are the point. Two of its six cards cannot be filled here at all — Live vs shadow and Par and reorder both stand on restaurant_inventory, which is keyed on master_wine_id, so a keg has no row to carry a count or a par (OD-113). They are drawn, hatched, with the reason rather than dropped: dropping them would make a keg look like a bottle whose stock merely happens to be unknown. And the velocity bars here cover only the days the till actually has evidence for — /inventory zero-fills a fixed fourteen, so a house with one day of POS data is shown thirteen zero-sales days it never had.

Live vs shadow ⊘

Nothing in this register can be counted into the cellar yet: restaurant_inventory is keyed on master_wine_id, so a keg has no stock row. There is no live count here to hold a shadow count against, so neither figure is drawn. (OD-113.)

Par and reorder ⊘

A par is a column on the inventory row (threshold_min), and this kind has no inventory row. Suggested order, reorder point and runway are all arithmetic over that par, so all four are withheld together rather than one of them being guessed.
