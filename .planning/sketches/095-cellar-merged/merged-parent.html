What A contributed, and what the merge kept of it. A answered the one question no table answers — where. What it could not survive was trust: storage_locations cannot tell a zone somebody walked from a zone a seeder invented, and a floor plan is the one surface where that difference IS the content. Measured on production 2026-09-04: 4 rows across 2 tenants, and all four carry one of the four names the seeded-defaults sweep named (87 rows across 7 tenants was the 2026-09-02 figure; 83 have since been deleted, so the proportion went from 84-of-87 to 4-of-4). So the floor is built in the cellar’s own infer-then-confirm shape: a zone is drawn once somebody in this house has confirmed its name, and never before. The rest are a sentence with a count and the control beside it — never a drawn room, and never “no zones”, which would be a claim about the building.

What is in a zone is counted from the inventory rows assigned to it, not read off current_occupancy — on the only tenant that has both, that column says 180/32/45 against 17/17/16 rows actually assigned. Confirming a zone’s NAME is not confirming a seeder’s arithmetic.

What B contributed, and what the merge kept of it. B’s idea is that a whisky, a keg and a Burgundy belong in one list with the kind as a facet, because that is what “see everything” literally means. Its cost, in its own sketch, was one row grammar for three kinds of thing — the exact thing the four-child IA was chosen to avoid. The merge keeps the idea and pays a smaller price: the registers stay as the place a kind is worked, and this is the place the whole book is read, with only the eight columns that mean the same thing in every register. On hand is deliberately not one of them — it is real for wines and structurally absent for the other six, so as a general column it would be an em dash on most of the page.

What each direction gave the merge

A · the cellar floor

The room, and the only answer to where. Kept as a strip on the parent, drawn only from zones that carry a real count and a real walk date. Its own cost — 84 of 87 storage_locations rows carrying invented names — is why it is a band and not the whole surface.

B · one register, kind as a facet

“See everything” taken literally. Kept as its own section with a general column set and a register facet, opened deliberately because it is one read per register. Its cost — one grammar for every kind — is paid only by the eight columns that survive being general.

The shipped book

The spine, unchanged: a parent of registers, each opening at full breadth, with depth on a reading stand. It is the only one of the three that needs nothing further, and it is what both of the others now hang off.
