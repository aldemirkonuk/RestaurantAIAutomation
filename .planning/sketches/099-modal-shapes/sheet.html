Purpose III

A team member — ten fields, on the charcoal ground

fields from pages/team/command/editors.tsx:143 · the specimen carries data-ground="charcoal" itself

This one is drawn on charcoal on purpose. A sheet is portalled to document.body, where no page ancestor exists to inherit from — so the overlay's own root must carry both .mudavym and the page's ground, or it renders unstyled over a dark page. The scrim changes with it: .28 of warm ink over charcoal is a haze, not a separation, so charcoal gets rgba(0,0,0,.5).

The boundary

What a sheet may never be

  • Never a questionA sheet that asks “are you sure?” wastes 440 pixels on one word and puts the answer where the eye is not. An ask is a panel. The only question a sheet may carry is one about the object it already holds — the hold-to-delete above.
  • Never a menuA sheet is not attached to the control that opened it, so a list of choices in one loses its anchor and its meaning. A choice is a popover.
  • Never a second objectOpening a vendor from inside a calendar entry's sheet stacks two records of different kinds and leaves no way back. Close, then open. If two objects must be seen together, that is a page, not an overlay.
  • Never the pageIf the thing under the scrim is never useful while the sheet is open, the sheet should have been a route. The test: can you still read the list? If not, it is a page wearing a scrim.
