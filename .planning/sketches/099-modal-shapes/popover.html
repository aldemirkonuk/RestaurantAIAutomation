Mudavym

The popover — a choice, hanging off its control.

A short list of options that belongs to the thing you just clicked. It does not dim the page, because the page is not what you are answering — the control is.

anchor.bottom + 10top
anchor.right − widthleft, right-aligned
max(16, …)clamped to the viewport
ink · 160msmotion token
translateY 4→0enter keyframe
transparentscrim — it catches, it does not dim
no aria-modalTab leaves; outside click closes

the arithmetic is hooks/useAnchoredDialogPosition.ts:21-32, carried into the primitive at Sheet.tsx:196-226 so the house shape depends on no legacy hook

Why this shape does not trap focus. A sheet and a panel say “nothing else matters until you deal with me”, so Tab cycles inside and the body stops scrolling. A popover says the opposite: it is one control's own drawer, and tabbing past it should leave it, exactly as tabbing past a select does. Radix ships this distinction as modal defaulting to false on Popover and true on Dialog; the primitive matches it (Sheet.tsx:254: const modal = shape !== 'popover').

The invite dialog — the hard case, anchored

from components/team/InviteTeamDialog.tsx, positioned by hooks/useAnchoredDialogPosition.ts · charcoal ground

A form in a popover looks like a rule broken until you ask what the reader must do next. This is not a record and not a command — it is two short choices about the roster you are looking at, and covering that roster would hide the answer to “who is missing?”. The tree already made this call: the invite dialog is the one Radix dialog in the product that takes an anchor. The house shape keeps the decision and drops the second implementation.

The boundary

What a popover may never be

  • Never a recordA vendor in a 320px drawer hanging off a card is a sheet that lost its shape. If the content survives the control being scrolled off screen, it was never a popover.
  • Never a commitmentA popover closes on an outside click, and people dismiss them by accident — Apple's own guidance says so. Nothing that spends money, sends a letter or tells nineteen people their shifts may be confirmed inside one. The seal never appears in this shape.
  • Never a long formFour fields is the ceiling, and only when they are about the thing the anchor names. Beyond that the reader is filling in a page through a slot.
  • Never anchorlessA popover with no visible trigger is a panel that forgot to centre itself. If you cannot point at what opened it, it is the wrong shape.
