Purpose III

A confirmation that carries the seal — publish week

the seal is components/mudavym/HoldToApprove.tsx · charcoal ground, carried through the portal

The house ceremony, rationed. A panel earns the seal when the act reaches people outside this screen and cannot be quietly undone — publishing a week sends nineteen notifications. Routine and bulk acts get the same die pressed dry: a button, no wax. The panel states the consequence in full before the thumb goes down, because a confirmation that only says “are you sure?” has told the reader nothing they did not already know.

The boundary

What a panel may never be

  • Never a recordA centred panel covers the middle of the page, which is where a list lives. Put a vendor in one and you have hidden the comparison the reader opened it to make. An object is a sheet.
  • Never a menuCentring a list of four choices breaks the tie to the control that produced them and makes a small decision feel like a large one. A choice is a popover.
  • Never a form of ten fieldsAt 620 × 76vh a long form scrolls inside a box inside a page. If it does not fit, it is an object being edited — a sheet — or a route.
  • Never a noticeA panel demands an answer. Something with nothing to answer — “saved”, “seven rows imported” — belongs on the page it happened on, not over it.
