  • The design is A, the margin note — your pick, "margin role lock it" (ADR 0251). One tip per page, no getting-started path across pages — "One per page (Recommended)".
  • Not now hides the tip at once; it may come back on a later visit. A snoozed tip comes back after four hours, even in the same session; two tips turned away in one session stop tips for the rest of it (ADR 0251 D2, as corrected).
  • Don't show tips again — every tip, every page, until you turn them back on in Help → Ways back in → Page tips — "All tips (Recommended)". Built on PR #570, still open; on main today it turns off only that page's tip.
  • A tour starts only from the tip's Show me; in the Mudavym shell, Help has no tour starter (ADR 0251 D2). A tour you start always plays.
  • Steps follow the job, not the screen. The Orders tour is built (PR #572, merged): Write an order → Approve it → Follow it → Check what arrived.
