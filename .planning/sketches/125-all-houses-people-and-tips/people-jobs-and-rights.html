Already decided, drawn as decided

  • A job and the right to do it are given in one step — your answer, "Both". The giver picks for this job or from now on (ADR 0253, round 2).
  • What staff do today — receive, count, create orders — is closed until given (round 2).
  • Zone setup: owners, managers "+ the people they assign" (your words, ADR 0238) — already built as one switch on the team sheet.
  • Pay: an owner's. An owner switches it on for a manager (ADR 0215), and only for someone who sees the house's money (ADR 0253 F14).
  • Sending to vendors: owners and managers send, and each send is sealed (ADR 0175). Letting someone else send, or giving "place orders up to an amount", is an owner's — or a manager's where an owner allowed that action, up to a cap the owner sets (your round-6 words, confirmed per action in round 12 — ADR 0253, on PR #566).
  • Managers get one of three packages: Full, Standard or Light (round 8). The order right a Standard manager gives carries no amount; the house's approval rule decides every amount (round 12).
  • Managers grant manager or staff (ADR 0162). Area leads act on cards only (ADR 0218).
  • ADR 0238 said: when a second per-person right appears, one register of rights starts paying for itself. There are two today (zones, pay). This sketch is that register.
  • The server refuses what a person may not do; the page only hides what would be refused.

  1. Money limits — who gives: "Yes, per action (Recommended)"; how much: "Owner sets the cap (Recommended)", which may be higher than the manager's own limit (round 12, after round 5's "Yes, any amount" and round 6).
