Open — yours to decide

  1. Which direction — A One Total, B The Ledger, C The Letter (or A with B one tap away).
  2. Who sees "All houses" — owners of 2+ houses only; or managers too, without money; or a new "group" role.
  3. Which houses are "all" — every house you own; your whole organisation; or saved sets like "the Antalya houses".
  4. What "profit" means today — till sales − goods delivered − hours worked needs a till in every house; without one, the house is named and left out.
  5. A house that can't be read — hold the total back, or show it marked partial.
  6. Two currencies — one total in one currency at the month's average rate, or a total per currency.
  7. Group goals — each house on its own (rolls up), or one target on the total; can a house change its own.
  8. Group decisions — per kind (prices, vendors, approvals, stock levels, how we serve): applies outright, may keep its own, or each manager accepts.
