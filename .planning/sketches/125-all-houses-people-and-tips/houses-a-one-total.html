Answered — your picks (ADR 0252)

These were the open forks when the frames were first drawn, on 2026-10-01. Every one is answered now, and the frames above draw the answers.

  1. Which direction — "A with B one tap away is great". A leads; B, the ledger, is one tap away. C is not taken.
  2. Who sees "All houses" — "Owners of 2+ houses (Recommended)". Round 5 widened it to managers of two or more houses, and round 8 gave it to Full and Standard managers only; Light managers don't get it (ADR 0253).
  3. Which houses are "all" — "All you own; changes list houses (Recommended)". Every house you own now. A goal or a decision lists its houses for you to confirm, never "all" by default.
  4. What leads — "Sales first, profit later (Recommended)". Sales, net of tax and tips, lead; goods bought and hours worked sit beside them. A house shows profit only when all three are known in one currency. A manager sees profit only with both the money right and pay access ("Needs money and pay (Recommended)", ADR 0253 F14).
  5. A house that can't be read — "Show it, from N of M (Recommended)". Three states: counted; not read (it read before and now fails, flagged); not set up (listed under setup, outside "N of M").
  6. Two currencies — in your words, the currency sign opens "a popover that will show our used currencies to choose from", and every figure is restated in the one you pick. With no rate typed: "Yes, ECB daily, dated (Recommended)" and "Each day at its own rate (Recommended)". The first drawing's single total at a month's average rate is gone.
  7. Group goals — "Each house its own, linked (Recommended)". The headline reads "N of M houses on target", with a "not measured" state; a money goal takes a typed target per house. Managers may change their house's goals, currency and time zone, and you're told ("Managers, but you're told (Recommended)").
  8. Group decisions — "Copy once, show drift (Recommended)". The value is copied into each chosen house; later this page shows which houses changed it. A money limit copies only between houses that share one known currency.
  9. Also answered — "Only when 2 can be read (Recommended)", "Whole houses, said so (Recommended)" and "Houses you own today (Recommended)".
