The idea. The same Friday morning at Sim Bistro, seen by four people. Owners and managers get the house; staff get their jobs. Staff see only what they need and the buttons to finish it — and the screen grows by exactly what they are given.

Already decided, drawn as decided

  • Staff work on the phone app and the web — your answer, "Phone app and web (Recommended)" (ADR 0253). Their screen opens on today's jobs.
  • Staff see their jobs plus the rooms their rights open; everything else is hidden and the server refuses it (round 5). What staff do today is closed until given (round 2).
  • Managers get one of three packages (round 8). Full: "All houses" with profit and pay, owners' wages still hidden; may give money and send rights where you allowed it. Standard: "All houses" with sales, goals and decisions, no profit or pay. Light: their own house only.
  • Pay: an owner's. An owner switches it on for a manager (ADR 0215), only for someone who sees the house's money; profit needs both (ADR 0253 F14).
  • Sending to vendors: owners and managers send, each send sealed (ADR 0175). Money and send rights are given by an owner, or by a manager where an owner allowed that action, up to the owner's cap (round 6, confirmed in round 12 — ADR 0253, on PR #566).
  • Zone setup: owners, managers "+ the people they assign" (your words, ADR 0238) — already built as one switch on the team sheet.
  • Managers grant manager or staff (ADR 0162). Area leads act on cards only (ADR 0218).
