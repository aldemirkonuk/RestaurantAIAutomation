Mudavym. The House.

Not a sixth idea. This is the founder's own keep-pile from 077–081, assembled into one identity and finished: the müdavim story, the double rule and the full stop, the Fraunces house voice, the settle row-expand, hold-to-approve — and one seal colour that belongs to nobody else.

§01Premise — the kept story
müdavimTurkish · noun
The regular. The one who is there every day — who the house knows, and who knows the house.

Most restaurant software watches the restaurant like a security camera. Mudavym sits at the bar like its most loyal regular: it knows every vendor by name, every bottle by vintage, every shift by feel — and it quietly takes care of things.

That is the whole brief, and it is also the whole design problem. A regular is warm but never familiar; precise but never pedantic; present every night without asking to be thanked. So the surface is paper, not chrome. The voice is a serif that only speaks when the product speaks. The numbers are mono, tabular, and never invented. And the whole system spends its colour on exactly one thing — a seal, pressed where a decision was made. Hospitality on the surface; arithmetic underneath; nothing loud anywhere.

Paper, ink, one sealThree material facts and nothing else. Surfaces are alpha tints of warm ink, not painted grays; structure is hairlines, not cards; colour appears only where a human decided something.
Serif speaks, sans works, mono countsFraunces at SOFT 42 / WONK 1 — a wine label that learned software — is rationed to once per screen. Instrument Sans runs the interface. JetBrains Mono keeps the books.
The hand that writes, settlesEverything lands on the house curve and stays landed. One emphatic motion exists — the stamp — because entering something into the book deserves a small ceremony, and nothing else does.
Warmth arrives quotedMudavym's own copy stays exact. The warmth on screen belongs to the people it quotes: the vendor's reply, the staff note, the drafted letter's courtesy. The brand is the room; the people are the warmth.
§02Logo — the double rule and the full stop, resolved

Three lockups from two kept marks

The founder kept the accountant's double rule and the full stop from 081 and rejected every mark from 077. So these are not three more sketches — they are one construction, developed: the wordmark is measured to the rule (textLength, not eyeballed), the period is the only coloured element in the identity, and every lockup carries its own clear-space and minimum-size rule. The Meter from 078 follows as the alternate, re-cut in the house face.

Lockup A · primaryThe Entry The accountant's mark under a final total: this figure is done, checked, and stands. Resolved by construction — the wordmark is set to exactly the rule measure, so the double rule is not decoration under the name but the name's own baseline of record. The full stop is the only colour in the system; it is the same mark that ends every verdict the product writes.
rule weight = stem weight of the M ÷ 2 rule gap = 2× rule weight clear space = cap height of the M, all four sides min width 128px / 34mm below min → use Lockup C
Lockup B · stationery & documentsThe Sealed Entry The seal is not a second logo — it is the full stop, pressed: same colour, same job, physical size. This is the lockup for purchase orders, vendor letters, exports and invoices, because it is the one place where a mark should look like it was applied rather than printed. The same wax lands in-product when an order is approved (§08 stamp), so the brand mark and the product's most important interaction are literally the same object.
seal Ø = 1.5× cap height seal-to-wordmark gap = seal Ø ÷ 3 strapline optional; drop below 300px seal alone = the approval stamp, never the logo
Lockup C · app tile, favicon, small sizesThe Stop Below Lockup A's minimum, the identity collapses to two elements that survive anything: an M and a period. The period sits on the M's baseline at its right shoulder — the place a sentence would actually end — so the tile reads as “M.” and not as a bullet. §03Slogans — five kept, three new

What the house says out loud

The five the founder kept, each with the rationale that earned it — then three new candidates written to the same grammar (balanced antithesis, rhythm-of-three, or name·period·claim). None of them contains an adjective about the software.

01KEPT · 077

Every vendor. Every bottle. Every shift.

Deadpan rhythm-of-three that inventories the actual product surface. The most literal of the set and therefore the most provable — every noun in it is a page in the app. Works as a footer line under any lockup, and as the three-beat spine of a homepage.

02KEPT · 077

Less counting, more cooking.

The balanced-antithesis structure aimed at the owner's real trade-off: hours in the cellar with a clipboard versus hours on the line. Names the enemy and the reward in four words, and never once claims the software is clever. The most campaign-ready line in the set.

03KEPT · 079

Set the table. We'll keep the books.

Division of labour, stated as an offer rather than a boast. It gives the reader the dignified half of the work — hospitality is yours — and quietly takes the half nobody wants. The only slogan in the set that says “we”, and it earns it by conceding first.

04KEPT · 081

Every bottle, accounted for.

Accountant-grade rigour meeting the wine-cellar heritage in three words. “Accounted for” carries both meanings at once — counted, and answerable — which is exactly the honesty doctrine in §04. Pairs naturally with the seal.

05KEPT · 081

Your müdavim, at the books.

The line that earns the name. The müdavim is the regular who never misses a night; this one sits at the back table keeping your ledger. Warmth underneath the rigour, and the only place the Turkish is allowed to appear in marketing — once, with the meaning right beside it.

06NEW

Warm room. Cold arithmetic.

The mandate said out loud, in two beats. The passion stays yours; the precision is ours. This is the only candidate that names the brand's whole tension explicitly, which makes it the strongest internal north star even if a softer line leads externally.

07NEW

Nothing counted twice. Nothing missed once.

The honesty doctrine as balanced antithesis — a promise you can audit rather than an adjective you have to believe. It is also a literal statement of what the lots-as-source-of-truth inventory model does, so marketing and engineering can both stand behind it.

08NEW

Mudavym. The house's own regular.

Name · period · category claim — the motion.dev grammar, and it uses the period that is the logo. Delivers the etymology in five words to a reader who has never seen “müdavim”, which is the one job the name cannot do by itself.

Sales material, not in-app: “Your back of house, front of mind.” — kept from 079 and flagged deliberately. It is a good deck line and a bad product line: it addresses the buyer's anxiety rather than the operator's night, and the brand's own rule is that the product never talks about itself while someone is working. Decks and one-pagers only; never a screen, never an email footer.
§04Voice — how the book writes

Serif speaks. Sans works. Mono counts.

One contract, three faces, no overlap. Kept whole from 079 (the Fraunces house voice) and 081 (§03 “how the book writes”), with 077's warmth allowed into the serif register — contained by rule 04, which is load-bearing.

Fraunces — the house speaks
Good evening, chef.

Variable serif at SOFT 42 / WONK 1 — warm, slightly wonky, a wine label that learned software. Used only when the product itself speaks: greetings, questions, decisions asked, verdicts given. At most once per screen. If two serif sentences compete, one becomes sans.

Instrument Sans — the house works
Approve & enter

Every control, row, label, button, empty state and error. It never performs and never charms; it is the hand doing the job while the house talks. Warmth here would read as chatter.

JetBrains Mono — the house counts
$1,860.00 · PO-1042

Money, counts, IDs, timestamps, kickers. Tabular figures, always, so a column never shivers. Numbers are never spelled out, never celebrated, and never rounded in a headline and contradicted in the detail.

  • Say what's known; mark what isn't.Unknowns render as “—”, never 0, never a filled bar, never an average passed off as a reading. An empty cell is information; faking it is the one unforgivable sin of this brand.
  • Every figure can show its working.Any number the product asserts opens into its arithmetic — price × qty, source, timestamp. If it cannot, it is labelled an estimate in those words.
  • Verbs first, numbers second, adjectives never.“Approve 12 bottles — $930.00” beats “Smart reorder suggestion”. Banned outright: smart, seamless, powerful, effortless, AI-powered. Confidence is shown by being exact.
  • Serif is the product speaking — once per screen.The greeting, the verdict, or the question. Everything else is working text. Without this cap the direction collapses into a themed notebook app.
  • Plain trade words, no software words.“Bottles in”, “close the night”, “the purchase book”, “at the door”. Banned: SKU, sync, workflow, leverage, dashboard (in copy). The reader runs a restaurant.
  • Ask before acting; record after acting.The AI drafts; the human enters. Every consequential action produces a visible line — who, what, when. Nothing auto-sends, ever.
  • Warmth arrives quoted.Vendor replies, staff notes, the drafted letter's own courtesy — serif italic, attributed. The frame around them stays deadpan. The brand is the room; the people are the warmth.
  • Endings are spoken slowly and plainly.Deletion and closure copy states exactly what is lost, in full sentences, no euphemism, no guilt-trip button, no tiny gray cancel. Respect at the exit is the last proof of honesty.

§05The seal — one colour that belongs to nobody else

Ten candidates, stamped on both surfaces

The open question, made decidable. Each candidate is shown as actual pressed wax on paper and on lamplight, with its dark-mode sibling re-tokenized (never the same hex), a one-line argument for why it is not a startup colour, and measured WCAG contrast — computed, not eyeballed. Click any card to try it on the entire board: logos, seals, chips, sample screens, everything.

Currently wearing İznik · #1A5E6B / #5FB0BC

Ruled out before the board started

  • #635BFF Stripe indigo, #5E6AD2 Linear violet, and the whole SaaS purple-blue axis — the default costume of every developer-tool brand of the last decade. A restaurant is not a dev tool.
  • Terracotta / clay (#C1653F family) — the house style of AI-generated design in 2025–26. It reads as “an LLM chose this”, which is the one thing this brand cannot afford.
  • #9E4249 and #CD2D5B — the incumbents. Generic burgundy is wine-shop signage; it says “we sell wine”, not “we keep your books”. Pomegranate and Wax Oxblood below are on the board as the honest, deepened versions of that instinct — judge them against the incumbents directly.
§06Palette — paper, ink, one seal

Two themes, one structure

One seal per screen.If the seal appears twice on a surface, one of them is wrong. It marks acts of record — approvals, drafted letters, the wordmark's full stop, kicker numerals — and nothing else. It never fills a background, never colours data, never decorates.
Charts draw in the ink ladder.Grayscale-warm ramps for every series; chroma appears only where a decision lives. The one coloured series on a chart is the anomaly, and it wears the seal.
Re-tokenize, never reuse.The dark seal is a different hex from the light seal, always. A colour that passes on paper will fail on lamplight and vice versa; the token name is the constant, the value never is.
ink-4 is not for content.It exists for the dash and for disabled states. ink-3 on paper (4.4:1) is the floor for any text a human is expected to read.

§08Motion — the hand that writes, settles

Nine tokens, five of them live below

Springs are written as stiffness/damping first and beziers second, so the same vocabulary ports to motion in React today and Reanimated withSpring on the phone later. One emphatic motion exists — stamp — because entering something into the book deserves a small ceremony and nothing else does. Every token is mirrored exactly in motion.json.

Reduced motion

the whole set collapses to state change

Every token above is gated on prefers-reduced-motion: springs jump to their target value, transitions collapse to ~1ms, the shimmer stops and the thinking line becomes static italic text. Nothing in this system carries meaning that only motion delivers — a row that expands is expanded, a stamp that lands is a stamp, whether or not it moved.
