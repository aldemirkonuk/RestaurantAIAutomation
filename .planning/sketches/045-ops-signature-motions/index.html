Moment 1

Ledger fold

Why it's signature

A manager's decision visibly becomes part of the permanent operational record.

When it triggers

A recommendation is committed with its reason and owner.

Motion + haptic (~320ms)

  • Reanimated: decision card compresses into burgundy ledger rule
  • Skia: timestamp draws as card folds
  • Rule slides into shift timeline as new entry
  • Haptic: one restrained rigid tick as it joins the timeline

Why not a gimmick

Clarifies the transition from pending work to recorded accountability.

Moment 3

Provenance stitch

Why it's signature

It makes chain-of-custody completion tangible without celebrating routine data entry.

When it triggers

A receiving lot passes all checks and its cellar location is saved.

Motion + haptic (~500ms)

  • Skia: fine thread connects supplier → lot → inspection → bin
  • Reanimated: line tightens at final node with spring
  • Each node highlights as thread reaches it
  • Haptic: one soft success pulse at stitch completion

Why not a gimmick

Every animated node represents stored provenance that staff can reopen.
