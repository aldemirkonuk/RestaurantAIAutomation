How this column is counted

Numerator: invoiced lines whose unit price equals the agreed price for that line, in the agreement's currency. Denominator: invoiced lines that have an agreed price. Lines with none — market lines, first orders, lines the paper could not be read for — are not comparable and counted beside the figure, not inside it.

Agreed means the order's confirmed price (the built behaviour of ADR 0103/0104); landed vs agreed from ADR 0054 is Proposed and not used here.

Sorting by this column puts too few and not comparable rows last, in their own group.
