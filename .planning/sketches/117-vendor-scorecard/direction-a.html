What changes for a Turkish house, and only this: money in ₺ with tr-TR separators (₺1.200,00), dates as dd.mm, the window chips in g for gün; the on-time line carries the ADR 0103 clock fact where it applies — a response window that closed by silence is recorded as what the law deems (delivery-clock.service.ts `lapseDeeming`), and the sheet prints that sentence rather than a tick. A vendor reached by phone has no reply record; the line says so instead of scoring the silence.

A prior window with 4 replies prints "under 5, no comparison" — the minimum is per measure, not per vendor, so the on-time line can compare while the reply line cannot.
