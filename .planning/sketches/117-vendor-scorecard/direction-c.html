The scorecard is a day book per vendor, filed in the Sorting Office on /documents-reports: a sixth drawer, Vendors, and a reading pane that lists every scored event the house's registers hold for one vendor in the window — a delivery landed, a line short at the door, an invoice line above the agreed price, a reply that came 5 h 12 after ours, a credit memo that settled a claim — newest first, each entry carrying the fact it contributes. The five tallies at the top are sums of the entries drawn beneath them, and a tally is also a filter: press it and the entries that remain are its rows. No figure exists on this page that is not also visible as the entries that produced it. The conversation list itself stays on /communications (ADR 0149, row 25); a reply here is a scored fact with a link to its thread, never the thread.

How Credits is counted

Numerator: the amount the vendor allowed on claims settled by a credit memo — never the amount we asked. Denominator: the amount asked on every claim opened in the window. A claim the rep promised is in the denominator and not the numerator; promised is not recovered, and the entry ages it in days.

A window with no claims prints $0.00 of $0.00 · no claims, which is not a clean record and is not a bad one.
