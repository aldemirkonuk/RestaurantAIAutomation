§2 · The adversarial pass on Track A1's bake-off protocol

ADR 0039 assigns this pass here by name. It runs before the bake-off, not after it reports.
SEALED 2026-08-28 These five axes are fixed before scripts/bakeoff/ is readable from this worktree. An axis added after reading the protocol must be marked added-after-reading, with its reason. A protocol reviewed after a scored run is reviewed by someone who already knows who won.
AXIS 1 · workload validity

Can the scored set say what "done" means?

102 cards · 58 read NONE (gap) · 8 execute

ADR 0039 names cards.json as the workloads. A bake-off scored where 57% of the workloads carry no verdict basis measures completion, not capability — the fastest finisher wins a race nobody defined.

Done when the protocol names its scored subset with a count and a per-card verdict basis — or a Sev-1 is written naming the gap and its size.
AXIS 2 · rigged axes

Which candidate wins each axis by construction?

3 named suspects, from the record

An axis about fitting the saga/DLQ model scores in-house 1.0 by definition; "harness overhead" measured without the messaging surface prices two candidates on different substrates; weights revised after a pilot run.

Done when every axis carries one line naming which candidate it favours before any data. No line = a finding. Weights revised post-run = Sev-1.
AXIS 3 · candidate set

What is not on the list?

OD-03 names 3 · OD-52 reframes the question

Under OD-52's reframe — "which reasoning layer sits ON our messaging infra" — the null candidate (no separate harness; a loop behind the model-client boundary that already exists and is already guarded) is a real candidate.

Done when the protocol includes it or excludes it in writing. This axis names gaps and never picks — the fork stays open.
AXIS 4 · hidden integration cost

What does the winner cost to actually adopt?

the guard · the core/ diet · 24 modules

A candidate calling providers through its own client breaks a blocking, two-runtime, shrink-only-debt guard. ADR 0039 forbids extending core/ while A1 runs — so a candidate whose adoption path is a core/ extension is unpilotable under the plan running the bake-off.

Done when each of the three is a priced line or an explicit out-of-scope statement. Neither = written up.
AXIS 5 · who grades the run?

ADR 0036's line, on its first exercise

RM-1 methodology / aio operation

If the protocol is authored and graded by the team that operates the harness, the line ADR 0036 locked is broken the first time it is used — and that is a finding against the seam, not against the protocol or its authors.

Done when the grader and grading basis are named. Author = grader → one finding to Research & Math, one line to Decision Office.
WHAT THIS PASS IS NOT

No pick. No score. No gate.

It does not choose a candidate, score anything, block the run, or require anyone's agreement to proceed. If A1's owners run the bake-off the day after receiving a Sev-1, that is within their rights — and the finding simply starts ageing.

Findings-only is locked (OD-16). Follow-ups: one revision round (7d), and a post-run read filed before OD-03 is flipped to Resolved.

§4.1 · Two documents disagree about what this function reviews

Shown as two differently-sized surfaces, because that is the actual disagreement.

Reading A — the charter

9 of 21 department-level line units. Reviewable at a fortnightly cadence — and it leaves L4's methodology owner outside the mandate, so the layer stack is reviewed everywhere except at its metric spine.

Reading B — ORG_STRUCTURE §3, as it reads today

foundation/ORG_STRUCTURE.md §3 · "All divisions" (+ a dated 2026-08-24 correction note)
21 of 21 — every division. 100 units and 76 teams beneath them, reviewed by a function with no build capacity and one human sweep a fortnight.
Filed, not resolved. Wave-3 rules forbid touching a charter, and a review function that quietly re-scopes itself in its own agenda is doing exactly what it exists to catch. Position offered to the founder: §3 is right in principle and unaffordable in practice — the honest form is "all divisions, on a published rotation, with the rotation itself reported as a coverage number." The call is the founder's either way.
