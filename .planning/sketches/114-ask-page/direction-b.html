The Book.

The folio of asks is the page: a ledger the house keeps, one folio per question, each with an address, a version, and the trail of what happened to its answer — the draft it became, the seal that refused it, the re-read that changed it. The open folio is a document: what was read comes first, then the sentence, then every row behind it, then the trail. Asking is writing the next line at the foot of the book. The shelf is one popover away and is the book's first page when nothing has been asked. This is the memory the ⌘⇧K panel structurally cannot have.
