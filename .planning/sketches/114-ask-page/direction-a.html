The Reading Room.

The shelf of fifteen house readings is the page. You ask by picking a reading (or by typing — Haiku maps the words onto one, and the page shows the pick before anything runs). Every reading is one object, so it opens as the right Sheet (440); a figure inside it opens to its rows with the 0fr→1fr settle. The book of past asks is the right rail. What Mudavym does not keep, and what it has not built, sit on the same shelf in their own words — never as an emptiness.
