The Pass.

One column, one ticket at a time, and the reading spent on screen: the pick, then each source as it returns, then the figures — big, first, each one a cell — then the sentence beneath them as a caption. ADR 0145's accepted cost (latency spent deliberately) becomes the page's material: the 60 s rule runs across the top, every line carries the instant it landed, and a denial is a ticket that visibly stopped at the line that failed. Earlier tickets stack beneath; the whole book is a Sheet; the shelf is a Panel and the empty page.
