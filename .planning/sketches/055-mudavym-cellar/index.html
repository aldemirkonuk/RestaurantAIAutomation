The Cellar

00 — Premise

The cellar, lit.

Mudavym's home is the room where the restaurant already keeps its most valuable things: dark, calm, temperature-controlled, lit by one flame. Dark is the primary theme — built for the hours the product is actually used, from prep to close — and daylight is the derived one. The darkness is wine-tinted, never gray. One accent, the candle, marks the only thing that ever matters on a busy Friday: what needs your hand next.

dark-first one luminous accent low-glare ink wine-tinted near-blacks attention = the candle
01 — Logo

Three marks, one room

Strokes are ink (currentColor); the flame is always the candle accent. Wordmark set in Fraunces — the voice face — except concept B, which pairs the quiet ring with the working sans.

A · The Vault
The cellar arch with a single flame inside — the room and its light. Primary candidate: reads at 16px favicon (arch + dot), and the flame alone works as an app icon.
B · The Regular's Ring
The ring a glass leaves on the bar — the mark of the müdavim, the one whose seat is always kept. Open circle + candle point. Quietest of the three; strongest story.
C · The Ember M
The monogram M as two cellar walls; an ember floats in the valley between them. Most conventional — a safe fallback if A/B read too abstract in the wild.
02 — Slogans

Six candidates

S1
The cellar remembers.
The direction's flagship. Heritage (wine cellar) and the actual product promise (the system never forgets a price, a case, a vendor's word) in three words. Deadpan, no adjective.
S2
The house that runs itself.
"House" is hospitality's own word for the restaurant; the autonomy claim lands in its native vocabulary. Name-period pattern: "Mudavym. The house that runs itself."
S3
Every night, like a regular.
The etymology made into a promise — müdavim, the regular who shows up every single night. Reliability as character, not as uptime percentage.
S4
Less counting, more cooking.
Balanced antithesis — names the pain it removes (counting) and the craft it returns (cooking). The most campaign-ready of the six.
S5
Set the table. We'll keep the books.
A clean division of labor: the craft stays theirs, the ledger becomes ours. Two short imperatives; warm without praising itself.
S6
Your back of house, front of mind.
Names the product's actual territory (BOH operations) with an idiom flip. Best suited to sales material aimed at owners rather than the app itself.
03 — Voice

The maître d', not the megaphone

Mudavym speaks the way a great floor manager does at 9pm on a Friday: quietly, precisely, already knowing what happens next.

1 · Calm is the register
Second person, present tense, never an exclamation mark. If the copy would sound wrong said quietly across a pass, rewrite it.
2 · Verbs first, outcomes over features
"Approve the reorder", never "Reorder approval workflow". Every sentence starts with what the reader does or what the house did.
3 · Numbers do the boasting
"12 bottles · $930 · Thursday" is the eloquence. No adjectives where a figure can stand — the brand never says "powerful" or "smart".
4 · Deadpan house, quoted warmth
Mudavym's own copy stays terse. Warmth enters only in quotation — a vendor's reply, a staff note, the agent's drafted words. The house never praises itself.
5 · Three voices, three faces
Serif (Fraunces) only when the product speaks to you — greetings, questions, decisions asked. Sans works the interface. Mono keeps the ledger: money, counts, IDs, time.
6 · Always say what happens next
Every message ends on a state or an action, never an apology. "It lands here" beats "sorry, nothing to show".
7 · A candle, not a siren
Something needing a decision is lit amber, calmly. Urgency words — now, immediately, warning — are reserved for genuine danger, which is red and rare.
8 · Empty states set the table
An empty screen is a table set before service, not a void. Say what will appear, and how to summon it sooner.

06 — Motion

Candlelight physics

Nothing snaps and nothing bounces — flames don't. Fast out of the gate, long warm settle: the house curve is cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), springs never oscillate, hovers move ≤2px, transform/opacity only, everything gated by useReducedMotion.

tokenphysicsfeel / usemotion (React) mapping
strike 140ms · (.16,1,.3,1) A match struck — hover, press, chip state. Movement ≤2px. { duration:.14, ease:[.16,1,.3,1] }
settle spring 260/32 ≈ 240ms A glass set down — toggles, chips landing, list reorder, layout shifts. { type:'spring', stiffness:260, damping:32 }
hearth 420ms · (.25,1,.5,1) A door swung open — drawers, row expands, sheets; reveal by unmasking, not fading. { duration:.42, ease:[.25,1,.5,1] }
pour spring 90/22 · ~900ms Wine poured — number tickers, KPI deltas; heavy start, gentle level-off. { type:'spring', stiffness:90, damping:22 } on AnimateNumber / useSpring
ember 1.9s · ease-in-out · mirror ∞ The flame breathing — AI working / draft ready. Replaces every spinner in the product. transition={{ repeat:Infinity, repeatType:'mirror', duration:1.9 }}
stagger 30ms First paint only — lists and KPI tiles arrive like candles being lit down a corridor. staggerChildren: 0.03
