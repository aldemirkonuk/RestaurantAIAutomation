Moment 1

Cork seat

Why it's signature

Committing a decision should feel like seating a cork — pressure, then lock — not a UI bounce.

When it triggers

Only on irreversible manager commits: approve/send PO, lock a desk decision, confirm a variance write-off.

Motion + haptic (~280ms)

  • Reanimated: control compresses ~8% on Y (cork into neck)
  • Skia: thin burgundy contact ring flashes once at seat point
  • Spring settle back to locked state
  • Haptic: impactMedium at contact

Why not a gimmick

Cork = sealed ops decision; reserved for rare commits so everyday taps stay silent.
