The Gazetteer.

The cellar page is one index of every title the house's books name, in a column on the left, and one bottle's whole record on the right — the record is the page, not a row that opens. "See everything" is the index: every register in one scroll, with running heads. "Not crowded" is the record: one bottle's depth at a time, at full width, with every card the founder named. It is keyboard-first: the arrows walk the index, Space peeks, Enter opens (sketch 103, 2a). The house itself is the first entry in the gazetteer, so /cellar with nothing chosen is the house's own record — the top boxes, the registers, the naming rule — and the h1 there is the name the founder already decided (The Cellar here; Soft drinks for the café). Every 1440 frame is drawn with the invoice book and the recipe book populated and says so; the Partial state is the ship state. The record is the page, so the hold that spends money sits in its acts — drawn mid-pour in §5 on Boğazkere.

What the two directions share

One record, three homes.

A: the row opens in place; the hold opens a 440 sheet from it, because a row that opens in place is one of the house’s non-modal surfaces and never carries the seal. B: the record is the page; the hold sits in its acts. C: the record is a 440 sheet; the hold sits in its par card. The cards are the same RowExpander cards in all three; only the home moves.

Drawn here so the founder sees the one act that spends money in every direction he is asked to choose between.
