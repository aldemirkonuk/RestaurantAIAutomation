The List.

The cellar page is the house's own drinks list, typeset the way a guest reads it — sections in the order the house sells them, prices leading — with the book's figures in the margin of every line. "See everything" is taken literally: one page, every line. It is not crowded because a list line is one line, and the ledger speaks in a quieter column. The registers become sections; /wines, /beer, /spirits, /cocktails and /non-alcoholic open this page at their section. A house with one register has one section, and the page is called what that section is called. The h1 keeps the name the founder already decided — The Cellar when wine or spirits are on, The Bar for beer and cocktails only, Soft drinks for an alcohol-free house (cellar-format.ts:196-225, 095 merged); the List is the name of this view, not of the page. Every 1440 frame is drawn with the invoice book and the recipe book populated and says so; the Partial state is the ship state. The one act that spends money — the hold — opens in a sheet from the row, never in the row itself.
