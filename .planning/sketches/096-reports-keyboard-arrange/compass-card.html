The trade. One floating card instead of a bar inside the cutting: the pad reads as a compass, and the four named outcomes underneath answer the strongest objection to arrow keys (Atlassian's own guidance, below). It costs a floating layer that has to follow a cutting as the cutting moves — and the cutting moves on every keystroke, which is a lot of repositioning to keep exactly right, and a lot of ways for the card to end up off-screen at the foot of a long sheet.

Not built. Worth building if the founder finds the in-cutting bar crowds a small cutting — it is the same handlers behind a different surface, so it is a swap, not a rewrite.
