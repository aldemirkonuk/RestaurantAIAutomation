Drag a cutting anywhere on the ruling; pull its corner to resize. Or reach it with the keyboard: Tab to its Move grip, Space to pick it up, arrows to move, Shift and an arrow to resize, Enter to place, Escape to put it back. Every one of those is also a button, so nothing here needs a drag.

What the live region says, right now

“Through the till is now at column 6 of 12, row 7, 5 columns wide, 6 rows tall.”

The sentence reports the position the RULING gave, read back out of the layout after compaction — never the position the key asked for. A nudge the ruling undoes says “did not move”.

Tabto the grip Spacepick up ← ↑ → ↓move one unit Shift+← ↑ → ↓resize Enterplace Escput it back

Three things this drawing is showing. The focus ring sits on the grip — a real button with a real name, not a tabIndex on the panel. The ghost is the dashed outline at column 8, where the cutting started, so “Escape puts it back” names a place you can see. The announcement is the panel below, which in the built page is a visually hidden aria-live region.

Everything above is built and behind mudavym_design_reports. The placing bar is not decoration: WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.7 asks for a single-POINTER alternative to dragging, and its Understanding document says keyboard equivalence is evaluated independently — so arrow keys alone would not satisfy it.
