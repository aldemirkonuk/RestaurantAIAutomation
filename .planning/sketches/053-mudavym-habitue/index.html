The Habitué

mü·da·vim/mew-dah-VEEM/ · Turkish · noun
The regular. The one who is there every day — who the house knows, and who knows the house.

Most restaurant software watches the restaurant like a security camera. Mudavym sits at the bar like its most loyal regular: it knows every vendor by name, every bottle by vintage, every shift by feel — and it quietly takes care of things. This direction puts that story everywhere: a serif voice when the house speaks, warm paper instead of software chrome, one glass of wine's worth of color, and motion that settles like a chair being tucked in. Confident. Never loud.

01 — Logo

Three ways to draw a regular

Wordmark set in Fraunces, 500 weight, near-tight tracking — a name on a menu, not a tech mark. Each concept carries one accent stroke in house wine; everything else is ink.

A · The Kept Seat

A table seen from above, one chair always held below it. The regular's seat — nobody else sits there. Reads at 16px as a favicon.

B · The Tally

The habitué's tab, kept in chalk: four visits and the fifth stroke — in wine — that closes the count. Loyalty you can draw with one hand.

C · The Homecoming Dots

“Mudavym” dropped the ü of müdavim to travel. The mark gives the two dots a home: an ü drawn as a cup with its dots restored, in wine. The dots alone become the app badge.

Recommendation: A as primary (quietest, scales smallest), C as the app-icon / loading mark — the two dots animate beautifully as a “thinking” state.

03 — Voice

How the house speaks

Two registers, strictly separated: the house (serif, warm, speaks first) and the ledger (sans/mono, plain, never performs). Warmth comes from knowing details, never from adjectives.

  1. Serif when the house speaks, sans when it works. Greetings, hand-offs and moments of care are set in Fraunces; rows, totals and controls stay in Instrument Sans.
  2. Talk like a maître d', not a dashboard. Second person, present tense, one clause where one will do.
  3. Verbs first. Every actionable line opens with what happens: Approve, Close, Walk through.
  4. Warmth is specificity. Name the vendor, the bottle, the shift — never reach for adjectives or exclamation marks instead.
  5. Numbers stay plain. The tab is the tab: tabular figures, no celebration, no dramatizing a variance.
  6. Empty is expectant, not apologetic. An empty state says what will appear here and how to invite it in.
  7. Grave things said gravely. Destructive and financial actions drop all charm: plain words, full consequence, no softening.
  8. The Turkish stays a whisper. The müdavim story appears at thresholds — first run, about, sign-off — never as a riddle in the middle of a working screen.

Daylight and after close

06 — Motion

Moves like the staff, not the lights

Springs everywhere motion exists (the react-motion lineage), tuned near-critical — spring-flavored, never oscillating. Decoration animates nowhere; state animates everywhere. All tokens live in one file and map 1:1 to the motion React library; everything gates through useReducedMotion.
