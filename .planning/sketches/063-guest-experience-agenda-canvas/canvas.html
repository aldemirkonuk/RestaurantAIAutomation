Findings — recorded, not fixed

  1. erasure_receipt_id dangles — one hit in the whole repo, the declaration. Under shredding the receipt is the only proof.
  2. Shredding does not shred a trained model. ADR 0037's revisit clause covers decrypt cost, not parameter residue.
  3. Derived ≠ destroyable. guest_pepper()'s HMAC pattern cannot back crypto-shredding; keys must be stored, not derived.
  4. Taste's entry trigger already fired (OD-11 closed 08-24). The real hold is NF-B's, and the charter says otherwise.
  5. k has no founding value anywhere — yet lowering it "below" that value is founder-only.
  6. The 2-quarter stop-clock currently runs against a founder-set hold.
  7. 5 of 6 card triggers have publisher NONE. The weekly schedule is the only live one.

  • A synthetic NF-B corpus to dry-run the research plan — synthetic rows make every denominator lie, and "it is only a fixture" is exactly the mechanism ADR 0029 §6.4 predicts.
  • Designing key management — ADR 0037 gives it to privacy-engineering.
  • Any recommendation on OD-05/OD-07, and any task that assumes an outcome.
  • Building either guard — specs here, builds addressed to Engineering.
