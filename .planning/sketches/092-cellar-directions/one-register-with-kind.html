Adapting to the house

In this direction the adaptation is invisible, which is its strongest argument: there is nothing to hide, because there are no register cards. The facet strip is built from the kinds present, so a house that pours one kind sees one chip and never learns the others exist.

What this direction costs

It was blocked until this pass: beverage_kind is computed in the database and was dropped by WinesService.mapWine before it reached the browser, so the facet had nothing to filter on. That field is now on the wire, and /beverages and /cocktails are served, so the facet is buildable today for browsing. What it still needs is the harder half: one table with one row grammar means one set of columns, and a whisky expression, a keg and a bottle of Burgundy do not share one. The four-child IA the founder chose keeps each kind’s own vocabulary; this direction trades that for a single view of everything. That is the fork, and it is the founder’s to settle.
