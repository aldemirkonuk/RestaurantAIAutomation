Three motions that give weight to real decisions

Each moment is spatially anchored, fires rarely, and maps to an irreversible ops action. Prototype timing and haptic cues here; ship in RN Skia + Reanimated.

Moment 1

Sediment settle

Why it's signature

A decision doesn't just "save" — it visibly settles like sediment falling still in a rested bottle, giving weight to a manager's judgment.

When it triggers

On confirming a consequential decision at the manager desk (approving a 86'd item, locking a par change, committing a shift plan) — not on trivial toggles.

Motion + haptic (~450ms)

  • Skia: fine dark particles suspended over the card
  • On release: drift down, compact into 2px "lees" bottom border
  • Card tint: pending amber → settled ink
  • Haptic: one soft mediumImpact when particles touch the line

Why not a gimmick

Spatially anchored to the decision, reads as finality/commitment, particle count tiny and monochrome.

Moment 2

Cellar breath

Why it's signature

The cellar map "breathes" once when you enter a low-stock zone, so scarcity is felt in the body before it's read in a number.

When it triggers

First time a zone view loads (or zone scrolls into focus) where a bin crossed below par — once per zone per session.

Motion + haptic (~300ms)

  • Reanimated: scale 1.0 → 0.97 → 1.0 spring on affected bins
  • Skia: cool slate-blue radial draft sweeps inward, dissipates
  • Below-par bins: 1px breathing outline ~1s
  • Haptic: light selection at contraction trough

Why not a gimmick

Replaces jarring red badge with somatic cue; fires rarely; stays in wine-cellar sensory world (cool air, stillness).

Moment 3

Cork commit

Why it's signature

Sending a purchase order ends with the tactile finality of seating a cork — the order is sealed, not merely submitted.

When it triggers

Final "Send order" in procurement/receiving handoff, after review — highest-stakes button in that flow.

Motion + haptic (~500ms)

  • Label recedes; Skia cork cylinder descends into button neck
  • Spring overshoot-then-settle; pressure glow pulses once
  • Button locks to sealed state
  • Haptic: soft press on entry, firm mediumImpact on seat (double-beat)

Why not a gimmick

Maps 1:1 to irreversible money-moving action; prevents double-sends; cork metaphor is intrinsic to wine.
