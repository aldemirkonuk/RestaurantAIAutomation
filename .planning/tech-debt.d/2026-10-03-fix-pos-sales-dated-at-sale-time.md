## The Toast door still dates a sale's stock when the webhook arrives, not when the order closed — OPEN — 2026-10-03

Found while building [ADR 0281](../decisions/0281-a-pos-sale-is-dated-by-its-check-and-the-import-says-what-it-did.md) on `fix/pos-sales-dated-at-sale-time`. That ADR dates the generic import's stock and consumption by one reading of the check's `closed_at`, and gives `apply_stock_movement` and `record_glass_pour` the optional `p_occurred_at` that makes it possible. The Toast door is a second, separate writer of the same two RPCs: `applyOrderSaleEffects` calls them at `apps/api-gateway/src/toast/toast.service.ts:654`, `:672` and `:683` with no `p_occurred_at`, so both still default to `now()`. The order's close time is in hand (`webhookDto.order.closedAt`, kept on the row as `toast_closed_at` at `:460`). A Toast webhook that arrives late, or is replayed, therefore books its stock on the day it arrived, exactly as A-007 measured on the generic path. Toast writes no `wine_consumption_log` row at all, so only the ledger date is wrong there.

**Fix.** Pass `p_occurred_at` at the three call sites, from `saleInstant(order.closedAt, Date.now()).at` (`apps/api-gateway/src/pos-hub/pos-hub.service.ts:121`), write `toast_closed_at` from the same call's `closedAt` so the order and its stock carry one reading, and count what happened the way `ingest()` now does. Not done on this branch: one operation per branch, and the Toast door has its own tests and fixtures.

## A check whose first delivery is already voided puts back stock it never took — OPEN — 2026-10-03

`applyStockEffects` books every line of a voided check as a `return` of `+qty` under the key `${idem}:void` (`apps/api-gateway/src/pos-hub/pos-hub.service.ts:1201`, `:1204`). It never asks whether the sale itself was ever booked. A POS that sends a check for the first time already voided (opened, voided and closed before its first webhook, or a CSV import of a day that includes voids) therefore adds stock to the shelf that no sale ever removed. ADR 0281 does not change this. It dates that return by the check's `closed_at`, and the import result counts it under `stock.booked`, so the phantom return is now visible as a count but is still written.

**Fix (needs a decision first).** A void could move stock only when a ledger row under the sale's own key exists, and otherwise be counted and skipped. Whether a never-booked void is skipped, queued, or booked is a product call, since a POS that never sends the sale would then never see the return.
