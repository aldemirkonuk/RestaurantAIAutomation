## POS money on seven role-open analytics routes reaches staff — OPEN — 2026-10-09

Entry (i) of this branch. Filed by `fix/cost-and-revenue-from-the-pos` (#617), from the ADR 0090 audit at `cc3bfdb10` (PR comment 6074681879, final adjudication item 2). Claim: `../decisions/claims.d/fix-cost-of-goods-reads-what-sold.jsonl:2` (status open).

**What is true at this branch's head.** `AnalyticsController` carries `@UseGuards(JwtAuthGuard)` on the class (`apps/api-gateway/src/analytics/analytics.controller.ts:117`) and nothing more on seven reads: `GET /analytics/financial/:restaurantId` (`:160`), `table-performance` (`:684`), `waiters` (`:702`), `basket` (`:719`), `hot-tables` (`:729`), `pos-revenue` (`:1110`) and `overview` (`:1168`); `route-access.expected.json` pins each as `"open"` (`apps/api-gateway/src/auth/guards/route-access.expected.json:3`, `:24-27`, `:46-47`). Each serves a sales figure to any member of the house: `pos-revenue` sums non-voided `pos_checks.total`; `table-performance` prints per-server revenue, average check and tip share (its description, `:698`); `waiters`, `basket` and `hot-tables` read the same checks through `TableAnalyticsService`; `financial` serves `revenue`, `cogs` and the margin dollars; `overview` composes `financial` with the other lenses (`:1172`). `pos_checks.*` is the `sales` class (`apps/api-gateway/src/ask-readings/reading-data-classes.ts:123`), and `ROLE_POLICY` (`:180`) gives `sales` to the owner and manager rows (`:181-182`) and not to staff (`:183`): the rule ADR 0290 §5 applies on the dashboard (`../decisions/0290-the-dashboard-tells-the-houses-day-true.md:44`) and ADR 0317 restates (`../decisions/0317-only-an-owner-or-manager-rules-a-day-out.md:31`).

**What this branch changes.** Before it, `financial` served a shelf-value proxy as revenue and delivered purchases as cost of goods; after it, `revenue`, `salesCoverage`, `cogs` and the margin are read from the till (ADR 0298). It widens one route that was already open and already served margin dollars, and `pos-revenue` served the same class of figure before this branch. Not a regression of this branch, and not fixed here, because the one rule belongs to every route at once.

**Owed, for its own lane.** One rule across the seven routes: either `RolesGuard` with `@Roles("owner", "manager")`, as the exclusion writes carry (`analytics.controller.ts:1464-1465`), or the dashboard's shape, `withSales = policyFor(role).sees.includes("sales")` with the sales fields withheld and named absent. Which of the two is that lane's fork to record: the dashboard withholds, the writes refuse, and the two differ for the platform `admin` as well (`RolesGuard` refuses it, ADR 0164; `ROLE_POLICY` reads it the owner row). The claim row holds once every one of the seven carries `@Roles(`, `policyFor(` or `withSales` between its decorators and the next route.
