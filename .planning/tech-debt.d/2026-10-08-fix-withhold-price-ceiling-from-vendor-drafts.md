## A vendor-letter draft can carry the house's ceiling price — CLOSED on `fix/withhold-price-ceiling-from-vendor-drafts` — 2026-10-08

Closes the entry of the same title filed OPEN on `data/f106-reconcile-pending-drafts` (`tech-debt.d/2026-10-08-data-f106-reconcile-pending-drafts.md`, #679). The draft that was found was never sent. A read-only production count, the same day, found 0 sent or send-unconfirmed letters holding a ceiling distinct from their target.

**Fix.** It has two layers, both in `services/agent-orchestrator/core/house_only_figures.py`:
- **The model never sees the ceiling.** `vendor_safe_intent` removes `HOUSE_ONLY_INTENT_KEYS` (`max_acceptable_price`) before `RESPONSE_SYSTEM_PROMPT`'s `{intent_description}` is built (`provider_conversation_agent.py`, `_generate_response`). The agent's own accept test still reads the full intent.
- **A draft that states it anyway is replaced.** The model can still meet the figure in memories or history. `withheld_figures_in` finds the figure as 1199, 1,199, 1.199, 1 199 (no-break and thin spaces included), 1'199, 1199.00 and 1.199,00, also when another number follows in the same sentence ("$1,199. 6 bottles", "1 199 1 090"). A separator counts as grouping only before exactly three digits, so "11,99" is not 1199. A hit replaces the draft with `order_letter_without_ceiling`: a fixed inquiry that asks for a quote on the wine and quantity, states the target price when the intent has one, and avoids the commitment phrases in `core/commitment_patterns.py`. The hit also sets `withheld_figure_dropped`, which is saved in `constraint_flags.audit_trail` on the conversation row (no screen reads that field yet). A ceiling equal to the target is not withheld, because that figure is the price the house proposes.
- **Why replace rather than refuse.** The OPEN entry asked for a refusal at staging. A refusal would leave the order with no letter, and an exact match is not proof of a leak: a quantity or a date can equal the ceiling. The replacement keeps the order moving with a letter that says nothing the house keeps to itself, and every letter still waits for the manager's approval.
- **Mock template.** `_mock_generate_response` no longer raises on a missing or non-numeric `target_price` under `negotiate_price`. It asks for the vendor's price instead. Before, the raise escaped `_generate_response`, because its `except` called the same template again.

Stripping was chosen over passing the ceiling as a withheld instruction ("do not reveal …"), because a model can ignore an instruction. It cannot state a figure it was never given, unless it meets the figure elsewhere, which the second layer catches.

**Tests.** `tests/test_house_only_figures.py`: 41 tests. Mutations, each caught: the prompt gets the whole intent, the output guard is off, there is no house-only key, and a ceiling equal to the target is not skipped (measured at f16882e); then, at this head, the old tokenizer (7 fail); the guard falls back to the generic template (1); the audit-trail key is removed (1); the template formats a text target (4). Full orchestrator suite: 1753 passed, 55 skipped. ruff and black are clean.

**Not covered:** a figure in words ("eleven hundred"), a rounded one ("about 1,200", "1.2k"), or one the model works out ("ten percent over our target"). The guard reads the intent passed to the call, so a ceiling left in the session from an earlier intent is not checked. Old drafts and memories that already hold a ceiling are not rewritten. The other writers were checked: `email_composer_service` passes the intent *type* string, not the intent.
