## A vendor-letter draft can carry the house's ceiling price — CLOSED on `fix/withhold-price-ceiling-from-vendor-drafts` — 2026-10-08

Closes the entry of the same title filed OPEN on `data/f106-reconcile-pending-drafts` (`tech-debt.d/2026-10-08-data-f106-reconcile-pending-drafts.md`, #679). The draft that was found was never sent. A read-only production count on 2026-10-08 found 0 sent or send-unconfirmed letters holding a ceiling distinct from their target; that was a one-off production read and is not re-checkable from this repo.

**Fix.** Scope: one house-only key, `max_acceptable_price`, read from the intent passed to the drafting call; the check is for its exact figure in the forms below. It has two layers, both in `services/agent-orchestrator/core/house_only_figures.py`:
- **The intent in the prompt no longer carries the ceiling key.** `vendor_safe_intent` removes the top-level `HOUSE_ONLY_INTENT_KEYS` (`max_acceptable_price`) before `RESPONSE_SYSTEM_PROMPT`'s `{intent_description}` is built (`provider_conversation_agent.py`, `_generate_response`). The agent's own accept test still reads the full intent.
- **A draft that states it anyway is replaced.** The model can still meet the figure in memories or history. `withheld_figures_in` finds the figure as 1199, 1,199, 1.199, 1 199, 1'199 (the apostrophe may also be U+2019, U+2018 or U+02BC), 1199.00 and 1.199,00, also when another number follows in the same sentence ("$1,199. 6 bottles", "1 199 1 090"). Before matching, the text is normalised in three steps: NFKC (so "1，199" with a full-width comma reads as "1,199"); every format character (category Cf: zero-width space, word joiner, BOM, soft hyphen, the bidi marks and the rest) is deleted, because the vendor sees "1<U+200B>199" as 1199; every separator character (category Z: all space separators plus the line and paragraph separators U+2028 and U+2029) becomes a plain space. Z characters are mapped rather than deleted so that two figures side by side stay two ("6 1199"). A separator counts as grouping only before exactly three digits, so "11,99" is not 1199. A hit replaces the draft with `order_letter_without_ceiling`: a fixed inquiry that asks for a quote on the wine and quantity, states the target price when the intent has one, and avoids the commitment phrases in `core/commitment_patterns.py`. Because the letter interpolates the intent's wine name and quantity, it is run through the same commitment check as a model draft and sets `commitment_language_detected` on a match. The Level-4 `draft_generated` decision-log entry is not written for a replaced draft; the replacement is logged as a warning instead. The hit also sets `withheld_figure_dropped`, which is saved in `constraint_flags.audit_trail` on the conversation row (no screen reads that field yet). A ceiling equal to the target is not withheld, because that figure is the price the house proposes.
- **Why replace rather than refuse.** The OPEN entry asked for a refusal at staging. A refusal would leave the order with no letter, and an exact match is not proof of a leak: a quantity or a date can equal the ceiling. The replacement keeps the order moving with a letter built from the wine, quantity and target fields without the house-only keys. All three `_generate_response` callers stage the result as pending approval (checked by the 600a1f3 gate); this PR does not change that.
- **Mock template.** `_mock_generate_response` no longer raises on a missing or non-numeric `target_price` under `negotiate_price`. It asks for the vendor's price instead. Before, the raise escaped `_generate_response`, because its `except` called the same template again.

Stripping was chosen over passing the ceiling as a withheld instruction ("do not reveal …"), because a model can ignore an instruction. It cannot state a figure it was never given, unless it meets the figure elsewhere, which the second layer catches.

**Not covered:** a figure in words ("eleven hundred"), a rounded one ("about 1,200", "1.2k"), or one the model works out ("ten percent over our target"). Shapes the tokenizer does not join: "1, 199" (separator plus space), a tab or a line break that is a control character between the groups (LF, CR, U+0085: category Cc, not Z, so unlike U+2028 they are not read as a space), "1_199", "1.199.00", "1 1 99", the figure run into other digits ("6,1199"), and the Arabic separators ٬ (U+066C) and ٫ (U+066B); `test_the_disclosed_misses_are_still_misses` pins each of these except ٫. Only the one key is guarded, and only its exact figure. The guard reads the intent passed to the call, so a ceiling left in the session from an earlier intent is not checked. Old drafts and memories that already hold a ceiling are not rewritten. The other writers were checked: `email_composer_service` passes the intent *type* string, not the intent.
