## A vendor-letter draft can carry the house's ceiling price — CLOSED on `fix/withhold-price-ceiling-from-vendor-drafts` — 2026-10-08

**The defect.** On 2026-10-08 the F-106 production dry run (PR #679) found a waiting vendor draft that told the vendor both the house's target and its maximum acceptable price ("My target price ... is around $1,090 per bottle, with a maximum acceptable price of $1,199"). The cause: `ProviderConversationAgent._generate_response` filled `RESPONSE_SYSTEM_PROMPT`'s `{intent_description}` with the whole intent, `max_acceptable_price` included. This entry stands on its own and does not depend on #679 merging first. #679 files an OPEN entry for the same defect; **when #679 lands, its own branch must strike that entry** (mark it CLOSED with a pointer to PR #680), because nothing in this PR can edit a file that is not on `main`. The draft that was found was never sent. A read-only production count on 2026-10-08 found 0 sent or send-unconfirmed letters holding a ceiling distinct from their target; that was a one-off production read and is not re-checkable from this repo.

**Fix.** Scope: one house-only key, `max_acceptable_price`, read from the intent passed to the drafting call; the check is for its figure in the forms below. It has two layers, both in `services/agent-orchestrator/core/house_only_figures.py`:
- **The intent in the prompt no longer carries the ceiling key.** `vendor_safe_intent` removes the top-level `HOUSE_ONLY_INTENT_KEYS` (`max_acceptable_price`) before `RESPONSE_SYSTEM_PROMPT`'s `{intent_description}` is built (`provider_conversation_agent.py`, `_generate_response`). The agent's own accept test still reads the full intent.
- **A draft that states it anyway is replaced.** The model can still meet the figure in memories or history. `withheld_figures_in` finds the figure as 1199, 1,199, 1.199, 1 199, 1'199 (the apostrophe may also be U+2019, U+2018 or U+02BC), 1199.00 and 1.199,00, also when another number follows in the same sentence ("$1,199. 6 bottles", "1 199 1 090"). A ceiling with a part below the cent is also looked for cut to the cent and rounded half-up to the cent (13.607 as 13.607, 13.60 and 13.61). Before matching, the text is normalised in four steps: NFKC (so "1，199" with a full-width comma reads as "1,199"); every format character (Cf), every combining mark (Mn, Me), every control character that is not whitespace (Cc) and five blank fillers (U+115F, U+1160, U+3164, U+FFA0, U+2800) are deleted, so "1<U+200B>199" and "1<U+FE0F>199" read 1199; every separator character (category Z: all space separators plus U+2028 and U+2029) becomes a plain space; the middle dots U+00B7, U+2027 and U+30FB become ".". Z characters are mapped rather than deleted so that two figures side by side stay two ("6 1199"). A separator counts as grouping only before exactly three digits, so "11,99" is not 1199. A hit replaces the draft with `order_letter_without_ceiling`: a fixed inquiry that asks for a quote on the wine and quantity, states the target price when the intent has one, and avoids the commitment phrases in `core/commitment_patterns.py`. Because the letter interpolates the intent's wine name and quantity, it is run through the same commitment check as a model draft and sets `commitment_language_detected` on a match. The replacement is logged as a warning, and under Level 4 the `draft_generated` decision-log entry is still written for the staged letter, with `inputs.withheld_keys` (key names only, no value) and `output.replaced_by_order_letter: true` (ADR 0326, fork 3). The spend ledger is written before the check (`_log_gemini_spend` at `provider_conversation_agent.py:2301`, the check at `:2316`), so a replaced draft's model call is still in it. The hit also sets `withheld_figure_dropped`, which is saved in `constraint_flags.audit_trail` on the conversation row (no screen reads that field yet). A form of the ceiling (itself, or its cut or rounded cent form) that equals the target (`target_price`, or `target_price_per_bottle` when that is not a positive number) is not looked for, because that figure is the price the house proposes. The ceiling is read from a number or from a string holding one figure with an optional currency symbol or three ASCII letters ("$1,199.00", "1.199,00 EUR"); a string read two ways ("1,199") is looked for both ways. An int or float ceiling of zero or less is treated as unset, without a warning: the gateway writers send `(price || 0) * 1.1`, so 0 is their "no price". A ceiling that is set but cannot be read (NaN, infinity, a bool, a string holding no positive figure such as "n/a" or "0") is logged as a warning and nothing is looked for.
- **Why strip, and why replace rather than refuse:** recorded in [ADR 0326](../decisions/0326-a-draft-that-states-the-ceiling-is-replaced-by-the-house-letter.md), with the rejected alternatives (refusal at staging, which the OPEN entry on #679 asked for; a do-not-reveal instruction), the measured replacement rate and the fact that `withheld_figure_dropped` is not shown on any screen.
- **Matches tied to the ceiling (outside ADR 0326's "about 1-3%").** That estimate is for coincidental matches in ordinary drafts. The gateway's two writers (`procurement.service.ts:4201`, `communications.controller.ts:895`) set the ceiling to the target × 1.1, so a round target gives a whole-number ceiling. Measured on 2026-10-10 with `withheld_figures_in`: of the integer targets 8 to 120, the 12 multiples of 10 do (ceilings 11, 22, ... 132). For each of them, a draft that states that number (as a quantity of bottles, for example) is replaced. As a day of the month, this happens only for targets 10 and 20 ("October 11", "October 22"). A target of 1800 gives 1980, which matches "the 1980 vintage". When the order's quantity equals the ceiling, every draft that states the quantity is replaced, and the letter itself states the figure, as the quantity. The letter is staged as it is and is not checked again, so there is no loop: one model call, one letter, one Level-4 entry (`test_a_letter_that_itself_holds_the_figure_is_staged_once`). None of these leaks the ceiling, since the figure is the order's own quantity, vintage or date. But in those orders the share of replaced drafts is not 1-3%, and no rate was measured for these slices.
- **Mock template.** `_mock_generate_response` no longer raises on a missing or non-numeric `target_price` under `negotiate_price`. It asks for the vendor's price instead. Before, the raise escaped `_generate_response`, because its `except` called the same template again.

**Not covered:** a figure in words ("eleven hundred"), a rounded one ("about 1,200", "1.2k"), one cut or rounded to whole units (13 for 13.60), or one the model works out ("ten percent over our target"). Shapes the tokenizer does not join: "1, 199" (separator plus space); the groups split by a whitespace control character (tab, LF, VT, FF, CR, U+001C to U+001F, U+0085: category Cc, so unlike U+2028 they are not read as a space); split by a hyphen or minus ("1-199", U+2010, U+2011, U+2212); "1_199", "1.199.00", "1 1 99", the figure run into other digits ("6,1199"), the Arabic separators ٬ (U+066C) and ٫ (U+066B); and any other character between the groups that the normalisation neither deletes nor maps, including characters some fonts draw blank whose category is Lo, So, Mc, Co, Cn or Cs (other than the five fillers it deletes). `test_the_disclosed_misses_are_still_misses` pins "1, 199", tab, LF, CR, VT, FF, U+001C, U+0085, "-", U+2010, U+2011, U+2212, "1_199", "1.199.00", "1 1 99", "6,1199", U+066C, U+066B, U+0903 (Mc), U+E000 (Co), U+0378 (Cn) and U+D800 (Cs); no Lo or So character is pinned, nor the words, rounded or worked-out figures; the whole-units miss has its own test. Only the one key is guarded. The guard reads the intent passed to the call, so a ceiling left in the session from an earlier intent is not checked. Old drafts and memories that already hold a ceiling are not rewritten, and `_get_db_context_for_prompt` can bring past message text back into the prompt. The other writers were checked: `email_composer_service` passes the intent *type* string, not the intent.
