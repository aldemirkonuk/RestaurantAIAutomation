**What was true.** The hub queues a wine line it cannot deplete in `pos_unresolved_lines` (`queueUnresolvedLine`, `apps/api-gateway/src/pos-hub/pos-hub.service.ts`), and the Toast door does the same (`toast.service.ts`).
