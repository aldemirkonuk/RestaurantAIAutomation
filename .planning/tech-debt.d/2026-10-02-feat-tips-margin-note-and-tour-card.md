## Which copy of the tip choices wins rests on device clocks, and no ADR records the rule — OPEN — 2026-10-03

Filed by `feat/tips-margin-note-and-tour-card` (PR #570), from its gate round-7 audit at 37841f602 (posted 2026-10-03 22:41 EDT, PR comment 5975870123). That audit blocked on a browser holding a read made before "Don't show tips again" was pressed in another browser: its "Not now" sent its whole guidance copy, built from that read, with a later stamp, the gateway's merge put `hide_all_tips: false` back, and the browser that had turned tips off took the later account copy and showed tips again, with nobody pressing "Turn tips back on". The gate round-7 fix makes every web guidance save send only the keys its action changed (the counts and the list of use cards seen are taken up under "What it leaves" below), and `apps/web/src/guidance/GuidanceProvider.test.tsx:513` runs that sequence, with one step added (the browser holding the old read has saved the switch before, so its own copy holds `hide_all_tips: false`), with the real provider, preferences hook and tip strip, one `QueryClient` and one stored copy per browser, and a copy of the gateway's merge. This entry records the rule as built and the cases it leaves.

**The rule as built.** It is the build of ADR 0251 D2 across devices ("Don't show tips again" holds "until the person turns tips back on"; ADR 0251 is on #566's branch, `docs/houses-decisions-0251-0253`, seen at de4e8cade on 2026-10-03), and it is not yet in an ADR: `git grep saved_at -- .planning/decisions` finds nothing at 37841f602 or at origin/main 619a068a9. A bracket under ADR 0251 is owed once #566 and #570 have both merged; #570 does not edit ADR 0251.
- A web save sends the account the keys its action changed and `saved_at`, the saving browser's clock (`apps/web/src/guidance/GuidanceProvider.tsx:300-324`; the savers at `:342-347`, `:459-462`, `:476`, `:479-503`, `:510-514`, `:524-553`). The gateway deep-merges it in (`apps/api-gateway/src/user-preferences/user-preferences.service.ts:32-53`, `:100`), so the account's copy carries the stamp of the last web save to reach it. The preferences hook merges the save into its cached copy the same way when it sends it (`apps/web/src/hooks/useUserPreferences.ts:105-122`, `:183-186`).
- This browser's own copy (`wineops_guidance_v1`) holds the saves made here, merged page by page, stamped with the latest one's time (`GuidanceProvider.tsx:96-140`). At a save it starts again from that save alone when it carries no readable stamp, or when the cached account copy carries a stamp as late as its own (`:311-314`).
- On reading, the account's copy stands alone when this browser holds no readable copy, or when the account's stamp is readable and this browser's is missing or earlier (`GuidanceProvider.tsx:180-189`, `:210`). Otherwise (this browser's stamp is later or the same, or the account's is not readable) this browser's `global`, `pages` and `setup_nudge` are laid over the account's, key by key and page by page (`:213-226`).
- Pinned in `apps/web/src/guidance/GuidanceProvider.test.tsx`: the newer copy wins (`:222-266`, the tie at `:249`), what each save sends (`:268-304`), and the two-browser tests (`:421-565`).

**What it leaves.**
- **Clock skew.** Every stamp is the saving device's clock (`GuidanceProvider.tsx:304`), and the copies are compared by it (`:185-188`). A browser whose clock runs ahead lays its own copy over saves made later elsewhere until the account's copy carries a stamp as late as its own: the gate round-7 audit reproduced a browser a day ahead staying off after "Turn tips back on" was pressed in another. In the same way, a save from a browser whose clock runs behind does not reach a browser whose own copy holds that key with a later stamp. **Owed as a gateway follow-up, not made in #570:** stamp `saved_at` on the gateway when it stores a save, and have the browser keep the stamp the gateway returns for its own saves, so no two device clocks are compared.
