## POS money on role-open analytics routes reaches staff — OPEN — 2026-10-31

Carried from entry (i) of PR #617 (`fix/cost-and-revenue-from-the-pos`, filed 2026-10-09 from its ADR 0090 audit at `cc3bfdb10`), shortened when #617 was split and the sales gate (ADR 0298, decision 9) moved into this branch. Claim: `../decisions/claims.d/fix-sales-gate-before-the-till.jsonl:1` (status open).

**What is true on this branch.** `pos-revenue`, `basket` and `insights` on `AnalyticsController` (`apps/api-gateway/src/analytics/analytics.controller.ts`) carry only the class-level `JwtAuthGuard`, and each serves POS sales to any member of the house. `pos_checks.*` is the `sales` class (`apps/api-gateway/src/ask-readings/reading-data-classes.ts:123`), and `ROLE_POLICY` gives it to the owner and manager rows, not to staff. `table-performance`, `waiters` and `hot-tables` carry `RolesGuard` + `@Roles("owner", "manager")` since ADR 0348; `2026-10-31-fix-analytics-money-routes-need-a-manager.md` lists the wider set staff can still read. This branch adds `salesGateFor` and hands the token's gate to `financial`, `risk`, `overview`, `consult` and `recommendations`. Nothing reads the till yet, so those routes serve what they served before it. Not in the claim, because a grep cannot tell them from the gated code beside them: the stored insights `overview` composes, the generated insights in `consult`'s evidence pack, and the check-based goal metrics (`wine_revenue`, `avg_check`, `checks`).

**Owed, for its own lane.** One rule across the three routes: `RolesGuard` with `@Roles("owner", "manager")`, or the dashboard's shape, `withSales = policyFor(role).sees.includes("sales")` with the sales fields withheld and named absent. ADR 0348 records the founder's ruling of 2026-10-31 for the rest: a follow-up after the ADR 0298 work withholds the figures and keeps the route. The claim row holds once each of the three carries `@Roles(` with `RolesGuard` (on the route or the class) among its own decorators, or `policyFor(`/`withSales`/`salesGateFor(` in its body.

## An MCP key outlives its minter's role, and opens the sales gate — OPEN — 2026-10-31

Filed from the CI audit of PR #617 at `90b7dadff` (PR comment 6547565625, security note 2), and moved here with the code when #617 was split.

**What is true.** The MCP financial reader passes the sales gate open (`apps/api-gateway/src/mcp-server/mcp-tool-readers.service.ts:235`), because only an owner or a manager of the house can mint or revoke a key (`mcp-keys.controller.ts`, `assertCanManageRestaurant`). A key is checked by its hash, its house and `revoked_at` alone (`apps/api-gateway/src/mcp-server/mcp-credentials.service.ts:232`, `:265`). Nothing re-reads whether the person who minted it still holds owner or manager. On this branch the gate guards nothing yet. Once `getFinancialSummary` reads the till (ADR 0298), a manager demoted to staff keeps every key they minted, and that key returns the till's sales-class figures where before it returned money-class ones. ADR 0298 records this (Consequences, the 2026-10-09 adversarial-pass bullet), and records that the revocation path was not traced end to end. The audit called it a small widening, acceptable for now because the key is already a high-privilege credential.

**What closes it.** One of three things:
- the reader opens the gate only while the key's `created_by` still holds owner or manager on the house (`houseMembersInRoles`, as the producers' sales-holder read does);
- a demotion revokes the keys that person minted;
- a recorded ruling that a key is the house's, not its minter's.
