## Tax-inclusive net and margins (F-620 / F-1537): what ADR 0341's lanes 1–4 leave owed — OPEN — 2026-10-12

[ADR 0341](../decisions/0341-a-house-says-whether-its-till-prices-include-tax-and-net-is-made-pre-tax-at-ingest.md), locked on the founder's picks of 2026-10-12 (F1 build, F2 only from now, F3 stamp the cost basis). Lanes 1–4 are built and committed locally, stacked to `22ac2e665` (`feat/house-tax-basis-schema` → `feat/house-tax-basis-setting` → `fix/pos-subtotal-pre-tax-at-ingest` → `fix/margin-advice-tax-basis`); none is pushed or merged. F-620 and F-1537 close for new checks and proven costs **only when those lanes merge**. Each item below is still open:

- **Nothing writes a cost tax stamp.** `procurement_order_items.price_tax_basis`, `inventory_lots.unit_cost_tax_basis` and `restaurant_inventory.last_purchase_price_tax_basis` exist (migration `house_tax_basis_and_rates`), but receiving, the door check, procurement and invoice reading never set them. Until one does, every inclusive house's margin advice reads "cost basis not stated" (`apps/api-gateway/src/pricing/margin-advice.service.ts`). This is F3 working as designed, and it is also the reason F-620 is not closed in practice. [2026-10-14, fix/sim-owed-cost-basis: partly resolved. The order line (`createOrder`), the receipt check (`verifyReceipt` → `applyReceiptAdjustment`) and the delivery verification (`DeliveryStockService.finaliseAtVerified`) now stamp, through `apps/api-gateway/src/common/cost-tax-stamp.ts`, at inclusive houses only: an owner or manager's stated value, else the country rule (TR includes_tax, US excludes_tax). Still owed: `restaurant_inventory.last_purchase_price_tax_basis` has no writer (nothing writes `last_purchase_price`); manual receive and opening-cost paths in `inventory.service.ts` are unstamped; a confirm-time price edit clears the line basis by trigger and does not restamp; no web control sends the new fields; the TR includes_tax default is an unverified premise (Turkish B2B unit prices are often KDV-exclusive) chosen because it refuses rather than misadvises.]
- **Reads across the switch say nothing (F2).** Checks before a house's switch keep the till's figure; nothing marks the switch date on reports, and count-and-say (ADR 0290) does not yet say that a window mixes two bases.
- **No web picker for item rates.** The gateway accepts `house_item` rates (`POST /settings/tax-rates`, house-scoped, 404 for another house's item), but Settings enters only whole-house and till-category rates. A house that rates by till category gets `no_tax_rate` on every wine.
- **`recommendations.service.ts` does not know the new advice states** (`cost_not_pre_tax`, `no_tax_rate`, `target_unconfirmed`): its "cannot be judged" count and a "confirm your target again" recommendation are not built.
- **ADR 0295 (draft, open PR #615) is not edited from here.** Its rule 1 owes the bracket "as the till sends it, made pre-tax at ingest where the house states its till prices include tax". Its PR-3 (`fix/pos-subtotal-means-net`) must set `subtotalPreTax` on any adapter it makes pre-tax from the till's own tax, or an inclusive house would have its rates divided out twice.
- **ADR 0341's README row** goes in its own PR, a pure append.
- **Ordering.** Lane 2's settings copy describes lane 3's ingest, so lane 2 must not ship ahead of lane 3.
- **Not verified:** Toast's tax fields in a live payload; the KDV status of Turkish *basit usul* houses; whether any Turkish generic till's CSV has a tax column. Production was not read.
