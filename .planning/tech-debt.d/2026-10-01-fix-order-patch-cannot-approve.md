## Any house member can approve an order and set its price through PATCH orders/:id — FIXED by `fix/order-patch-cannot-approve` — 2026-10-01

Filed by `fix/order-patch-cannot-approve`. This **closes** the OPEN entry of the same title in `tech-debt.d/2026-10-01-fix-closed-stays-closed.md`, which lives on the unmerged branch `fix/closed-stays-closed`; whichever of the two branches merges second strikes that heading to `~~OPEN~~ CLOSED by fix/order-patch-cannot-approve`. Line numbers in **What** are at `origin/main` 4bd11a00e.

**What.** `PATCH procurement/orders/:id` carries no `@Roles` and passes no user to the service (`procurement.controller.ts:251-263`). `updateOrder` refused only a move to CANCELLED without a reason (`procurement.service.ts:3302`); every other move went through `assertStatusTransition` (`:3312`), and the transition table makes PENDING, APPROVAL_NEEDED and NEGOTIATING → APPROVED legal edges (`order-transitions.ts:103-126`), because the sealed act walks them. So any member of the house could PATCH `{ "status": "APPROVED" }` and skip the seal (`POST orders/:id/seal-challenge` then `POST orders/:id/approve`, `procurement.controller.ts:513`, `:538`) and the approval-rule gate that runs inside it. The same PATCH wrote `quotedPrice`, `negotiatedPrice`, `finalPrice` and `totalCost` on an order in any state, so a sealed approval's total could be changed after the seal.

**Fix.** `updateOrder` now refuses `status: APPROVED` from anyone, before any read or write: 422, reason `approve_through_the_sealed_act`, message "An order is approved through its own act — holding Approve, which checks this house's approval rules and seals the approval to the person holding — not by editing its status. Nothing was changed." (`ProcurementService.APPROVE_THROUGH_THE_HOLD`). A body carrying any of the four price fields is refused with 422, reason `price_settled_by_approval`, unless the order is in `ORDER_PRICE_OPEN_STATUSES` (PENDING, APPROVAL_NEEDED, NEGOTIATING; `order-transitions.ts:222`), with words from `refusePriceChange` that end "Nothing was changed.". The state is read as it is, not as the body asks, so a body stepping APPROVED back to NEGOTIATING with a new price is refused too. The UPDATE repeats the allowlist (`procurement.service.ts:3421`), so an order approved between the read and the write is refused, not repriced. Notes and the other non-price fields stay editable in every state. ADR 0175 D7 (approval always needs authority) is **not** built here; its claim `ADR-0175-APPROVE-ORDER-ALWAYS-NEEDS-AUTHORITY` stays open.

**Pinned by** `apps/api-gateway/src/procurement/order-patch-cannot-approve.spec.ts` (31 cases; 22 red against 4bd11a00e before the fix, the step-back case added after). Seven mutations, each applied alone and restored byte-identical, each turned it red: approve refusal removed (4 failed), price read check removed (6), PATCH write guard removed (1), merge lookup back to the old denylist (3), merge write guard removed (1), APPROVAL_NEEDED admitted to the mergeable set (2), APPROVED admitted to the price-open set (8).

**No route-access row.** The refusal is the same for every role and sits in the service, and the route-access census (`auth/guards/route-access.spec.ts`) measures only what `RolesGuard` lets through on controllers carrying `@Roles(`. A row would read "every role: allowed" both before and after this fix.

## A second order request overwrote an approved, parked or half-received order — FIXED by `fix/order-patch-cannot-approve` — 2026-10-01

Filed by `fix/order-patch-cannot-approve`, found alongside the entry above. Line numbers in **What** are at `origin/main` 4bd11a00e.

**What.** `createOrder` folds a new request for the same restaurant + inventory + provider into the newest open order. "Open" was a denylist of seven statuses (`procurement.service.ts:1078-1086`, applied at `:1109`), so APPROVED, APPROVAL_NEEDED and PARTIALLY_RECEIVED orders matched. The merge UPDATE (`:1123` onward) then wrote the new request's quantity, prices and total over them: a sealed approval's figures changed with no seal, an order parked by the approval gate had the figures it was parked on replaced, and a half-received order had its quantity rewritten under the receipts already booked against it.

**Fix.** The lookup and the merge UPDATE both name the states a request may fold into, `ORDER_MERGEABLE_STATUSES` = PENDING and NEGOTIATING (`order-transitions.ts:239`; `procurement.service.ts:1109`, `:1148`). If the open order leaves those states between the lookup and the write, the UPDATE matches no row and `createOrder` starts a new order instead (`:1162`). Pinned by the same spec's third block, and by mutations 4-6 above.

## PATCH orders/:id still moves an approved order onward without the seal — OPEN — 2026-10-01

Filed by `fix/order-patch-cannot-approve`, observed while fixing the entries above and left alone because the brief scoped the fix to the move into APPROVED and to prices.

**What.** The transition table lets an APPROVED order move to NEGOTIATING, CONFIRMED, IN_TRANSIT, DELIVERED, PARTIALLY_RECEIVED, CANCELLED, REJECTED or FAILED (`order-transitions.ts:130-139` at 4bd11a00e), and `PATCH orders/:id` still walks those edges for any member, with no seal. A PATCH to DELIVERED books no stock: `updateOrder` makes no ledger call. A member can also step an APPROVED order back to NEGOTIATING and then reprice it in a second PATCH; it then has to be approved again through the sealed act, so this is an un-approval, not an approval bypass. Whether a member may approve an order no rule fires on is ADR 0175 D7, not built.

**Also seen.** `apps/web/src/services/api/orders.ts:92` (`updateOrder`) and `:107` (`updateOrderStatus`) call this route, and `apps/web/src/lib/supabase.ts:264` (`updateOrderStatus`) writes `procurement_orders` directly; a search of `apps/web/src` and `apps/mobile/src` found no screen calling any of the three.

**Fix (deferred).** Which of those moves a member may make by PATCH, and which need a person with authority, is a product choice ADR 0175 has not ruled.
