## The delivery path posts the landed cost; seven things stay open — OPEN — 2026-11-07

Filed by `fix/delivery-path-uses-landed-cost` (cost-math proposal lane 1.4; each fork below is the session's call under the founder's /goal of 2026-11-07). Its fixtures are synthetic, not real TR papers.

**What is true.** At VERIFIED, `DeliveryStockService.agreedPrices` reads each invoice's header money columns and line kinds and hands every line to `landDelivery` (`canonical/paper-landed-cost.ts`). That function runs `landedCost` (`procurement/landed-cost.ts`, unedited) once per paper and pools each item as Σ accepted landed value ÷ Σ accepted bottles, with accepted bottles taken from what the door booked. Pinned by `claims.d/fix-delivery-path-uses-landed-cost.jsonl` and by the "VERIFIED posts the landed cost (lane 1.4)" block in `delivery-stock.service.spec.ts`.

**Audit follow-up.** The gatekeeper's 1% total cross-check (`lot-unit-cost.ts`) read every printed total as the line before anything came off, so a total printed after its allowance (iskonto) or with its line deposit inside it was held as a misread unit before `reconcileLineValue` could net it, and with charges on the paper it held every other item too. `perBottleOfLine` now asks the gatekeeper about each reading the reconcile accepts and keeps the refusal only when none matches; specs (c2)-(c4). Fixtures are synthetic.

This closes the freight/fee/discount half of "Landed cost is unwired" (`2026-11-07-fix-lot-cost-is-per-bottle.md:43`) **for the delivery path only**. The other half — a per-case price stored as `bottle` with no base and no total — is unchanged. It also wires "The landed-cost rule is unwired" (`2026-11-07-feat-landed-cost-one-rule.md:1`) into the delivery path; `verifyReceipt` still costs its own way.

Still open:

- **The cost is not posted in one transaction.** `finalise_delivery_cost` is called once per item, and a failure partway leaves some lots final and the rest provisional. Corrected 2026-11-07 (QA round 15 (4)): this note used to say a retry was idempotent, but nothing re-posted — a re-verify only repaired printed prices, and the delivery cannot be verified twice. Now a re-verify calls `finishProvisional`, which posts the landed cost of the items whose lots are still provisional, and only those, so a final or hand-revalued lot is never overwritten (ADR 0103, QA 10a (6)); a second re-verify posts nothing (spec "a re-verify finishes a post that failed partway"). A delivery-wide RPC that posts every item or none still needs a migration.
- **No tax column.** Every lot is costed as printed. `houseTaxForLotCost()` returns `unset` (ADR 0359 C2, Proposed in #784; lane 3.5a builds the columns). The null `cost_tax_basis` and the tax sentence live only in `FinalisedItem.landed` and in the revaluation's `reason` text; no column stores them.
- **The order path is untouched.** `procurement.service.ts` `verifyReceipt` (#787) and the order-side cost (lane 1.3) do not land cost.
- ~~**A narrow re-verify regression against #838.**~~ Closed 2026-11-07 (QA round 15 (1)-(2)): when the paper's landed cost is held, `repairPrintedCost` posts the line's own per-bottle price with a basis saying the charges are not in it; a line that refuses, or one on a paper not in the house's money, is flagged instead.
- **A paper in another currency is held, never converted (QA round 15 (5)).** A paper not in `restaurants.currency` (or naming none when the house names one), and an item pooled from two currencies, stay provisional with the reason in words; an accepted proposal on such a line does not cost it. A house with no currency named, or one recorded as something that is not an active ISO 4217 code, costs nothing: every paper is held until the house names its money, and a re-verify then posts it (QA round 19 (1)). Written forms such as `TL`, `₺` and `US$` are refused as not a currency, as everywhere else in the gateway (`common/iso-4217`); a Turkish paper that prints `TL` waits until intake writes `TRY`. Conversion waits for a recorded rate (rule 3 of `20260905120000_a_house_names_its_money.sql`).
- ~~**Six places only on the landed path.**~~ Closed 2026-11-11 (cost-math R7, `fix/lot-cost-six-places-every-path`): `toLotCostPrecision` rounds to six places like `toLandedPrecision`, and `perBottleLotCost` no longer rounds to four first, so a line-only price and an accepted proposal post six places too (100 per case of 12 posts 8.333333, not 8.33; 142 / KS(12) posts 11.833333, not 11.8333). A re-verify still recognises a lot an older build booked at the printed price in cents (`toCentsAsBooked`).
- **A fee printed as a line is not shared.** Only the header's charge totals (freight, fuel, split-case, delivery, other) are shared; a `fee` or `deposit` line is named in the paper's left-out sentence. Whether intake ever files one fee as both a line and a header total is untraced.
- **An unpriced line with bottles now holds.** #838 skipped it. With charges on the paper, every item on that paper stays provisional, because the charge cannot be shared without that line's value.
- **Two unattached lines are not paired.** One booked item and two lines with no `inventory_id` (say, paid + free) stay unpriced, unless a proposal settles them.
