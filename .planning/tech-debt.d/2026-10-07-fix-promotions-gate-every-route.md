## Provider intelligence served staff what GET /promotions refuses them — CLOSED on `fix/promotions-gate-every-route` — 2026-10-07

`apps/api-gateway/src/providers/provider-intelligence.controller.ts` carried `@UseGuards(JwtAuthGuard)` and nothing else. Every read was scoped to the caller's house (ADR 0147) but not to a role. So a staff member could read through `/providers/promotions/active|expiring|compare|savings` and `/providers/:id/promotions` the same `provider_promotions` rows that `GET /promotions` refuses them (`promotions.controller.ts:63-64`, ADR 0124:357-362). The same was true of sentiment (ADR 0207:298, round 3: "owners and managers only, staff never see it"), the leverage signals and the Digital Twin's vendor price points. The legacy vendor sheet panel made those calls for every role. Found by the money-policy refuter on 2026-10-07 (M1 item 2; `refute:money.missed_surfaces`). It was already named as still open at `tech-debt.d/2026-09-28-fix-websocket-role-gate.md:41`.

**Fixed on the same branch.** Fifteen of the controller's seventeen handlers now carry the `GET /promotions` gate, `@UseGuards(RolesGuard)` + `@Roles("owner", "manager")`, on the method. Pinned by `provider-intelligence-roles.http.spec.ts`, by `route-access.expected.json` (17 rows added, 217 in all) and by `claims.d/fix-promotions-gate-every-route.jsonl`. On the web, `TwinSheet.tsx` mounts the panel for owners and managers only.

**Coordinator's call under the founder's 2026-10-07T20:04:10Z delegation** (none of these changes a decided feature; each applies a rule already decided):

1. **The Digital Twin is gated too** (`GET :id/knowledge`, `GET :id/knowledge/contradictions`, `PUT :id/knowledge/:knowledgeId/verify`), although the M1 list did not name it. `provider_knowledge` holds `pricing/price_point` rows, which are vendor quotes (F7, the quote book by role). It also holds `financial` rows and `relationship/leverage_signal` rows, which are a vendor's negotiating position (ADR 0124:357-362). The agent writes them as free-form JSON (`provider_conversation_agent.py:1573-1700`), so no field list can strip them reliably. Gating only `intelligence/leverage` would also have left `?category=relationship` open on the knowledge route. Verifying a price fact is a money write, so it goes to holders.
2. **Conversation memory, its search, sessions and session summaries are gated.** `extracted_entities` is the AI's free-form extraction, including price changes. The sessions carry negotiation intents (`price_check`, `promo_discovery`) and summaries. F13 ("mail kept, AI summaries neutral") governs the house's mail surfaces, and this branch does not touch them.
3. **`POST :id/outreach` and `POST :id/onboard` stay open.** They return no figure (`{success, message}`). Who may start a vendor conversation is a send-authority question (ADR 0175 D9/D10: a grant is a row, not a token role), not this gate's question.
4. **The web panel mounts for owners and managers only.** This follows the precedent of *How their mail reads* (ADR 0207 round 3). The panel ignores errors, so a staff member would have seen "nothing learned" over three 403s. As a result, staff lose the panel's *Actions* menu (outreach, onboarding) in the UI, even though those two routes still answer them.

**Still open, stated.**
- When the M2 money helper with allowlist DTOs exists, staff could be shown the non-money twin categories (`company`, `people`, `logistics`, `compliance`). Nothing reads them for staff until then. The vendor sheet's own sections (record, terms, contacts, branches, ledger card) still serve staff.
- Outreach and onboarding check no grant, and no role. Whether a staff member without a live grant may start one is ADR 0175's question. It is not answered here.
- `GET /providers/promotions/savings` still reads columns that exist in no migration (OD-158). This branch gates it but does not fix it.
- `ProviderComparisonView.tsx` (compare, leverage) and `useActivePromotions` are mounted nowhere, so nothing on the web called those routes for staff.
