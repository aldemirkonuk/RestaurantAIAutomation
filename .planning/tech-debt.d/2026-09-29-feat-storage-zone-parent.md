**[2026-09-29, feat/storage-zone-parent: the verifier's deadlock nit, mapped, not removed.** A soft delete of a parent and a concurrent move of a zone under it take locks in opposite orders (the delete's UPDATE holds the parent row, then its orphan trigger waits for the child row and, through the guard, the house's advisory lock; the move holds the child row and the advisory lock and waits to `FOR SHARE` the parent), so Postgres aborts one with 40P01, which the gateway answered as a 500. `StorageLocationsService.dbRefusal` now answers 40P01 and 40001 as a 409 that says nothing was saved and to try again, and `deleteLocation` goes through `dbRefusal` (it threw a flat 500 before). Taking the advisory lock first in the orphan trigger was considered and not done: the parent's row lock is taken by the UPDATE before any row trigger runs, so the order would not change. Removing the deadlock needs the lock taken before either row (for example a gateway RPC that takes `pg_advisory_xact_lock` and then writes); not built. Evidence: `storage-locations-hierarchy.spec.ts` "a write that lost a lock race" (3 of 4 red before; the 500 case is a pin). The same branch builds the founder's nested-totals answer ("Show both (Recommended)"); see ADR 0080 and CLAIMS `ZONE-PARENT-2026-09-29-NESTED-TOTALS-SHOW-BOTH`.]**
