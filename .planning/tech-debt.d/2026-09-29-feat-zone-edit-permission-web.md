## Who may edit storage zones — the fork in #516's entry, answered (ADR 0238) — CLOSED — 2026-09-29

This closes the fork paragraph "**Founder fork, not decided here: who may edit zones.**" in `.planning/tech-debt.d/2026-09-29-fix-storage-locations-house-scope.md` (entry "Storage locations took a location id from another house on trust"). That entry is #516's and is not edited here (one author per fragment, `tech-debt.d/README.md`); this entry points forward from it.

The founder was asked *"who may create, rename, resize or delete a zone, and who may place wines in zones? Today every house member can do all of it"* and answered, verbatim: *"managers/owners+ the people they assign"* (2026-09-29, ADR 0238).

**Gateway (PR #518).** `POST /storage-locations/:rid`, a `PATCH /storage-locations/:rid/:locationId` carrying any field but `current_count`, `DELETE /storage-locations/:rid/:locationId`, and `PUT /cellar/:rid/zones/:zoneId` when it renames, are 403 with nothing written unless the caller is an owner or manager here (`readRestaurantRole`, strict) or a staff member whose active membership carries `user_restaurant_access.zone_setup_access` (migration `an_owner_or_manager_may_assign_who_sets_up_zones`). An owner or manager of the same house sets it with `PUT /storage-locations/:rid/setup-access/:userId` (staff only; 404 for another house's person; compare-and-set), audited as `zone_setup_access_changed` and the person told; `GET /storage-locations/:rid/setup-access` answers the caller, and lists who is assigned to owners and managers only. Pinned by `apps/api-gateway/src/storage-locations/zone-setup-access.spec.ts` and claim `ADR-0238-ZONE-SETUP-IS-OWNERS-MANAGERS-AND-ASSIGNED` (`claims.d/feat-zone-edit-permission.jsonl`).

**Web (this branch, `feat/zone-edit-permission-web`).** `StorageLocationManager` hides or disables the setup controls and the cellar floor makes the name read-only for a member nobody assigned, saying why; the roster sheet shows a "Sets up storage zones" switch to owners and managers. Pinned by `apps/web/src/components/inventory/zone-setup-access.test.tsx`.

**Still open.** Placing, removing and counting wines stay open to every member — the answer does not separate them from setup — so that is **OD-200**. A staff member assigned, promoted, then demoted back finds the assignment again (a role change does not clear the column; ADR 0238, Consequences).
