# Instruments & Equity — Full Agenda

> **PROVISIONAL — no work done yet.** This is forecast, not fact.

> Not legal advice, and nothing below is drafted legal text. This plans a function that
> will commission these instruments from a qualified lawyer.

## What

Get the **gates built while the register is empty**, and get the two instruments whose
absence produces no symptom in front of counsel first. Nothing on this agenda is a
contract, and that is the point: at zero instruments, the useful work is entirely
structural.

| Deliverable | Why |
|---|---|
| **The register, for these six types** — states (`requested` → `terms stated` → `in counsel review` → `signed` → `executed`), and what each transition requires | Without it, [[instruments-equity-loops]] L-IE-1 is a review rather than a gate, and a review happens after the signature |
| **The gates adopted** — IE-1 (no same-day), IE-2 (consequence model), IE-3 (consent ordering), IE-4 (cap table derived from paper) | Every one is free today and contested later. That asymmetry is the entire argument for doing this now |
| **Counsel engaged and briefed on the entity** | [[legal-directive]] R1 routes everything here to "counsel reviews". With no counsel, R1 is a sentence in a file |
| **Founder agreement + IP assignment commissioned** | The two instruments whose absence is invisible until an outsider audits ([[instruments-equity-premortem]] M2) |
| **The commitment list** — anyone who believes they were promised anything | L-IE-4's first run. Likely empty; needs to be *known* empty rather than assumed empty |

## How

**Sequence: gates → counsel → the two silent instruments → everything else on demand.**

1. **Gates first, because they cost nothing today.** A waiting period agreed while no
   instrument is in flight is administration. The same waiting period proposed on the day a
   term sheet lands is a negotiation about the waiting period, and it will lose.
   [[instruments-equity-premortem]] M1 is a story about a rule that did not exist yet.
2. **Counsel before the first one-way door**, not before the first NDA — the repeatable
   class next door can wait, this class cannot.
3. **Then founder agreement and IP assignment.** Not because they are urgent — they are
   not, and nothing external is asking for them. Because they are the only two instruments
   in the six that generate **no symptom at all** until diligence looks. Every other
   failure in [[instruments-equity-premortem]] eventually announces itself; M2 does not.
4. **Everything else on demand.** SAFE, board consent, stock purchase and advisor
   agreements are drafted when a real counterparty exists. Pre-drafting them would produce
   four plausible documents with no counsel behind them — which is
   [[legal-premortem]] M5 with the highest possible stakes.

### The observation this team must state, and the line it must not cross

The repo contains a substantial codebase. The register contains no IP assignment. Those
are two facts about a register, and stating them is this team's job.

What is **not** this team's job — and what this document is not qualified to do — is say
what that means legally, who owns what today, or what any instrument should say about it.
That is counsel's. The only conclusion drawn here is a **sequencing** one: put it first,
because it is the item most likely to be discovered by somebody else. If counsel says the
concern is misplaced, that closes the item at essentially zero cost, and closing it cheaply
is itself the argument for raising it early.

## Why now

- **Because the cost curve runs the wrong way.** Every rule in
  [[instruments-equity-directive]] is free at zero instruments and expensive at one.
- **Because M2's signal is already showing.** [[instruments-equity-premortem]] M2 is the
  only mechanism in this vault whose earliest observable signal is *present today* — a
  register with no IP-assignment row. It has been showing the entire time. Nothing about
  waiting improves it.
- **Because activation is not in this team's control.** [[positioning-fundraise-readiness-charter]]
  decides whether a raise happens (`corporate.md:421-422`); an advisor conversation can
  happen in a week. This team gets no warning, so its preparation cannot be
  demand-triggered.
- **Because the merge condition should be tested fairly.** [[legal-loops]] L-LEG-5 will ask
  in two quarters whether this team should exist. It deserves to be judged on a quarter
  where the gates were actually built, not on one spent waiting.

## Questions for the founder

1. **What is the waiting period in IE-1?** It has to be a specific number decided now. A
   period decided when it first bites is a period that does not exist. Name it.
2. **Founder agreement and IP assignment first — agreed?** This agenda argues for it on
   sequencing grounds. If the real first instrument is a SAFE or an advisor grant, say so
   and the ordering changes; the gates do not.
3. **Has anything already been promised?** Any equity, any advisory role, any "we'll sort
   the paperwork" — to anyone, in any conversation, at any point. L-IE-4 asks this monthly
   forever, and its first run is only useful if this one is answered honestly.
   [[instruments-equity-premortem]] M4 is entirely a story about the gap between that
   conversation and the register.
4. **Who is counsel, and are they engaged for this class specifically?** The gate is
   absolute in [[legal-directive]] R1. An absolute gate with no one behind it is a queue.
5. **Is the cap table allowed to exist before the first instrument?** This agenda says yes,
   as an empty derived artifact with its provenance rule attached — because creating it
   *after* the first instrument is exactly how the spreadsheet becomes the source of truth
   ([[instruments-equity-premortem]] M3). Confirm, or defer it.
6. **Do you want this team at all?** `corporate.md:116-121` says Legal is the trim candidate
   and this split is structural rather than evidential. This team has zero artifacts and
   may issue nothing for a year. Keeping it costs one charter set; L-LEG-5 will re-ask in
   two quarters. Merging it now is a legitimate answer and this team will not argue it.
