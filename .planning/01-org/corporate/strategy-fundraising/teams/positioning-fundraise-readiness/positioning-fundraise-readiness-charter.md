# Positioning & Fundraise Readiness — Charter

Division **Corporate** → Department [[strategy-fundraising-charter]] → Team
`positioning-fundraise-readiness` (§5.1 of
`.planning/foundation/teams/corporate.md:417-458`).

> This team owns **the claim**, not the paper and not the craft. It does not draft
> instruments ([[instruments-equity-charter]] does), it does not build collateral
> ([[narrative-collateral-charter]] does), and it does not define metrics
> ([[metric-contract-truth-assurance-charter]] does). It owns whether what we say about
> ourselves is true at the moment we say it.

## Mandate

The wedge and the one sentence; the competitive read; the investor narrative and materials;
the **YC path**; the diligence surface — data room, cap-table hygiene, metric provenance;
and **sequencing requests into Legal §1.1** for SAFE, board consent, stock purchase and
advisor agreements (`corporate.md:421-422`).

Both halves of that list answer **the same question**: *is the story we tell outsiders true
and provable?* Narrative failure is an **unevidenced** claim; diligence failure is an
**unevidence-able** one. Same discipline, same evidence base, one cadence
(`corporate.md:423-425`).

## Why this is one team and not two

This is the only team in Corporate whose charter has to argue for its own singularity, so
it is argued here rather than assumed.

The commissioning brief expected a narrative team and a fundraise-instrument team. **The
second has no mandate of its own at v0.** Legal drafts the paper; the founder decides the
terms; what remains is diligence-pack assembly, which is a checklist, not a standing unit.
A second team's weekly agenda would read *"wait for a raise"* — and
[[ORG_STRUCTURE]] §4's anti-sprawl rule would correctly mark it fiction inside 60 days
(`corporate.md:409-415`). That is org cosplay, and this charter names it as such.

**The split trigger is the first term sheet** — a live term-sheet conversation, or the
first instrument actually issued (`corporate.md:457-458`). Written here so the deferral has
a named end rather than becoming permanent by inertia.
[[positioning-fundraise-readiness-loops]] L-PFR-5 watches it in both directions: firing is a
split; **not firing for twelve months** is evidence the second half was never a team at all,
which is also a recordable finding rather than a silence.

Staged for the founder as **CORP-F3** (`corporate.md:496`).

## Boundaries

Owns outright:

- **The claim register** — every claim in force in outward material, its audience, its
  evidence (query id / `path:line` + symbol / recorded demo), and the date that evidence
  was last re-verified. Including **spoken** claims.
- **The send checklist** — the operational form of
  [[strategy-fundraising-directive]] R1–R3, applied per artifact.
- **The wedge sentence in practice** — keeping outward material reduced to
  `YC_WEDGE_PLAN.md:312`, and flagging drift.
- **The competitive read** — `YC_WEDGE_PLAN.md:328` and its successors.
- **Metric provenance** — for every number the company says out loud, where it comes from
  and what its weakest honest verb is.
- **The one-page diligence index** — where each artifact *would* live, who owns it. Not the
  artifacts (R4).
- **Request sequencing into Legal** — what instrument is needed, when, in what order.

Explicitly **not** owned:

| Not ours | Whose | The line |
|---|---|---|
| Drafting SAFE, board consent, stock purchase, advisor agreement, IP assignment, founder agreement | [[instruments-equity-charter]] | Legal drafts equity instruments; this team sequences and requests them; **the founder decides terms** (`corporate.md:505-506`) |
| The deck, the case study, the demo script, the customer-facing story | [[narrative-collateral-charter]] (Media & Brand M2) | *"Corporate → Strategy & Fundraising owns the YC path and the process. This team owns the craft of the artifact, never the decision to apply"* (`commercial.md:513-514`). One sentence, two owners: we decide what it says and whether it is true; M2 makes every artifact lead with it |
| Defining `dollars_recovered` | [[metric-contract-truth-assurance-charter]] | They own the definition; we own **not overstating it to investors** |
| Producing the recovery number | [[design-partner-operations-charter]] | S1 counts credits watched landing. We publish nothing stronger than S1 produces |
| Pricing and the revenue model | [[finance-pricing-charter]] | We consume the $20–50/mo lock as an input to OD-23; we do not set price |
| Whether an internal document is still true | [[standards-verification-charter]] | 2.3 owns corpus truth; we own **outward** truth at the moment of sending |
| Editorial approval of marketing content | [[editorial-gate-charter]] | Same rule, different surface |
| Deciding to raise | **The founder** | We prepare the decision and state its consequences |

## Metrics it moves

| Metric | Definition | Baseline |
|---|---|---|
| `strategy.claim_to_evidence_coverage` | % of claims in the current external narrative backed by a live citation — query, `path:line`, or reproducible demo | **Unmeasurable — no register exists.** This is the team's primary metric (`corporate.md:449-452`) and its first deliverable is the ability to compute it |
| `strategy.citation_drift_rate` | % of `path:line` citations in outward material that no longer resolve to the claimed content | **≈29%** — ≥2 of the 7 sources in `YC_WEDGE_PLAN.md` §6 have drifted or inverted |
| `strategy.claim_overstatement_count` | Claims published with a stronger verb than the evidence supports. **Target 0** | **0 published.** An unread zero — nothing has been published |
| `strategy.wedge_metric_instrumentation` | Binary: is the headline metric a live query or a slide? | **A slide** (`corporate.md:446-448`) |
| `strategy.diligence_pack_completeness` | % of a named diligence checklist that exists and is current. **Secondary** (`corporate.md:452`) | **0%.** No checklist, no data room, no cap table |

**Why the primary metric fails in the same direction as diligence** — and this is the
mechanical reason one team can hold both halves: a claim with no live citation is exactly a
claim that cannot be evidenced in a data room. The narrative failure and the diligence
failure are the *same defect*, observed at different moments by different readers. Two teams
would produce two registers and discover the divergence at the worst moment.

## Entry conditions — what this team is doing now

Unlike its Legal siblings, this team is **not** armed-and-waiting. It has continuous work
from day one: the claim register, and the send gate. Four units —
[[narrative-collateral-charter]], [[editorial-gate-charter]],
[[design-partner-operations-charter]] and Growth's content engine — are about to start
producing outward claims, and the register is far cheaper to establish before the first
artifact than to retrofit across twenty.

What is **deferred** until the split trigger: the data room, the diligence pack, the cap
table hygiene work, and any request into [[instruments-equity-charter]]. Beyond a one-page
index, none of it is built in advance ([[positioning-fundraise-readiness-directive]] R4).
Building it early is [[positioning-fundraise-readiness-premortem]] P4, and it is how a
one-team department grows the team it declined to charter.
