# Social & Community (M3) — Full Agenda

> **PROVISIONAL — no work done yet.** This is forecast, not fact.
>
> **This team is dormant.** Everything below is what happens *after* the entry trigger, plus
> the two things that are worth deciding before it.

## What

**Now:** one weekly line — has an article cleared G3? And two decisions worth making early,
both of which are registrations rather than launches.

**After the trigger:** distribution of Growth's output where restaurant operators gather,
with a published reply-routing rule and a metric that measures activation rather than
attention.

## How

### While dormant

```
weekly:  has an article cleared G3?  ─── no ──→ log it, stop
                                      │
                                      └─ yes ─→ wake the team, run the launch list
```

That is the entire operation. It is one line because a dormant team that generates weekly
deliberation is not dormant.

### The launch list, written now so it is not improvised later

1. Confirm the reply-routing rule and the address it points at. **Blocked on
   [[brand-identity-charter|M1]]** — today it would point at `support@wineops.ai`
   (`apps/web/src/pages/Help.tsx:18`).
2. Confirm the metric is reportable. **Blocked on [[conversion-funnel-charter|G5]]** — no
   product analytics exists today, so referred sessions cannot be attributed.
3. Pick one platform, not three. A single surface posted well beats three posted thinly, and
   three surfaces triple the unanswered-reply exposure in premortem mechanism 4.
4. Post the article that fired the trigger. That is the first post, by definition.
5. Publish the routing rule where a replier can see it, not only where we can.

### The two early decisions

**Handle reservation.** Reserving is not launching. A reserved handle with no posts is a
registration; a dormant feed is a feed. The company has just renamed itself, which is
exactly the window in which a new name is unclaimed. This is a founder decision, raised
below.

**Which platform, and where operators actually gather.** Worth researching before the
trigger, since the research does not require having anything to post. **No external research
was done in this session** — no platform, community, or audience was verified. That is
stated so the eventual choice is made on evidence rather than on the assumption baked into a
planning document.

## Why now

Only two things are "now": the trigger watch, and the handle question. Everything else is
deliberately not now.

The argument for staying dormant is written into [[social-community-charter]] and is
stronger than the argument for a soft launch: with one customer, no articles, and no
analytics, the account would have nothing to distribute, no way to measure whether it
worked, and would occupy the first result for the one search the company is guaranteed to
own. Fork **CM-F6** exists precisely because reasonable people disagree about whether a
dormant charter is preserved ambition or dead weight — this charter takes the preserved-
ambition side and makes the dormancy cheap enough to be honest: one line, once a week.

## Questions for the founder

1. **Reserve the handles now, or not at all?** Reserving is a registration, not a launch.
   The cost of not reserving is paid at the exact moment the team wakes up. The cost of
   reserving is a small amount of admin and the temptation to post.
2. **Which surface do you believe restaurant operators actually gather on?** Nothing was
   researched in this session, and a guess written into a charter becomes a fact six months
   later.
3. **CM-F6:** keep this team chartered dormant, or drop the charter and re-create it when
   the trigger fires? A charter that does nothing for a year is either preserved ambition or
   a document that makes the org look bigger than it is.
4. **Is the entry trigger right?** "First article clears G3" ties this team to Growth's
   pipeline. An alternative trigger — the first verified recovery number, which is a story
   worth telling regardless of whether an article exists — would tie it to Sales instead.
