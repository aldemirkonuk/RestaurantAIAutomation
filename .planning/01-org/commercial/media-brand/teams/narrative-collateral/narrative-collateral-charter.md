# Narrative & Collateral (M2) — Charter

**Parent:** [[media-brand-charter|Media & Brand]] · Commercial.
**Evidence grade: `PARTIAL`** — the argument exists and has been peer-reviewed; not one
artifact has been produced.

## Mandate

Own **the company story** — a narrative, not a feature list — and the artifacts that carry
it: the one-sentence pitch, the deck, the demo script, the case study, and a **simple
internal reference slide deck**. The unit of work is an argument built for a specific room,
in a specific order, ending in a specific ask.

The founder's framing is load-bearing and is recorded here verbatim in substance: *a
narrative, not just a feature list.* This repo's standing risk is that it contains a great
deal of good work, and reciting it is the most natural thing in the world to do.

## Why distinct from siblings

[[brand-identity-charter|M1]] owns what we are called; M2 owns what we claim, to whom, in
what order. Identity failures are noise — a mismatched wordmark is survivable. Narrative
failures lose the room in ninety seconds and do not get a second attempt. Different craft,
different cadence (deadline-driven rather than continuous), different failure mode.

## Boundaries

Owns outright: the story, the sentence, the deck, the demo script, the case study, and the
internal reference deck. Owns the *order of the argument* — which is the actual deliverable,
since the facts are already established elsewhere in the repo.

## Explicit non-goals

- **The YC path and the decision to apply** belong to
  [[strategy-charter|Corporate → Strategy & Fundraising]]. M2 owns the craft of the artifact
  and never the decision to send it.
- **Whether a number may be stated** belongs to [[editorial-gate-charter|Growth G3]]. M2
  writes the claim; G3 verifies it. This is not a formality — see the metric note below.
- **The metrics themselves** belong to [[analytics-bi-charter|Intelligence → Analytics & BI]].
- **The recovery number** belongs to [[design-partner-operations-charter|Sales S1]], which
  owns *verified dollars recovered*. M2 reports it; M2 does not produce it and may not
  estimate it.
- **Product and interaction design** belongs to [[design-charter|Product → Design]]. A deck
  is outward creative; a screen is not.
- **Brand voice definition** belongs to [[brand-identity-charter|M1]].

## Metrics it moves

**Primary: one headline claim.** Binary, checked per artifact: does this artifact lead with
the same sentence as every other one? Not a percentage, not a score. The failure this
measures is proliferation — a second sentence appearing in a second room, both defensible,
neither retired.

**Secondary, and it is a gate rather than a metric:** every number on an outward artifact
carries a source line. [YC_WEDGE_PLAN.md:31-33](../../../../YC_WEDGE_PLAN.md) establishes
that "dollars recovered" currently means *we asked*, not *we received* — verified recovery
requires watching an 812 credit memo arrive. Publishing the stronger claim would not be
marketing gloss. It would be false, and a number is the one thing a reader checks.

## The two deliverables, distinguished

The founder asked for two things and they have different obligations. Confusing them is
failure mechanism 2 in [[narrative-collateral-premortem]].

| | The story / external deck | The internal reference deck |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | Investors, customers, partners | Us |
| Claim standard | Every number source-lined; G3 fact-check mandatory | Honest about uncertainty; may say "we asked, not received" |
| Failure | A false claim in public | Being mistaken for the external one |
| Gate | G3 | Marked internal, on every page |
