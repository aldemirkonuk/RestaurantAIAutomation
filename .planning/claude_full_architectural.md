The art of building the nervous system that survives, scales, and evolves.                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  PROLOGUE: The Analogy That Will Shape Everything                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  A backend kitchen isn't a metaphor — it's literal. A real kitchen has:                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  - Mise en place — Everything in its place before cooking starts (state initialization)                                                                                                                                                                                       
  - Stations — Each chef owns a specific task (agent bounded context)                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  - The pass — Where orders flow through with tickets (message bus)                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - The expeditor — Coordinates all stations, ensures order arrives together (orchestrator)                                                                                                                                                                                    
  - The dishwasher — Unglamorous but critical (cleanup, garbage collection)                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  - The walk-in — Cold storage for raw materials (database)                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  - The line cook's "heard!" — Acknowledgment when order is received (ack/nack)                                                                                                                                                                                                
  - The dupe book — Audit trail of every order (event log)                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
  - The call-back — "86 the ribeye" (state invalidation)                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The best kitchens don't rely on geniuses. They rely on systems so good that an average cook executes at a Michelin-star level.                                                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Your backend must be the same. The goal is not to have agents that are individually brilliant. The goal is to have a system where an average agent performs flawlessly because the infrastructure carries them.

  PART I: THE SEVEN CORE PRINCIPLES                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  These are non-negotiable. If you violate any of them, the kitchen collapses under pressure.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Principle 1: Determinism                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Rule: Given the same input, the system must produce the same output. Always.                                                                                                                                                                                                 
  
  Why it matters: Agents make decisions. If the same situation produces different decisions, you can't debug, you can't test, you can't trust the system. You're building on quicksand.                                                                                        
                                                    
  What breaks without it:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
  - "Why did the agent accept that price yesterday but reject it today?" → unanswerable
  - Test results are flaky                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
  - Production bugs are irreproducible
  - Learning systems learn noise                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
                                                    
  How to implement:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - Freeze LLM temperature to 0 for decisions (use high temp only for creative generation)
  - Version every prompt (prompt_v2.3.1)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
  - Snapshot all inputs to every decision (what did the agent see when it decided?)
  - Seed all random choices with deterministic seeds                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
  - Use pure functions for business logic — no hidden state, no time-of-day effects                                                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The test: "Can I replay yesterday's events and get the same outcomes?" If no → you have a determinism leak.                                                                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Principle 2: Idempotency                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Rule: Every operation can be safely retried without causing duplicate side effects.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Why it matters: In distributed systems, messages WILL be delivered twice. Networks fail. Processes crash. If your system isn't idempotent, you'll double-charge customers, send duplicate emails, create ghost orders.                                                       
  
  What breaks without it:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
  - Supplier receives 3 identical negotiation emails
  - Shadow stock gets decremented twice from one order                                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  - Provider profile has contradictory updates from the same message
  - Users get notification spam                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  How to implement:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - Every message has a unique message_id (UUIDv7 preferred — sortable)                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  - Every operation checks "have I processed this before?" before executing                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  - Use INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING instead of blind inserts         
  - Deduplication tables with TTL (Redis with 24-hour expiry)                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  - Use natural keys where possible (not random_uuid, but order_id + supplier_id + attempt_number)                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The test: "Can I replay the same message 100 times and get the same result as playing it once?" If no → you have an idempotency hole.                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Principle 3: Replayability                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Rule: Any event in history can be replayed to rebuild current state.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Why it matters:                                   
  - Agent crashed mid-negotiation? Replay the session.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         
  - Bug in negotiation logic? Fix it, replay affected conversations.                                                                                                                                                                                                           
  - Customer asks "why did you do X?" Replay the exact sequence.    
  - Learning algorithm improved? Replay training data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  What breaks without it:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
  - You lose data forever when things go wrong                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
  - Debugging production requires cloning production data (security nightmare)                                                                                                                                                                                                 
  - You can't improve the system by re-processing historical events           
  - Audits become impossible                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  How to implement:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - Event sourcing for critical workflows (negotiations, orders, inventory changes)                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - Append-only event log (never UPDATE or DELETE events)                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
  - Event schema evolution strategy (never remove fields, only add new ones)                                                                                                                                                                                                   
  - Snapshots of state at checkpoints (so you don't have to replay from the beginning every time)                                                                                                                                                                              
  - Separate "event store" from "current state" (CQRS pattern)                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The test: "Can I blow away the current_state tables and rebuild them from the event log?" If no → you have ephemeral state bleeding in.                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Principle 4: Observability                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Rule: You must be able to answer any question about the system's current behavior in under 60 seconds.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Why it matters: You can't fix what you can't see. Most backend failures aren't code bugs — they're behavior bugs. "Why is this agent slow?" "Why is this queue backing up?" "Why did the LLM return JSON that failed parsing?"                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  What breaks without it:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
  - Debugging takes hours instead of minutes        
  - Silent failures accumulate                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
  - Performance regressions go unnoticed
  - Customer complaints precede your awareness                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
                                                    
  How to implement:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - Distributed tracing (OpenTelemetry) — every request has a trace ID that follows it through all agents
  - Structured logging (JSON, not strings) — every log entry has context (agent, session, correlation_id)                                                                                                                                                                      
  - Metrics for everything:                                                                              
    - RED metrics: Rate, Errors, Duration (per agent, per operation)                                                                                                                                                                                                           
    - USE metrics: Utilization, Saturation, Errors (per resource)                                                                                                                                                                                                              
    - Business metrics: Negotiations closed, margin captured, agent success rate                                                                                                                                                                                               
  - Alerts that matter (not "CPU is at 80%" — that's noise. Alert on "negotiation success rate dropped below 85%")                                                                                                                                                             
  - Log aggregation (Grafana Loki, Datadog, or even simple PostgreSQL for small scale)                                                                                                                                                                                         
  - Dashboards per agent: Every agent has a "health card" showing its current state                                                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The test: "Can a new engineer debug a production issue in 15 minutes without calling you?" If no → you're the bottleneck.                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Principle 5: Isolation (Bulkheads)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Rule: One failing component cannot take down the entire system.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Why it matters: If ProviderConversationAgent crashes because of a bad LLM response, it shouldn't prevent InventoryEngine from tracking stock. This is the blast radius concept.                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  What breaks without it:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
  - A single poison message crashes all 23 agents   
  - One supplier's unusual email format breaks the whole negotiation pipeline                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  - A database slowdown blocks all user requests                             
  - A memory leak in one agent exhausts shared resources                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  How to implement:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - Circuit breakers — if an external service fails 5 times, stop calling it for 60 seconds                                                                                                                                                                                    
  - Bulkheads — each agent has its own thread pool, its own database connection pool                                                                                                                                                                                           
  - Timeouts on everything — no unbounded waits, ever                               
  - Dead letter queues — poison messages go to DLQ, not to a retry loop that exhausts resources                                                                                                                                                                                
  - Graceful degradation — if LLM is down, use cached responses or fallback rules                                                                                                                                                                                              
  - Quotas per agent — no single agent can consume >20% of resources                                                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The test: "If I kill Agent X, do the other agents continue operating?" If no → you have shared state coupling.                                                                                                                                                               
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ---                                               
  Principle 6: Temporal Reasoning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Rule: Time is a first-class citizen, not a timestamp.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Why it matters: Your domain is deeply temporal:                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
  - "This promotion expires Friday"
  - "Supplier responds faster on weekdays"                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
  - "Q4 pricing is different from Q1"               
  - "Wine aged 30 days needs different handling"                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  - "Recurring orders fire every Monday"                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Most systems treat time as a created_at timestamp. You need more.                                                                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  What breaks without it:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
  - Missed expirations (promotion ended yesterday, system didn't notice)                                                                                                                                                                                                       
  - Bad scheduling (follow-up sent at 3 AM)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  - Seasonal intelligence is lost          
  - Historical analysis is wrong (comparing summer to winter without normalization)                                                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  How to implement:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - Temporal database patterns — valid_from and valid_to on records that change (prices, allocations, profiles)                                                                                                                                                                
  - Bi-temporal tracking — distinguish "when it happened" from "when we learned about it"                                                                                                                                                                                      
  - Scheduler as first-class service — use Temporal.io, Airflow, or dedicated scheduling (not cron jobs hidden in code)
  - Time zone awareness — always store UTC, convert to restaurant's local time for display                                                                                                                                                                                     
  - Deadline propagation — every workflow has a deadline, and sub-operations inherit it                                                                                                                                                                                        
  - Time-travel queries — "What did we know about Supplier X on January 15?"                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The test: "Can I ask 'what was the price of this wine 30 days ago?' and get an answer?" If no → you have a temporal blind spot.                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Principle 7: Evolvability                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                    
  Rule: The system must change faster than the market changes.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Why it matters: You're not building a fixed system. You're building a system that will change 100x in the next 2 years. If every change breaks something, you can't iterate fast enough.                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  What breaks without it:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
  - Adding a new agent takes weeks (too much coupling)
  - Schema changes require downtime                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - Feature rollouts are risky (big bang releases)
  - Deprecating old features is "too scary to try"                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  How to implement:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - Schema migrations with versioning — backward-compatible changes, no field removals                                                                                                                                                                                         
  - Feature flags — every new behavior is toggleable per restaurant, per user, per percentage                                                                                                                                                                                  
  - Contract testing — services declare their contracts, tests verify compatibility          
  - Blue-green deployments — always have a rollback plan                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
  - Semantic versioning for agents (v1.2.3) — old agents can coexist with new ones                                                                                                                                                                                             
  - Agent interface stability — the message formats agents expect should rarely change; internals can change freely                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The test: "Can I deploy a new version of an agent without breaking the system?" If no → you have tight coupling that will kill you.                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  PART II: THE ARCHITECTURAL PATTERNS (Battle-Tested)                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  These are the patterns that the world's best distributed systems use. Each solves a specific problem.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Pattern 1: Transactional Outbox                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The problem: You need to update a database AND publish a message. If one succeeds and the other fails, you have inconsistency.                                                                                                                                               
                                                    
  Example:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
  - Agent creates order in DB ✓                     
  - Agent tries to publish "order.created" event                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  - Network fails ✗                                 
  - Order exists in DB but no downstream agent knows about it                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
                                                             
  The fix: Write the message to an outbox table in the same transaction as the business data. A background worker polls the outbox and publishes messages. The transaction guarantees both happen or neither.                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  BEGIN TRANSACTION                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
    INSERT INTO orders (...)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
    INSERT INTO outbox (event_type, payload)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
  COMMIT
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  -- Separate worker:                               
  SELECT * FROM outbox WHERE published = false                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
    → Publish to RabbitMQ                           
    → Mark as published                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  
  Why you need it: Without this, you WILL lose messages under failure conditions. It's not "if," it's "when."                                                                                                                                                                  
                                                    
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Pattern 2: Saga Pattern (Orchestrated)            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The problem: Multi-step workflows need to either complete entirely or rollback cleanly.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Example: A negotiation workflow:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
  1. Create draft order
  2. Publish conversation intent                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  3. Generate LLM email                             
  4. Send via Gmail API                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  5. Wait for response 
  6. Parse response                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  7. Update order status                            

  What if step 4 fails? You need to:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
  - Delete the draft order
  - Cancel the conversation session                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - Log the failure reason                          
  - Notify someone                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                  
  The fix: Define a Saga — a sequence of steps with compensating actions for each.                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  saga = Saga("negotiation_workflow")
  saga.step("create_order", create_fn, compensate=delete_order)                                                                                                                                                                                                                
  saga.step("generate_email", generate_fn, compensate=discard_email)                                                                                                                                                                                                           
  saga.step("send_email", send_fn, compensate=mark_email_failed)                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  saga.step("wait_for_response", wait_fn, compensate=close_session)                                                                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  If any step fails, the saga runs compensations in reverse order.                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Tool recommendation: Temporal.io is the gold standard for this. It handles:                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  - Retries automatically                           
  - Compensations                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
  - Long-running workflows (days, weeks)            
  - Crash recovery (workflow resumes where it left off)
  - Version migration (workflows in flight get new logic)                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Why you need it: Your negotiations are multi-step, long-running, and failure-prone. Without sagas, you have orphaned half-completed workflows everywhere.                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Pattern 3: Event Sourcing (For Critical Aggregates)                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The problem: You need a complete audit trail AND the ability to replay history AND the ability to change how past events are interpreted.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Example: A provider's relationship history:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  - Today: We learn Supplier X is unreliable                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
  - Yesterday: We placed an order with them                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  - 3 days ago: They were late on a delivery        
  - 7 days ago: They offered a discount                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  If you only store "current state," you lose the rich history. If you store events, you can:
  - Replay events with new logic (e.g., "reliability score" algorithm v2)                                                                                                                                                                                                      
  - Answer "when did we first learn X?"                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  - Audit every decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The fix: For critical aggregates (providers, orders, inventory), store events instead of (or alongside) current state.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  events table:                                     
  - event_id                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
  - aggregate_id (provider_id, order_id, etc.)      
  - event_type ('PriceChanged', 'OrderPlaced', 'DiscountOffered')                                                                                                                                                                                                              
  - payload (JSON)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
  - sequence_number                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - created_at                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
                                                    
  Current state is a projection of events — rebuildable at any time.                                                                                                                                                                                                           
  
  Why you need it: Your system is fundamentally about LEARNING over time. Without event sourcing, you're learning from the snapshot, not the journey.                                                                                                                          
                                                    
  Warning: Don't event-source everything. Only aggregate roots that have temporal importance. Keep simple lookup tables as regular CRUD.                                                                                                                                       
                                                    
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Pattern 4: CQRS (Command Query Responsibility Segregation)
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The problem: Read patterns and write patterns are fundamentally different.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  - Writes: "Update the provider profile with new sentiment data" → small, transactional, consistent                                                                                                                                                                           
  - Reads: "Show me the dashboard with 47 providers, their sentiment trends, open negotiations, and recent messages" → large, aggregated, denormalized
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  If you use the same model for both, you get:      
  - Slow reads (too many joins)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                
  - Slow writes (too many indexes)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
  - Complex queries that break when schema changes
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The fix: Separate write models (optimized for transactions) from read models (optimized for queries).                                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Write side:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  - Normalized tables (providers, messages, sessions)                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  - Event log                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
  
  Read side:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
  - Denormalized views (provider_dashboard_view)    
  - Materialized aggregates (negotiation_stats)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                
  - Updated by subscribing to events
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Why you need it: Your dashboards are slow, and they'll get slower. CQRS fixes this at the architecture level.                                                                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Pattern 5: Actor Model for Agent Coordination     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The problem: You have 23 agents. If they share state directly, you have race conditions, deadlocks, and concurrency nightmares.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The fix: Treat each agent as an actor — an isolated entity that:                                                                                                                                                                                                             
  - Has its own state (no one else can read/write it)                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  - Communicates only via messages                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
  - Processes one message at a time                 
  - Can crash without affecting other actors                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
                                            
  This is the Erlang/Akka model, and it's perfect for agent systems.                                                                                                                                                                                                           
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Implementation:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              
  - Each agent has a mailbox (their own queue)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
  - Agents NEVER share memory (only via message passing)                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
  - Agent supervisor restarts crashed agents            
  - "Let it crash" philosophy — don't try to recover from weird state, just restart                                                                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                   
  Why you need it: Without this, your 23 agents WILL interfere with each other under load. It's mathematically certain.                                                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  In your stack: RabbitMQ + individual queues per agent gets you 80% of the way. You're already doing this partially. Formalize it.                                                                                                                                            
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Pattern 6: The World Model                        
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The problem: Every agent needs to know "what's the current state of the world?" If they query the database every time, you have:
  - Slow decisions                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
  - Inconsistent views (agent A sees one state, agent B sees another)                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  - Database overload                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The fix: A world model — a single place (Redis or similar) that holds the current operational state. Agents read from the world model for fast decisions, write to it when state changes.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  world_model:                                      
  - active_negotiations: { provider_id: session_state }                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  - stock_levels: { wine_id: current_quantity }                                                                                                                                                                                                                                
  - provider_sentiment: { provider_id: current_sentiment }                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
  - active_promotions: { promo_id: status }                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Why you need it: Agents making decisions on stale data is a silent killer. The world model ensures they have the latest view.                                                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Warning: World model is NOT the source of truth — the database is. World model is a fast read layer. If it's lost, it can be rebuilt from the database.                                                                                                                      
                                                    
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Pattern 7: The Decision Log                       
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The problem: Agents make decisions. You need to know WHY they made those decisions.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The fix: Every decision is logged with its full context.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  decision_log:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                
  - decision_id                                     
  - agent_name
  - decision_type ('accept_price', 'counter_offer', 'escalate')
  - inputs (JSON: everything the agent saw)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
  - reasoning (JSON: LLM output, rules applied)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                
  - output (what the agent decided)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            
  - confidence (0-1)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
  - created_at                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Why you need it:                                  
  - Debug: "Why did the agent reject this price?"
  - Learn: "Which tactics worked best with Supplier X?"                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  - Audit: "Show me all decisions the agent made for Restaurant Y"
  - Improve: Train ML models on decision data                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The test: Can you pick a random negotiation from last week and explain exactly why the agent made every decision it made?                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  ---                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  Pattern 8: Chaos Engineering from Day One                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                    
  The problem: Production failures happen. If you've never tested them, you don't know how the system behaves.
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  The fix: Deliberately inject failures in development/staging and verify the system recovers.                                                                                                                                                                                 
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Chaos experiments:                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           
  - Kill an agent randomly — does it restart? Do other agents handle the temporary absence?
  - Add 500ms latency to database calls — do timeouts fire correctly?                                                                                                                                                                                                          
  - Drop 10% of messages — does DLQ capture them? Do retries work?   
  - Simulate LLM timeout — does fallback logic execute?                                                                                                                                                                                                                        
  - Fill disk to 95% — does the system degrade gracefully?
  - Corrupt a message in transit — is it rejected safely?                                                                                                                                                                                                                      
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Tools: Chaos Monkey (Netflix), Litmus, Gremlin, or simple custom scripts.                                                                                                                                                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               
  Why you need it: Resilience is a property you can't test by hope.
